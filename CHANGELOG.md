## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- **Plugins** — Plugins: `plugins/<name>/` directories built as `ge::<name>`, each declaring the engine targets and plugins it depends on; the package now publishes `ge::mountain` (the generated ski mountain: `mountain/mapgen/*.hpp`, `mountain/analysis/*.hpp`) and keeps `ge::racing`, whose headers now come from `plugins/racing/` under the same `racing/*.hpp` include spelling.
- **The window and the GPU device** — `engine/platform` opens a window over SDL3 and reads its input into a per-frame snapshot; `engine/gpu` makes a native GPU device over Dawn's prebuilt release (the adapter, its feature tiers, validation and agent-actionable error lines), compiles embedded WGSL, creates pipelines through a manifest that counts any made after load, presents to the window at vsync, renders offscreen and reads frames back, and times passes with timestamp queries. `make shaders-check` compiles every shader with Tint offline. Both are built with the engine's own tree (`GE_PLATFORM`, `GE_GPU`) and are not part of the published package yet.
- **GPU and window labs** — `Lab::shot` takes a `Det`: a `measured` picture (a GPU frame, which each adapter rasterises in its own way) keeps its pixels out of the lab's digest, and its sidecar and contact sheet say so. Two engine labs land with it: `gpu-capture` (the GPU test scene captured headless and held to its golden on the pinned software adapter) and `frame-pace` (the window's frame loop timed: present intervals and the run clock's steps a frame).
- **The frame core** — `engine/render` (built with the GPU as `ge_render`) draws a frame: a `FrameView` and its reversed-z camera, a pass table checked for order, per-pass GPU timings and stats, a physically based sky from lookup tables built by compute with aerial perspective behind one haze function, a GPU-culled geometry clipmap for any heightfield, and a post pass (exposure from the sky, tone curve, dither, encoding). Fall Line's window now shows its generated mountain, and the `world` lab photographs it from named views headless. Not part of the published package yet.
- **The synth** — `engine/audio`, published as `ge::audio`: a deterministic synthesizer rendering 48 kHz stereo float with no platform. One-shot tones (sine, square, saw, triangle; glide, detuned pair, vibrato, soft saturation) and coloured noise (white, pink, brown), each through a sweeping state-variable filter and an attack/hold/decay envelope; steered layers for continuous beds; four buses for faders; a shared echo and a soft-knee master limiter. Calls cross from the game thread to the render thread through a lock-free ring, and the same calls render the same bytes on every platform. Sounds are plain data (`SoundDef`, `PlayShape`, `play_def`), beds are racks of layers (`rack_apply`), and `measure` meters a buffer (level, peak, octave bands, centroid, onset, length, balance) and `wav_bytes` encodes it. `engine/platform` gains the audio device (`open_audio`), pulled on SDL's audio thread.
- **The ski area's massif, lifts, runs and lanes** — The `mountain` plugin builds a ski area's first stages: the massif, the lifts and their station pads, every run walked down the mountain, and the access lanes, drag lifts and branch lanes that make every piste reachable from a lift and back (`resort_build.hpp`).
- **The ski area's runs graded and pressed** — `resort_graded` grades every run of a ski area onto the mountain in the order walked, lays its drops, kickers and drifts clear of the junctions down it, presses it into the ground and the packed field without touching the runs pressed before it, and measures each run's colour on the pressed mountain.
- **Labs** — Labs: an engine-level contract (`engine/lab`: `LabSpec`, `GE_LAB`, the runner, captioned pictures on a CPU raster, PNG, reports and digests) that every subsystem, plugin and game plugs its sim, scenario, picture, drive and bench labs into, built as the source tree's `labs` executable and checked by `make labs-smoke`; the first labs are `noise`, `run-clock`, `level`, `analyze` and `mapgen-cost`. Labs build only in the source tree and are not part of the installed package.
- **The ski area's ramps off the tops and its access on the pressed mountain** — `resort_access` lays a groomed ramp from every top station's pad down to each run leaving it, settles the drag lifts on the colours the pressed runs measure, slides every bottom station clear of the runs (refusing an area where one fits nowhere), and grades the way to the peak's chair's queue again.
- **The ski area's own features, its hub and its wind tunnels** — `resort_features` stands the off-piste kickers and cliffs clear of every run, pad, ramp and the way to the next queue, folds the wind crust into the packed field, lays the hub across the valley floor and grooms it (cutting back the drifts it reaches), grooms the pads, the way and the ramps, and lays the two wind tunnels along the hub on beds graded into the floor.
- **The ski area's woods, its day and its courses, and the resort and junctions labs** — `resort_woods` grows the woods by height under the tree line (closed low down, thinning through the ecotone, a region's own curve) and keeps every trunk off the runs' corridors, the lifts' lines, the pads, the ramps, the way to the next queue and the hub. `resort_courses` deals the day, turns the face to its sun, and composes a course down the network from every piste's top to the village (`compose_course`, `course_arc`), each in R28's length band and coloured by its steepest stretch; `build_resort` runs every stage in order. The `resort` lab draws a ski area from above and with its courses picked out, with its lifts, runs, courses and attempts as tables; the `junctions` lab finds where the groomed snow steps where runs meet and draws the worst as relief.
- **The skier plugin** — The skier plugin, `ge::skier` (`skier/*.hpp`): a skier and his pair of skis on a mountain's snow. `step_skier` advances one step (the snow's sink and friction, the edge's grip, the carve and the pivot, the tuck and the skater's push, the jump, flight and landing, trunks and the wipeout); `limits.hpp` states what a skier can do for any pilot to plan on (`carve_speed`, `terminal_speed`, `edge_for_curvature`); `synthetic.hpp` and `scenarios.hpp` are the hand-built maps and the staged ride scenarios its tests and labs share.
- **The ge command line** — `ge new` scaffolds a game repo on the engine release it belongs to, `ge skills sync` keeps that repo's engine skills on its pinned release, `ge doctor` holds the repo to the engine's rules (guidance links, skills, the pin, FP flags, sources, diagnostics) and `ge lessons` reads its lessons back; packages ship `bin/ge`, and the lab framework is published as `ge::lab`.
- **The mountain's own shadow** — The terrain now shades itself: `engine/render/horizon.hpp` keeps two horizon maps either side of the sun's bearing, baked by compute and rebaked one at a time as the sun turns, and the terrain's draw blends them into soft ridge shadows that move with the hour. The `shadows` lab draws the maps from above and counts the bakes of a sun swept across a day.
- **The depth prepass, the depth pyramid and TAA-U** — The frame now draws the ground's depth first and shades each covered pixel once in an `equal`-tested pass that also writes a motion target. `engine/render/hiz.hpp` reduces the depth to a pyramid of farthest depths for culling, and `engine/render/taa.hpp` resolves a jittered frame, rendered at 25 to 100 % of the output's size, into a history at the output's size (Halton jitter, motion reprojection, a YCoCg clip box, a reactive mask, a reset on a cut). The `world` lab takes `--taa` and `--scale` and measures the shimmer and the overdraw; the new `taa` lab measures ghosting on a moving camera against the converged still.
- **The skier's beds and Fall Line's sound** — `plugins/skier` gains a presentation half, `ge_skier_app` (published as `ge::skier_app`): the wind on the skier's body (rush, roar, whistle, flutter, buffet) and his skis on the snow (the groomer's hiss, the powder's hush, the edge, chatter and the skid) as steered beds, read off a `SkierState` by `ride_bed_update`. A plugin's `app/` half is now built when its `ge_add_plugin` call names an `APP` list. Fall Line gains its bank of one-shots, the event route (gates, misses, the finish, landings, wipeouts, trunks, shoulders, resets), the listener table and the faders, the `audition` lab, and a `fall_line` window that plays the bot's run through the audio device.
- **Generator version 8 builds a ski area raced down one of its courses** — `generate_level` at version 8 builds the ski area and stands one of its courses up as the map: the course's line is the track, its kickers, drops and drifts are named by their arc down it, slalom gates are set down it off its own hash (`course_gates`: alternately left and right of the line, inside the piste, on the line over a kicker or a drop), its day is its own (`course_day`: the sun held to the face's bearing), and the area is published as `Level::resort` (its runs, lifts, courses, village, hub and wind tunnels). An attempt is accepted when `analysis::analyze_resort` passes the area and at least one course passes `analysis::analyze_course`; the course raced is the one asked for by id (`GenerateOptions::course`), else the nearest colour asked for (`grade`), else the colour the seed deals (`deal_grade`). Versions 9 and 10 are refused by name until the ski routes are built. Fall Line's bot weaves through gates that stand off the line. The `level` and `analyze` labs take `--version=1,8`.
- **A slalom set over a built map (R31), its analysis and the slalom lab** — `mapgen::set_slalom(level, run)` sets a slalom over a built map (a single piste or a ski area's course): the steepest stretch of the piste that drops a slalom's vertical, prepared for the race (`course_prep`: the piste's kickers levelled, a start drop cut out of the hut's door, the course groomed hard and combed smooth, the woods cut for it and its finish arena), and its pole gates set like a course setter sets them (open gates in a rhythm, hairpins, verticals and delays down the middle, the last gate fast), with the racing line round them. The second run is set afresh on the same stretch. The map names the course in `Level::race` and keeps the map it was set over. `analysis::analyze_race` holds a course to R31, and `analyze_level` answers it on a map with a course. The `slalom` lab draws both runs straightened and the map from above, with the gate table.
- **Instance families with two-phase Hi-Z culling** — `render/instances.hpp` draws repeated meshes as GPU-driven instance families: packed vertices pulled from storage, up to four LODs a mesh with a dithered band between them, one indirect draw a LOD bucket, and a two-phase cull against the last frame's and this frame's depth pyramids; `render/mesh.hpp` builds and packs their meshes, and `skier/app/figure.hpp` poses a placeholder skier from a `SkierState`.
- **The downhill, super-G and giant slalom set over a built map (R32, R33, R36) and their lab** — `mapgen::set_downhill`, `set_super_g` and `set_giant_slalom(level, run)` set the speed events over a built map, each as a new map whose `Level::race` names the course and keeps the map it was set over. The downhill runs the whole piste (on a ski area, the course with the most vertical in its band, `downhill_course_of`): a racing line that bends less than the piste, red speed gates centred on it about every 80 m and none on a jump, the A-nets along both edges and a speed trap late on a straight. The super-G and the giant slalom start lowered down the course with the most vertical (`most_vertical_course_of`) into their band, their gates turning the racer side to side as many as the vertical's direction changes ask, each turning pole inside the line's apex, none on a jump's lip or landing; the giant slalom has two runs. The course preparation now shaves the crests round for a speed event. `analysis::analyze_race` holds each to its rule, and the `speed-events` lab draws every course straightened and the map from above, with the gate tables.
- **A speed-skiing track cut down a built map's face (R34) and the speed-ski lab** — `mapgen::set_speed_ski(level, run)` cuts a speed-skiing track straight down a built map's face. The line is searched over every column of the face and a few bearings either side of the fall line. Each line is graded to a profile that never rises, with its crests and knees rounded and held within a few metres of the ground. Every start on it is skied by a point mass with the top class's numbers. The track whose final comes through the 100 m timing zone nearest the speed the map deals, inside 180–240 km/h, wins, provided its run-out stops the racer before the map ends. The track becomes the map's `track`: its ground is graded and groomed, its 25 m safety margin and finish enclosure are cleared, and it keeps clear of the lift stations. Its two runs are the qualification, from a start lowered until it gives 12 km/h less, and the final, from the top. `speed_ski_aim` gives the zone speed a run was set for and `speed_ski_lines` the braking line and the finish enclosure. `analysis::analyze_race` holds the track to R34, and the `speed-ski` lab draws its profile from the side and the map from above.
- **A ski-cross course built over a map (R35) and the ski-cross lab** — `mapgen::set_ski_cross(level)` builds a ski-cross course on a stretch of a built map's piste: on a ski area, down the course `ski_cross_course_of` picks. The stretch is the one whose course comes nearest an 18 % mean gradient, crossing the fewest drops and lying lowest. The course is a line of its own, 14 m wide: a 100 m start straight below a four-door start gate and a steep start ramp, then legs swung either side of the piste's line and rounded into turns, then a finish straight onto a 60 m finish area. Its profile is the ground combed along it, never rising, its crests and knees rounded. Every turn of more than 20° is a berm banked up to 28°. The straights carry rollers, jumps and step-downs dealt off the map's seed, with a jump on the start straight and a finish jump on the last. The ground is graded and groomed to the built line, the course fenced, the trees within 12 m of it and in the finish area cleared, and the piste's kickers and drops on it removed. The gates are triangular flags: a turning gate on the inside of every berm and a corridor gate before every feature, red and blue in turn. The course's jumps are kickers on its track. `analysis::analyze_race` holds the course to R35, and the `ski-cross` lab draws it close up, from the side and on the map.
- **The terrain park on a tricks map (R20) and the park lab** — `GenerateOptions::tricks` builds a map for a tricks run: the seed's mountain with a terrain park laid down the line it is raced on. The park is a ladder of groomed kickers in three sizes (a 1 m lip for a grab, a 2 m lip for a flip and a 3.2 m lip for a flip with a spin), laid in turn from 120 m past the start gate to 120 m short of the finish, 20 m of piste apart, each where the line barely turns and falls no steeper than 22 %. Every kicker is built against the horizontal past its lip: a level deck, a landing slope dug under the piste and a run-out back up to the line. The gates keep off every kicker, with a run-in falling at least the lip's height from the gate before each ramp. On the single piste the park is planned inside the attempt, so the drifts keep off it; on a ski area it is planned down the chosen course (or the first other course that carries one) and the course's gates are set round it. An attempt with no park that stands is refused for the next one. Park kickers carry their size (`Kicker::trick`) and their built shape (`Kicker::shape`), are named `T1…`, and are held by `analysis::check_park`. A map built without tricks is unchanged. The `park` lab draws the park close up, from the side and on the map.
- **The ski routes (R42) on generator versions 9 and 10, and the routes lab** — Generator versions 9 and 10 now build: a ski area with its SKI ROUTES, the orange runs past black. A route is found on the built mountain, never sculpted: two cheapest-path searches over a grid of 10 m squares (from the rim of every chair's and gondola's top station, and back from every run's corridor) are joined through one square whose fall line is steeper than a black and no steeper than 110 %, and the joined line is kept only where it is 400–3000 m long, falls 250 m or more, never pitches past 130 % over 20 m, stays off the pistes' shoulders and is clear of the trees and the lifts. Each top keeps its best line and the map up to two, apart. Version 9 lays the shortest line down. Version 10 lays the WILD line: a step costs more straight down the fall line and less through the woods, along an edge and over a cliff's face, a line may drop off a crag, and it is threaded between the trunks; the trunks on it are felled and up to three jumps (`O1…`, `Kicker::route`) with a dug gap past each lip are cut into its rolls. The routes are laid before the ski area is analysed, and `analysis::check_routes` holds them. Maps from versions 1 and 8 are unchanged to the digest. The `routes` lab draws the area with its routes, each route close up and each from the side.
- **Ski jumping's hills (R43) set over any built map, and the skijump-hills lab** — A ski jumping hill can now be set over any built map (`set_ski_jump`). The LARGE hill (HS 140) and the NORMAL hill (HS 106) are each built element by element from the construction norm's formulas over a row of free choices (`venue_rules::ski_jump`): the start tower's deck, the in-run at 35° with its transition and table to the edge, the knoll, the landing radius through P, K and L, and the transition onto a level out-run, with the start gates no more than 0.4 m of height apart and the design gate found by a point mass down the in-run. The hill is laid as a STRAIGHT VENUE (R37, `straight_venue.hpp`): a line of its own down the face where the profile cuts and fills least, clear of the lifts and the village, the ground graded to it, groomed, and the woods and the finish arena cleared. The hill is an analytic surface (`Level::venue`) that `ground_at` and `normal_at` answer off inside its width, with the grid cut beneath it and the in-run a raised deck. The map's digest reads the hill where there is one; every other map is unchanged. The `skijump-hills` lab tabulates the earth each hill moves and draws its profile and its map.

### Changed

- **core/num** — `core/js.hpp` and `core/js_text.hpp` are now `core/num.hpp` and `core/num_text.hpp`, in namespace `ge::core::num`: the simulation's number semantics (rounding, integer wrap, NaN-aware max/min, sign, remainder) and numbers as text, unchanged in behaviour.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- **Published releases** — Every release is a GitHub Release with a CMake package (`find_package(gameengine2)`, targets `ge::core`, `ge::racing` and `ge::fp`) per platform, the source, SHA-256 checksums and build provenance, mirrored to GitHub Packages as an OCI artifact at `ghcr.io/niclaslindstedt/gameengine2`.
