title: A generated face has no flat ground for a lake; cut the slope above into a headwall
date: 2026-11-01

On seed 38 the flattest open site 60 m round in the upper half of the mountain still falls 43 m
across 180 m (about 13 degrees): a search that asks for a flat site (relief under 0.4 of the
radius) finds none on a generated face. A tarn takes the shore's LOWEST ground as its surface, so
the ground above it is higher than the water: ease that slope down to the shore over the same
reach the low side is raised over, and the cut reads as a cirque's headwall. Allow relief up to
0.8 of the radius. Keep the reshaped reach (twice the shore out) clear of the runs, not just the
shore, or the cut moves a piste.
