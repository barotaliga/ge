---
title: Plan a feature the map must carry inside the attempt, so a refusal moves to the next one
date: 2026-10-22
scope: plugins/mountain/mapgen/generate.cpp
concepts: [terrain-park, attempt, refusal, drift, sweep]
---

The park was first laid on the finished, accepted map, with the map kept without a park where none
fitted. Only 9 of 30 single pistes carried one: the drifts had already been dealt across the
straight, moderate stretches a kicker needs, and an accepted map had no way back. Planned inside
the attempt, before the drifts (which then keep off it), with the attempt refused when no park
fits, 30 of 30 single pistes carried one. A ski area takes the same rule
one level up: try every course, and refuse the attempt when none carries a park. A feature a map
must carry is a condition of the attempt, not a decoration of its result; and planning it before
whatever is dealt round it keeps a map built without it unchanged, because nothing is drawn.
