---
title: A prop's sink is a share of its width, so a flat shape must sink by a small share
date: 2026-10-28
scope: engine/detail/props.cpp
concepts: [props, sink, moss, dome, buried, lab]
---

`sink_share` sinks a prop by a share of its SIZE, which is its width. The moss cushion is about a
fifth as high as it is wide, and the default rule sank it by 35% of its width, so nearly every
cushion sat under the floor and the `detail-props` close-ups showed only slivers of green. Nothing
failed: the scatter counted the moss as placed. Set a rule's sink from its shape's height over its
width (a dome, a clod, a tuft's root: a few percent; a round lump: a quarter to a third), and look
at a close-up of every new kind at standing height before trusting a count.
