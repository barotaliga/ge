title: Measure what a fall adds against a twin layer with nothing falling
date: 2026-10-31

The old snow under a fall settles while it falls, fastest near 0 C, so "the column's depth minus
what it started at" undercounts the storm and can come out negative on a mild day (20 cm of
powder at -1 C sinks faster than 12 kg/m2 of new snow builds). The `snow-weather` lab's `light`
chart reads what the storm added as the difference from a second layer under the same air with
`fall` emptied, which isolates the new snow's own depth (14.3 cm at -12 C, 9.2 cm at -1 C).
Any comparison of falls across temperatures wants the same twin.
