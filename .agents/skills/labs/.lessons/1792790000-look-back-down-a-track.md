The first TURN shot stood behind the skier's place and looked on along his way, at the run's last
sample: the snow ahead held no track yet, and the picture showed nothing of the turn. A track is
what is behind the skier, so a view of one stands past the place and looks back, and the place is
picked a second or more before the run's end. Finding a racer coming down another's line, the
player never did: a field keeps its lanes from the start, a lane apart. Search every pair of
racers, after the start (where they stand abreast within a few metres), with a reach a lane wide.
