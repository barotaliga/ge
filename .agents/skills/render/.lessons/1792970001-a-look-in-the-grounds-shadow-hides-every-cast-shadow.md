At seed 38's own hour, the runs the `crowd-look` lab films lie in the mountain's shadow (the
horizon map's). A figure's shadow is only darker where the sun reaches, so the atlas drew fifteen
amateurs (the `shadows.figures` pass went from 2 ms to 14 ms) and the pictures were the same to
the bit. A pass that costs more and changes no pixel is a view with no sun on it, not a broken
pass. Before judging a shadow change, film it at an hour that lights the look (`--hour=11` there),
and read the pass's GPU time beside the picture diff.
