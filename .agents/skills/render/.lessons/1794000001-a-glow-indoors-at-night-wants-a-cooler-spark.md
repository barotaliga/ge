A blackbody's light climbs steeply with its heat. At 2300 K a spark is about a hundred thousand
times brighter than a torch-lit room the night's exposure is set for. A hundred of them, under
the modern look's bloom, turned the whole frame white. The classic look has no bloom and showed
the same sparks as clean points, so it hid the problem. Explosions at 1650 K, embers at 1400 K
and a teleport's column at 1700 K, cooling fast, read as fire in the modern look. Tune a glowing
particle in the look with bloom, in the darkest room it will be seen in.
