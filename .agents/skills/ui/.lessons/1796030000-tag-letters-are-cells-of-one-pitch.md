The sequel's tags set every letter in a cell of an even 17 px pitch (16 or 17 classic px) and the
tag's whole 32 px height, so a letter is its cell, rims and all, and cells laid edge to edge read as
one tag; no trimming and no gaps. Measure each tag's letter rows first: GAME's sit a row lower than
the other four, so its G is lifted. The tags differ by a tenth in brightness: shade every piece to
the darkest tag's surface or each seam shows. Lay pieced letters over a plain column that is flat
from rim to rim (OPTIONS' column after its S): a column near a tag's end carries the end's curve
into the rims as a dotted line. A small letter keeps the cell's rims and drops four band rows and
three columns (non-adjacent), and the lit set takes the plain set's dropped lines so both are the
same width. Prototype the piecing on the pack's PNGs in a scratch script first: a W from two cropped
V halves read badly, one from two V's sampled every other column read well.
