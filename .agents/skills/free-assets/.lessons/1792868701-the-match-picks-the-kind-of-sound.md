---
title: A match on length, bands and envelope finds the right kind of sound without listening
date: 2026-10-24
scope: games/shooters_edge/content/sounds
concepts: [sound, match, spectrum, envelope, audition, replacement]
---

Ranking a keyword pool of candidates against the original by the distance in log length, in
log energy over eight bands and in the loudness envelope over ten slices put a fitting sound in
the top three nearly every time (a cannon for a double shotgun, a crunching explosion for the
explosion, a short metal tick for a nail on a wall). The pool decides more than the score: a
pool too wide lets a gunshot win "fire crackle". It says nothing about feel, so the PR says the
choice was not listened to and an audition page goes to the user.
