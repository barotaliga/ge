On the reference Mac one install holds the re-release's single 179 MB `pak0.pak`, another the
classic `pak0.pak` and `pak1.pak`. The demo benchmark's rounds (#324) ran on the classic one; a
round on the other measures other content and reads as a change that is not one. Say which
`--basedir` a number came from (its packs' sizes or SHA-256, never its home path). The enhancer's
before-and-after pages from the player's pack stay on that machine, outside the tree.
