---
title: A surface's night cooling must fade after sunrise, not stop at it
date: 2026-10-31
scope: engine/wind/climate
concepts: [surface_at, sunrise, radiative-cooling, snow, discontinuity, chart]
---

`surface_at` cooled a clear night's surface up to 4 K until the moment of sunrise and then not at
all; on bare ground the sun's warming hid it, but on snow (no sun warming) the surface jumped
2.5 K at dawn, plain on the `snow-weather` lab's `thaw` chart. The cooling now fades over the two
hours after sunrise, the same shape `night_chill` uses. Chart any function of the hour over a
whole day (or two) before trusting it: a jump at sunrise or sunset is invisible in a table of a
few hours.
