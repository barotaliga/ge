A camera 7 m over the algal pond looking steeply down drew the running jump's long crater and its
rings as nothing but flat brown: an opaque water shows its waves only through the sky mirrored in
their slopes, and the Fresnel share looking down is a few percent. To show a shape on the surface,
look along it from low (3 m up and 6 m back caught the leaning rings; 1.6 m from the side caught
the crown). An analytic slope on a far field that drops the amplitude's own derivative is only near
the height's a few waves out: test a slope against the height's differences over the crater, not
over the far field.
