title: Campaign bots finish Ground Zero's last map
---

On Ground Zero's last map, a campaign bot that got down into a grinder pit stood still there for the rest of the run. Its guard at the pit's lip now keeps off the killing floor itself rather than everything near it, and the pit's floor round the killing strip is now off-limits, so the bot no longer goes down there. The bot also never headed for the map's exit. The exit wants the nuke at a lock and then ends the game, and the bot only knew exits that change the level directly. It now follows the lock and the relays to the end. The unit lab also counts the game's last exit, which asks for no next map, as finished. With the gear the earlier maps bring, the bot now finishes the map in 228 s.
