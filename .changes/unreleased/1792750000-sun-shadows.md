type: Added
title: The sun's cached shadow cascades and the figures' shadow atlas
---

`render/shadows.hpp` draws the sun's shadows of what stands on the ground: two cascades round the eye, redrawn only when the eye drifts or the sun turns past a threshold (`update_cascade`, a pure CPU decision), and an atlas of tiles that follows each moving figure, redrawn every frame. Instance families gain caster slots (`create_family_casters`), the terrain and every instance read the maps through `sun_shadow`, and Fall Line's `world` lab gains a `wood` view and the cascades' redraw counter over a whole run.
