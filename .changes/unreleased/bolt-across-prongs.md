title: The lightning gun's arc crackles across its prongs
---

While the lightning gun fires in the enhanced look, a fine, jagged arc of the same bolt crackles across the gun's two prongs, struck afresh with the bolt, in place of the pack model's own flash. The prongs' tips are read off the model's own arc (`classicenhance::find_arc`, `viewmodel::HeldModel::arc_ends`), so the arc rides the firing animation.
