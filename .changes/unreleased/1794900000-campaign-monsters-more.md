Our free campaign mod (`qc/campaign/`) has five more monsters: the swordsman swings while charging, the brute saws up close and lobs bouncing grenades from afar, the ghoul throws flesh and shrugs off small hits (a big one knocks it down until it stands again whole), the flyer keeps level with its enemy and spits two at a time, and the eel bites in its water. Monsters' missiles and the rifleman's blast now aim up or down at their enemy instead of always rising.
