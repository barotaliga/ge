title: Ground Zero's map things work: its plats, steam jets, angered monsters and the disguise
---

Under Ground Zero's rules a `func_plat2` goes to whichever end a player wants (called, toggled, resting at the top, or waiting for a first use), twice as quick in deathmatch. Steam jets puff and, fired from a timer, last their second (temporary entity 40 on the wire). A `target_anger` sets its monsters firing at a point, `trigger_disguise` and the cheat `disguise` make monsters take a player for one of theirs until their own shot gives them away, and co-op on the rising-lava mine starts above the lava, leaves out what is marked not for co-op (`coop_baseq2` 1 restores the base game's rule). Monsters that lose sight of a player follow the map's hint paths toward them. The reactor core, the black light, the orb and the player killer work too.
