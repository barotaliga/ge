type: Added
title: The sequel's weapons fire and its players fight and die
---

The sequel's rules give every player a weapon in hand that steps through its frames ten times a second: each of the ten weapons fires at its own period, the machinegun, chaingun and hyperblaster keep firing while attack is held, an empty weapon clicks and the best one with ammo is raised, and a number key or `use <weapon>` changes it. Bullets, pellets and the rail are lines; bolts, grenades, rockets and the BFG's ball fly. A blow pushes its target (a rocket at the feet lifts its shooter), is shared with the armour and takes the rest from health; a player who dies is scored and named in an obituary. Lab `se-sequel-fight` charts every weapon's damage over time and the rocket jump.
