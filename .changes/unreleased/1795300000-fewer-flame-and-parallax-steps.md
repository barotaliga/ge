Flames march 14 steps through their gas instead of 28, and the textures' parallax takes two steps for each texel its ray crosses (at most as many as before): the modern look's GPU frame at 1440p on the M3 Max drops from 4.34 to 4.04 ms. A thin wind-torn flame shows a finer grain in a single still frame; in motion the resolve averages it out.
