---
type: Added
title: A pool over open air shows its underside from below
---

Under classic-enhance, a pool whose water hangs over open air (like the start map's drop to the fourth episode) now shows its underside to a player in the room below. It is drawn the way the top is: the same waves, the room below mirrored in it, and the room above seen bent through the water. Before, nothing was drawn there and the room above showed through a hole. The engine's water takes this as `render::WaterDraw::open_below`.
