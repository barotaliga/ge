title: The army's snowmobiles
---

Fall Line's hunt now sends the army's snowmobiles after him (`games/fall_line/sim/army_sled.hpp`), two up on the free ride's own machine and the bot's hands: the roster's `snowmobiles` from three stars and the ground waves' on top, called from beside the snowmobile spot by the village. Each reads the snow ahead (`army_drive.hpp`: a fan of headings costed by the rise, the drop, the wind tunnels, the map's edge and the trunks), climbs the runs while he is far and goes straight at him when near. Where the army shoots to kill it makes short halts side-on to him outside its stand-off; where it does not it runs him down and its crew get off and take him in. Its crew right it when it rolls, dig it out when it is caught, and wait below a face too steep to climb. Once the hunt is over the machines drive home. The `army-sleds` lab shows it on seed 38, and `tests/golden/fall_line_army_sled.txt` holds it. `fall_line_army.txt` and `fall_line_wanted.txt` move because the machines join every hunt on the free ride.
