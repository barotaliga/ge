type: Added
title: A hub's buttons open the game
---

`<game>_cli link register` makes the game's links open in it (Linux, macOS, Windows), and `<game>_cli link open <link>` starts the window from a hub's Join, Spectate or Play button: on the server, as a spectator, or on a demo fetched from the hub and checked against its hash. Shooter's Edge spectates with `set spectator 1` and plays a hub's replay files.
