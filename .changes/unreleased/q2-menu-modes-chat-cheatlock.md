type: Added
title: Quake II's video modes in the menu, its chat lines in its own letters, the version reply and the cheat lock
---

Quake II's VIDEO page lists the full-screen modes you can pick. On a joined server or in a demo, centre messages and the on-screen chat lines now show under Quake II's own status bar, in the letters `scr_font` picks. A chat line ending in `!version` is answered with your version, and on a server with more than one player slot the cheat settings stay off whatever your own `sv_cheats` says.
