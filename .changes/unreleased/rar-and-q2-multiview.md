type: Added
title: Demos open from RAR archives, and Quake II's multiview recordings play
---

Shooter's Edge opens a recorded game shared in a RAR archive (RAR 4 or 5), like the other archives. Quake II's server recordings of a whole match (`.mvd2`) play as any player's view, the demo player switches to another player at any frame, and `convert` writes that view as an ordinary `.dm2`.
