---
type: Added
title: The Quake mission packs' missiles leave at the press
---

On a QuakeWorld server playing Scourge of Armagon or Dissolution of Eternity, your own laser bolts, proximity grenades, lava nails, multi grenades, multi rockets and plasma balls now fly from the moment you fire, as the base game's nails, rockets and grenades already do, instead of a round trip later. `cl_predict_projectiles 0` turns it off.
