title: Quake II's spawns, respawns, drops, co-op rules and screens play as the original
---

In Quake II a spawn now kills whatever stands where the player appears, the dead come back only on a fresh press after the first second (in co-op on any button), and the intermission waits for a button. A weapon thrown with `drop` carries no ammunition, keys and power armour can be thrown, and in co-op the items that stay cannot be. Co-op scores its monster kills, reads the team and weapons-stay flags, and clears the power-cube marks at a unit's end. A deathmatch exit without allow-exit kills. The scoreboard shows while dead, the help computer and inventory stay current while open, and chat flooding is guarded only in deathmatch and co-op, in the game's words.
