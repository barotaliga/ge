type: Added
title: A Quake II mod's own HUD and effects in the game's window, on Quake maps too
---

A mod of Quake II playing in the window now draws its own status bar, scoreboards and inventory in place of Quake's bar, and its sparks, blood, blasts and rail trails as the window's effects. Mods that read a full player setup now put the window's player in the game instead of making them a spectator.
