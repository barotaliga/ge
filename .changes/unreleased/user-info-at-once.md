title: A joined server sees a new name, skin, hand, fov or msg at once
---

On a joined server of Quake II or Quake, changing your name, skin, handedness, field of view or
message level now reaches the server straight away instead of at the next join.
Quake II's glow maps on model skins are taken out again: the classic games' look is classic
enhance's own.
