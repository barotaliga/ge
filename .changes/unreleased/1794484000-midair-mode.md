type: Added
title: Midair, the rocket duel scored by height
---

Servers can play midair: `k_midair 1` arms every spawn with the rocket launcher and endless rockets, takes the items off the map, and counts a rocket only on a player at least `k_midair_minheight` above the floor (64 to 1024 units), killing him and scoring 1, 2, 4 or 8 by how far he was above the shooter. Players can vote it on for a map. The rules are shared by every game's rules (`plugins/shooter/midair.hpp`); the sequel's maps play it today.
