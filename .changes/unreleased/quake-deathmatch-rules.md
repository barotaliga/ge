type: Added
title: Quake's own deathmatch rules when Quake's packs are loaded
---

A Quake deathmatch without a mod of its own now plays Quake's own rules on top of the arena's: players show their pain, firing and death frames, cry, gib and leave their last four bodies on the map; they stand again on a press once the body lies still, at a random spawn spot with its flash; `kill` costs two frags; damage goes quad, armour, push, pentagram, then teamplay's own rule for each mode; items come back on each deathmatch mode's own times and weapons stay where the mode says; every weapon has its own spread, damage and sounds (the axe lands its blow a fifth of a second after the swing, the double shotgun its wide cone, the lightning gun its start and hit sounds); liquids are heard going in and out and drowning grows; keys open their doors for good; secrets, relays, counters and level exits work; the match ends in the intermission camera with its music and moves on to the map's next one on a button press, carrying each living player's kit. The full list of what Quake and QuakeWorld have, and what is still missing, is kept item by item.
