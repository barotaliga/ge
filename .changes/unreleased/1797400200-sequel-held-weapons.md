title: the sequel's weapons in the player's hand
---

A sequel game now draws the weapon in the player's hand: each of its eleven weapons is the game's own first-person model in its own skin, and it plays the frames the game's rules put it on as it is raised, fired, held and lowered. In the modern look the railgun now fires a straight blue-white beam with one faintly glowing line circling it, softly lighting the room along its path as it fades and leaving a rising vapour, and each blaster and hyperblaster bolt rides a streak of two plasma strands that sheds sparks.
