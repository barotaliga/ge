title: Quake II's own teleport, respawn, entry and item-return particles
---

In Quake II's recordings and on a joined server, a teleport or respawn now throws Quake II's own grey burst, an item coming back its green sparkle, a player entering or leaving its green or red flash, and a teleporter pad its fountain, in place of Quake's teleport fog.
