---
type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot crosses boss1's ferries and presses its floor plates
---

On Quake II's boss1 the campaign bot no longer freezes on a ferry over the lava or steps off beside one. The checks that keep it off lava now count a moving platform's top as floor. A button press or a dodge no longer takes over its keys while it boards a ferry or jumps. It is no longer shoved off a ledge while it waits for the ferry. It rides the pillar up to the boss at the pillar's middle. A floor plate that does not move when pressed now counts as pressed. The bot now reaches the boss floor in about 75 seconds.
