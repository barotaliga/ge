---
type: Added
title: The HUD as data
---

`ge::hud::Layout` (`engine/hud/layout.hpp`) declares a HUD as a table of elements a game fills
once a frame, with every element's place (shown, anchor, offset, scale, opacity) a `hud_<id>_`
cvar a player sets from a cfg or the console, the whole HUD's `hud_scale`, `hud_alpha` and
`hud_margin`, and `hud_reset`; `ui::DrawList::fade` fades every command drawn since a mark.
