title: The skier plugin
---

The skier plugin, `ge::skier` (`skier/*.hpp`): a skier and his pair of skis on a mountain's snow. `step_skier` advances one step (the snow's sink and friction, the edge's grip, the carve and the pivot, the tuck and the skater's push, the jump, flight and landing, trunks and the wipeout); `limits.hpp` states what a skier can do for any pilot to plan on (`carve_speed`, `terminal_speed`, `edge_for_curvature`); `synthetic.hpp` and `scenarios.hpp` are the hand-built maps and the staged ride scenarios its tests and labs share.
