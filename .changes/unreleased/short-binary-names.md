---
type: Changed
title: Shooter's Edge's programs have short names
---

Shooter's Edge's executables are now `se` (the game's window), `sesv` (the dedicated server; `sesv --sequel` serves the sequel's rules on its own protocol), `sefwd` (the forward), `seproxy` (the proxies; `seproxy near` and `seproxy far` for one end each) and `setv` (the TV node; `setv --sequel` for the sequel's), all at the build's root. They replace `shooters_edge`, `shooters_edge_server`, `shooters_edge_sequel_server` and `shooters_edge_hops <kind>`; the flags are unchanged.
