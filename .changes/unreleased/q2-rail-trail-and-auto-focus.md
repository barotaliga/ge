type: Added
title: Quake II's rail trail settings and sound out of focus
---

Shooter's Edge's Quake II gains its rail trail settings: `cl_railtrail_type` (the game's specks, a
beam, or a beam with a spiral), `cl_railtrail_time`, and the beam's and spiral's colours, width and
radius. Every game gains `s_auto_focus`: silent while minimised (1) or while another window has the
focus (2).
