title: Fog from the air, drawn over lakes and pools
---

What the air says lies at a place (`wind::mist_at`) is handed to the frame's fog by `render/mist_fog.hpp`: a bank as deep as the air laid it, steam fog as thin wisps rising off the water, and a valley's fog as a level sea. Fall Line asks the air over a lake (`water_mist`): a still, clear dawn fogs it and a windy one does not. A classic map's pools of water and slime steam under the level's evening air (`FogRows::air`). A fog still forming now veils a view hardly at all: its visual range eases in from the clear air's.
