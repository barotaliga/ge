title: The ski routes (R42) on generator versions 9 and 10, and the routes lab
---

Generator versions 9 and 10 now build: a ski area with its SKI ROUTES, the orange runs past black. A route is found on the built mountain, never sculpted: two cheapest-path searches over a grid of 10 m squares (from the rim of every chair's and gondola's top station, and back from every run's corridor) are joined through one square whose fall line is steeper than a black and no steeper than 110 %, and the joined line is kept only where it is 400–3000 m long, falls 250 m or more, never pitches past 130 % over 20 m, stays off the pistes' shoulders and is clear of the trees and the lifts. Each top keeps its best line and the map up to two, apart. Version 9 lays the shortest line down. Version 10 lays the WILD line: a step costs more straight down the fall line and less through the woods, along an edge and over a cliff's face, a line may drop off a crag, and it is threaded between the trunks; the trunks on it are felled and up to three jumps (`O1…`, `Kicker::route`) with a dug gap past each lip are cut into its rolls. The routes are laid before the ski area is analysed, and `analysis::check_routes` holds them. Maps from versions 1 and 8 are unchanged to the digest. The `routes` lab draws the area with its routes, each route close up and each from the side.
