type: Added
title: Save and load cards with pictures in Fall Line
---

A menu item can carry a picture shown large beside the column while it has the focus. Fall Line's front door offers CONTINUE and a LOAD card of the run's saves, each with its picture, and the pause page a SAVE card of six slots; the window keeps the player's saves in their folder and autosaves every three minutes of play. `play::load_run_into` loads a save into a run already open, and `play::setup_text` gives the setup line a save's meta holds.
