type: Added
title: Classic remix dresses Quake in Quake II's weapons
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items q2`, Shooter's Edge's classic game plays its own maps with Quake II's weapons and armour from the next map on: Quake II's guns in the hand and lying on the floor, their missiles, their firing, bouncing and exploding sounds, and its jacket, combat and body armour with their pickup sounds. The classic rules keep their own damage and rates, and Quake II's models keep their own colours.
