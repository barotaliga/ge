title: Votes and the server settings page on our Quake II server
---

Our Quake II server takes votes on its rules (`vote fraglimit 30`, `vote map q2dm1`, `vote kick <player>`; the owner's settings refused with why, every change put back at the next map) and sends its settings to our window, so OPTIONS ▸ SERVER SETTINGS lists them there as it does on our QuakeWorld server. A stock client is sent nothing new.
