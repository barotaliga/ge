type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge draws a mod's skeletal models (.iqm)
---

A model a mod names as `.iqm` is posed through its clips into frames (a clip a frame group, `plugins/morph/skeletal.hpp`), its materials' pictures laid into one skin, and drawn by the window's model pass like a classic model, with the floor's light, both looks, frame blending and warps. The `se-models` lab's `--models=iqm` draws the halls with one in every model's place.
