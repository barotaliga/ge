title: Quake II's bots plan only the jumps its air lets them make
---

Quake II's move barely steers in the air unless the server sets an air acceleration, yet its bots planned and flew their jumps and drops as if they could strafe in mid-air like Quake's players. The nav now proves each jump with Quake II's own air, and the pilot flies it that way, so a bot no longer runs at a jump it cannot make. Companions in single player and co-op use the same move.
