---
type: Added
title: Enhanced textures lit by their relief maps
---

The BSP renderer's modern look reads a surface atlas beside the texture atlas (`Scene::surface`, filled through `TextureSource::surface`): each texel's normal under every light, the sun and the sky, a GGX highlight by its roughness, and parallax occlusion with a crevice shade from its height (`Options::relief`, `specular`, `parallax`, `parallax_reach`); Shooter's Edge reads classic-enhance's normal, height and roughness maps into it (`classicenhance::cached_surface`), switched by `ce_relief` and the new `ce_parallax`.
