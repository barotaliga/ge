---
type: Added
title: Brush detail
---

`ge::detail` dresses a level built of brushes: bevels on exposed sharp edges (edges buried against a neighbour stay sharp), decals clipped onto its surfaces that age in a fixed pool (scorch marks, bullet holes, blood that dries and fades), and seeded detail props (rubble, debris, moss) scattered by rules read off the level's shape, with the `detail-bevel`, `detail-decals` and `detail-props` labs.
