type: Added
title: Rockets, doors, lifts, speakers and teleporter pads are heard in Quake II
---

Playing Quake II, you now hear the sounds that travel with things in the world: a rocket's roar and a blaster bolt's hiss in flight, doors and lifts grinding as they move, looping speakers in a level, the hum of a teleporter pad and of a railgun in hand, the chaingun spinning. Our own server of Quake II sends them to every player, and doors, lifts and buttons now also play their start and stop sounds for players who joined over the network.
