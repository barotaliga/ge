title: Quake II's tanks, mutants, Jorg and the Makron are heard walking
---

The tank, the tank commander, the mutant, Jorg and the Makron now make their footstep sounds as they walk, run, strike and fall, as the game was made. The lighter monsters' steps (`g_monsterfootsteps 1`) fall on the exact frames of each walk, run, flinch and fall.
