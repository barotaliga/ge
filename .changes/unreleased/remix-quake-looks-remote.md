---
type: Changed
title: A remote player sees Quake's hit effects for Quake's weapons on Quake II's rules
---

Under classic remix, Quake's weapons fired on Quake II's rules now show Quake's own hit effects to players who joined over the network too: spikes, pellets, blood, the rocket's blast and the lightning gun's bolt. Before, only the player hosting the game saw them. Other Quake II clients and recordings are sent none of these effects.
