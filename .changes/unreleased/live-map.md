title: The live map: a match watched from above on TV and in recordings
---

While watching on TV or playing a recording, M shows the match from above with the map alive under it: every player and item as the game's own model at its own frame and angles, rockets and grenades with their smoke, taken items counting down to their return, the water glinting. `demo_map 1` fills the screen above the followed player's HUD, `demo_map 2` puts it in the top right corner of his view (`demo_map_scale` sets its size). Space follows the next player, who is ringed and named ("Following <name>"). It is never shown on a server joined live.
