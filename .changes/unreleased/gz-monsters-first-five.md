title: Ground Zero's daedalus, medic commander, turret, turret brain and stalker
---

Under Ground Zero's rules Shooter's Edge now spawns five of the pack's monsters: the daedalus (a stronger hover with a front power screen), the medic commander (which calls in groups of guards, each after a grow, on spots round it), the turret on a floor, a ceiling or a wall (a wall unit slides out when used), the hidden brain that aims and fires a turret breach, and the stalker, which walks ceilings, jumps, pounces and feigns death. Their green bolts land in green sparks, live and in demos.
