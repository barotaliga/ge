Our free campaign mod (`qc/campaign/`) has the rest of its roster but the bosses: the leaper claws and leaps, the giant swipes, smashes and throws lightning and takes only half of a blast, the caster fires a homing ball, the trooper fires laser bolts, the spawn bounces at its enemy and bursts when it dies, and the heavy swordsman swings, charges and casts a fan of slow bolts. A thing in the common mod code can now take a share of a blast (`blast_share`).
