title: Classic remix plays the classic game's maps in the sequel
---

With classic remix on, Shooter's Edge's sequel plays the classic game's maps on its own rules: the map's walls in its own textures, its items the sequel's. A classic map's point tree is given brushes so the sequel's movement and shots trace it, and every swapped item now keeps its place on the floor in both directions instead of starting inside it.
