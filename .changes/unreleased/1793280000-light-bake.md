type: Added
title: A CPU light baker with colour bounce and directional lightmaps
---

`engine/bake` (`ge::bake`) works a static world's light out once into a lightmap atlas: planar charts packed into one atlas, a BVH ray tracer, direct light from point, spot and sun lights with soft shadows, gather passes of bounced light coloured by what it bounced off, glowing faces and the sky, and the light's mean direction a luxel so normal and relief detail catch the room's real lights (`directional_scale`). It gives the same bits on any number of workers. The `bake` lab shows a hall baked old-style, rebaked and read directionally.
