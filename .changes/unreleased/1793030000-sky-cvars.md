The sky over a Fall Line run is set from cvars: the server's `sv_weather` (clear, fair, flurries, high, overcast, snow, storm, fog, or the map's own), `sv_snowfall`, `sv_fog`, `sv_wind`, `sv_winddir`, `sv_cover` and `sv_skyrate` (the sky's hours per hour of the run, after `sv_hour`), and the player's `r_clouds`. A picked sky replaces only the map's weather (`mountain/mapgen/weather.hpp`'s `with_sky`), and the app moves the sun and the night as the sky's hour runs. The `world` lab takes `--cfg` to photograph them.
