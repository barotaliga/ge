title: the sequel's TV plays the full game's own recordings
---

The sequel's TV (`--demo=<file.dm2>`) now plays a recording of an older protocol, such as the full game's own demos, by converting it to the protocol every stock client takes as it opens it. A viewer that asks to download a file the recording names is told the TV does not have it and goes on watching, instead of waiting on the loading screen.
