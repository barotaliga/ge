---
type: Changed
title: The classic maps' pools move at a calmer pace
---

Under classic-enhance a map's water now moves at 0.7 of a real water's pace: its swell, its chop, its ripples and its gusts all slow together, while the rings round a swimmer and the rain's rings keep real time. Shooter's Edge's new `r_water_speed` setting (archived, 0.7 by default; 1 a real water's, lower slower) tunes it live. The engine's water takes the same knob as `render::WaterDraw::pace` and `render::set_water_pace`.
