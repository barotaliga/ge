type: Added
title: Classic remix plays the other game's maps
---

With classic remix on, Shooter's Edge's classic game plays the sequel's maps: the map's walls and sky in its own textures, its items the classic game's, its weapons balanced against the rest of the map (`cr_strong_min`, `cr_strong_share`, and `cr_strong_same` copies of one strong weapon at most, the one nearest a strong armour lowered first) and its small health and armour pickups gathered into the classic game's packs (`cr_pool_radius`). The lab `se-remix` surveys fourteen real maps, and `se-client --remix` draws one in the window's frame.
