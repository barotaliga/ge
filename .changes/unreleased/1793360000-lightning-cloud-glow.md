title: Lightning lights a patch of its cloud
---
A lightning flash now lights only the part of its cloud near the channel, as in night photographs of strikes, instead of turning the whole storm deck white.
The channel is drawn with a soft glow instead of a hard edge, and the black rim that showed round it (the anti-aliasing blending in the last frame's dark, and the sharpening pulling its neighbours below black) is gone.
