type: Added
title: Quake II's footsteps and loop switches
---

Shooter's Edge's Quake II gains `cl_footsteps` (0: no footsteps heard) and `s_ambient` (which
looping sounds are heard: none, all, only those of something you can see, only your own), and its
own spellings `scr_showpause` and `netgraph`.
