---
type: Changed
title: The classic maps' fires move like real fire
---

Under classic-enhance the torches', braziers' and bonfires' flames now move as a real flame does: the hot gas speeds up as it rises and its licks stretch, each flame puffs at a real flame's rate for its width (a torch about three times a second, a bonfire about once) and pinches its tip off as pockets that rise and burn out, the whole flame snakes, and the light on the walls swells and dims with the same puffs. The grain that boiled over the flames at the frame rate is gone. `r_fire_pace` is replaced by `r_fire_speed` (1 a real fire's pace).
