type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot rides Dissolution of Eternity's own lifts
---

The bot's map did not know the lift that Dissolution of Eternity uses in 13 of its 15 maps. It
treated each lift's shaft as open air, and the rooms the lift served as out of reach. A lift that
rests at the bottom and is ridden by touch is now a plat to the bot, and its travel is the size of
its height. Two kinds are still left out. A lift that rests at the top is called from below only
from under it, and r2m5's is a pillar that nothing can get under. A lift with a name answers no
touch until it is used, the use moves nothing, and nothing the bot sees says when that has
happened. Across the pack's 14 single-player maps, the bot kills 372 monsters where it killed 292
on the same code without the change. r2m5 goes from 16 to 64 of 99 kills and r2m1 from 19 to 40 of
93.
