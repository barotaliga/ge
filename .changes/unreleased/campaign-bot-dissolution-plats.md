title: The campaign bot rides Dissolution of Eternity's own lifts
---

The bot's map did not know the lift that Dissolution of Eternity uses in 13 of its 15 maps. It
treated each lift's shaft as open air, and the rooms the lift served as out of reach. A lift that
is ridden by touch is now a plat to the bot. Its travel is the size of its height, and it is
boarded at the bottom of its ride whether it starts at the top or at the bottom. A lift with a name
waits for a use before it answers a touch, and nothing the bot sees says when that has happened, so
it is still left out. Across the pack's 14 single-player maps, the bot kills 351 monsters where it
killed 301. r2m5 is the pack's first map it finishes (54 of 99 kills, it had 16), and r2m1 goes
from 19 to 34 kills.
