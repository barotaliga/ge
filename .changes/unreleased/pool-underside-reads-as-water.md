title: A pool's underside reads as water
---

A pool hanging over open air (the start map's drop to the fourth episode) now looks like water from the room under it: its bottom shows its waves, mirrors the room and catches the lamps' glints, and through it the top is seen as from inside the water, the room above bright and wobbling in Snell's window and the water's inside mirrored past it.
