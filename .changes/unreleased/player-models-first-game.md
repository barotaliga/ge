type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge lets first-game players choose a figure with the model setting
---

In the first game a player's `model` setting names a folder under `players/`: copy in a figure (a `tris.md2` from the second game with its skins, or a `tris.mdl`) and anyone who names it is drawn as it, in the folder's picture of their skin, else its first. The game's poses play on the figure's own frames of the same kind (standing, running, attacking, pain, death). Without the folder, the game's own figure is drawn.
