title: Every classic-enhance switch now draws in both classic games
---

The sequel's figures throw shadows over its lightmaps (`ce_shadows`). Flames' light wavers with their fire and flickering lamps glide between their steps (`ce_flicker`). Shiny floors mirror the room, flipped as a mirror shows it, by `r_reflect` (`ce_reflections`; rough stone and metal never do), the weapon in hand is lit by the room's lamps and every flash near it (`ce_weapons`), and sounds ring by the room they are in (`ce_acoustics`). Each is on in the modern look and can be turned off from the console or the CLASSIC ENHANCED pages.

Removed: `ce_arches` and `ce_bevels`, which a classic map's few curves and edges barely showed.

Fixed: the sequel showed no weapon in hand when only its own packs were installed.
