The sequel's figures throw shadows over its lightmaps (`ce_shadows`). Flames' light wavers with their fire and flickering lamps glide between their steps (`ce_flicker`). Each is on in the modern look and can be turned off from the console or the CLASSIC ENHANCED pages.
