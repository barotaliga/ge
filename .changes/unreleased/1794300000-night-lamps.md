title: Fall Line's lamps after dark
---

Fall Line lights its night: floodlight masts down the piste (two floods each, laid to a recreational night piste's class), street lamps along the village's streets, the lit windows' warm spill and a headlamp on every racer's helmet, all coming on with the dark, the nearest and brightest casting shadows, and the eye adapting to the lamps' light on the snow ahead; `r_lights` and `r_light_shadows` switch them (`plugins/mountain/app/night_lamps.hpp`, `games/fall_line/app/lamps.hpp`). An instance family now carries its own lamp-shadow slots and a game deals as many of the atlas's tiles as it gives them (`create_family_casters`'s `lamp_tiles`, `LampShadowSettings::tiles`).
