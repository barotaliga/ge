title: A real face's streams and rivers, cut into its map and frozen in winter
---

A real face's hints keep the brooks and rivers its open map data draws (`HintStream`, after the lakes in the packed hints; the `real-hints` lab's `--water` bake reads them, `labs/osm_water.hpp`), and `mountain/tarn/brook.hpp` lays them on a map built off the face: a surface that only falls, a channel cut under its banks, the line broken where the generator's ground stands over it, a frozen gully in winter. Fall Line draws each open one as a channel (`app/lakes.hpp`), and the `world` lab's `stream` and `stream-high` views follow the longest. A frame's moving water now reads 64 bodies' optics.
