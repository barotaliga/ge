type: Added
title: Ground Zero's carrier and black widow fight in the pack's campaign
---

Ground Zero's carrier and both stages of the black widow now fight as the pack's bosses: the carrier with its machine guns, grenades, rockets, rail and the fliers it launches; the widow with her turret blaster, rails, kick, stalkers and her answer to a player's power-ups, then her beam-out; her second stage with a heat beam, a disruptor, a tongue that pulls, and a death that takes every stalker with her.
