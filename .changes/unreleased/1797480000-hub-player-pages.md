---
type: Added
title: Player, Match and Leaders pages on the hub
---

A hub that runs the account service now shows each player's stats: a Player page with their matches, wins, frags, efficiency, every weapon's accuracy and the recent matches, filtered by map, mode, modes left out (no ffa) and ping; a Match page with the scoreboard and each player's accuracy per weapon; and Leaders by any stat, all time or over the last days. The same is served as JSON under `/v1/players`, `/v1/matches` and `/v1/leaders`.
