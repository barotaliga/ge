---
type: Added
title: Quake II's cl_maxfps sets its moves a second, r_maxfps its frame cap
---

With Quake II loaded, `cl_maxfps` is its own setting: how many moves a second you send the server (62 by default, up to 125), and `r_maxfps` caps the frames drawn, starting at the cap you had and kept with Quake II's settings; the other games' `cl_maxfps` frame cap comes back as it was.
