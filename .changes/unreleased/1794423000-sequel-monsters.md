type: Added
title: The sequel's monsters
---

The sequel's single player now has its enemies: guards, troopers, gunners, berserkers, parasites, flyers and tanks that stand guard or walk their rounds, notice a player they can see or hear firing, call each other in, chase him, shoot, club and drain him, flinch when hurt, and fall dead or burst apart. They block players and shots, drop what they carry, and keep out of deathmatch.
