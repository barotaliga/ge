The `shooters_edge` window draws the weapon in the player's hand: the game's free models, switched with the number keys, kicked and flashing as the mod fires them, and lit by the map's baked light under the player. The flash also lights the walls. `-models <dir>` points the game at the models' folder, and the view-model cvars (`r_drawviewmodel`, `r_viewmodel_fov`, `cl_gunx`, `cl_guny`, `cl_gunz`, `cl_gunsway`) are in the console. The brush plugin gains `light_below`, the baked light on the floor under any point.
