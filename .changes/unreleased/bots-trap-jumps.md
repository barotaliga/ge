---
type: Fixed
title: Bots no longer take jumps whose miss drops them where they cannot get back from
---

A bot's jump whose miss ends somewhere with no way back on foot to the rest of the map is now treated like a jump over lava: it is tried again past its target and from a slower run, and kept only when both still land. Quake II's scripted playthrough no longer jumps onto the rail above base1's river and falls in.
