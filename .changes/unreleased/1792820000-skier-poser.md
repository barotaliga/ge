type: Added
title: the skier poser, the built skier model posed from a SkierState
---

`skier/app/rig_pose.hpp` poses the skier's rig every frame from a `SkierState`: the lean through two lags so the body's speed never jumps at a change of turn, each ski rolled onto its edge about its widest line so it never cuts into the snow, the legs and arms by two-bone IK with the pelvis lowered smoothly when a leg runs short, and the pole tips trailing at a fixed height over the snow; `bind_rig` and `rig_locals` turn a pose into the skier model's joint transforms for `assets::pose_globals` and the GPU's skinning. The `anim` lab rides it by default (`--figure=model`, `--figure=pieces` for the placeholder) and bakes the run onto the model as a clip for the `models` lab.
