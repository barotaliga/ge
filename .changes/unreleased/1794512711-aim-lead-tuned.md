---
type: Changed
title: The aim help's missile lead is tuned on recorded matches
---

The aim help now leads a missile by the rule that hit most often against real players' paths: his velocity and his jump's arc as they are, nobody slower than 150 units/s across led, and his run foreseen at most 0.6 s ahead. The new `se-aimlead` lab scores lead rules by hit rate with rockets, grenades and nails fired at every player of a folder of multiview recordings.
