title: Bots walk down steep ramps, and the campaign bot retries jumps it gave up on
---

Bots now find their way down a steep ramp from its flat top, where they used to see no path
(e3m1's south half was cut off). The campaign bot no longer writes off an air tube as a missed
jump when the draft sets it down off the landing. When it has nowhere left to go, it tries the
jumps it had given up on again instead of standing still.
