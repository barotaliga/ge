title: The waterline, specks in the water and a wet lens
---

A camera at the surface sees the picture split along the waterline, with a meniscus, as the waves and rings move it. Under water the light is brighter looking up, and specks drift in the water, thick in a brown lake or an algal pond. Coming out, `render/wet_lens.hpp` lays a sheet of water that drains down the lens and leaves drops that slide, bead and dry. The `gpu-plunge` lab pictures a first-person player jumping into a dirty pond.
