type: Added
title: Bots fight with the Quake mission packs' own weapons
---

On Scourge of Armagon the bots now pick up, choose and fire the laser cannon and Mjolnir (the proximity gun is known when taken, never fetched); on Dissolution of Eternity they choose and fire the lava nailguns, the multi grenade and rocket launchers and the plasma gun, counting each pack ammunition apart. They keep their distance from the packs' close fighters (the scourge, the gremlin, Armagon, the spike mine, the phantom swordsman, the mummy, the eel and the dragon).
