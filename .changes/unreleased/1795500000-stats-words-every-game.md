---
type: Added
title: The stats words on QuakeWorld servers
---

`stats [player]`, `teamstats` and `laststats [player]` now answer the same way on QuakeWorld and Quake II servers: the match so far (between matches, the last one's), each team's kills, deaths and damage, and the last match. A QuakeWorld mod that has its own stats words keeps them. A Quake II server's log says once that a player entered the game.
