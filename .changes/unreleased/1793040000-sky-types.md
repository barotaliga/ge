Sky types (`render/sky_style.hpp`): a world's air (Rayleigh per channel, the dust's scattering, absorption, asymmetry and height, the ozone) built into the atmosphere's tables, and over the sky a sized and tinted sun, a second sun, a lit ringed planet, a nebula, the stars by day and a glow from below. Ten named types (earth, dust, alien, hell, void, nebula, violet, ice, ash, toxic) and knobs over them; Fall Line picks one with `sv_skytype` and turns it with `sv_skyair`, `sv_skydust`, `sv_sunsize`, `sv_sun2`, `sv_planet`, `sv_planetdir`, `sv_planetel`, `sv_ring`, `sv_nebula`, `sv_skystars` and `sv_skyglow`. The `world` lab gains a `sky` view. The Earth's sky is unchanged.
