title: The campaign bot presses the button that reopens doors that started open
---

Doors that start open and are shut by a trigger now count as shut for the campaign bot, so it goes back for the button that opens them again. On Scourge of Armagon's hip2m2 the bot used to stay shut in the fiend arena; now it gets out and kills 88 of 103 monsters.
