type: Added
title: Fog, mist, dust and glowing haze in areas
---

A game can now place fog where it wants it: `render::FogVolume` boxes and ellipsoids (turned, thinning upward, soft-edged, carved into drifting wisps, or lying on the ground) are lit into one froxel volume a frame by the sun past its shadows (shafts through a wood), the fog's own shadow and its orders of scattering, the sky's light and their own glow, and every material, the sky and the cloud are veiled by it through `haze`. With no volume in reach nothing changes. Fall Line's frame takes them through `FrameScene::fog`; the `fog` lab shows a wood's mist, its shafts, a sea of fog in the valleys, a glow at night and a drifting dust.
