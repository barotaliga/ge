title: Fall Line opens on its front door over the live mountain
---

Fall Line now opens on its front door, with the title over the bot's run down the map. PLAY starts the run, OPTIONS sets the camera's view, the sound and the master volume, and QUIT leaves. On the run, Escape pauses it under a dimmed pause page with RESUME, OPTIONS and QUIT TO TITLE. The menus make three new sounds: a focus tick, a press and a page. They are drawn by a new `ui` row at the end of the frame, which draws any 2D overlay over the picture. `fall_line --play` skips the front door. The new `front-door` lab photographs every page over the frame at three screen sizes.
