title: Dissolution of Eternity's items in remix, and its second episode loads
---

Remix plays Dissolution of Eternity's lava nails, multi rockets and plasma as the ammunition of the same weapon in another game. Its second episode's first map loads: a map key written with trailing spaces is read as the key. The `se-q1-campaign` lab lays a mission pack's folder over the game (`--mod`) and counts every class of entity the run sees (`--census`).
