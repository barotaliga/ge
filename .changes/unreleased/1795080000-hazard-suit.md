---
type: Added
title: Quake's hazard suit, and sv_dropsuit
---

Quake's rules now have the hazard suit: for 30 s its wearer keeps a full breath under water, takes nothing from slime and burns in lava once a second instead of five times; a second suit starts the 30 s again, and a warning comes 3 s before the end in a loaded game's words. With `sv_dropsuit 1` (off by default) a dead wearer leaves it for others with the time it had left, as `sv_dropquad` and `sv_dropring` do.
