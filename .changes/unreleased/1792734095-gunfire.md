title: Gunfire and wounds
---

Fall Line's army fires: rifles, light machine guns and door guns aim and lead their bursts, every round is flown with drag and gravity and struck against the snow, the trunks and the skier's body, and a round in him is a penetrating wound judged on `Racer::body`, so enough hits kill and end the hunt `killed`; the `gunfire` lab tables the hit rates by range and the hunts.
