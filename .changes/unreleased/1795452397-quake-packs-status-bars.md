type: Added
title: Quake's mission packs show their own status bars
---

With Scourge of Armagon or Dissolution of Eternity loaded, the classic status bar draws the pack's own pictures, read from its graphics archive. Scourge of Armagon shows the laser cannon, Mjolnir, the proximity gun beside the grenade launcher, the wetsuit, the empathy shields, and its keys on the main bar. Dissolution of Eternity shows its two inventory bars, the lava nailguns, the multi launchers, the plasma gun, its ammunition, armour, power shield and anti-grav belt, and the team border in its capture-the-flag modes. The server now sends a mod's `items2` to the client, and a newly picked-up weapon flashes on the bar for a second, as it did in the original game.
