type: Added
title: Team coaches on Shooter's Edge's servers
---

In a team match on a Quake II server, a spectator can ask to coach a team in the warmup (`coach <team>`), and the players elect him with `yes`. A coach reads and writes his team's team chat, and his chase camera stays on his team's players. He steps down with `coach` again. The referee can name a coach without a vote (`coach <team> <player>`) or take one off (`uncoach <team>`), and `coaches` lists them.
