---
type: Added
title: Team bots hear the game, keep a map of their team, keep areas and use the map's buttons
---

Shooter's Edge's bots now hear what their client is sent, by skill: an expert takes in every sound and knows what each means, an average player catches close and unique sounds, a novice only close ones and never their meaning. A pickup heard tells which item went and starts its timer, a spawn heard places a fresh enemy, and an enemy heard is chased for a moment. Only an expert who took the mega health times it, from when his own health is back down to 100. An expert keeps where each teammate last was for ten seconds and reads a key area none of them is in as likely lost; an average player goes by a teammate's `lost` line. An expert holder with 20 or more rockets fires at the teleporters' exits and the powers' spawns near his area while its item is away. Experts rate each map's key items by how scarce they are. Help is called only over a key area being lost, and a novice says nothing but his status. A map's buttons come off its own entities, shot or touched, and an average or expert bot goes through them for the items behind their doors; a novice tries any button he sees. In deathmatch the baseline mod now plays the spawn fog's sound where a player comes in and kills a living player standing on that spot, so two players never spawn stuck inside each other.
