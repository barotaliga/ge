title: The sequel's locks, counters, lasers, barrels and other level pieces
---

The sequel's levels now have their locks and hazards: doors that want a key and say which, counters that tell you how many are left, lasers that burn, barrels that blow a moment after they are shot, earthquakes, rising and falling water, breakable walls, light that fades up or down, and the clocks and counters drawn out of digits.
