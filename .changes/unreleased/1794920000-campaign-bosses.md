Our free campaign mod (`qc/campaign/`) has its two bosses: the lava boss rises when the episode's rune is taken, lobs fire, and falls only to three strikes of the map's lightning; the final boss dies only when the player teleports into it, through a teleport destination that rides a path, and its death ends the game. In the campaign, teleporters now send monsters too and kill whatever a teleport lands inside.
