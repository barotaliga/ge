title: The mission packs' disc music taken in from every release
---

Ground Zero's disc copy (an `.mdf` with its `.mds` sheet) now gives up its ten music tracks, not only its files, and the Quake mission packs' shared disc gives its eight tracks to both Scourge of Armagon and Dissolution of Eternity, whose levels both play them. The Reckoning's blue hyperblaster flash no longer plays a sound when a player fires it.
