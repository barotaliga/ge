type: Added
title: Lightning beams that light the room and burn where they land
---

Held electric beams for any game (`render/beam.hpp`, `render/beams.hpp`): a channel struck afresh many times a second from a source to its hit, a white-hot core in a glowing sheath in physical units, flicker by strike and a ripple along it, a row of local lights along the beam and one at the hit, and scorches left as it sweeps (pressed by the game's decals) whose embers cool to soot; 28 named knobs and three presets (`arc`, `bolt`, `braid`), pictured and recorded by the `gpu-beam` lab.
