---
type: Added
title: Quake II's ink lines round models, stepped explosions and joined areas
---

Quake II now has `gl_celshading`, a black ink line round each solid model that thins and fades
with distance (the engine's morph pass can outline any figure), `cl_smooth_explosions` 0 for
explosions that move on frame by frame, and the server's `map_noareas`, which joins every part of
a map for finding faults in it.
