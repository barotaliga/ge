title: Shooter's Edge draws the things on a map
---

The Shooter's Edge window draws every model, sprite and brush item the server lists (weapons, armour, health and ammo boxes, torches and flames, other players in their colours), lit by the floor under them; the baseline mod gains the quad and the shield and keeps the torches and flames; the morph pass takes a game's light in the scene's white (`MorphDraw::light_in_white`); the `se-models` lab shows them.
