type: Added
title: The classic game's monsters under the sequel's rules
---

With classic remix's `cr_monsters 1`, a map played by the sequel's rules spawns the classic game's monsters in place of their sequel partners, each with the classic campaign's health, size, speeds, attacks, model, frames and sounds: riflemen, hounds, enforcers, swordsmen, brutes, death knights, flyers, ghouls, fiends, vores, shamblers, eels and spawns.
