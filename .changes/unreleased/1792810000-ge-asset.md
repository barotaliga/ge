title: ge asset, built models with a stamp and a size budget
---

A game's models are built in headless Blender by Python builders off the game's own data: `ge asset build <name>` feeds a builder its `GE_ASSET_DATA` set from the new `assets` executable, runs it under Blender or a Python with the bpy module (with the engine's `ge_asset.py` library on its path), and commits the `.glb` beside a stamp of its sources' hashes; `ge asset list` and `ge asset check` (and `assets check` in a test) fail a model older than its builder, sources or data, or over the size budget its `asset.txt` states; the skier plugin ships the first one, the skier on the reference skis with his poles, rigged on the skeleton stated in `skier/app/rig.hpp`.
