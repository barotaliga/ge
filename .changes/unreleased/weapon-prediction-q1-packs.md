title: Scourge of Armagon's and Dissolution of Eternity's guns predicted on a joined server
---

On a joined QuakeWorld or Quake server running Scourge of Armagon, the laser cannon (its twin and single bolts), Mjolnir and the proximity gun now fire at the press, a round trip before the server answers, with their own sounds and frames; key 6 toggles the grenade launcher and the proximity gun as the pack does. Under Dissolution of Eternity the lava nailguns, the multi launchers and the plasma gun fire at the press too; a change of weapon there is left to the server. A pack weapon whose bit is past the protocol's short travels as its bit number, so stock servers and clients are unchanged. A shot a correction moves back onto a command already shown is played then, instead of only as the server's late copy.
