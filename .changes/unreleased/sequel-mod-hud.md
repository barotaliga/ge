type: Added
title: A sequel mod's own HUD and effects in the game's window, on first-game maps too
---

A mod of the sequel playing in the window now draws its own status bar, scoreboards and inventory in place of the first game's bar, and its sparks, blood, blasts and rail trails as the window's effects. Mods that read a full player setup now put the window's player in the game instead of making them a spectator.
