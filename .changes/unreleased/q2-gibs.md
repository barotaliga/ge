title: Bodies under Quake II's rules come apart into pieces
---

A body gibbed under Quake II's rules now throws its pieces: meat, bones, a head, a chest, or metal and a gear for the machines. They fly by the blow that did it, spin, land or bounce, and are gone after 10 to 20 seconds, bleeding in Quake II's own reds as they fly; a gibbed player lies as his head. God mode wears a white shell.
