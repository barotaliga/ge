type: Added
title: Blood pools, drips and drying in the classic games' modern look
---

With `ce_blood` on, blood that falls under a wound now gathers into one pool on the floor that grows as the fight goes on and spreads out from its edge, a big splash on a wall runs down it in a few drips ending in beads, and every mark is a darker red that darkens fast at first and then slowly to a near black as it dries. A wet pool mirrors the room with the floors' reflections (`ce_reflections`). Two new settings: `ce_blood_time` (how long blood stays, 120 s; 0 until newer marks need its place) and `ce_blood_reflect` (how strongly a wet pool mirrors the room, 1). Engine: `engine/detail` has the `blood_pool` and `blood_drip` decal kinds, a decal's own life, and blood that spreads (`DecalLook::spread`, `MaskSample::arrive`); the beams pass's paint mirrors the kept scene on floors (`prepare_beams`'s `paint_mirror`). Blood now goes as a wound bleeds: a nail goes through and most of the blood leaves the exit wound behind the body, only a fine mist comes back out of the entry, and an entry pours down the body. Wounds soak the clothes round them and their blood runs down them, darkening as it dries; fresh wounds drip on the floor; a player who steps in a wet pool leaves a few bloody boot prints. Engine: `render/morph.hpp`'s `MorphBlood` (wounds on a figure's skin, fixed to its rest frame) and the `blood_print` decal kind with a decal's `strength`. New lab `se-blood`.
