The sun and the moon shine through thin cloud: every layer in front of them adds the glow its droplets or crystals diffract (a soft glare through altostratus, coloured corona rings round the moon in thin altocumulus), the ice kinds ring them with the 22 degree halo, and a new `veil` kind is the thin whole-sky ice the sun shines through brightest. A deck under a low sun is lit through its top, grey with the sun a pale disc in it. The `world` lab gains the `veil` view (straight at the sun or the moon), a lens for turned views (`veil@0:52:14`) and `--day` to light the map on another day of the year.
