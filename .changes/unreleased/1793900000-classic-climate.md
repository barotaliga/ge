`plugins/classicenhance/climate.hpp` gives every classic level its air: a `wind::Climate` played in the evening or at night, in a wind under low cloud, with the level's temperature, swing, humidity and pools' warmth, so pools steam, breath shows and the fall is snow or rain by the wet bulb. A map the table does not hold gets `fallback_air()`. The `classic-climate` lab draws each level's air and fog.
