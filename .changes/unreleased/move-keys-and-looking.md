type: Added
title: The classic configs' move speeds, look keys and fov
---

Shooter's Edge reads the move and look lines every classic config carries: `cl_forwardspeed`, `cl_backspeed`, `cl_sidespeed`, `cl_upspeed` and `cl_movespeedkey` (400 walking in the first game, 200 in Quake II, running twice that), `cl_pitchspeed` and `cl_anglespeedkey` for the turn and look keys, `+lookup`, `+lookdown`, `+klook`, `+mlook`, `freelook` and `m_forward` (the mouse moves you with freelook off), `centerview` and `force_centerview`, and `fov` as each game's spelling of `cl_fov`.
