---
type: Changed
title: Remixed armour, health and ammunition follow their own game's rules
---

Under classic remix, the armour, health and ammunition laid on a map are now taken by the rules of the game they come from, in both directions. On Quake's rules, Quake II's armour adds onto the armour worn, up to its limit, and its boxes give Quake II's amounts. On Quake II's rules, Quake's armour replaces the armour worn when it is better, and its health boxes give 15, 25 and 100.
