---
type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters fight and suffer like the original's
---

Monsters burn in lava and slime and drown, hear the impacts of your shots and your jumps, and hold their pain for its whole animation. The tank, gunner, flyer, berserker, infantry and soldiers fire their original volleys and spreads. The screen flashes on damage and pickups and takes the colour of water, slime, lava and the power-ups; items and bodies ride lifts; the quad, invulnerability and power armour make their sounds; the first grenades you pick up go in your hand.
