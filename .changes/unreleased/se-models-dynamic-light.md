title: Shooter's Edge's models take the light of muzzle flashes, rockets and explosions
---

A model near a dynamic light (a muzzle's flash, a rocket's glow, an explosion, a power's glow) is now lit by it the way the floor under it is: brighter and warmer on the side facing the light. The `se-models` lab gains a `player-glow` view.
