title: Labs
---

Labs: an engine-level contract (`engine/lab`: `LabSpec`, `GE_LAB`, the runner, captioned pictures on a CPU raster, PNG, reports and digests) that every subsystem, plugin and game plugs its sim, scenario, picture, drive and bench labs into, built as the source tree's `labs` executable and checked by `make labs-smoke`; the first labs are `noise`, `run-clock`, `level`, `analyze` and `mapgen-cost`. Labs build only in the source tree and are not part of the installed package.
