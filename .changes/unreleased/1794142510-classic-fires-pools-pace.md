---
type: Changed
title: The classic maps' fires and pools move at a real pace
---

Under classic-enhance the torches', braziers' and bonfires' flames churn and flicker at 0.4 of their former pace, the light they throw on the walls slowing in step (`r_fire_pace`, 1 the old pace), and a fire under a roof wavers in a gentler draught. A hall's pool no one is in lies nearly still: a lower swell and none of the quick ripples under 20 cm (`render::WaterDraw::chop_shortest`).
