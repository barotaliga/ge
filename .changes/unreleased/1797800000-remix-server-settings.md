---
type: Changed
title: Classic remix is the server's, and cr_sounds covers every player sound
---

Classic remix's `cr_` settings are now the server's: a match you host tells everyone who joins which remix it plays, and joining such a match (or playing its demo) with Shooter's Edge plays it the same way, its maps, figures and sounds from the other game, whatever your own settings say. `cr_menu` stays your own and now picks the letters too; `cr_charset` is gone. `cr_sounds` now covers the players' sounds both ways: the jump, the gasps and the other sounds both games ship by the same name are the chosen game's own, choking and drowning under water and a telefrag have their partners, swimming splashes are Quake II's wading, and Quake's rules heard as Quake II's give the players Quake II's footsteps.
