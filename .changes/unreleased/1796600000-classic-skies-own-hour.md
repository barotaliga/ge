---
type: Changed
title: Classic skies no longer repeat, and each map keeps its own hour
---

A classic map's clouds no longer show the same pattern again and again across the sky: a warped cloud layer now repeats only every 56 cells instead of every 8. And each classic map is seen at the hour its own sky shows: a bright sky by day, a dark one at evening or night (`sv_hour -1`).
