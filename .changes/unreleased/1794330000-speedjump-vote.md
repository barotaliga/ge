type: Added
title: Players vote the easy jump to one player for a map
---

Shooter's Edge's servers take `vote speedjump <id|name> on|off|<share>`: when more than half of the people on the server say yes, that player is granted the easy jump (or has it taken back) until the map changes, disclosed to everyone as an administrator's grant is. The next map puts back what he had. `sv_vote_deny speedjump` keeps the grant to the administrator.
