title: QuakeWorld's zombie time and localid, the old ammunition box, the setup figure, and a demo's coloured bursts, explosion2 and grappling beam
---

A QuakeWorld player who is dropped keeps getting the goodbye for `zombietime` seconds and shows as ZOMBIE in `status`, and the server info carries the match's settings from the start. Quake's old ammunition box (`item_weapon`) lies on maps that still have it, and a big ammunition box now looks like the big box. The setup page gains the hostname row and shows the player's figure in the chosen shirt and pants. A server browser on the same computer can send the game commands with `localid`. Recorded demos and the campaign now show particle bursts in any colour, the mission packs' coloured explosion and the grappling beam.
