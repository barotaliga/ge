Shooter's Edge's campaign saves: `save <name>` in the console keeps the single-player game as it is, and `load <name>` brings it back exactly, the monsters, the doors and every shot in flight where they were. Saves live in the game folder's `saves/`. A match, coop, a dead player and the level's end are refused with the reason. Lab `se-campaign --save=20` plays a loaded game on beside the one never saved and compares them every frame.
