title: The thrown body: a wiped-out skier tumbles as a ragdoll
---

A skier who wipes out is now thrown off his skis as a body of thirteen points: the hips, the shoulders, the head, the knees, the feet, the elbows and the hands. The body has a rigid torso, limbs that bend only as joints allow, and muscles that brace in the air and go slack once he is down. The air slows it, the snow drags and ploughs it, and trunks stop it. It goes over the way the cause sends a body, and it tumbles until it lies still. The ride lab's new `thrown` table measures every wipeout's tumble, how far and how high the body went, and when it lay still.
