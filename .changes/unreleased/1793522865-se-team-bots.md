type: Added
title: A four-on-four team game of bots, recorded as a multiview demo
---

Shooter's Edge's bots now play as teams: two teams of four fighters on our server with `teamplay 1` (no damage to a teammate, a backpack dropped on every death), a strongest player holding the best armour's area, two rushing the quad before it is due, the quad carried to the enemy last called out, items left to the teammate who claimed them, backpacks picked up when short of ammunition and fire held while a teammate is in the line. The server records the match as a multiview demo. The fighters read a map's buttons (pressed or shot first), its teleporters and plats, swim out of water no route leaves and dive into pockets only the water reaches. The `se-team` lab plays a match on the fixture halls or, with `--basedir`, on a map of the player's own pack, and writes the demo with `--save`.
