title: The campaign bot opens key doors and skips exits that only deathmatch has
---

The campaign bot now walks into a key door it holds the key for, since those doors open only to a touch. It no longer heads for a level exit that only exists in deathmatch, and it no longer jumps down past a shut floor. On Scourge of Armagon's hip2m2 it now kills 94 of 98 monsters, and on Quake's e2m1 it finishes the map.
