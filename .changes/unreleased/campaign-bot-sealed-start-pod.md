type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot breaks out of The Reckoning's start pod and counts Scourge's finale
---

On The Reckoning's xship the player starts in a sealed pod under a hatch that breaks. The campaign bot never got out. It rocket-jumped into the hatch for the whole map. Now it shoots a breakable lid that covers a way out, then takes that way. A bot's mouse hand that has looked far up can now lift the mouse and set it down again when it runs out of pad. Before, it could not look far enough down for the rocket jump. The bot also turns to face a standing jump while it walks to where it jumps from, instead of walking there first and then turning round. On Scourge of Armagon's finale, killing Armagon now counts the map as finished, since the end of the game leads to no next map.
