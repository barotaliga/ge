---
type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign chase and hear as Quake II's do
---

With `cr_monsters q2`, a Quake II monster that loses sight of you in Quake's campaign now goes to where it last saw you and then follows your trail, as in Quake II, instead of homing in on you through the walls; one that has not found you yet turns toward a shot it hears within 1000 units and walks to it.
