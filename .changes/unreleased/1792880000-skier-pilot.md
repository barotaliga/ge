title: The skier's pilot
---

`skier/pilot.hpp` is the pilot any bot on skis flies: `lay_pilot_plan` plans the speed along a line the game lays (the curvature, what the pair can carve round each bend, eased to every kicker's lip and carried back so every slowing can be braked for), and `skier_pilot` pursues a rider's lane down it, brakes over the plan at his pace and tucks under it, recording its `line` and `speed` decisions on an `ai::Why`.
