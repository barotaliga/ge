type: Changed
title: Rocket and grenade jumps drawn on the campaign bot's routes
---

The route pictures of the Quake campaign bot and of recorded fast runs now draw every rocket or
grenade jump as an orange flight from its take-off to its landing; a recorded run's are found by a
hit taken with a launcher in hand followed by a speed no run reaches. The bot also no longer walks
off a lift halfway up its shaft (e1m4).
