---
type: Added
title: The watched player's record while a multiview demo plays
---

`mvd_status 1` shows the watched player's record beside a multiview demo, gathered as it plays: the items taken, deaths, the average and last three lives (time, frags, teamkills), the last weapon fired, the weapons and quad lost at death, kills and teamkills by weapon, and the last three quad runs. `mvd_status_x` and `mvd_status_y` place it; a seek starts it over.
