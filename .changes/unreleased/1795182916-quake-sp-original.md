title: Quake's single player looks and answers like the original
---

With the game's own packs, Quake's single player now shows the original's screen tints (the quad, suit, ring and pentagram, the gold pickup flash, the damage flash by how much armour took, the water, slime and lava tints by where the eye is), the intermission and finale over the undimmed view with the finale's title picture and its line layout, the intermission camera's sway with no weapon, the solo panel while dead and the level time from the server's clock. The tracks the game asks for at the intermission and an episode's end play, splashes and the leap's landing thud are heard, and the console's `god`, `notarget`, `noclip`, `fly`, `give`, `kill`, `restart` and `changelevel` work as in the original. In co-op the game no longer pauses for the host's menu, and players who joined get the intermission and the level's real totals.
