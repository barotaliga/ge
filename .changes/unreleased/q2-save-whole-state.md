---
type: Fixed
title: A Quake II save plays on as the game left it
---

Loading a Quake II game saved mid-level (the base game, The Reckoning or Ground Zero) now plays on exactly as the game would have: a monster no longer forgets the footstep it just weighed, a noise or a shot it was about to hear, or (in the mission packs) its shield, repair work, dodges, blind fire, hint path or boss move; the pieces flying and lying, the player's move time budget and Ground Zero's bad areas come back too. Older saves still load.
