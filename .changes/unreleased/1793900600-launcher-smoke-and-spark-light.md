title: The launchers' muzzle smoke is a short grey puff again; sparks off metal flick a light
---

With the classic pack's own weapons, the rocket and grenade launchers and the shotguns left a large white cloud at the muzzle that hung in front of the blast; their smoke is now a short grey puff, a hand or two across and gone in about a second. A nail striking metal now flicks a brief warm light with its sparks.
