type: Added
title: Blood that sprays by the weapon and paints the walls
---

Shooter's Edge's modern look, under the new classic-enhance switch `ce_blood`: a body hit sprays blood shaped by what hit it (a pellet's puff and spray out the far side, a nail's jet along its flight, an axe's sideways fling, the lightning's sizzle, a blast's burst away from it) and splashes the walls and floor near with blood that dries darker and fades, in both Quake and Quake II. Blood decals are drawn as paint in the light they lie in (`render/marks.hpp`), where a scorch only darkens.
