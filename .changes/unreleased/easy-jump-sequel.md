---
type: Added
title: The easy jump works on the sequel's maps
---

With `cl_speedjump 1`, holding forward and jump now hops and gains speed on the sequel's maps too, by that game's own jumping rules: the jump is let go in the air and pressed again on landing, the hops weave to both sides of where you aim, and when you turn far away at speed the game lands and turns you on foot before hopping on. The jump key now jumps on those maps. `pm_speedjump` sets the share there as it does elsewhere, and an admin's or a vote's easy jump for one player carries over.
