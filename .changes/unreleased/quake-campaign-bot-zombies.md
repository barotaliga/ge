---
type: Changed
title: The campaign bot leaves zombies until it can blast them
---

The bot that plays Quake's campaign at 100% no longer counts a zombie as a stop on its route until
it carries a grenade or rocket launcher with rockets (anything less only knocks one down), and it
shoots a monster out of its way when one blocks a corridor it is walking. e1m3 went from 10-18
kills to 30-36 of 47.
