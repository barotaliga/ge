type: Added
title: loc_draw place markers and $cl_surface in every classic game
---

`loc_draw 1` stands a marker at every place point within `loc_dist` of the player (Quake's the player's head, Quake II's the ancient head), the nearest one bobbing under a white light, so a place file can be checked and edited by eye. `$cl_surface` gives the texture straight ahead and its surface flags. Both work in Quake and Quake II.
