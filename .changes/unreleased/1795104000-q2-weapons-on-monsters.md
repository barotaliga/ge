type: Fixed
title: Quake II's weapons hit monsters as the game was made
---

In Quake II's single player a rocket or grenade that hits a monster no longer hurts it twice, the railgun passes through every monster in its line, the BFG's lasers and final burst hurt monsters, a blast no longer reaches a monster through a wall, and the first shot on a monster that has not noticed anyone does double damage.
