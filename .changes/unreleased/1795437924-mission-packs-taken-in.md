title: Mission packs found and taken in
---

Shooter's Edge knows the four official mission packs (Scourge of Armagon and Dissolution of Eternity for Quake, The Reckoning and Ground Zero for Quake II) by their folders and their first packs' digests. Taking a game in copies its packs beside it; a pack's own folder, its disc (an `.mdf` disc copy too), a release `.zip` or a `.7z` of its disc image is taken in alone, with its movies, its patch of the players' weapons and its disc music, and the game says when the base game is still to be added. Packs on this machine are offered in the console at the start and listed by `secli import`; SELECT GAME shows a row for each pack, which starts it over its game.
