title: Monsters' attacks in the modern look
---

In Shooter's Edge's classic games the monsters' shots now draw with the engine's effects, as the player's weapons do: the flier's acid spit drips and glows green and splashes off the wall, the knight's fire bolt streams flame and embers and bursts where it lands, the caster's ball is a knot of violet light with motes spiralling off it, the boss's lava ball burns, smokes and drips molten drops, the enforcer's laser is a red streak that lights the walls and sparks where it ends, a zombie's thrown flesh drips dark drops, and a soldier's or an enforcer's gun shows its muzzle fire. It is a classic-enhance switch, `ce_monsters` (MONSTER ATTACKS), on in the modern look; off, or in the classic look, the classic specks, which are now drawn where no trail was before. The pack's shot models are named in classic-enhance's replacement table (`missile.<kind>`), and a mod's models with the format's tracer bits get the same looks. Lab `se-monster-fx` films each monster's attack on a real map, the classic look beside the modern look's variants.
