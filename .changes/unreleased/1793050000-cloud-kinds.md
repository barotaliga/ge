Cloud kinds (`render/cloud_kinds.hpp`): fourteen named kinds of cloud (inversion, stratus, stratocumulus, cumulus, congestus, cumulonimbus, nimbostratus, altocumulus, altostratus, lenticular, cirrocumulus, cirrostratus, cirrus and wisps), matched against photographs of real skies. A frame takes up to four cloud layers, each with a form: billowed edges off a cellular noise, strands along the wind, patches of cells, an anvil's spread top. Sheets are lit by diffusion so a deck is grey under and bright on top, a cirrostratus lights a 22° halo, and the cloud's shadow across the air throws sun shafts. Fall Line picks a stack with `sv_clouds` (such as `cumulus+cirrus`); overcast, snow and storm skies now stand a sheet under their deck. The `world` lab gains a `clouds` view.
