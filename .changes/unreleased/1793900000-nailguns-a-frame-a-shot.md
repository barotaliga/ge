title: The nailguns fire from each barrel in turn
---

Held firing, the nailgun's two barrels now take turns and the super nailgun's cluster turns one barrel a shot, as their models animate: each shot steps the firing frames on by one instead of restarting them, and the shot's muzzle light and puff leave from the barrel that fired. With the classic pack's own weapons, each fires its classic muzzle look at its true size; a nailgun throws no flame and only a faint breath of haze. The lightning gun's frames step the same way.
