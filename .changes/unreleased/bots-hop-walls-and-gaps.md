type: Changed
title: Shooter's Edge's bots fly their bunny hops into walls less often and keep their speed through gap tricks
---

The bots' hop now turns hard in the air when its way bends, picks a copied hop start by where its takeoff heads and whether the flight after it is clear, does not jump into a wall under a fight's view, and holds its jump for the landing frame ahead of a gap trick. On dm2 the experts' fast flights that end in a wall fell from 59 % to about half, and they spend more of their time above 320 units a second.
