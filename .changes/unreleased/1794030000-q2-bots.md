title: Bots play deathmatch under Quake II's rules

Shooter's Edge's bots now play a deathmatch on a Quake II map: they see through its walls as the map is built, find its items, ride its plats, pick up and switch to its weapons and score frags. The se-q2-bots lab plays four of them on a hall and draws where each went.
