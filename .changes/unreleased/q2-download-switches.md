title: Quake II's download switches, cl_nodelta and cl_gunalpha
---

Shooter's Edge's Quake II takes `allow_download` and its kinds (maps, players, models, sounds,
textures, pictures, others) to choose what joining a server fetches, `cl_nodelta` to have every
frame sent whole, and `cl_gunalpha` to draw the held weapon see-through.
