---
type: Added
title: single-player clients watch recordings through the TV
---

`setv --demo=<recording>` now also serves the recording as a netgame on the single-player protocol, on `--netgame-port` (26000 by default, 0 for none). An unmodified single-player client connects with `connect <host>` and watches it at its own pace, in the recorder's view, through new levels and the loop. A multiplayer client's or a multiview recording is converted to the single-player form first.
