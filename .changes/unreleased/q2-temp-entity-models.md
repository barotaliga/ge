title: Quake II's explosions, hits, cables and the BFG laser as the game draws them
---

In Quake II's classic look an explosion is the game's own fireball model: its frames, its skins
as it burns out, its fade and its light, with `cl_smooth_explosions`, `cl_disable_explosions` and
`cl_dlight_hacks` as the game has them. Bullet hits leave their puff of smoke and flash, a blaster
bolt its flare and yellow glow, the BFG's burst its green sprite. The parasite's drain, the medic's
cable and the grapple are drawn as their chain of segments, the BFG's laser as a thin green beam,
and a splash of sparks crackles.
