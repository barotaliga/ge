type: Added
title: Demos follow the best player and jump to the end
---

`mvd_autotrack 1` (or `autotrack`) makes a multiview demo follow the hottest player, and `cl_hightrack 1` the top fragger. `demo_jump_end` jumps to just before the end, `demo_jump_skip_messages 0` prints what a jump passes over, and Quake II's `seek` takes tenths and percentages.
