title: ge record
---

`ge record [<log>]` plays a run back (an input log, or the pilot's run of `--seed`) and draws one of the game's views at every frame, each with its time, step and view burned in, then encodes it with ffmpeg as an H.264 MP4, a VP9 WebM or a GIF, or keeps PNG frames (`--format=png`, no encoder needed). Flags set the resolution (`--size=WxH`), frame rate (`--fps`), playback speed (`--speed`), range (`--from`/`--to`, a step or seconds), and quality (`--crf`, `--bitrate`, `--encoder-preset`, `--codec`). A contact sheet beside the video shows `--thumbs` stills in a grid, each labelled with its time and step. The play executable gains `play record`, and the lab raster gains `resized`.
