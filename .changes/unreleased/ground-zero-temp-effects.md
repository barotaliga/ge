title: Ground Zero's hits, beams, bolts and blasts drawn and heard as Quake II draws them
---
Ground Zero's fourteen temporary effects now look and sound like the pack in Quake II's own look,
in a recording, on a server joined and on your own: the green blaster's and the flechette's hits,
more blood, electric sparks, the heat beam's sparks and steam, the tesla's crackling bolt, the
heat beam's spinning segments with the rings that slide along your own, the disruptor's dark burst,
the A-M bomb's growing blast and the plain, big and speckless explosions. The Reckoning's gekk
bleeds green and its plasma explosion is the game's own.
