type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge's held weapons
---

Shooter's Edge holds its own eight free weapons in first person, with no classic pack needed. Each has its own mount, place in the view, kick and muzzle light. A weapon change puts the held weapon away and raises the next, and every shot lights the muzzle. The player can move the weapon with `r_drawviewmodel`, `r_viewmodel_fov`, `cl_gunx`, `cl_guny`, `cl_gunz` and `cl_gunsway`. The `se-viewmodel` lab shows every weapon held, fired in the dark, switched and moved.
