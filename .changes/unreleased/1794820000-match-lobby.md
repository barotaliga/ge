Shooter's Edge's matches open each map in a lobby's warmup: every player and bot on the map, free to roam and fight with nothing counted, on the red or the blue team (`team red|blue|auto|switch`, F2), until everyone says `ready` (F1; `break` or F1 again takes it back); then a countdown (`sv_countdown`) and the map restarted for the match, and back to warmup when the map changes. `addbot [novice|average|expert]` and `removebot` seat the fighters, `start` lets the host start whoever is ready, `lobby` prints the roster, `sv_lobby 0` starts matches at once, and the window shows the banner, the countdown and both teams' roster (F3). The new `se-lobby` lab plays it through on the arena.
