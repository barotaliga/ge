type: Added
title: A full server can send newcomers on to another (sv_redirect)
---

`sv_redirect <address>` sends a player who joins a full server on to another server instead of turning them away: they are told "Server is full. Redirecting you to ..." and their game connects there. With a leading `!` (`sv_redirect !<address>`) every newcomer who is not a VIP is sent on, full or not. Both QuakeWorld and Quake II servers have it (Quake II's `sv_redirect_address` is the same setting), and our game follows the redirect on either.
