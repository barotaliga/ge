type: Fixed
title: Shooter's Edge's built-in arena plays its sounds
---

With no classic game's folder open, the built-in arena was silent. It now plays the game's own free recordings under the sound names the mod uses (`content/classic.assetmap`), so shots, pickups, doors and deaths are heard. A classic game's folder still hears only its own recordings. The `se-audio` lab adds a real bot fight on the arena beside its scripted one.
