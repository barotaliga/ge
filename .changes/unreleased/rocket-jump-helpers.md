type: Added
title: Rocket jump helpers, +rjump and +rrjump
---

Bind `+rjump` to a key and one press does a rocket jump for you: the game fires a rocket at your feet as you jump, choosing the rocket launcher if it is not in your hand and waiting for it if it is still reloading. `+rrjump` is the reverse rocket jump: the rocket goes out behind you at your own pitch, so looking down throws you forward. Your view never turns, and it is a real rocket jump: the ammo, the damage and the push are the same as doing it by hand. It works in both games, on servers that allow it: `sv_rjump` and `sv_rrjump` (off on a dedicated server, on in a match you host) can be voted on, and a server can allow it for one player.
