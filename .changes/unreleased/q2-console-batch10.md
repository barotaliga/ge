title: Sound status, restart answers and a few of Quake II's console words
---

`soundinfo` shows the sound device, its rate and the sounds playing, or "Sound system not started." when there is no device. Typing `snd_restart` or `in_restart` explains that sound settings and input devices apply on their own, so no restart is needed. `remotemode` with no server set shows its usage. `ogg play` is refused while a cinematic plays. A second word after `connect` is said to be ignored.
