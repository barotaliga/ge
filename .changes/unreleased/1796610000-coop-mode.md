---
type: Added
title: Co-op as a mode, with friendly fire, push and episodes opened in turn
---

Shooter's Edge's `sv_coop 1` makes the campaign a coop game: from a cfg or the command line the game starts as one on the start map, from the console one starts at once, with `sv_coopbots` companion bots (up to seven, for eight players). Teammates are spared unless `sv_friendlyfire 1`, and a spared teammate's blow does not push you either unless `sv_friendlypush 1` (then a rocket at a friend boosts him without harm). Players spawn on coop starts no one stands on. On the start map the episodes open one at a time, each once the one before is done, and the window keeps what you finished in `sv_episodes_done`.
