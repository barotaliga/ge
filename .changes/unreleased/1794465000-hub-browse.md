type: Added
title: A hub lists the servers already out there
---

A game whose servers speak a status protocol of their own gives its hub that query (`HubSpec::browse`), and `<game>_hub --browse-master=<host:port>` (or `--browse-server=`) lists those servers beside the engine's own, with their players, teams and phase. Shooter's Edge's hub reads the classic masters and status query.
