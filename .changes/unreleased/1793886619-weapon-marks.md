title: The weapons leave their marks
---

In Shooter's Edge's modern look, under `ce_decals`, a rocket's or a grenade's blast leaves a scorch on the surface it went off against, its embers glowing as it cools; a pellet or a nail leaves a bullet hole in the face it hit; the lightning bolt burns where it lands. The marks are the engine's decals clipped to the map's own faces, kept in a pool of 128 and drawn with the beams' burns. The classic look, which never had marks, shows none.

A shotgun's or a nailgun's hit is subtler and more physical: chips and grit in the wall's own colour, flakes that fall and hop on the floor below before they lie still, a small puff of dust and a thin wisp of smoke. Each pellet leaves its hole where it struck; a body hit takes wounds, a hole with blood splattered round it, pinned to the figure's skin so they move with it until it respawns. The engine's particles can carry a floor of their own to hop on (`floor_y`, `bounce`).
