title: Multiplayer networking's foundation (engine/net)
---

`engine/net` (the `ge::net` target) carries a game's multiplayer: IPv4, IPv6 and simulated addresses; non-blocking UDP sockets on every desktop and phone OS; a simulated network whose links take latency, jitter, loss in bursts, duplicates, reordering, a bandwidth cap and a queue, every fate drawn off a seed on a virtual clock; packet captures as pcap files; the engine's wire format with a stateless challenge handshake; connections with acks, a smoothed round trip, measured loss and unreliable, sequenced and reliable-ordered channels; and nodes (host, client or both) of up to 32 peers, the maximum the game sets. The `net-link` lab runs fake traffic between a host and its clients under six condition presets, from a LAN to a phone on a train, and reports what arrived, how late, what the links measured and what crossed the wire. The plan for the rest (TCP, lobby, chat, teams, NAT traversal, replication, lockstep, voice, accounts and friends) is `docs/networking.md`.
