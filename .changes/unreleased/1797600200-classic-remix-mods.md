title: A classic mod with a compiled module plays its own rules, on Quake II's maps too
---

A classic mod folder that holds a compiled module (`qwprogs.qvm`) now plays the window's match on that module, and under classic remix it plays on Quake II's maps too: a tournament mod brings its own weapons and physics there. The mod sets the boundaries, so `cr_items` and `cr_physics` step aside while it plays; the maps and the players' figures are still remixed.
