title: A hub's server card plays the game in the page through a forwarder
---

A forward's status record says what it reaches (`reach`), and one that takes pages its WebTransport port and its certificate's SHA-256 (`webtransport`, `cert`, `cert2`); `forward::status_reaches` and `forward::status_cert` read them. `shooters_edge_hops forward --master=<host[:port]>` lists the forward on a master. The hub's poller asks a forward on its own protocol, a server card shows Play here when a listed forward reaches that server (`hub::poller_page_forward`), and `/join/{host:port}` starts the player joined through it.
