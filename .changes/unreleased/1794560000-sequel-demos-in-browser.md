type: Added
title: The sequel's demos play in the game and on the hub's pages
---

The game reads the sequel's client recordings (`.dm2`, plain, packed or as a replay file) with
the same player as the first game's demos: the seek, the speeds, the highlights and the video
export. Each game opens only its own recordings. The hub's web viewer plays either game's demos
with that game's colours; a sequel demo's content pack carries its map's texture files and sky,
and the Demos page lists its map, mode and players.
