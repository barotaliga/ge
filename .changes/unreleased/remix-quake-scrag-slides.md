type: Changed
title: Quake's scrag in Quake II slides and fires as Quake's does
---

Under Quake II's rules (`cr_monsters q1`), Quake's scrag now hunts as in Quake: near the player it slides sideways square to him, 8 units a frame, turning about only when blocked; far off it flies straight at him. It spits by its own chances (0.9 close, 0.6 near, 0.2 at mid range), waits a flat 2 s after a spit (none at the hardest skill), and flinches only from a blow big enough.
