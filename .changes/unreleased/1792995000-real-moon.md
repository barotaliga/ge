title: The real moon in every sky
---

`core/moon.hpp` places the moon for a place, date and hour: a truncated lunar theory with the nutation, the parallax of a place on the surface and the refraction, within 0.01 degrees of a reference ephemeris. It also gives the phase, the libration, the axis's tilt and the Earth's shadow at the moon. The sky pass draws the disc over a public-domain albedo map of the real moon (`render/moon.hpp`, mipmapped), so the seas sit where they are, with the earthshine on the dark part and an eclipse's penumbra and red umbra. The moon shows in every sky whenever it is above the horizon, by day as a pale disc and by night as the key light, and an eclipse dims the moonlight on the ground. The old dated moon in `core/solar.hpp` is replaced, and the new `moon` lab draws a month of phases, a year's heights, the full moons' paths and an eclipse.
