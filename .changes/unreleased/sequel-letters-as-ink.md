type: Changed
title: The sequel's menu letters cut out as ink on dirty metal
---

In the sequel's menu, the words the pack has no tag for (SELECT GAME, DEMOS, the games' names, the written page titles) are now letters cut out of the pack's tags as see-through ink and laid on a tag of any width made of the banners' dirty metal. No letter carries pieces of its neighbours or a grey box any more, and the pieced letters (B, C, F, H, J, K, W, X, Z) and the small letters are redrawn from the clean cut-outs.
