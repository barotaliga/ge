type: Added
title: Turn the notify lines off, clear them with the console, and a start-up demo
---

`con_notify 0` hides the lines shown at the top of the screen while the console is closed, and with `con_clearnotify 1` (the default) opening or closing the console clears them (the console keeps every line). `cl_startupdemo <demo>` plays a demo once when the game starts and you asked for no map, demo or server.
