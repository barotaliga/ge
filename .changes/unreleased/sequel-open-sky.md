title: The sequel's maps find their open sky and their pools' depths
---

On a version 38 map, the water's sky reflection, the sun on a pool, the wind and rain in at its windows, the flames' and the smoke's open air, a pool's depth and the view from under its water all used to look for the first game's leaf codes, so they found no sky and no liquid. They now ask the brush survey, whose new `march` treats a wall entered through a sky face as the sky.
