type: Added
title: A ski-cross course built over a map (R35) and the ski-cross lab
---

`mapgen::set_ski_cross(level)` builds a ski-cross course on a stretch of a built map's piste: on a ski area, down the course `ski_cross_course_of` picks. The stretch is the one whose course comes nearest an 18 % mean gradient, crossing the fewest drops and lying lowest. The course is a line of its own, 14 m wide: a 100 m start straight below a four-door start gate and a steep start ramp, then legs swung either side of the piste's line and rounded into turns, then a finish straight onto a 60 m finish area. Its profile is the ground combed along it, never rising, its crests and knees rounded. Every turn of more than 20° is a berm banked up to 28°. The straights carry rollers, jumps and step-downs dealt off the map's seed, with a jump on the start straight and a finish jump on the last. The ground is graded and groomed to the built line, the course fenced, the trees within 12 m of it and in the finish area cleared, and the piste's kickers and drops on it removed. The gates are triangular flags: a turning gate on the inside of every berm and a corridor gate before every feature, red and blue in turn. The course's jumps are kickers on its track. `analysis::analyze_race` holds the course to R35, and the `ski-cross` lab draws it close up, from the side and on the map.
