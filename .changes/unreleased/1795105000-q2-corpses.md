title: Quake II's dead monsters can be shot apart, and maps' monster hooks work
---

In Quake II's single player a dead monster's body can be shot and blown apart, a monster's death fires the map's `deathtarget`, a `combattarget` sends an angered monster to its post, a combat point holds a monster only when the map asks, the parasite's drain no longer reaches across the room, and a flyer bursts when it dies.
