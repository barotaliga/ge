title: The Quake II campaign bot clears base2 and base3 to the last monster
---

In its 100% mode the Quake II campaign bot now clears base3 (every monster, the key and both objectives) and, on its return to base2, every monster, objective and secret there. Water that a button moves (base3's tank) is now water in Quake II: the player swims in it and out of it.
