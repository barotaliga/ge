title: The snow's weather read off the air
---

A snow layer's weather can now be read off the world's climate (`snow/air.hpp`): its air is `air_at` at the layer's place over a run of days, new snow lies at the density of the air it fell through (a cold day's fall is lighter and deeper), and `feel_at` names the snow underfoot (powder, squeaky, soft, sticky, slush, crust) with a ski's glide and whether it squeaks. A warm day then a frost leaves a crust. The ground's surface no longer jumps warmer at sunrise: the night's cooling fades over the two hours after it.
