---
type: Fixed
title: ge goals free names a row's own sub-rows
---

`ge goals free M18k` now answers the next sub-row under M18k (M18k1), and `ge goals claim --under=M18k` claims it, instead of treating M18k as M18 and answering the next letter. Under a row ending in a digit the sub-rows are letters (M23d1 → M23d1a).
