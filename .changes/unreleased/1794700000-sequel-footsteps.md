title: Footsteps in the sequel, and its recordings' footsteps and landings heard
---

In the sequel, a player running faster than a walk now makes a footstep with each stride, heard by everyone nearby. Playing the sequel's own recordings, or a server of the sequel's own, now plays the footsteps, landings, item respawns and teleports those carry as entity events, which were silent before.
