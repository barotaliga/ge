type: Added
title: The hub keeps and serves demos
---

`<game>_hub --archive=<dir>` keeps replay files by their SHA-256: a recorder drops them in `<dir>/inbox`, the hub reads each one's map, mode, server, players, teams and final scores from its meta, keeps the same file once, and prunes the oldest untagged ones with `--keep-gb` and `--keep-days`. The Demos page and `/v1/demos` search them by player, mode, map, team, tag and age, newest first, with the scores hidden until asked; `/d/abc/<sha256>.ged` downloads one.
