---
type: Fixed
title: Walking no longer pulls a mouse-look player's view level
---

`lookspring` (on in Quake II's own default config) only drifts the pitch of a player who looks by keys; with `freelook` on or `+mlook` held the view stays where the mouse points it while you walk. Letting `+mlook` go with `freelook` off and `lookspring` on levels the view, as in Quake II.
