---
type: Added
title: A sea of fog in the valleys
---

`FrameView::valley` (`render::ValleyFog`) fills the valleys with fog up to a level top for any game: drawn per pixel with its depth along each ray in closed form, so its edge on a slope is sharp at any range; its top waves and drifts on a tiled noise and is lit by the sun on its billows and the sky over them, and under it the eye is in a grey gloom. Every material, the sky and the cloud are seen through it; with no fog nothing changes. The `valley-fog` lab shows a seed's sea from the summit, from the air, at dawn and from under it, and the `fog` lab no longer draws a valley.
