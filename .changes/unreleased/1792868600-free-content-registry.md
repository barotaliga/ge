title: Shooter's Edge's own sounds and models, and where free ones come from
---

Shooter's Edge has a registry of every sound and model it needs under its own names, with the event that plays each, and `classic.assetmap` from the classic content's names (including the numbered door, button and lift sound sets) to ours. 99 of its 129 game sounds and 27 of its models now have CC0 replacements, credited in `games/shooters_edge/content/CREDITS.md`. The new `free-assets` skill lists the free sources, the licences the repository can carry, and how a replacement is found, matched, converted and credited.
