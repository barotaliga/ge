---
type: Added
title: Chat ignore lists
---

Any multiplayer game on the engine can let players stop reading someone's chat: `ignore <name or number>`, `unignore`, `ignorelist`, `ignore_team`, `unignoreall`, and the settings `ignore_mode` (team chat too), `ignore_opponents`, `ignore_spec`, `ignore_flood` with `ignore_flood_duration`, and `ignore_tv`. Shooters add `ignore_no_weapon` and `ignore_not_enough_ammo`. Shooter's Edge applies them to the classic games' chat on a server joined or watched, and reads Quake's `ignore_qtv` as `ignore_tv`.
