title: Quake II's maps take classic enhance's sky and water
---

In Shooter's Edge's modern look a version 38 map now gets the enhanced sky and water the first game's maps get: its pools are found from their leaves' liquid bits (before, none were, so it had no water of ours at all), a pool the map draws opaque (mud) takes a muddy look, and the map's own sky box stays for the classic look and for `ce_skies 0`. A colour brighter than the half-float colour target can hold is capped, so a lamp's face no longer stores infinity, which the bloom spread into a black screen.
