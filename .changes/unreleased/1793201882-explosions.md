type: Added
title: Explosions drawn as fire and smoke
---

`engine/render/explosion.hpp` says what a blast is at each moment of its life (one code for every blast, its look a set of 26 named knobs for the fire, the smoke, the billows, the sparks and debris and their durations, with the presets `explosion`, `explosion.rocket`, `explosion.grenade`, `explosion.small` and `explosion.large`; a fireball that swells, rises, cools as a blackbody and turns to smoke; the flash's intensity and colour for the local lights; sparks and debris dealt off its seed; the scorch it leaves), and `engine/render/explosions.hpp` draws each one as a single quad whose pixels march the fireball through the cloud noise, clipped exactly by the scene's depth, at most 32 a frame. `engine/render/blackbody.hpp` gives the colour and luminance of anything glowing by its heat, and particles can glow and cool (`Particle::kelvin`, `cool_s`). The `gpu-explosion` lab pictures a blast through its life by day and at dusk, a rocket against a grenade and the knobs at their ends, and prices the pass.
