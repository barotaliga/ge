title: The army on foot
---

Fall Line's hunt now sends soldiers after him (`games/fall_line/sim/army.hpp`): from three stars, squads put down below the load line of the lift whose top is over him ride it up and ski down at him; each ground wave's men on foot are put down out of his sight down the runs and climb them in a column. Each soldier is a cheap troop model (`army_troop.hpp`): the hill's pull checked to a pace, skating, the herringbone and the traverse on skins, clear of trunks and of each other, a side-step out of where he is caught. Where the army shoots to kill they hold with him in sight at a stand-off; where it does not, the first to reach him while he is slow or down arrests him. Every soldier is one of the hunt's pursuers, and the men far from him are sent home once the hunt is over. The `army` lab shows it on seed 38; `tests/golden/fall_line_army.txt` holds it, and `fall_line_wanted.txt` moves because the army now joins every hunt.
