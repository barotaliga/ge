---
type: Added
title: Muzzle fire drawn as fire and smoke
---

`engine/render/muzzle.hpp` gives a weapon's muzzle fire as the explosions' fire shaped for a barrel: a flame stretched along the barrel and carved into prongs, placed each frame on the muzzle's pose so it follows the firing animation; a smoke puff that leaves the muzzle and slows in the air; sparks in a cone; and the flash's light. Each weapon is a look of 19 named knobs (and the puff's own explosion knobs as `smoke.<knob>`), with the presets `muzzle.shotgun`, `muzzle.nailgun`, `muzzle.grenade` and `muzzle.rocket`. The explosions pass draws the flames beside the blasts (`MuzzleFire`), and a blast can now carry a velocity (`Blast::v`). The `gpu-muzzle` lab pictures each weapon firing and prices the pass.
