title: The crosshair as the player sets it
---

Shooter's Edge's `crosshair` picks the mark (none, the HUD's own, or a dot, a cross, an open cross, a ring, a ring with a dot or an X), `crosshaircolor` its colour (a palette number or red, green and blue), `crosshairsize` its size and `crosshairalpha` how solid it is.
