---
type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot no longer waits on a lift held down by a secret slab, or walks into a walled-off teleporter
---

A lift whose trigger lies under a closed secret slab cannot rise, so the bot no longer drops into
its shaft to wait there for the rest of the map; it opens the slab first. A teleporter with a wall
in front of it in single player is no longer on the bot's routes. On Quake's e3m1 this takes the
bot from 8 to 39 kills.
