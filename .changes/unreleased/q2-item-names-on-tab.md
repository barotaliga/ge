title: Tab completes Quake II's item names, and its LOAD and SAVE pages hold fifteen slots
---

In Quake II, Tab after use, drop or give completes the item names the game listed. help on its own opens the help computer, and holding ESCAPE with the inventory or help computer up opens the menu once it is put away. LOAD shows the autosave and fifteen slots, SAVE the fifteen.
