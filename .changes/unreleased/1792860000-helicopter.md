type: Added
title: The helicopter plugin
---

A new plugin, `plugins/helicopter` (`ge_helicopter`), flies a light single-rotor helicopter over any snow mountain:

- **The rotor:** its thrust follows momentum theory, with ground effect near the snow. The cyclic tilts the disc, the fuselage hangs under it, the pedals turn it against the torque, and the lever acts through a lag.
- **The ground:** the skids judge a landing. The rotor's tips, the body and the trees' crowns are tested for strikes, and an air band at the map's edge turns it back.
- **The rest:** the downwash is exposed for a game's snow and people (`wash_at`), and a bot flies it to an aim, keeping its height over the snow (`heli_pilot`).

The new `heli` lab runs twelve staged scenarios, and `tests/helicopter_scenarios_test.cpp` holds them.
