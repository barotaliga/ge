The replay file's field codec has a second revision: new text is written as a copy of a recent line with only its middle spelled, a team's info line takes its place from where the player stands, and player frames, sound numbers and entity origins are guessed better. A 20-minute 4on4 now packs to 0.49 to 0.50 of its zstd-packed size. Files packed with the first revision still open.
