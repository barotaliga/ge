type: Added
title: The team game counts hit rates per weapon
---

A Shooter's Edge team game now counts every player's shots and hits with each weapon, the way a match's statistics do. The scatterguns count pellets and the arc counts cells, and a rocket's direct hit is counted apart from its blast. The team lab prints every player's rates and each skill's, so the bots can be held to the rates real players reach in each division.
