---
type: Changed
title: Carried weapons fit the hands
---

The weapons players carry in the first classic game now sit in their hands: each is drawn about as long as the figure's own gun (a longer weapon a little longer), its grip under the gun where the hands close round it, instead of small and set back. A new lab draws every carried weapon close up, evenly lit, in flat colours with the hands marked, as wireframe, and from the side, above and in front.
