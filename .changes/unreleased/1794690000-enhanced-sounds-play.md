---
type: Fixed
title: The enhanced sounds play in the game, its demos and its videos
---

With `ce_sounds` on, a classic game's sounds come from classic enhance's cache (cleaned and brought to 48 kHz) wherever the game plays one: in a game played in the window, a demo played back and a demo's video export. A sound the cache has no copy of plays the pack's own. Turning the setting off or on takes effect at the next frame. Both the first game and Quake II read it the same way.
