---
type: Changed
title: Quake's weapons are fired whole on Quake II's rules under classic remix
---

With `cr_items q1`, Quake II's rules now fire Quake's own eight weapons (axe, shotgun, super shotgun, nailgun, super nailgun, grenade launcher, rocket launcher, thunderbolt) by Quake's numbers, shots, missiles, frames, sounds, effects and kill messages, instead of Quake II's weapons dressed in Quake's models. A player spawns with the axe, the shotgun and 25 shells, and Quake II's items give Quake's weapon of their role and Quake's amounts.
