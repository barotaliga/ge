---
type: Added
title: The classic game's monsters under Quake II's rules
---

With classic remix's `cr_monsters 1`, a map played by Quake II's rules spawns the classic game's monsters in place of their Quake II partners, each with the classic campaign's health, size, speeds, attacks, model, frames and sounds: riflemen, hounds, enforcers, swordsmen, brutes, death knights, flyers, ghouls, fiends, vores, shamblers, eels and spawns.
