title: The ruleset in the menu, servers that require one, and f_fakeshaft
---

OPTIONS has a RULESET row in Quake and Quake II. A server started with `sv_ruleset smackdown` (Quake II: `sv_ruleset duel`) takes only players on that ruleset and tells anyone else how to pick it; spectators may watch on any, and the server's settings page shows the ruleset. The client answers `f_fakeshaft` with its `cl_fakeshaft` setting and says it when the player changes it during a match.
