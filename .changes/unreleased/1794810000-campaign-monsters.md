Our free campaign mod (`qc/campaign/`) has its first monsters: the rifleman and the hound stand or walk their paths, notice a player in front of them (or one who fires nearby), chase him, shoot, bite and leap, turn on each other when hit, and count toward the level's monster total when killed. The `se-client` lab now draws the server's models on a pack's map and takes `--progs=` (our program, or `pack` for the pack's own) and `--settle=`.
