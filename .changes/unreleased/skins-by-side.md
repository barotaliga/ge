type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge puts one skin or colour on every teammate or enemy
---

`teamskin` and `enemyskin` put one skin on every teammate or every enemy, and `teamcolor` and `enemycolor` (`off`, one colour, or shirt and pants from 0 to 13) their colours. A teammate is a player on your team while teamplay is on; everyone else is an enemy. `allskins` still puts one skin on everyone. They work live and in demos.
