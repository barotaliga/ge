---
type: Added
title: Quake II team matches with captains, a referee and stats
---

A Quake II server with teams (`dmflags` 64 or 128) and `sv_lobby` now plays a team match: named teams in their own looks, captains who lock, rename, invite, pick and remove, team scores and the winner said at the end. A player with `g_admin_password` referees the match (end it, set the time limit, change the map, kick, ban, mute, shuffle). `stats`, `teamstats` and `laststats` show each player's shots, hits, kills, deaths and damage per weapon.
