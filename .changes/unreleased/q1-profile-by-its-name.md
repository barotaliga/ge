type: Changed
title: Quake's profile is called q1
---

Shooter's Edge's tables for Quake (weapons, items, words, sounds, the move's feel, remix's swaps with Quake II) are `kQ1*` in the code, its profile `q1_game()` and its content row `q1_profile()`. Its profile id is `q1` too: Quake's imported files live in `data/q1/`, and an install kept under the old id, as Quake II's under its old id, is asked for again once.
