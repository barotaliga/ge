Fall Line's free ride carries its village (`games/fall_line/sim/village.hpp`): the streets laid clear of the snowmobile's spot and the helicopter's pad, the trees felled for them gone from the run (the skier, the machines, the crowd and a thrown body no longer meet them), and the traffic on them; a skier meeting a moving vehicle or a parked car is held out of its body and put down past 3 m/s of closing speed, a `traffic` event either way. `ge::skier::build_tree_index` takes the trees to leave out.
