type: Added
title: Ranked ladders and league seasons on the hub
---

Duels and two-on-two matches now earn a chess-style rating. Two players register as a duo on the hub's Account page, each asking for the other under one name, and their two-on-two matches together rate the duo. Every season (14 days by default, `--season-days` on the hub) the season ratings start again, and the best three of each ladder are kept for good as that season's champions. The new Ladder page shows the season's standings, the all-time ratings and every past season's champions, and `/v1/ladder` serves the same as JSON.
