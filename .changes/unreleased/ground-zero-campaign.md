---
type: Added
title: Ground Zero's campaign starts on its own first map and its saves keep its weapons' state
---
A new game starts on the map the game's own `default.cfg` names (`rmine1` for Ground Zero,
`xswamp` for The Reckoning), and co-op loops back there after the victory picture. The pack's unit
configs give its weapons by name, and the last level hands out the A-M bomb. Saves keep the pack's
mines, tesla, disruptor bolts, double damage, IR goggles and chainfist swing. Items are drawn on the
floor they dropped to, so a loaded game no longer drifts from the one saved.
