title: Bullet holes and blood from a game's events
---

`ge::detail::shot_hit` presses a bullet hole where a round's ray lands, facing the shooter, and `ge::detail::bleed` presses blood scaled by the share of health a hit took: spatter on along the shot onto what is behind the victim, a pool under the wound and drops round the feet. A fresh scorch's embers now glow and cool over its first 1.5 s (`DecalLook.er/eg/eb`, the mask's `ember`, the atlas's blue channel). The `detail-hits` lab shows all three.
