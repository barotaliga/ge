title: "Quake II's scripted playthrough leaves base1 by its lift and plays on into base2"
---
The scripted playthrough now rides base1's exit lift: the lift's car counts as a lift, not a
locked door, and its button is pressed before the exit it carries him to. On base2 he no
longer goes back the way he came, walks through the lift car instead of being pushed onto its
button, and a shot fired into a monster pressed against him now hurts it.
