In Quake's campaign a lift or door carries things through the triggers they pass and into what they meet, resting things stay put, the speed limit holds each axis, falling corpses land on what is below, autoaim aims from the shooter's feet to the target's, a monster sees the view a player had a moment ago, blasts chain things in the original's order, angles come back in whole degrees, triggers fire in the original's order, and currents count as water.
