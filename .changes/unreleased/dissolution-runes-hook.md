---
type: Added
title: Dissolution of Eternity's runes and grappling hook shown on the server
---

A new lab, `se-dissolution-dm`, runs Dissolution of Eternity's own program on the server from the player's pack. It shows the deathmatch runes at `gamecfg 1`: they come out, are taken one each, fall when their holder dies and move when left untaken. It also shows the grappling hook at `teamplay 4`: thrown, its chain sent as a beam, pulling the player to the wall and gone when attack is let go.
