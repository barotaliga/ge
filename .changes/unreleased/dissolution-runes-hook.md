type: Added
title: Dissolution of Eternity's runes, grappling hook and team modes shown on the server
---

A new lab, `se-dissolution-dm`, runs Dissolution of Eternity's own program on the server from the player's pack. It covers:

- **The runes** (`gamecfg 1`): they come out, are taken one each, fall when their holder dies and move when left untaken.
- **The grappling hook** (`teamplay 4`): thrown, its chain sent as a beam, it pulls the player to the wall and is gone when attack is let go.
- **Capture the flag**: two flags, one flag and three teams.
- **The tag token**.

A Quake program's team now follows the player's pants colour whenever it changes, so a pack can refuse a team change, as the pack's capture the flag does.
