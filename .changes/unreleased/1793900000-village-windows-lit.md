The free ride's village lights its windows at night (`plugins/mountain/app/building_look.hpp`). Once the sun is down, the homes light up in the evening and go dark toward midnight. The lodge stays lit until it closes and the shops until they shut, and the hotels' night porters, first aid's lamp, the garage (while the groomers are out) and the pump house keep a light on. Each building goes dark at its own moment as the sky's hour moves on.
