---
type: Added
title: Why a bot did it (ge why)
---

Bots can explain themselves: `ge::ai::Why` (the new `ge::ai` library) records each decision a bot makes (what it read, the options it weighed with their scores, the choice and its margin), a game opts in with `Run::trace`, and `ge why <log>` replays any input log to print a bot's decisions round a step, every change of mind (`--flips`) or a series file for `ge graph` (`--timeline`); the play protocol gains a `why` op.
