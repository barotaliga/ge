title: The synth
---

`engine/audio`, published as `ge::audio`: a deterministic synthesizer rendering 48 kHz stereo float with no platform. One-shot tones (sine, square, saw, triangle; glide, detuned pair, vibrato, soft saturation) and coloured noise (white, pink, brown), each through a sweeping state-variable filter and an attack/hold/decay envelope; steered layers for continuous beds; four buses for faders; a shared echo and a soft-knee master limiter. Calls cross from the game thread to the render thread through a lock-free ring, and the same calls render the same bytes on every platform. Sounds are plain data (`SoundDef`, `PlayShape`, `play_def`), beds are racks of layers (`rack_apply`), and `measure` meters a buffer (level, peak, octave bands, centroid, onset, length, balance) and `wav_bytes` encodes it. `engine/platform` gains the audio device (`open_audio`), pulled on SDL's audio thread.
