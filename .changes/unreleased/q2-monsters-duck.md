title: Quake II's monsters duck your shots
---

In Quake II's single-player game a monster that faces you may duck a blaster bolt, a rocket or a BFG ball fired at it: soldiers duck or duck and fire back, and the infantry, gunner, medic, brain and chick drop low for a moment, shots passing over their heads. Easy skill offers fewer chances, and a duck is kept in a saved game.
