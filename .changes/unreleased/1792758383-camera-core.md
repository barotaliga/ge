title: The camera core (ge::camera)
---

`engine/camera` (`ge::camera`): a genre-agnostic camera for any game on the engine. Followers solved exactly over each frame, a first-person rig (view height, step smoothing, bob, strafe roll, view kick, landing dip, zoom), a chase boom, a worn lens on a neck, orbit, planted and free lenses, trauma shake with a comfort scale, cut, blend and inertialized transitions between warm rigs, and a camera stack; `render::FrameView` gains a roll. Four labs (`camera-follow`, `camera-rigs`, `camera-transitions`, `camera-shake`) hold it.
