title: The easy jump (cl_speedjump) on a joined server of Quake II, at the share our server tells
---

With `cl_speedjump 1`, the easy jump now strafes for you on a server of Quake II you join, at the share our server states in `pm_speedjump` (0 turns it off), or 0.8 on a stock server.
