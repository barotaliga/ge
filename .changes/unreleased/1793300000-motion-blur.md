type: Added
title: Motion blur
---

Motion blur along the motion target, for any game: `render/motion_blur.hpp`'s `motion_blur.tiles` pass (each 16 x 16 tile's fastest and slowest motion, then over its neighbours) and a gather in the post pass behind `PostLook::motion_blur` (the shutter's share of a frame), with a one-load early out for still tiles and a fast path where a neighbourhood moves as one; `post_group` takes the motion, depth and tiles views (`PostMotion`). Fall Line's `r_motionblur` (off by default).
