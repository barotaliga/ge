---
type: Changed
title: Classic remix's item settings are named cr_items_*, and health keeps its total when it is resized
---

The settings that weigh a remixed map's items are named for `cr_items` now: `cr_items_strong_min`, `cr_items_strong_share`, `cr_items_strong_same`, `cr_items_weak_same` and `cr_items_pool_radius` (they were `cr_strong_min`, `cr_strong_share`, `cr_strong_same`, `cr_weak_same` and `cr_pool_radius`).

Health is gathered both ways round. Quake's fifteens and twenty-fives on Quake II's rules become Quake II's boxes of twenty-five and ten and its stimpacks, and Quake II's on Quake's rules become Quake's sizes, each group laid down as the sizes that come nearest what it held. A group that needs more pickups than it had places stacks the rest on them, so one of Quake's fifteens is a box of ten and two stimpacks.
