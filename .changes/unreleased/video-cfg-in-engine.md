---
type: Changed
title: video.cfg and the demovideo settings are the engine's
---

The settings a demo video is made with (`demovideo_size`, `demovideo_fps`, `demovideo_codec`, `demovideo_text`, `demovideo_messages`, `demovideo_hud`) and the order `video.cfg` is read in now come from the engine, so any game that exports videos gets them; each game says which of its look settings a video resets. Shooter's Edge's videos are made as before.
