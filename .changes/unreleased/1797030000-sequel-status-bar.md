type: Added
title: the sequel's own status bar
---
The sequel now draws its own status bar in the game's own pictures: health, ammunition and armour with their icons, the selected item, what was just picked up, the power-up's countdown, the help icon and, in deathmatch, the frags. The rules send the bar as the game's layout program with the stats it reads, so recordings, TV and a stock client of that game show it too. The new `se-sequel-hud` lab stages five moments and draws each one.
