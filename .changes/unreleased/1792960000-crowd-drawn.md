title: Palette families, and Fall Line's crowd drawn
---

Many figures of one skinned mesh, each in a pose of its own, can now be drawn as one **palette family**:

- **The engine:** `create_family`'s `palette_joints`, `palette_source` and `set_palettes` (render/skinning.hpp). Each instance keeps its own palette, this frame's and the last frame's, and the draw's vertex stage poses every vertex by it, motion included. No pass runs and no vertex is stored per figure. `pose_palette` is its CPU twin.
- **Fall Line:** the free ride's crowd is drawn as the skier model, one instance an amateur, posed from his place, lean, crouch and falls, sized by his body and in his own jacket.

The `crowd-look` lab and `tests/render_palette_test.cpp` hold it.
