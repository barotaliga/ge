type: Added
title: M flies between the live map and the followed player's eye
---

Watching TV or a recording with the whole live map up, M now dives into the followed player's view: the map fades into the level itself seen from above, which drops onto him and tilts up into his eye as it lands. The recording waits while it dives and then catches up quickly, easing back to normal speed, so nothing is missed. M again climbs back out to the map. The corner map stays available through `demo_map 2`.
