type: Added
title: Opening a port on the home router (PCP, NAT-PMP, UPnP IGD)
---

`net/portmap.hpp` asks a player's router to forward a public port to the game's socket, trying PCP, then NAT-PMP, then UPnP IGD (SSDP, the router's description, SOAP), renews the lease at half its life and closes it when the game is done. A host passes the port to the rendezvous with `reach_host_forward`, and every client reaches it straight, whatever either side's NAT. `net/sim_router.hpp` answers all three protocols on the simulated network, so the whole exchange is verified headless; the `net-reach` lab shows each protocol opening, renewing and closing a port under each condition preset.
