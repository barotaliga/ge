type: Added
title: Classic remix pairs the games' monsters
---

`cr_monsters ""/q1/q2` chooses whose monsters a single-player map is played with under classic remix: the played game's own, Quake's or Quake II's. Each game's campaign monsters are paired by the part they play and then by strength (`profiles/remix_monsters.hpp`); bosses stay the map's. A map from the other game now meets the played game's monsters in their partners' places, and a monster the played game's rules cannot run yet stays its own game's partner.
