---
type: Added
title: Menu and HUD pictures from PNG or TGA files
---

Shooter's Edge reads the HUD's and the menus' pictures through the classic game's adapter (`pictures`). A PNG or TGA of a picture's name under `gfx/` (`gfx/sbar.png`, `gfx/conchars.tga`) stands in for the pack's, alpha kept, at its size or a whole multiple of it; a copy at a whole step under the set's density is scaled up to it.
