---
type: Changed
title: Quake's monsters in Quake II decide as Quake's do
---

With `cr_monsters q1`, Quake's monsters on a Quake II map now see, attack and fight by Quake's own rules instead of Quake II's: they spot you by Quake's ranges and facing, attack by Quake's chances at every skill, hounds and fiends leap from Quake's distances, knights and death knights strike and charge as in Quake, and they hear nothing and follow no trail but make straight for you. A hound or fiend whose leap lodged against the player no longer hangs in the air.
