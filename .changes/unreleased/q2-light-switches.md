---
type: Added
title: Quake II's light settings for walls and models
---

Quake II now has `gl_modulate_world` and `gl_modulate_entities` to split `gl_modulate` between the
walls and the models, `gl_doublelight_entities`, `gl_dotshading` for the models' shading,
`gl_coloredlightmaps` for the walls' light's colour, `gl_dlight_falloff` for a flash's edge on the
walls, `cl_lerp_lightstyles` to blend flickering lights smoothly and `cl_rollhack` for the way
other players lean.
