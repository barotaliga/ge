type: Added
title: Quake II's music settings
---

Shooter's Edge's Quake II takes its own `ogg_enable` (the music on or off) and `ogg_volume` (the
music's loudness, the same as `bgmvolume`), `ogg_shuffle` (every track in a random order) and
`ogg_menu_track` (a track while no game runs). Turning the music back on (`cd on` too) plays the
level's track again.
