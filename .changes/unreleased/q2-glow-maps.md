---
type: Added
title: Glow maps on Quake II's model skins
---

A model's skin can have a glow map (`<skin>_glow.png` or `.tga` beside it) that lights its eyes,
lamps and screens whatever the light round it, as bright as Quake II's `gl_glowmap_intensity`
says. The engine's morph skins can now light a texel by part of its colour.
