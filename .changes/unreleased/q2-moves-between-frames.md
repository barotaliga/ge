---
type: Changed
title: Other players' moves sent between the frames of our server of Quake II
---

Our server of Quake II now sends the other players' moves to a window that asks about 38 times a second, between its 10 Hz frames, so players who turn are drawn closer to where they are (10.6 units off instead of 17.1 for a runner circling at full speed on a broadband link). The frames and the game's timings are unchanged.
