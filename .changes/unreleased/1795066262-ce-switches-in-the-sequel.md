title: Classic enhance's switches in the sequel, and its shells and item pulse in the first game
---

Bodies burn and players wade in the sequel's lava and slime (`ce_burning`, `ce_wade` now read either map family's liquids), the sequel's maps take the cache's rebaked light under `ce_rebake`, and `ce_smooth` and `ce_glow` now change the picture in both games. The sequel's coloured power shells and pulsing items are offered to the first game as `r_powershells` and `r_itempulse`: on in the sequel, off in the first game.
