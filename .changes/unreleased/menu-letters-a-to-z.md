title: the classic menu writes every letter A to Z and a to z in its own carved letters
---

The classic menu could write a word only with the fifteen letters its own rows spell. Now it cuts every capital and small capital its strips hold, grows a small capital into its capital (and shrinks a capital into its small capital) where the strips hold only one size, builds the F from the E and the I, and carves the six letters the strips hold in neither size (B, X, Z and their small capitals) after traced shapes in the pack's own colours. Every letter is drawn from the player's own pack at load; none ships.
