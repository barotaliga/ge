title: Skimming Fall Line's tarn, and swimming out of it
---

A skier fast enough onto Fall Line's open tarn skims across it on his skis (a `skim` event),
slowed by the water until he reaches the far bank or goes in; slower, he goes in, swims for the
shore as the engine's clothed swimmer in 4-degree water and climbs out onto the bank (an `ashore`
event), or is reset if he drowns or tires. Going in and climbing out splash; the `tarn-ride` lab
rides a ladder of speeds onto it.
