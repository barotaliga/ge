title: The crowd on Fall Line's free ride
---

A free ride can now be dealt a crowd of amateurs (`deal_crowd`, after `create_free_ride`):

- **Who:** groups of solos, couples, families, friends, school classes behind an instructor and drunks, of nine kinds, each amateur with his own skill, pace, width, wobble and turn style.
- **The runs:** chosen by their colour and the group's least skill; the leader chooses, the rest follow or ski his track; they stop, fall and get up.
- **The lifts:** a queue at a run's foot, seats on the carriers, let off at the top and skated down its ramp onto a run.
- **The player:** pushed apart from an amateur by their masses, knocking him down past his knock, with a `bump` event.
- **Determinism:** the crowd draws from its own stream, so a free ride dealt none keeps every digest.

The `crowd` lab and `tests/fall_line_crowd_test.cpp` hold it.
