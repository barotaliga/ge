type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters in Quake step round what blocks their way
---

Under classic remix (`cr_monsters q2`), Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign that have lost sight of the player now step round whatever blocks their way to where they last saw him, as they do in Quake II: when their path is blocked they first take a short step to the clearer side, then carry on to the spot. Before, they walked straight at the block and relied on the campaign's own steering to find a way round.
