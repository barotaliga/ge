title: Quake II's death, join, leave and limit lines are the game's own, and co-op spawns and carries as it should
---

Quake II's obituaries are the game's own lines word for word, with the victim's gender; a single-player death says only that he died; players joining and leaving are announced to everyone; the limits say `Timelimit hit.` and `Fraglimit hit.` and the next map follows the game's order. Teleporter exits and deathmatch spawn spots are discs a player stands on, and the window predicts against them. Co-op players come in at the right spots on the maps that misname theirs, and a dedicated co-op server keeps what each player carried across a level change.
