title: Ground Zero's pushers, gravity triggers, barrels in lava and plats monsters ride
---

In Ground Zero a pusher can start off and be switched by its uses (or stand as a wall when nothing targets it) and can blow silently, a gravity trigger takes a fraction of the level's gravity, reaches players and can be switched on and off, a barrel that falls into lava goes off, a plat crushes a body in its way, keeps monsters from stepping onto it while it rises, and is called up or down by a monster chasing its enemy to another floor.
