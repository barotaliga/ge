---
type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge plays a demo flat out and reports its frame rate
---

The `shooters_edge` window reads single-player demos and multiplayer client recordings, and `benchmark <demo>` (or `-benchmark <demo>` on the command line) plays one with vsync off, one demo frame a drawn frame, then prints the average, max, min, 1% low and 0.1% low frame rates; the `se-timedemo` lab runs the same loop headless.
