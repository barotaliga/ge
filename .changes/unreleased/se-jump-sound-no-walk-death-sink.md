type: Changed
title: Shooter's Edge plays the jump's sound, has no walking, crouches on C, and lowers the weapon on death
---

A jump is heard again (`player/plyrjmp8.wav` from the game's own packs), and a swimmer pushing up splashes. The classic game has no walking, so `cl_run` and the ALWAYS RUN option are gone: SHIFT (`+speed`) is the run key and never slows the player. C crouches (`+movedown`, with `+moveup` beside it): the sequel's move ducks on it, and the classic game swims down. When the player dies, the weapon in his hand sinks out of view over 0.6 s instead of hanging in front of a dead man's eyes; recorded games and joined servers show it too.
