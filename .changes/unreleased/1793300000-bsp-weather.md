type: Added
title: Wind, rain, snow and storms on BSP maps, kept to where the open sky reaches
---

BSP maps get weather, turned by cvars: `sv_weather`, `sv_precip` (how hard it falls), `sv_rain` (snow, sleet or rain), `sv_wind`, `sv_winddir` and `sv_gust` on the server, and `r_precip`, `r_precip_density`, `r_splashes`, `r_wet`, `r_snowcover` and `r_lightning` for the player. The v2 renderer draws a depth map along the way the fall comes, so the rain, the snow, the splashes, the wet faces, the puddles (ringed by the drops while it rains) and the drifting snow fall only where the open sky reaches as the wind slants them: through a window as far as the wind drives it, and never into a room behind a wall. A storm flashes with lightning over what is open and darkens the sky under its deck. The engine's precipitation gains the snow's cover (`step_cover`) and the lightning (`flash_at`, a pure function of the clock), and any world can bind the same shelter map to its fall (`render::bind_precip_shelter`). The new lab `gpu-bsp-weather` shows eight weathers on a test map of a hall, a yard and a store.
