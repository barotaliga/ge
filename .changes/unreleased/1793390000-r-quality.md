title: One quality setting for Fall Line's picture
---

`r_quality` picks the whole picture at once: `low`, `medium`, `high` or `top` by name, a number from 0 to 1 (the best-looking picture for that share of the way from the cheapest to the top, by each setting's measured cost), or `auto` (the best inside `r_budget` milliseconds of GPU). The default, `custom`, keeps each `r_` setting as set. On an M3 Max at 2560x1440 a frame takes 2.1 ms at `low`, 4.6 at `medium`, 6.0 at `high` and 7.9 at `top`.
