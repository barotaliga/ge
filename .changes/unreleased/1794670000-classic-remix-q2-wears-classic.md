---
type: Added
title: Classic remix dresses Quake II in Quake's weapons, armour, ammunition and health
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items q1` in Quake II, from the next map Quake II's rules wear Quake's things: the classic guns lie on the floor and are held in the hand, the armour is the classic armour in its colour, the ammunition and health are the classic boxes, and the guns, blasts and pickups sound as Quake's. A dedicated Quake II server does the same with `sv_wear q1`.

The HUD follows the items: Quake II then shows Quake's status bar, its guns, counters, armour and power-ups read from Quake II's player.
