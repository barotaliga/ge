type: Added
title: Quake II's scr_chathud_x and scr_chathud_y
---

In Quake II, `scr_chathud_x` and `scr_chathud_y` place the chat lines on screen in the game's own 2D pixels (8 and -64 by default: near the lower left, 64 pixels up). Quake and QuakeWorld still place them by `hud_chat_x` and `hud_chat_y`.
