type: Added
title: Quake II's chat lines placed by scr_chathud_x and scr_chathud_y; gzipped demos by name
---

In Quake II, `scr_chathud_x` and `scr_chathud_y` place the chat lines on screen in the game's own 2D pixels (8 and -64 by default: near the lower left, 64 pixels up); Quake and QuakeWorld still place them by `hud_chat_x` and `hud_chat_y`. A Quake II demo named without its extension is also found as `.dm2.gz` or `.mvd2.gz`. A server's relay password is `qtv_password` in every classic game; Quake II's `sv_mvd_password` still sets it.
