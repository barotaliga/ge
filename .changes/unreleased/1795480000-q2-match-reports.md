---
type: Added
title: Quake II matches reported to the master
---

A Quake II server now reports each match to its master like a QuakeWorld server does: the mode, map, length, how it ended, the teams, and every player's frags, deaths, account, ping and weapon stats, so a player's totals on the hub count their Quake II matches too. A match report may now carry up to 96 columns.
