The `shooters_edge` window draws the game's effects. Rockets leave trails of embers and smoke and glow as they fly. Explosions throw sparks and smoke and light the walls round them. A hit flashes the screen red, and the screen is tinted under water, slime or lava. The world takes the game's two looks, `r_look classic|modern`, with the picture's `r_` cvars. The classic look grades the picture to the packs' palette. `r_look` replaces `r_bspmode`, and the missile markers are gone.
