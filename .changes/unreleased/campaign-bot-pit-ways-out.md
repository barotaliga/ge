type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot climbs out of pits it falls into
---

Bots now find a way out of a dry pit that has no way up on foot, by a rocket jump onto ground they can get back from. On The Reckoning's xship the campaign bot used to fall into the landing bay soon after leaving its start pod and stay there to the end of the map. It now climbs back out and plays on, with 20 of 51 kills where it had 6.
