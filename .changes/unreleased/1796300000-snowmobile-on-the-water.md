title: The snowmobile on the tarn's open water
---

Fall Line's free-ride snowmobile, driven onto an open tarn faster than 11 m/s, planes across it on its belt's paddles: it holds 18 m/s flat out, and over the far shore it is back on the snow. Slower than 6 m/s it goes under to the tarn's bed and throws its rider into the water, where he swims out. His reset hauls the machine out onto the bank and puts him back on it. The `tarn-ride` lab adds the machine's ladder of speeds and a picture of its lines.
