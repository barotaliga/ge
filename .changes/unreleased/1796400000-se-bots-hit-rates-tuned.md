type: Fixed
title: Bots' hit rates read true, and teleporters telefrag
---

A Shooter's Edge team game no longer counts the ammunition a respawn empties as shots, which had made every bot look like it hit almost nothing. A teleporter in the deathmatch mod now kills whoever stands on its destination, as a spawn point already did, so players no longer get stuck inside each other. Bots fire only when the view they actually send is on the target, fire as loosely as their aim trait (a novice sprays, an expert waits for the shot), keep the double scattergun for close range and aim close rockets at the body. The team lab's new weapon drill gives every player one weapon, so each weapon's hit rate can be measured on its own.
