title: Quake's ground shadows, glow discs, the see-through bar's column, episode and level rows, the quit jokes
---

`r_shadows` draws Quake's half-dark shadows under every figure, monster and item, all leaning one way; `gl_flashblend 1` draws the flashes of guns and blasts as glowing discs and leaves the walls unlit by them. With the classic bar's panels off (`hud_sbar 0`, QuakeWorld's `cl_sbar 0`) the guns owned and the four ammunition counts stand in a column over the inventory row's right end, or its left with `hud_swap 1` (`cl_hudswap`); the options page has both rows. The host page picks a map by EPISODE and LEVEL as Quake did, and Quake's quit box asks with one of its eight jokes and takes Y or N.
