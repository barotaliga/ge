type: Added
title: Other players' gun flashes light the room, and cl_muzzleflash
---

Shooter's Edge: when another player or a bot fires, its gun's flash lights the walls and floor around it for a tenth of a second, in a game on our server, in a classic server's game (its muzzle-flash message) and in a played recording. `cl_muzzleflash` sets it: 1 every gun's (the default), 2 the others' alone, 0 none. Lab `se-demo-video` takes `--muzzleflash=0` for the unlit before.
