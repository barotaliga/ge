type: Changed
title: The live map shows weapons from above and hides teleporter ways in a match
---

On the live map, weapons and the other turning pickups are seen straight from above, still turning and larger than life. The teleporters' ways no longer show while a match is watched (they would give away where players go); a map's preview still shows them, flowing toward where each teleporter puts you with an arrowhead at the far end, so a one-way teleporter reads as one.
