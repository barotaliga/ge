The renderer draws vertex-animated models (morph meshes). Each one is drawn in its team's colours from one skin, blends smoothly between its animation frames on the GPU, glows where its skin says, and can be sharp (the classic look) or smooth. A model can be lit by the sun and sky or by the light a game samples where it stands. The `gpu-morph` lab shows a runner in four teams, its frames and in-betweens, at dusk and indoors.
