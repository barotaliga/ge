type: Added
title: Quake II's bot lab plays the player's own deathmatch maps
---

The se-q2-bots lab takes `--game` and `--map` to play its four bots on a deathmatch map from the player's own packs. The bots now take the view a spawn spot or a teleporter turns them to, so they no longer walk in circles after one, and they plan their walks with Quake II's ground speed and friction.
