---
type: Added
title: Ground Zero's monsters dodge, strafe, fire blind, jump ledges and follow hint paths
---

In Ground Zero every monster ducks a high shot and side-steps a low one, more often on harder skills, strafes while it fights, fires blind at the place it last saw you, and jumps down or up a ledge to reach you. Medics give up on a body they cannot reach and never raise the same monster twice, and a raised monster no longer counts toward the level's kills. Monsters that lose you follow the map's hint paths to find you again.
