Shooter's Edge's team bots call the quad, their items and the enemy over team chat, and know the map by their skill: an expert times the key items he sees taken, an average player knows the map but not the timings, and a novice learns where the items are as the match goes; team chat now reaches only the speaker's team, and bots no longer drown walking along a pool's floor.
