type: Added
title: Mission pack discs and zips offered
---

A mission pack's disc image (`.iso`, `.cue`, `.bin`, `.img`, `.mdf`) or release `.zip` lying in the folders Shooter's Edge searches is offered at the start and by `secli import` too, found by reading only its listing.

A cue sheet renamed together with its image (as a download names both after the release) is read through the image with the sheet's own name, and the folder a `.7z` was unpacked in is removed with the image.
