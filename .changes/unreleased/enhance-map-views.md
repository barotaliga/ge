title: The enhance lab draws a map from its starts with the cache's textures
---
The `enhance` lab now draws a map from its player start and other starts twice side by side: once with the pack's own textures and once with the enhanced copies read back from the cache, both lit by the map's lightmaps. On the lab's fixtures the brush fixture map wears the painted textures enhanced in the lab; with `--source`, `--cache` and `--maps` a local pack's maps are drawn from the cache the classicenhance tool wrote.
