type: Changed
title: The hub's web viewer downloads in a quarter of the bytes, with a progress bar
---

The web build leaves the other worlds' moon maps out of the viewer, and the build writes gzip
copies of the viewer's files that the hub sends to browsers that take gzip. A first visit to a
Demo page fell from 6.8 MB to 1.85 MB. A bar under the picture and the status line show the
download in megabytes as the viewer, the demo and the game's files come.
