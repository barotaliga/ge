type: Fixed
title: Shooter's Edge's players run and stand, swimming too
---

The baseline mod now animates every player's body as the classic games do: a frame every 0.1 s, the run cycle while he has any speed across the plan (on the ground, in the air or swimming) and the stand cycle when he has none, the blade's own cycles with the blade in hand. Before, a player held one pose wherever he was. The `se-swim` lab swims a player through a real map's water and draws him from beside him.
