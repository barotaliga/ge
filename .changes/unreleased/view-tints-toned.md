---
type: Added
title: The view's tints toned by the player
---

Shooter's Edge lets a player tone each tint of the view: `v_contentblend` for water, slime and lava, `v_damagecshift` for a hit's flash, `v_quadcshift`, `v_pentcshift`, `v_suitcshift` and `v_ringcshift` for the powers, `gl_cshiftpercent` for all of them, and `gl_polyblend 0` to turn them off.
