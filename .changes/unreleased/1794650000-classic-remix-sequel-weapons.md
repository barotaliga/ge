type: Added
title: Classic remix dresses the classic game in the sequel's weapons
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items 2`, Shooter's Edge's classic game plays its own maps with the sequel's weapons and armour from the next map on: the sequel's guns in the hand and lying on the floor, their missiles, their firing, bouncing and exploding sounds, and its jacket, combat and body armour with their pickup sounds. The classic rules keep their own damage and rates, and the sequel's models keep their own colours.
