title: Linked doors, key doors, shot doors and waiting plats in our programs

Our programs' doors now open in groups: doors whose boxes touch open and shut together unless spawnflag 4 keeps one apart. A door that needs a key (spawnflag 16 the silver, 8 the gold) opens only for a player holding it and tells anyone else which key it needs; a door with health opens to a blow; a named door shows its words when touched; and a named plat waits at its top until it is used.
