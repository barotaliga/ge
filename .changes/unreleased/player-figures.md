title: Shooter's Edge draws each player of the second game in their own figure and skin
---

In the second game each player is drawn in the figure and skin their `skin` setting names, "<model>/<skin>": the figure from `players/<model>/` with the weapons it carries, its skin from the pictures beside it (.tga, .png or .pcx). A figure you do not have is drawn as male, a skin the figure lacks as male's of that name or else the figure's grunt, and `noskins 1` draws everyone as male in grunt. Skins are read the first time someone wears them, so every skin the game ships can be worn, in the first game too.
