---
type: Added
title: Co-op helper bots that work the level near you
---

In a Quake or Quake II co-op game you host, `addbot helper` (with a skill too, as in `addbot expert helper`) seats a bot that works the level instead of keeping at your shoulder. Helpers share out buttons, keys, secrets and monsters among themselves by who can walk there soonest. They take nothing far from the players and never take the exit, which stays yours. With nothing left near you, a helper comes back to you. `addbot escort` (the default) keeps a bot at your shoulder as before.
