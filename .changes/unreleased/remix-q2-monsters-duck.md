---
type: Changed
title: Quake II's soldiers in Quake's campaign duck your shots and fire on the run
---

With `cr_monsters q2`, Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign now dodge as Quake II's do: a nail, rocket or bolt fired at one from in front is ducked one time in four, and above the easiest skill a soldier holds its duck until the shot has passed and sometimes shoots back from it. A soldier that finds you 500 units or more away also runs at you firing half the time, as in Quake II.
