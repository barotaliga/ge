title: Quake's weapons on Quake II's rules allow for the shooter's lag
---

Under classic remix, Quake's shotguns, lightning gun and axe on Quake II's rules now hit other players where the shooter saw them on screen, as Quake II's own hitscan weapons already did. With lag compensation for projectiles turned on, Quake's nails, grenades and rockets do the same.
