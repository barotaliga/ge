title: The woods seen through by Fall Line's camera
---

The trees between Fall Line's camera and the skier are seen through. An instance family can be drawn seen through to a point (`render::set_see_through`): every upright instance whose crown holds the eye or crosses the line to that point keeps only a dithered share of its pixels, so nothing pops. Fall Line's frame does this for the forest on every rung of its camera ladder. A camera lens is now also kept off the bark of a trunk that a wiped-out skier is thrown into.
