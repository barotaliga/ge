type: Fixed
title: The console's suggestions walked with the arrow keys
---

In every game's console, Down steps into the list of suggestions under the input line and down it, stopping at its last row; Up goes back up and, from the first row, returns to the line being typed so typing goes on. Up with no row chosen recalls the last line sent, and Down from there brings back the line not yet sent. A recalled line edited lists its suggestions again, and Tab takes the chosen row under Quake II's own Tab too.
