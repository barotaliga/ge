---
type: Changed
title: Lava seen from inside is a see-through glowing goo
---

With a game's reach (`Liquids::under_reach`; Shooter's Edge's 3 m), an eye under a lava's level sees a few metres into a thick, light-red, glowing goo instead of the opaque melt: what is in it is lit red by its glow, the scene fades into it with red carrying farthest, and the surface overhead stays opaque churning melt. Without a reach lava stays opaque.
