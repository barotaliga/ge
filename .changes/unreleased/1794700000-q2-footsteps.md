type: Added
title: Footsteps in Quake II, and its recordings' footsteps and landings heard
---

In Quake II, a player running faster than a walk now makes a footstep with each stride, heard by everyone nearby. Playing Quake II's own recordings, or a server of Quake II's own, now plays the footsteps, landings, item respawns and teleports those carry as entity events, which were silent before.
