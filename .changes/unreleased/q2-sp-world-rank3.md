type: Changed
title: Quake II's single player closes its last differences from the original
---

Door teams and trains move as one and only the first sounds, water never jams, a rider on a turning floor barely turns, delayed messages say their usual chime, falling light ramps step like the original's, power armour switches off when its cells run out, breaths under water keep the original's beat, coop players each take every power cube, the Makron leaves his torso writhing when he falls and the technician stands two ways.
