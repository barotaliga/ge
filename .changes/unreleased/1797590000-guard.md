type: Added
title: The guard, the server's anti-cheat checks
---

`engine/net/guard.hpp` checks every input a replication server receives. A field off its range is clamped, a field off the controls' grid is snapped, more inputs than ticks counts as a flood, and a view from the future is held to the server's tick. In peer games, a step longer than a tick allows is refused. Each flag raises a score that decays, and past a line the client is out. The `cheats` lab plays five cheats against honest players on every link.
