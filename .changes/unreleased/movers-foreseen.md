---
type: Added
title: Lifts, doors and jump pads keep up with you on QuakeWorld and Quake II servers
---

On a server you join, a jump pad or wind tunnel now throws you the moment you step in, and lifts, doors and trains are run on at their own speed while your moves are predicted, so riding a lift or running through an opening door no longer jolts you when the server's word arrives. The lift is drawn under your feet where your prediction has it. `cl_predict_push 0` and `cl_predict_movers 0` go back to waiting for the server.
