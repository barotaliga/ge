type: Fixed
title: Quake II co-op plays through every campaign map
---

In Quake II co-op a player who dies now comes back when he presses attack, keeping his keys. Keys and other items resting on a moving platform now stay on it instead of falling through, so city3's security pass can be taken, and an item beside a turning machine is no longer swept away (city3's data CD). Entering fact3 with a companion no longer crashes the game.
