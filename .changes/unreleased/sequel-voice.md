---
type: Added
title: Voice chat on our servers of the sequel
---

Joined to one of our servers of the sequel, you can now talk to the other players with the same keys as in the first game: talk to your team, to everyone, or only to the players near you. Muting everyone or one player works the same way, and so does the nearby chat line. Players on stock clients are unaffected. Teams decided by skin or model now also hold on our servers of the sequel.
