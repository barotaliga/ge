Shapes in the 2D layer (`ui/draw.hpp`), on the CPU raster and in the GPU's 2D pass alike: lines and polylines, arcs with flat or round ends and any paint, convex polygons of up to 16 points, boxes turned about a pivot, and nine-sliced images whose corners keep their pixels at any size. A HUD's dials, needles, ring meters, framed panels and turned maps are drawn from them; the `menu` and `menu-gpu` labs picture an instrument panel built of every one.
