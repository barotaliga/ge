Shooter's Edge: a console or message-feed line too long for the screen wraps onto the rows under it instead of being cut; a server's refused stuffed commands and a demo the player left are no longer announced; a pickup told in pieces reads as one line. Under water the picture still wobbles but the view and the held weapon no longer breathe (`r_waterwarp` switches the wobble). A Quake II new game, single player or co-op, starts with no lobby; the lobby's F1, F2 and F4 (F5, F6 and ENTER on its screen) are its own over a pack's binds; Quake II's lobby is drawn in its own letters; `lobby_scale` and `scores_scale` size the lobby and the scoreboard apart from the menu. `r_particle_scale` draws blood, sparks and a shot's or a nail's hits larger, and the teleport dust and arrival flash have their own EFFECTS page.
