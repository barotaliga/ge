type: Added
title: A client whose clock runs fast is logged or dropped
---

`sv_speedwarn` and `sv_speedkick` hold the move time a client's commands ask for against real time over `sv_speedwindow` seconds (16 by default): above the first the server logs it, above the second the client is dropped and the others told. Both are off by default; the sequel's `sv_timescale_warn`, `sv_timescale_kick` and `sv_timescale_time` are its spellings.
