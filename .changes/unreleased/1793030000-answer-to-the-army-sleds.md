title: The skier's answer to the army's snowmobiles
---

On a hunt, the army's snowmobiles now have a hull the skier is held out of, and a hard meeting throws him down. Skiing into a machine at a run knocks its crew off it, and running down a soldier on foot puts him over; the men lie a few seconds before they get up, and either one raises the heat. A machine its crew left standing can be stolen with the machine press, ridden like the free ride's own, and taken again after hopping off; the theft raises the heat too. The `army-sleds` lab gains an `answer` scene with a close-up of the knock and the theft.
