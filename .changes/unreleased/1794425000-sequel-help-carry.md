title: The sequel's help computer and the body carried between levels
---

The sequel's single player now has its help computer: a new objective beeps three times and waits for `help`, which shows the skill, the level, both messages and the kills, goals and secrets found. A level change carries everything a player holds to the next level and brings him in where the exit says; a new game starts afresh, and flags set on one level can open the way on another.
