type: Added
title: The sequel's doors, lifts, buttons, trains and triggers
---

The sequel's levels now move: doors open as a player walks up, when used or when shot, and close after their wait; lifts come down, carry whoever stands on them up and wait at the top; buttons press in and fire what they name; trains follow their path; anything caught in a mover's way is pushed along or crushed. Triggers fire once or again and again, relays and timers pass the word on, push pads launch, hurting fields bite, teleporters move a player (killing whoever stood there), and a level exit takes the game to the next map. Shots stop at doors, and doors with health open when shot.
