type: Added
title: The sequel's teams, scores and bodies that stay
---

The sequel's deathmatch now plays in teams by skin or by model, with friendly fire on or off and a point taken for killing a teammate; `score` lists the scores, which everyone also sees when the level ends; the field of view can be fixed at 90 for the match; and up to eight bodies of the fallen stay where they dropped until a blast throws them apart.
