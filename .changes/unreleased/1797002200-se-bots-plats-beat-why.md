title: Shooter's Edge's bots board plats like people, hold a fight through a peek, foresee their bumps, and say why they did it
---

The bots wait clear of a plat until it has come down and stopped, and step onto it only then; one that ends up under a raised plat no longer walks into its legs again and again (dm2: the bots 21 u/s faster and a fifth more frags a minute). A fight is held through a moment out of sight instead of flickering to a chase and back. The walk looks a quarter second ahead and eases off before a wall or another player rather than running into them, and walks round a player standing in its way. `se-team --why` writes every bot's decisions to a log, `make bot-why` answers why a bot did something at a moment of a match, and `make bot-video` plays a match of the bots and renders it. A window takes `-config <file>` (its own config, read and saved there) and `-logfile <file>`.
