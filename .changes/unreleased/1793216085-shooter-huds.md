title: Picture HUDs and Shooter's Edge's three looks
---

The HUD kit draws pictures, digit sheets, strips of cells and rings, hangs an element from a
parent (`hud_<id>_parent`, `hud_<id>_attach`), snaps pixel art to a whole scale and samples it
nearest (`ui::Box::nearest`); Shooter's Edge has a classic, a modern and a compact HUD picked by
`hud_style`, in its own art or the classic set's pictures.
