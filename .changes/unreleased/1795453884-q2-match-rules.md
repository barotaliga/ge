type: Added
title: Quake II servers play a match, with a tournament's switches
---

Our Quake II server now warms up, counts down on `ready` and restarts the map as the match, with timeouts (Quake II's `time`, `calltime`, `ctime` and `hold` too), overtime, prewar and the join lock, on the match rules QuakeWorld's server shares. New rules switches: `g_fast_weap_switch`, `g_teleporter_nofreeze`, `g_spawn_mode`, `g_removed_weapons`, `g_removed_powerups` and `g_warmup_loadout`. Words said during a timeout now reach the players. The recipe commands are refused under every competition ruleset.
