type: Changed
title: Settings the classic games share answer to one name
---

QuakeWorld's `scr_consize`, `scr_conalpha`, `gl_consolefont`, `r_drawparticles`, `cl_net_clientport`, `in_grab_windowed_mouse` and `vid_reload` now reach the settings every game shares, and Quake II's `gl_swapinterval` the window's vertical sync. `gl_modulate`, `gl_anisotropy`, `in_grab` and `net_clientport` are now every classic game's, keeping their values when another game is loaded.
