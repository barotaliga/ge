title: Shooter's Edge has four more HUD looks: bare, arena, tactical and minimal
---

`hud_style bare` draws the classic status bar's pieces without either bar's panel. `arena`, `tactical` and `minimal` place and size health, armour, the rounds, the guns and the powerups after today's shooters (big figures at the bottom centre and the guns down the right edge; a shield bar over the health bar and the loadout bottom right; small figures only). Every look draws a loaded classic game's own pictures, and the options menu's HUD row cycles all seven.
