type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge's HUD has three more looks and options for the bar, the side and the guns
---

`hud_style arena`, `tactical` and `minimal` place and size health, armour, the rounds, the guns and the powerups after today's shooters. Over every look: `hud_sbar 0` draws the classic bar's pieces without its panels; `hud_swap 1` puts every look but the classic one on the other side of the screen; `hud_guns ammo|pairs|row` groups the guns two to a line by the ammunition they share (each line led by its count, or the counts apart) or puts them in one row; `hud_guns_all 0` and `hud_ammo_all 0` keep only what is held. The classic bar now sits flush on the screen's bottom edge and raises the weapon in hand above it. Each option has a row on the options menu's VIDEO page, and every look draws a loaded classic game's own pictures.
