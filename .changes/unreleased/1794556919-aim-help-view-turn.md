type: Added
title: The aim help turns the helped player's view
---

A player the aim help helps now sees his view turn toward the player his shot was bent onto while he fires, and back to his own aim after he lets go, so his recording looks like his own aim. The server names the target with the game's own message (a classic client is never sent it); `cl_aimhelp_turn 0` keeps the view still. The `se-aimturn` lab charts the turn on a strafer.
