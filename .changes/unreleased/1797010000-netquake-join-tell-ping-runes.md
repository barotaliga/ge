type: Added
title: Join a Quake server of protocol 15, Quake's tell, ping and version, the runes and gates in deathmatch
---

The window joins a Quake server of the single-player protocol (protocol 15, port 26000) with `connect`: it signs on with your name and colours, plays with your moves, sends your console lines to the server and leaves cleanly. A Quake server answers `tell` (one player by name) and `ping` (everyone's ping), and `version` prints the game's version in the window and on the dedicated server. Quake's deathmatch on the arena now has the runes ("You got the rune!"), the episode and boss gates, the trigger only the full game opens and the spinning teleport ball. Quake's spare program settings (`noexit`, `temp1`, `scratch1` to `scratch4`, `saved1` to `saved4` and the rest) are there for mods to read. The menu opens at any page by its classic console word (`menu_singleplayer`, `menu_load`, `menu_save`, `menu_multiplayer`, `menu_setup`, `menu_keys`, `menu_video`), `scr_printspeed` sets how fast an episode's ending types out, `ambient_level` and `ambient_fade` set the water and wind around you, `m_side` scales the mouse's sidestep, `msg` and `noaim` go to a joined QuakeWorld server, `user` shows one player's user info (on the server and in the window) and a QuakeWorld player's `msg` command sets their print level, and the mod compiler builds a call with too few arguments with a warning, as older mods need.
