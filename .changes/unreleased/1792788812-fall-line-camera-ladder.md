title: Fall Line's camera ladder on the engine's camera
---

Fall Line's run is seen through `games/fall_line/camera`: the tips, helmet, chase, far, high and orbit lenses as rows of engine rigs, the skier as the camera's subject (his lean, his landings and resets, the worn eyes set off his figure), the trunks a boom is pushed off and the gates' posts it pulls in for. The window walks the ladder with C, by an inertialized hand-over between booms and a blend to and from a worn lens; the play renderer's views are the same rungs. The `camera-world` lab pictures every rung at a run's moments and holds every lens out of the trunks and the snow.
