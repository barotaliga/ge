---
type: Changed
title: The same match stats and top scores for QuakeWorld and Quake II
---

Quake II now counts the stats QuakeWorld keeps: accuracy by weapon (each shotgun pellet counted), rocket and grenade splash, the rockets' average damage, mid-airs and their height, damage given, taken, to team mates and to oneself, armour and power-ups taken, quad time, streaks, quad runs and spawn frags. Both games count them by the same rules into the same columns and show the same lines when a match ends; a QuakeWorld match's end-of-match stats, which a report's extra aim column had kept from printing, now show. With two players or more the end adds the top scores (frags, efficiency, damage, mid-airs, streaks, quad runs, spawn frags). With `sv_autorecord 1` a Quake II server now records each match by itself, as QuakeWorld's does.
