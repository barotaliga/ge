type: Fixed
title: The classic axe plays one swing a swing
---

A classic game's axe used to play both of its swings, eight frames, on every swing. Each swing now plays one of the two, the one the game's own program picked, so the swings alternate at random as they do in the classic game. A held model may now carry several takes of one clip (`viewmodel::count_held_clips`), and the `se-client` lab's `--swings=<n>` films the axe swinging on a real map.
