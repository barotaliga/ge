type: Added
title: The real ski areas' lifts, pistes and woods on the real faces (R25): `real_hints.hpp`, the generator leaning on them and the `real-hints` lab
---

Every real face now carries its real ski area, read off OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0; the notice is in every generated file): its lifts by kind (chair, gondola, drag), its pistes in their signed colours with the width of each piste's area, and its woods by height (eight bands of cover and a tree line, where the map draws forest). `real_hints.hpp` decodes them; they are baked into `plugins/mountain/mapgen/real_hints/` by the new `real-hints` lab (`--bake --fetch --write`), which reads the open map API with curl, needs no credentials and caches what it read under `.cache/osm/`. The varint and base64 codec the faces' heights used is now `packed.hpp`, shared by both. On a face's first eight attempts the generator leans on them (`GenerateOptions::hints`, on by default): its stations and lifts move onto the real ones within reach, a piste slot is laid on the real piste leaving nearest its start (its colour, its line and its width) or steered through a real piste's bends, and a run is billed its real piste's colour where it measures within a colour of it, which the analysis now holds every run to (a dealt run is billed exactly what it measures). The face's woods follow the real cover by height and its tree line is the map's. The `real-maps` lab gains `--hints=0` and an `off real m` column: how far the pistes lie from the real ones. No dealt map moves.
