title: The paramotor flies the same at any step rate
---

The wing read the lines' angle before it had moved with the pilot in the same step. That leaned the lines back by the pilot's speed times the step, so the wing flew at a higher angle of attack and a lower speed the coarser the step: the launch topped out at 63.8 km/h at 60 Hz, 66.4 km/h at 120 Hz and 68.6 km/h at 480 Hz. The wing now moves on with the pilot before its lines are read, and the trim is set so the 120 Hz flight is as before (a trim near 55 km/h and a glide near 4). The launch now tops out within 0.3 km/h from 60 to 480 Hz.
