---
type: Added
title: Teleports happen the moment you walk in, on QuakeWorld and Quake II servers
---

On a server you join, walking into a teleporter now shows you at its far end and turns you to face its way on the very frame you step in, instead of a round trip later when the server's word arrives. It works on any server that runs the game's own rules, in QuakeWorld and in Quake II, and if the server does not send you after all, your view is put back within a moment. `cl_predict_teleport 0` turns it off.
