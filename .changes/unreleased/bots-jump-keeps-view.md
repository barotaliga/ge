type: Fixed
title: A bot's jump keeps its view
---

A bot lining up, running at or flying a jump no longer has its view held by a spot it watches (a button across a pit it means to press), so the jump is made on its line. Quake's e1m4 campaign bot now makes the jump off the south trap room's ledge, and `se-spot` no longer crashes on a map read from a pack.
