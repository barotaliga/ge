---
type: Added
title: Quake II's scoreboard and help computer
---

In a Quake II game, `score` now shows the deathmatch and co-op scoreboard over the view (faces, names, scores, pings and minutes, kept up to date while it is open, and shown when you die and at the level's end), and `help` opens the help computer with the level's objectives and the kills, goals and secrets found.
