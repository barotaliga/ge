type: Added
title: Classic remix shows Quake II's status bar, ammunition and health
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items q2`, Quake's HUD becomes Quake II's own status bar (health, the ammunition of the gun in hand, armour, the weapon just picked up, a power-up's time and the frags, in Quake II's pictures), and its ammunition boxes and health packs lie on the floor as Quake II's, with Quake II's pickup sounds.
