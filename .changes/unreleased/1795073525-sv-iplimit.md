---
type: Added
title: sv_iplimit caps the connections one address may hold
---

A server may now cap how many players connect from one address with `sv_iplimit` (an IPv6 address counts by its /48 network; the server's own machine is never counted). A client past it is told "Too many connections from your IP address." The first classic game's servers read it from their `server.cfg`, with no limit by default.
