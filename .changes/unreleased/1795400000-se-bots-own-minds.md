type: Added
title: Team bots with minds of their own, team message keys and place names
---

Shooter's Edge's team bots no longer share a planner: each reads the team lines his own client receives, a person's as well as a bot's, and decides alone. They use a team game's usual message keys (status reports, took, safe, lost, help, coming, point, enemy, have quad, get quad, quad over, "quad in 20"), read them in the spellings people type, and read and time by skill: an expert reads every line and times exactly, an average player catches repeats and is a few seconds out, a novice reads little and nothing in a fight. At the start each takes on the nearest of the launcher, the best armour and the quad and calls it safe; items a bot has no use for, and the best armour, the launcher and the lightning gun when a teammate has the quad, are left for a teammate with a point. Maps get place names from a `locs/<map>.loc` file in the game folder, or from their own items.
