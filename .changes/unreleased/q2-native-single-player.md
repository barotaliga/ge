---
type: Added
title: Quake II mods with their own game library play single player with saves
---

A Quake II mod that ships its own game library, such as a mission pack, now plays its campaign as the original server runs it: you arrive at the spawn point the exit names, levels you leave within a unit are kept and come back as you left them, each level makes an autosave, and save and load work, putting you back where you saved. Dying opens the load menu.
