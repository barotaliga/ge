type: Added
title: Voice chat
---

Voice chat over a session (`engine/net/voice.hpp`): Opus at a constant bitrate with in-band FEC, push-to-talk or voice activity from the microphone (`platform::open_audio_in`), the host relaying each voice to everyone, a team, one player or the players near, a jitter buffer that rebuilds and conceals lost frames, each talker mixed onto a new synth stream (`audio::Synth::stream`) with its own volume and mute, the `voice_*` cvars, and the `voice` lab.
