type: Added
title: A game's own Tab in the console
---

The engine's console view takes a game's own Tab (`ConsoleView::list_mode`, `list_threshold`): the
word completed to what the matches share and the matches listed one a line, in columns, or by kind,
asking first past a threshold. Quake II's `com_completion_mode` and `com_completion_treshold` turn it
on, and its `in_enable` 0 leaves the mouse alone.
