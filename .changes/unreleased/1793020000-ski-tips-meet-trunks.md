type: Changed
title: The skis meet the trunks at their tips, and a fallen skier stays down
---

A trunk is now met by the skis ahead of a skier before his body reaches it, at the tips and halfway to them. Met fast, the skis stop a ski's length short and he is thrown on over them; met slowly, the tips glance off and turn the skis aside. A thrown body lying on the snow no longer pumps itself back up off it when a joint presses a limb into the snow. The `injuries` lab gains a curve for a trunk anywhere across his line, which at 43 km/h now averages about what real tree collisions do.
