title: The sequel's hand grenade, drops at death and the intermission
---

The sequel's rules now have the hand grenade (its fuse lit as it is raised to throw, cooked while attack is held, thrown farther the longer it was held, and going off in the hand when held too long), drop the weapon in hand and, with the quad-drop rule, the quad at death for others to take, let a player `drop` a weapon, ammo or a powerup, throw a body apart under a heavy blow, hold everyone at the intermission spot at a level's end until a button is pressed, and follow the deathmatch rule bits for weapons that stay, infinite ammo, no health, no powerups, no armour, the same level, forced respawns and spawning farthest away.
