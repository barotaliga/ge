---
type: Changed
title: Quake's monsters in Quake II flinch as Quake's do
---

Under Quake II's rules (`cr_monsters q1`), Quake's monsters now feel pain by Quake's own rules: each kind waits its own time before the next pain (most 1 s, the brute 1.5, the shambler 2, the vore 3) instead of Quake II's 3 s, a shambler above 400 health shrugs off half its blows, and at the hardest skill each pain puts the next 5 s off. Quake's campaign reads the same numbers and is unchanged.
