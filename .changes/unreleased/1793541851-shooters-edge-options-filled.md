The ESCAPE menu's dimmed rows now work: CUSTOMIZE CONTROLS (the moves are console binds; ENTER, then a key), ALWAYS RUN (`cl_run`, SHIFT walks), LOOKSTRAFE, BRIGHTNESS (`gamma`), TEAMPLAY and SKILL, and SETUP's name and shirt and pants colours. OPTIONS ▸ CLASSIC ENHANCED holds the settings a player will change, a page a subject: textures and models; light and shadow; water, lava and sky; fire and wind; weather; with ENHANCE A PACK. VIDEO gained HUD PICTURES and HELD WEAPON. The window keeps every setting in `<game folder>/settings/config.cfg`.
