title: GPU and window labs
---

`Lab::shot` takes a `Det`: a `measured` picture (a GPU frame, which each adapter rasterises in its own way) keeps its pixels out of the lab's digest, and its sidecar and contact sheet say so. Two engine labs land with it: `gpu-capture` (the GPU test scene captured headless and held to its golden on the pinned software adapter) and `frame-pace` (the window's frame loop timed: present intervals and the run clock's steps a frame).
