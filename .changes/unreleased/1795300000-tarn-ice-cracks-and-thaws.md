title: The tarn's ice cracks, fits its shore and thaws
---

Going through Fall Line's tarn ice now cracks before the splash (`ice_crack`). The ice sheet is
clipped to the lake's shore (`ice_mesh`), and in the winter's last 40 days the tarn thaws
(`tarn::thaw_of`, `MapLake::thaw`): its ice melts away and goes rotten, bearing less than its
thickness says, drawn as grey slush and dark wet ice over a moat of open water at the shore.
