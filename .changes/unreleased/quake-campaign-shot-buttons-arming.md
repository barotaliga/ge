---
type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot shoots buttons from where it can stand and re-arms when out of shots
---

A button that is shot rather than pressed is now aimed at from the side it faces, from a place the
bot can reach. On Dissolution of Eternity's Cave of Death the bot now shoots the button across the
lava that slides out the bridge; before, it never left the start. A bot with no shot left in any of
its guns while monsters remain now goes for the nearest gun or ammunition it can use. Triggers that
answer only a shot are never walked into. Walls standing in the game block its routes. A door whose
key it lacks stays shut to its plans. A place it can only drop into is taken once nothing else is
left, even after a step it had done comes undone again.
