title: Quake's doors, plats and trains move their full distance, and the view drifts for keyboard players
---

In Quake's campaign, doors, buttons and secret doors travel their whole width, plats drop their whole height, trains ride as the original's and triggers reach as far; a keyboard player's view drifts to the slope as in the original (`v_centerspeed`, `v_centermove`); each level prints its banner in the console; `startdemos` and `stopdemo` work; models and sprites get the original's boxes; and every sound starts at its entity's middle and reaches every co-op player.
