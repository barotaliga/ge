title: The scrag's acid spit looks like the original's, made modern
---

In Shooter's Edge's modern look the scrag's spit keeps the original's two rows of green drops either side of its line, now as soft glowing drops that drift off and fade, with a green halo round the glob and a green splash, fizz and haze where it lands. Particles can be drawn as a soft round glow (`Particle::glow`). The classic look is unchanged.
