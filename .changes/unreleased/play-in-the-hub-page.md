title: Play a match against bots on the hub's own page
---

The hub has a Play page: pick a map and play it in the browser against three bots, the game's
own server running in the page. The keys and mouse work as in the game (a click locks the mouse to
the picture, Escape lets it go), with the weapon in hand, the status bar, the lobby's countdown and
the scores on TAB. Each map is sent as a small pack of only the files a match on it needs (about
1 MB for the third deathmatch level), and the player is a download of its own, so a page that
only watches stays as light as before.
