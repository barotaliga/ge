---
type: Fixed
title: The campaign bot no longer routes through floors that have closed, and fetches a launcher on zombie maps
---

A floor that closes over a hole, or a secret slab lying flat, now stops the bot's routes through
it, so it stops waiting under one for the rest of a map. On a map with zombies the bot fetches a
grenade or rocket launcher first, since only a blast puts a zombie down.
