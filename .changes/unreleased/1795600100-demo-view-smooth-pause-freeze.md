title: A played demo's view rolls and bobs smoothly, and a paused demo freezes whole
---

Shooter's Edge: watching a Quake or QuakeWorld recording, the view's strafe roll and bob follow the player's own velocity drawn between the recording's frames, so starting a strafe or walking forward and back no longer twitches. A paused demo, or one behind the menu, now freezes whole: flames, smoke trails, grenades, particles and the held weapon stop with it, and at a slower or faster demo speed they keep its pace.
