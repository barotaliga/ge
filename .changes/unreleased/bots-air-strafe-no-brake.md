type: Fixed
title: Bots no longer brake mid-jump while steering in the air
---

A bot steering a jump onto its target in the air pressed the side key with its view led into the turn, so the key pushed against the flight and stopped it: on e1m4 the jump across the slime pit fell short into the slime. The side key's push now stays at most a right angle off the flight, turning it without losing speed.
