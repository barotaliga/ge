---
type: Fixed
title: Campaign bots no longer get stuck under e1m4's crusher or in a closet whose door stays shut
---

On e1m4 a campaign bot could stand pinned for the rest of the map under the crusher that two shot buttons bring down into its pit, or drop into the ogres' closet over the slime pit and wait there, because its door only opens from a trigger in the pit. Bots now keep off the floor under a crusher that comes to rest for good (its top stays the way on), and count a place they can only leave through a door that stays shut as a trap, visited last. Over six seeds e1m4's kills went from 133 to 199 of 252, and no seed stops at 2 kills any more.
