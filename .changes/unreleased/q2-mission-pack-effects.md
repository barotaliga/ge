title: Ground Zero's effects in Quake II's own look
---

Quake II draws its mission packs' hits and blasts as they look in the packs: the heat beam from your gun (and a monster's), the tesla's lightning, the flechette's and the green bolt's flares, the disruptor's dark burst, the A-M bomb's growing blast, the bigger and the quieter explosions, the heat beam's sparks and steam, with their sounds.
