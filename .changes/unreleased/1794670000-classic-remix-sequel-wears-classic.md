---
type: Added
title: Classic remix dresses the sequel in the classic game's weapons, armour, ammunition and health
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items 1` in the sequel, from the next map the sequel's rules wear the classic game's things: the classic guns lie on the floor and are held in the hand, the armour is the classic armour in its colour, the ammunition and health are the classic boxes, and the guns, blasts and pickups sound as the classic game's. A dedicated sequel server does the same with `sv_wear classic`.
