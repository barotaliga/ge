type: Added
title: The music command, +use, the gun commands and F1 to F12 over a menu
---

The console's `music` command plays a track by hand once through before the level's own again (`info`, `play`, `stop`, `next`, `pause`, `toggle`; Quake II spells it `ogg`); `+use` sends the use button; `gun_next`, `gun_prev` and `gun_model` show a held weapon's frame or another weapon of the set; over a menu only F1 to F12 run their binds. Quake II says when `cl_maxfps` is rounded to whole milliseconds (`cl_warn_on_fps_rounding`), when a map has no visibility, and "No player models found." instead of opening an empty player page.
