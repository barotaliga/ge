type: Fixed
title: Quake II monsters brought in inside a wall stay where they are put
---

A Quake II monster a trigger brings in with its box overlapping a wall fell through the floor and out of every shot's reach, so a player hunting it (or a bot) never found it. Caught in solid, it now stays where it was put. On The Reckoning's first map the campaign bot killed 16 of the 49 monsters rather than 6 and went on to the next map.
