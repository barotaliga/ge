title: Combo jumps in the record runs and the campaign bot
---

The record-run reader now picks out combo jumps (a grenade laid, then a rocket fired on it as it
bursts), draws them violet on the route pictures and keeps them as their own trick rows. The
campaign bot can make one: it lays the grenade, switches to the rocket launcher and fires straight
down as it jumps, and only takes a combo its own movement proves lands.
