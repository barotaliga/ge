---
type: Changed
title: Quake's level loops merged and s_mixahead shared by every classic game
---

Quake and QuakeWorld now hear every looping level sound of one file (a hall of torches, a row of
humming lights) as one voice whose ears are the sum of theirs, each held to full, so a crowded
room is no louder than the game meant and never runs out of channels. `s_mixahead` is one setting
for every classic game; Quake's configs set it by its own spelling, `_snd_mixahead`.
