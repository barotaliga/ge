---
type: Fixed
title: Bots give up a dive that stalls under water
---

Shooter's Edge's bots no longer stay in the water for minutes on a dive whose way onto the ledge fails: a dive more than three seconds behind its planned swim, or short of air with the way back shorter than the way on, is given up and not tried again for thirty seconds. The bots' own random draws (where a novice explores, which team lines a player reads) now come from the engine's seeded stream.
