type: Added
title: The view under water in a lit pool
---

`underwater_look("pool")` adds four knobs to the view under water for a pool under a lit room: the water lit by the room round it, a rippled surface that wobbles Snell's window's edge and bends the world through it, the water below mirrored past the window, and caustics on the walls and floor under the surface and on the ceiling over it. Shooter's Edge's window uses it.
