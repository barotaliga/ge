---
type: Changed
title: Quake's scrag hunts as it does in Quake
---

In Quake's campaign the scrag now decides as in Quake: near the player and in his sight it slides sideways around him instead of flying straight at him, it spits by its own odds (often up close, rarely from afar), and after each spit it waits a flat two seconds (none at Nightmare). Its spit is now Quake's timing, two shots 0.3 s and 0.8 s into the attack, and a light hit makes it cry without flinching. Before, it flew straight in and attacked by the odds every other monster uses.
