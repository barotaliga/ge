---
type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign decide as Quake II's do
---

With `cr_monsters q2`, Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign now see, choose when to attack, pick their attack, keep an attack going and turn on each other by Quake II's own rules, the same code Quake II's campaign runs them by: they see you close by whichever way they face, fire less often at the middle skill and twice as often from skill 2, the easiest skill lets three blows in four go by, the tank picks its weapon by range, and a monster hurt by its own kind joins it instead of turning on it.
