title: Lamps on the spray, the falling rain and snow, the water and the morph models; lava lights its pit
---

The local lights now light the particles (a headlamp's beam in the spray), the falling snow and rain (drops flash as they pass before a lamp), the water (a lamp's glitter across a lake, with the facets' masking at a graze, its light into the water and on the foam) and the morph models. A glowing body hands the frame a few lamps of its own: `liquid_lamps` lays up to 16 point lights over a lava or glowing slime's plan, each as bright as its cell of surface, so a pit's walls glow at night. The `gpu-water` lab pictures a lake in the rain under a street lamp, `gpu-lava` the pit lit by its lava, and `precip --hour=22` the fall under the piste's floodlights.
