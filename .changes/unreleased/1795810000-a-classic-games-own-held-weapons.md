---
type: Changed
title: A classic game draws its own first-person weapons
---

With a classic game's folder open, Shooter's Edge draws that game's own held weapons (the first game's `progs/v_*.mdl`), read through its adapter (`held_weapons`), placed at the eye where the files author them and playing their own firing frames on a shot. A game whose files lack one draws no weapon and names the missing file; Shooter's Edge's own weapons are kept for its own content and never stand in for a classic game's.
