title: Scourge of Armagon's keys, title demos and remix roles

With Scourge of Armagon loaded, 9 picks the laser cannon and 0 picks Mjolnir, as the pack's own key layout has them. The pack's zoom aliases come along too, while your own keys stay as they were. The title now plays the pack's own four recordings, the ones its start file names, and a mission pack's console background is its own. In remix, the laser cannon, Mjolnir, the proximity gun and the wetsuit stand in for Quake II's hyperblaster, BFG, grenade launcher and environment suit.
