title: Mission packs' keys, title demos and remix roles
---

With Scourge of Armagon loaded, 9 picks the laser cannon and 0 picks Mjolnir, as the pack's own key layout has them. The pack's zoom aliases come along too, while your own keys stay as they were. The title now plays the pack's own four recordings, the ones its start file names, and a mission pack's console background is its own. In remix, the laser cannon, Mjolnir, the proximity gun and the wetsuit stand in for Quake II's hyperblaster, BFG, grenade launcher and environment suit. Ground Zero's ETF rifle, plasma beam, prox launcher and disruptor, with their ammunition and double damage, take the parts of the nailgun, the lightning gun, the grenade launcher, the rocket launcher and the quad in Quake.

A new setting, `cr_expansions`, decides whether classic remix takes the mission packs in. It is on by default: the other game's installed packs add their maps, their own weapons and items swap by what they do, and a map played with Scourge of Armagon loaded gets its laser cannon and Mjolnir where Quake would lay down the lightning gun. Turn it off to remix the base games alone.
