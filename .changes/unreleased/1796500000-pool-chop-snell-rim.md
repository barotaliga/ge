type: Changed
title: Pools ripple, Snell's window's rim softens, flooded passages see the far pool
---

Water drawn by `engine/render` can keep a chop (`WaterDraw::chop`): short waves running every way that a pool in use never loses, with no wind, in the surface's slopes and its caustics. Seen from below, the window's rim is taken per colour, averaged over the slopes a pixel cannot resolve and n² as bright as the air, and every pool the eye is under is drawn, the light between them weighed by distance (`level_over`), so a flooded passage between two pools no longer sees through the far one or changes its light halfway. Shooter's Edge's pools ripple from the players in them.
