type: Added
title: Hot marks burn a little, then smoke
---

A glowing mark on a wall (a beam's burnt line and melt, a slug's, a blaster bolt's or an energy ball's mark) now burns in a few small flames of burning gas while it is fresh; they shrink and go out one by one as it cools and thin grey smoke rises off it instead, gone a little after the glow (`engine/render/mark_fire.hpp`). Quake II's blaster and hyperblaster bolts now leave a small glowing mark where they land. Shooter's Edge draws them with its decals (`ce_decals`).
