---
type: Added
title: The player setup page shows your figure live
---

Quake's, QuakeWorld's and Quake II's player setup pages now draw the player's model live beside the rows, in the skin and colours chosen: a colour picker of swatches sets the shirt and pants, a SKIN row lists the skins the game's files hold (downloaded ones too), and a WEAPON row picks what the figure holds (the carried weapon when visible weapons are on). Click the figure to fire it; drag it to turn it, and it turns slowly when left alone.
