title: Quake II's map repairs, item teams and its commands' own words
---

Quake II's single player keeps the game's built-in map repairs (the unnamed starts of eight maps, fact1's intermission view, fact2's spawner, a trigger on the map `command` kept on hard) and a world's `music` key. Items sharing a team show one at a time. `use`, `drop`, `give` and the cheats answer in the game's own words, `cycleweap` and `prefweap` switch through a bind's weapons, and animated models and sprites cycle their frames.
