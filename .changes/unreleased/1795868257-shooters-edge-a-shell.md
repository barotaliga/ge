---
type: Removed
title: Shooter's Edge's free stand-in models
---

Shooter's Edge is now a shell over the player's own classic games, with no game of its own: no free stand-in for a classic game's content is planned. The 48 free models nothing drew (the player, pickups, keys, runes, creatures, projectiles, props and the explosion) are gone with their credits, and the registries keep only what the built-in arena, the headless tests' fixture, plays and shows: its 131 sounds and its eight built held weapons.
