---
type: Fixed
title: No see-through wedges where a server puts a figure on your spawn
---

A QuakeWorld match server that marks spawn points with see-through figures no longer leaves stray see-through wedges in your view when you stand on one: a model the eye stands inside is not drawn. A window started with `-recipes` also lists the entities in view (`entlist`) and hides or shows them by number or model (`enthide`, `entshow`).
