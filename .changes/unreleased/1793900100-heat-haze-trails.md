title: Heat haze behind nails and rockets
---

A new render pass bends the light through hot air (`engine/render/haze.hpp`): a game lays a trail a stretch a frame, and each stretch's ribbon shows what is behind it slightly displaced by drifting, rising cells, fading as the air cools. In Shooter's Edge's enhanced look, a nail's flight leaves a faint thread of it and a rocket's motor a wider wake beside its smoke and flame; the classic look is unchanged.
