---

Measured against the menu strips: their first row of words sits a pixel higher than every other row, so in the classic main menu SINGLE PLAYER stood a pixel above SELECT GAME, MULTIPLAYER and the rest; it is now set a pixel lower and every row's capitals and small capitals share one top and one foot. The cut Q and G now keep their whole tails, a space between words is the strips' own (5 px before a round capital, 10 before a stem), and on SELECT GAME "playing" stands on the name's line with the install's rows clear of the Q's tail.

The cut letters lost their strays: the k no longer carries a column of the e beside its arm, the G no longer the a's foot beside its jaw, the a's point no longer stands above the other letters' tops, and the c is as wide as the other small capitals, its top and foot curling round.
