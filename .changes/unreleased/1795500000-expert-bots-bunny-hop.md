title: Expert bots bunny hop like a recorded pro, and labs follow a demo's player through a real map
---

Shooter's Edge's expert and perfect bots now bunny hop between items the way a recorded pro does: the side key alone, the view kept on the velocity and turned as fast as the air allows, the side switched mid-air toward the route, the jump on the command they land, and a wall on the arc ahead turning them the other way; average bots hop the simple way, forward and the side key of the way they turn, which keeps their speed on a straight and gains in curves; novices walk. A new reader turns any player's track (a demo's, a bot's run) into its jumps, chains, turn and speed gain. Two labs show it on a real map: `se-moves` follows one player of a demo as a wireframe from above, at an angle and from inside the map with his path drawn as a line coloured by speed, and `se-hop` replays his longest trips with the bots' ways of moving from his own start and speed, flagging the trips he does much faster as tricks or shortcuts.
