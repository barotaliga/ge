title: A Quake II boss teleporting out swirls as it should, and a teleport no longer smears the view
---

A boss teleporting out now throws its own swirl of specks, pulled back in toward it and rising as they fade, with its sound heard everywhere; before it reused an ordinary teleport burst. On a server that speaks the newer protocol, a teleport snaps your view to where you came out instead of sliding the picture there over one frame.
