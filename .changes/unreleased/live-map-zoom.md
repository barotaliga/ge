---
type: Changed
title: The live map zooms in on itself before the level takes over
---

Pressing M over the whole live map now zooms the drawn map in onto the followed player, keeping its own background and no walls, and the level itself takes its place only near the ground, just before the view tilts up into the player's eyes. The flash of a player arriving no longer shows when the camera reaches him. Older sequel recordings now draw with the right colours, and each player wears his own look, with the standard one when his custom model is not installed.
