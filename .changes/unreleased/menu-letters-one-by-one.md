title: the classic menus' big words stand level and clean
---

SELECT GAME, DEMOS and the games' names in the classic menu were pieced from whole runs of the strips' carved words, so their letters sat at different heights and dragged stray pixels of their neighbours along. Each carved letter is now cut out of the strips into its own picture when the pack loads, with any speck of a neighbour left behind, and the menu lays a word out from them on one line with an even pixel between letters. In the sequel's menu every plate of a word is shaded to one surface, so no letter's cell shows its edge, and the space between words is cut from plain plate.
