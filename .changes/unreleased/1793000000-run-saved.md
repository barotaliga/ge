`Run::save` and `Run::load` on the play contract, with `save_run`, `load_run` and `check_saves` (`play/saves.hpp`): a save carries its setup and its step and reopens on its own. Every state the skier, snowmobile, helicopter and paramotor plugins step is described for saves, and so are the mountain's track, the racing tape recorder and the bots' hands, memory and steering; `describe` also walks C arrays and `shared_ptr`. `ge save write`, `load`, `check` and `diff`, and the `save-run` lab.
