title: Door gunners
---

Fall Line's army helicopters fire their door guns: the wanted level's gunners man two doors on each hunting machine, lay each gun onto the skier inside its arc and fire bursts through the gunfire by the rules of fire (none at one star, warning fire at two, aimed from three); a machine flying echelon is crabbed side-on to give its inward door the line, and the `gunfire` lab gains a doors table.
