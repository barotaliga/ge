title: Bots fight Quake II's monsters
---

Quake II's monsters now reach the bots as solid while they live, so bots take them for foes and fight them; before, a bot walked into a pack of soldiers and stood on their heads. A monster a bot bumps or stands on is felt though it lies outside its view, and a bot stuck against a monster or a barrel steps round it instead of jumping onto it.
