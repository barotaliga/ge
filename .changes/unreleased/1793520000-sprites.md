The renderer draws sprites: flat pictures stood in the world, such as flames, fireballs, bubbles, bushes and signs. Each one faces the viewer by one of five rules, upright or turned with the camera or fixed in the world, and is either cut out cleanly or blended as light or over the picture. It can glow on its own or take the sky's light, and look sharp (classic) or smooth. The `gpu-sprites` lab shows every kind by day, from above, close up, as an animation and at dusk.
