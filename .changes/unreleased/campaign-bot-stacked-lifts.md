type: Fixed
title: Bots no longer try to ride a lift from underneath it
---

Bots no longer plan a ride up a lift from a spot under its floor. On The Reckoning's xship, two lifts share one shaft. The campaign bot rode the lower one up and down for five minutes, waiting to board the upper one from below. Each way a bot finds out of a pit by a rocket jump is also now added once, not again on every pass.
