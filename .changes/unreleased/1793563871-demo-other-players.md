type: Fixed
title: a played demo shows the other players
---

Shooter's Edge's `playdemo` and `timedemo` now draw every entity a demo holds (the other players in their own colours, items, missiles and the level's statics), as `-demovideo` already did. A multiplayer demo recorded on a mod plays from that mod's game folder when it sits beside the base folder, so its own models are found; a `-game` you pass still wins.
