type: Added
title: Ranked games from the game's console and servers that offer their seats
---

A server with `sv_ranked 1on1` (or `2on2`) offers its free seats to its account master's matchmaker every 20 seconds, signed with its key. In Shooter's Edge, `ranked 1on1` (or `ranked 2on2 <duo>`) looks for a ranked game by your rating and connects to the server once one is found; `ranked leave` stops.
