---
type: Changed
title: Shooter's Edge opens on its menu with no game of its own
---

With no classic game's packs open, the window no longer starts the built-in arena behind its title: it shows the menu over the moon backdrop, SELECT GAME first, with SINGLE PLAYER and MULTIPLAYER dimmed until a game is open. No menu lists the arena, and a game's map that does not load goes back to the title instead of the arena. `+map arena` still starts it for tests and labs.
