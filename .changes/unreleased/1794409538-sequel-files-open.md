type: Added
title: The classic game's sequel opens and its maps are entered
---

Shooter's Edge reads the sequel's game folder: its content and game rows, its own adapter on the calls every classic game shares (now one file, `client/adapter_parts.hpp`), wall texture files (`plugins/brush/wal.hpp`) for any map that keeps its pictures outside (in whichever game's folder it sits), and its palette from a PCX picture. Lab `se-sequel` enters each map from the player's start and draws it four ways in the classic and modern looks.
