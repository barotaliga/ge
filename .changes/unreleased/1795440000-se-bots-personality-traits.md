---
type: Added
title: Bots have personalities: traits from 1 to 10
---

A Shooter's Edge bot is now a set of traits, each from 1 to 10: its aim with each kind of weapon, its reaction, its timing of items, its knowledge of the map, camping, discipline, moves, hearing, communication, teamplay and aggression. Novice, average and expert are presets over the traits and play as before, so a bot can now be, for example, an expert with the rockets who never times an item. The team and bot labs take `--traits` (`aim_rl=9/timing=3`) and list every bot's traits.
