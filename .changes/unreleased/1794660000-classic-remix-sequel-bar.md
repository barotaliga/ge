type: Added
title: Classic remix shows the sequel's status bar, ammunition and health
---

With classic remix on and `cr_items 2`, the classic game's HUD becomes the sequel's own status bar (health, the ammunition of the gun in hand, armour, the weapon just picked up, a power-up's time and the frags, in the sequel's pictures), and its ammunition boxes and health packs lie on the floor as the sequel's, with the sequel's pickup sounds.
