type: Added
title: What a death leaves is a setting, and the arena has the cloak
---

Shooter's Edge's arena gains the cloak (a ring of invisibility: its holder is seen only as a pair of eyes for 30 seconds) and three settings for what a death leaves: `sv_droppack` (the backpack, on by default), `sv_dropquad` and `sv_dropring` (the Quad Damage and the cloak with the time they had left, off by default).
