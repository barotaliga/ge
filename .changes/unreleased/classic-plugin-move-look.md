title: Quake II's move and the classic look move to the classic plugin
---

Quake II's own player move and the classic look settings (`r_look` and the `r_` and `ce_` settings it sets) move from the shooter plugin into the `classic` plugin. The shooter plugin no longer depends on classic enhance or the program VMs, so a shooter of our own links neither. Nothing a player sees changes.
