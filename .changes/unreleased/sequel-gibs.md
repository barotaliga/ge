title: Bodies under the sequel's rules come apart into pieces
---

A body gibbed under the sequel's rules now throws its pieces: meat, bones, a head, a chest, or metal and a gear for the machines. They fly by the blow that did it, spin, land or bounce, and are gone after 10 to 20 seconds; a gibbed player lies as his head. God mode wears a white shell.
