Under Ground Zero, soldiers run and fire more often and can stand blind until woken, the gunner
lobs grenades only along a clear line, the parasite drains only within reach (and when its way is
blocked), the hover's long flinch grows rarer with skill, and the iron maiden wears the pack's
own model.
