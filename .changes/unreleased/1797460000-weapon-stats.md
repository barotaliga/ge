---
type: Added
title: Weapon stats for every match
---

Shooter's Edge now keeps each player's weapon stats over a match: attacks, hits, kills and deaths for every weapon, rocket and grenade splash hits and the rockets' average damage, mid-air rocket hits and their heights, damage given, taken, to team mates and to oneself, armour, mega healths and power-ups taken, quad seconds, spawn frags, the longest streak and quad run. Every player reads them in the console when the match ends, and they go to the account master with the match's report, where they add up per map, mode and ping like the rest of a player's totals (the longest streak and the highest mid-air keep their best). A report too big for one datagram is sent in pieces.
