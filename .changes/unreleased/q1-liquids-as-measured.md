type: Fixed
title: Quake's lava, slime and water feel and sound as in the original
---

In Quake's campaign, going into lava or slime hurts twice at once as in the original, every liquid has its splash going in and out, lava and slime burns cry out, a swimmer gasps on coming up after a long time under, drowning hurts more each second, and liquids slow a swimmer down. Lava and slime damage now comes from the engine's liquid rows.
