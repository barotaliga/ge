type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge draws a demo's and a joined server's explosions, trails, hits and beams
---

A played demo (`playdemo`, `timedemo`, `-demovideo`) and a game joined on a server now draw what the recording or the server throws: explosions with their light, rocket and grenade trails, a shot's and a spike's sparks, blood, lava splashes, teleport flares and beams, as a game played here draws them. The `se-timedemo` lab gains a `blast` picture and counts the demo's explosions and effects.
