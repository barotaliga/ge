title: the sequel's monsters show their drain, heal cable and muzzle flashes
---

In the sequel's single-player game, the parasite's drain and the medic's cable now appear as a line from the monster to its target, and every monster's shot flashes at its muzzle. The classic look draws them as specks and a light; with `ce_monsters` on, the drain writhes, the heal cable twists, each ends in a glow, and a gun or a rocket launcher fires a flame from the muzzle.
