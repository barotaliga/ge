type: Added
title: The sequel's coop weapons stay and its lifts wait for the others
---

In the sequel's coop game a weapon stays where it lies for everyone. A player who already holds that weapon may take each copy that nobody has taken yet once, for its ammunition (`coop_pickup_weapons`, on by default). A lift that someone steps on waits `coop_elevator_delay` seconds (1 by default) before it rises, so the others can board.
