type: Added
title: Shooter's Edge draws each player in their own skin
---

Each player wears the picture their `skin` setting names, `skins/<name>.tga`, `.png` or `.pcx`: a .pcx in their colours as the figure's own skin is, a .tga or .png as painted. `baseskin` (base) is worn by a player who names none or one you do not have, `allskins <name>` puts one skin on everyone, and `noskins 1` draws every player in the plain skin. Your own `skin` setting is sent to the server. Players with colours 8 to 13 now keep their skin's shading, as those colours' palette rows run from bright to dark.
