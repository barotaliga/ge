title: Campaign bots shoot hangar1's secret wall open and get out of biggun before it blows
---

On Quake II's hangar1, a bot fighting a hover from the pool under it kept its view on the pool's edge while trying to climb out, so it never shot the hover. It now gives the climb 3 seconds, then lets its aim have the view for a moment. The secret wall upstairs had been given up for good while the bot was stuck in that pool. It now gets one more try once the bot reaches somewhere new. The bot also shoots the wall while it is on its way to a gun behind it, and stops holding still once the wall opens so it can walk through. The bot now kills 22 of hangar1's 28 monsters, up from 13, though it still runs out of time. On biggun, the strips armed by the countdown button no longer hold back the way to the exit, so the bot leaves at 370 s instead of 375 s.
