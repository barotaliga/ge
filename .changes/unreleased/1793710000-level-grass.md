type: Added
title: Grass grows on a level's grass floors
---

A room level's material grows grass when its surface names it: `surface turf grass meadow height=0.6` in a level's text, or `surface grass1 grass lawn` in a cloned map's texture map. `ge::rooms::level_grass` makes one grass kind per recipe and names any material whose recipe does not read, and `grow_on_brushes` / `grow_on_rooms` (`plugins/rooms/grass.hpp`) take that material's floors into one `grass::Cover`: a face at least 37 degrees off a wall grows, and walls, ceilings, hidden, clip and liquid faces never do. The `rooms` lab draws the vault's grass floor grown, and the `clone` lab grows the textures `--grass` names as a lawn.
