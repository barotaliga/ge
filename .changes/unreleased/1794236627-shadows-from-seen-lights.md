type: Fixed
title: Figures' shadows in classic maps fall only from lights you can see
---

In the modern look, a player's or a monster's shadow now falls only from a light whose source shows: a torch, a flame, a globe, or a lamp at its glowing fitting. The fill lights a classic map hangs unseen in the air, and the glow of lava, slime and teleporters, light the room without throwing one, and the light bounced round a room is no longer darkened round a figure. `r_fill_shadows 1` brings back the old look.
