title: Clouds skied through, and a layer's thickness
---

A cloud layer lower than the mountain lies across its slopes, so the skier skis onto its top, through it and out of its base. Fall Line's `sv_cloudthickness` sets how tall the lowest layer stands (as dense a metre, so a taller layer is darker under it), and every layer starts at the top of the one under it. The cloud opacity knob `sv_thick` is renamed `sv_clouddensity`.
