title: Frost on Fall Line's village after a clear night, black ice in a freezing rain, and lake ice
---

A clear, calm night now leaves hoar frost on the village's parked cars, their windscreens rimed white, until the morning's sun takes it (`air::frost_on`, over `wind::deposit_on` and the new `wind::frost_loss`); a freezing rain glazes its streets to a dark, glossy black ice (`air::glaze_at`). A lake grows ice over a winter's freezing days (`air::lake_ice`) and is drawn frozen, snow on it but for the wind's swept patches, young ice clear and black (`mountain/app/ice_look.hpp`, the frame's `freeze_water`). An instance family's materials can be written again (`render::set_family_surfaces`).
