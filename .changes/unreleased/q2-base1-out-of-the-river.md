---
type: Fixed
title: Shooter's Edge's bots no longer walk back into water they climbed out of, or explore traps
---
A bot just out of the water no longer walks back down a bank to the spot it climbed out at, and in a fight it no longer takes a place in the water while standing dry. On a map with much of it behind doors not yet open, a bot no longer takes itself for trapped in a pool. Exploring a map looks only at dry ground the bot can walk back from, and a pocket that a drop leads into and nothing out of now counts as a trap even when a start spot reaches it.
