title: Quake II's server console lines, spectating mid-match, the chase camera and the hit report
---

The server console says what a map's entities lack as it loads and counts what was left out. In
deathmatch a player can move to the sidelines and back mid-match and ride a chase camera behind
another player. A player's blows are reported to our own client for its hit marker.
