title: Shots leave the weapon where it is drawn at every screen size
---

At screen sizes 100 and 110 the held weapon was raised over the status bars on its own, hiding its rockets, muzzle flashes and the crosshair while the shots left from lower down. The whole view now rises over the bars together (the world, its effects, the weapon and the crosshair), and the weapon options page has a HEIGHT slider (`cl_guny`) to hold the weapon lower.
