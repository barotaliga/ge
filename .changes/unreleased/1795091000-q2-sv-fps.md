type: Added
title: Quake II's server steps its rules up to 60 times a second (sv_fps)
---

`sv_fps` on a Quake II server (10 to 60, by tens, from the next map) steps the doors and lifts, rockets, grenades, dropped items and gibs that many times a second. Monsters, items and the players' weapons keep their ten-a-second beat, and each door and lift stands at every tenth where it would at 10, so play stays the same. At the default 10 nothing changes. Stock clients, demos and TV still get ten frames a second; our own client gets every frame. Every client's frames are now sent as changes from the newest frame it confirmed, so a slow connection no longer forces whole frames.
