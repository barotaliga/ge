The water's sound. `water/sound.hpp`: `hear` asks `water::sample` on rings round an ear out to 96 m and turns what it finds into an intensity per voice, the power the water spends there over the square of the distance (the waves' energy on a shore, a current's bed friction as rho c_f |v|^3, a waterfall's fall as rho g Q h), so a fall twice as far is half as loud and a fresh wind is heard on the shore it blows onto. A water bed of seven noise layers (the lapping and the surf, a river's rush and babble, a fall's roar and its spray, the room under water) is steered from it; under water the bed loses its highs, the room comes up, and `muffle` does the same to a game's own beds. One-shots: splashes by their speed and size (a pebble plips, a boulder booms) with the bubbles they take down, a swimmer's strokes, breaths and gasp, and bubbles ringing at their Minnaert pitch, which also make a river's babble and the churn under a fall. `water::Sample` gains `hs`, the sea's significant height at the point including the waves too short to lay. The `water-audition` lab meters the bank, seven splashes, eleven places by the water, the roar against distance and a swim.
