type: Added
title: Ground Zero's maps start and play with the pack's own map things
---
Every Ground Zero map now starts and runs. Triggers the pack switches on and off no longer fire at
once (the ammo depot no longer sends you straight to its next level), items a trigger spawns wait
unseen until it fires and then drop into place, doors and crates the pack starts inactive wake on
their first use, pushes and quakes the map marks silent are silent, and a laser that hurts a door
or a train goes on through it. A new lab, `se-q2-map-sweep`, starts every map of a game folder and
names each fault and missing file with the maps it comes from.
