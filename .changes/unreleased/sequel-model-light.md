title: The sequel's models lit by its own rule
---

In Shooter's Edge a model on a version 38 map takes the floor's light under it in full from every side and as much again toward a light fixed above one side of the map, a face turned away losing a little of it, so figures and items read with shape where they read flat; the floor under one is looked for further down. The morph pass gains `MorphDraw::shade_back`, the share a face turned away loses (0 keeps every other game's models as they were).
