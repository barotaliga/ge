type: Added
title: TV nodes broadcast a match to viewers and relays
---

`engine/tv` takes a match's replay file stream from a source, holds it back by a delay in game time and fans it out to viewers and to other TV nodes. A viewer that joins late starts at the newest keyframe block, a slow viewer slows no one else, chat goes up the tree and comes back down to everyone, and every node knows how many watch. It runs over engine/net on UDP, TCP or the simulated network. The `tv` lab measures each block's lag and the bytes a viewer takes under every network preset.
