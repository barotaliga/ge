title: Quake II's guns heard and seen through their muzzle flashes
---

Quake II's shots go out as muzzle flashes, as the game's protocol carries them, so other players' and monsters' guns are heard and lit from a joined server, a recording and the window's own game alike; the monsters' guns, silent before, are heard. A flash plays its tails (a shotgun's pump, a launcher's reload) and a silenced shot plays quietly. Splashes and laser sparks are drawn from the wire. A hosted game library reads each player's real ping, `sv_idlekick` is Quake II's idle drop, and a client with no rate gets 5000.
