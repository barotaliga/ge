type: Added
title: The 2D layer and menus over backdrops (ge::ui)
---

A new module, `ge::ui`: paints (colours, linear and radial gradients), a draw list of rounded boxes and text runs under clips, images (fit, sample, blur), TrueType fonts over stb_truetype with a built-in family (Barlow Condensed, SIL OFL 1.1, its licence installed beside the package), a CPU raster, backdrops (a colour, a gradient, a glow, a scrim, a vignette, a slowly drifting picture, the live scene) and menus as a model (pages, items, focus, events) with a responsive view for any screen shape. The `menu` lab pictures a front door over every backdrop at a desktop's and a phone's sizes.
