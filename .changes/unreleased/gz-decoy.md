title: Ground Zero's Doppleganger decoy, its co-op spawn rule and the Makron's red shell
---

In Ground Zero's deathmatch the Doppleganger sets down a decoy in its player's look that stands and turns for 30 seconds and, when someone wounds it and brings it down, sends a sphere after them. Co-op leaves out what the pack marks as never in co-op and shows its co-op-only things at every skill (`coop_baseq2 1` keeps the base game's rule), the Makron's torso glows red while a medic raises him, and the A-M bomb, the Doppleganger and the hunter and vengeance spheres stay off the map outside deathmatch.
