title: r_particles turns the effects' specks off
---

Shooter's Edge: `r_particles 0` throws no new sparks, blood, smoke, trails or splashes; the explosions' fire and the beams still show. Either look; 1, the default, throws them. Lab `se-demo-video` takes the runner's `--cvars` to set its look (`--cvars="r_particles 0"`).
