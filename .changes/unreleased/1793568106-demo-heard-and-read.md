title: a played demo is heard and its deaths and chat are shown
---

Shooter's Edge's window now opens the audio device: a demo played with `playdemo` plays its sounds and the players' voices (at the new `volume` setting, the menu's SOUND VOLUME), and the lines the server printed to the player you watch (deaths, chat and team chat) appear in the HUD's feed and the console, with centre messages in the middle of the screen.
