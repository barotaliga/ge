type: Changed
title: Campaign bots find their way past force fields, rubble and buttons set in walls
---

The campaign bot treats a force field, a wall that is blown away and a train that waits as shut until they move, and walks to whatever opens them. It shoots a switch it cannot reach from a spot that sees it, presses a button set in a wall from its front, and no longer gets pinned under a raised floor on legs. On Quake II's bunk1 it now kills 37 of 68 monsters (21 before). A floor button is pressed again on Quake's e3m3, and base3's blue door is no longer waited at before its key is found.
