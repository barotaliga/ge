type: Added
title: Choose the sound device
---

Every game with a window gets `s_device`, which names the sound device the game plays on. It
applies at once, `?` or `s_devices` lists the devices, and a name that is not there is reported
and the sound stays where it was. The platform adds `audio_devices` and `move_audio`. Quake II
spells the setting `al_device`.
