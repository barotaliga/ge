---
type: Fixed
title: Bots with only the axe stop fighting monsters they cannot reach
---

A bot out of ammunition kept fighting a monster it could see but not reach with the axe: on e1m4 it held such a fight across a pit for up to nine minutes. With only a melee weapon loaded and the enemy in sight beyond its reach for five seconds, the bot now turns from the fight and goes for a weapon, armour or health first.
