title: Our campaign played through a free episode
---

The `se-campaign` lab's `--free=<s>` now plays our own campaign program through a whole free episode of three fixture maps, without any pack: from the hall into a map of five monsters, on to a map with a gold-key door and the episode's rune, and back to the hall with the rune held, through each intermission and the episode's closing words. The lab's smoke run holds the whole episode in its digest.
