type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters flinch, fall and fidget like the original's
---

Monsters flinch by how hard they are hit, with the original's two or three cries, shrug off small blows and keep their nerve at nightmare. Soldiers fire on the run and fall to a head shot, the prisoner kneels, crawls and screams, and a player in a dark corner goes unseen until he fires. Monsters fidget while standing, the tank stamps on the fallen, flies gather on corpses, fliers dodge sideways and are knocked back, and the chaingun spins up and down.
