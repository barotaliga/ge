title: Fall Line's ground waves bring carriers, trucks and tanks
---

From three stars Fall Line's ground waves bring the vehicles their mix names. Tracked carriers (two units on a steered hitch, a section of eight in the back), trucks (twelve men, coming in along the village's road out) and, from four stars, tanks set off from below the wave's column on its way and drive up the runs, reading the snow ahead as the army's snowmobiles do: slowed up a rise and stopped by one too steep, turned at their own rate (a truck no tighter than its wheels, backing round when it is held short), stopped by a trunk, a wind tunnel or the vehicle ahead. A carrier halts near him and puts its section down to go after him; a truck does the same, or, held below a rise it cannot take, puts its men down to climb with the wave. A tank stands off him with its turret on him and, where the army shoots to kill, runs him down. The skier is held out of their steel and is knocked down by a front driven into him. Every vehicle drives home once the hunt is over. The new `army-vehicles` lab tables and pictures them on seed 38.
