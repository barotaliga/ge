type: Added
title: Team messages know what you picked up
---

In Shooter's Edge, what you pick up (a powerup, armour, a weapon, the big health, a rune, rockets, cells or a pack) is remembered for `tp_tooktimeout` seconds: `%i`, `%X` and `%Y` name it and where, `tp_msgtook` tells your team, and a config's `f_took` alias runs.
