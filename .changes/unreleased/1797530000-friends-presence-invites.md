type: Added
title: Friends, presence and invites on player accounts
---

The account service keeps friends for every game: both players ask to become friends, a block is silent, presence (online, in a lobby, playing, with the server's address) lapses on its own, and friends can invite each other. The game CLI gains a `friends` group: `list`, `add`, `remove`, `block`, `unblock`, `invite`, `invites` and `status`.
