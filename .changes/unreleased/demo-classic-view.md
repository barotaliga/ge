title: The classic bob, strafe roll and damage kick in demos and on joined servers
---

Shooter's Edge now rebuilds the first game's view on a played demo's eye, on a server joined over the classic protocol and in a demo turned into a video: the stride's bob, the lean into a strafe, the kick toward a blow and the view lying on its side when dead, all by the player's own `cl_bob`, `cl_rollangle` and `v_kick*` settings. Before, those views showed the eye exactly as recorded, with none of them.
