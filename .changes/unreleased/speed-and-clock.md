type: Added
title: Your speed and a clock in the corner (show_speed, scr_clock)
---

`show_speed 1` shows your speed over the ground (2: your whole speed) and `scr_clock` a clock (1 the time of day, 2 the time on this map) in the top-right corner, under the frame rate. They are the engine's, so any game can turn them on with its own defaults; in Shooter's Edge both start off.
