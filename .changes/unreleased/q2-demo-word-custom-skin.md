title: Quake II's demo command, demo names on Tab, and custom skins
---

In Quake II, `demo <name>` plays a recording from the demos folder, with or without its extension, and a leading slash plays one from the top of the game's files. In every game, Tab after `playdemo`, `timedemo` or `demo` completes the names of the recordings you have. A server or recording on Quake II's newer protocol can now dress a model in any picture it lists, as that game's own client does.
