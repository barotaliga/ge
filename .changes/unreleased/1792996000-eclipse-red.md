type: Fixed
title: An eclipsed moon is copper red at night
---

The night's rods desaturated the moon's disc with the dark scene around it, so an eclipse's umbra was drawn grey and a full moon blue-grey. The moon's true brightness is always bright enough for the cones, so its disc now keeps its own colour: the sky pass gives the sky in front of it to the rods and the post pass leaves the disc alone (`on_moon` in `wgsl/frame.wgsl`). A pixel bright enough for the cones also keeps its colour elsewhere, so the brightest stars keep their tint.
