type: Added
title: The hub keeps the results of matches played on classic servers
---

A hub that lists the classic servers already on the internet now notices when one of their
matches ends, from what each server shows in its status answers, and keeps the final scoreboard as
a result: the server, the map and mode, when it ended and how long it ran. The new Results page
and `/v1/results` show the newest first, for every server or one. These results have no accounts
behind them, so they count in no player's stats or ladder.
