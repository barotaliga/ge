---
type: Fixed
title: A counter or a relay removes what its killtarget names
---

In Quake II, a `trigger_counter` or a `trigger_relay` now removes the entity its `killtarget` names when it fires. On boss1 the wall in front of the lift up to the boss stayed after both buttons were pressed, so the level could not be finished. The campaign bot now also reaches the buttons' ledges from the ferries, waits for the lift clear of its shaft and rides it up.
