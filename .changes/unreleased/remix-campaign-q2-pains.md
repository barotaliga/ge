type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters in Quake flinch and cry as in Quake II
---

Under classic remix (`cr_monsters q2`), Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign now react to a hit as they do in Quake II: each kind picks its flinch and its own cry by how hard it was hit and the skill (a tank ignores a scratch and often shrugs off a light hit, a soldier picks one of three flinches, a monster knocked into the air plays its knocked-up flinch), waits 3 seconds before the next pain, and at the hardest skill only cries. Before, every kind gave one cry and a short flinch at most every 3 seconds.
