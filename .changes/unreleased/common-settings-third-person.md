---
type: Changed
title: The camera behind you works in every classic game
---

`cl_thirdperson` and `cl_thirdperson_angle`, the view of yourself from behind (a cheat), are now every classic game's, with Quake's `chase_active` as their Quake spelling. Their distance is `cam_dist`, the same distance a watched player in a demo is seen from; Quake's `chase_back` and Quake II's `cl_thirdperson_range` (60 by default in Quake II) both reach it.
