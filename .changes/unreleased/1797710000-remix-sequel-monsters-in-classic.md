title: The sequel's monsters in the classic campaign
---

With classic remix's `cr_monsters 2`, the classic campaign spawns the sequel's monsters in place of its own partners, each with the sequel's health, size, speeds, attacks, model, frames and sounds: guards, troopers, gunners, berserkers, gladiators, flyers, tanks and the rest of the table. A medic's raising of the dead and a power screen are not run yet.
