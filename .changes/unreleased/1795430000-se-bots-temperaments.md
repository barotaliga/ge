---
type: Added
title: Bots have a temperament: aggressive, balanced or coward
---

Shooter's Edge's bots now take or turn from a fight by temperament as well as skill, weighing their own health, armour and weapon against what they have heard of the enemy's (the weapon he fires, the armour, mega health and quad he takes). An aggressive bot takes every fight and plays for frags, a balanced one takes the fights his skill wins (an expert's with the starting gun, a novice's only well armed), and a coward fights only when far stronger; an outmatched bot backs off for items or his teammates. The team and bot labs take `--tempers`.
