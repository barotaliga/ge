type: Fixed
title: Bots that fall into slime or lava swim out instead of floating still
---

A bot's hand drops any movement key whose step would carry it into lava or slime. It did that even once the bot was already in it, where every step is a step into it, so a campaign bot that fell into e1m4's slime pit pressed nothing for the rest of the map. Once in, the keys are pressed again and the bot swims out. Over six seeds of e1m4 four runs now reach the exit (none did before), two of them with every monster killed.
