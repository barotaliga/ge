title: Flames drawn as burning gas, with the light they cast
---

`render::Flame` and the "flames" pass (`render/flame.hpp`): fire drawn as a marched teardrop of turbulent gas painted with a blackbody's colours from Planck's law, its edge fading smoothly, stopped at the scene's depth, bent by the wind; `flame_light` gives each flame's `LocalLight`, flickering with it. Presets for a torch, a candle, a brazier and a camp fire, and the `gpu-flames` lab.
