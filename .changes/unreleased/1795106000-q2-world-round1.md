title: Quake II's pickups that set off events, timed explosions, map messages, space's gravity, still push pads and map-wide speakers

In Quake II's single player picking up an item sets off what the map hangs on it (doors, monsters, messages), timed explosions go off in sequence instead of all at once, map messages break their lines where the map says, the space map plays at its low gravity, push pads with no direction stop you instead of flinging you, and announcements meant for the whole map are heard everywhere at their own volume.
