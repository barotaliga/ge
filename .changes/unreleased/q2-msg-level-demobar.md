---
type: Added
title: Quake II's message level, public flag, minimise on switch and demo bar
---

A player's `msg` setting now holds back the less important messages on a Quake II server. `public` says whether a Quake II server announces itself to the master servers. `vid_minimize_on_focus_loss` (Quake II's `vid_flip_on_switch`) minimises a full-screen window when you switch away. `scr_demobar` shows Quake II's demo progress bar with the share played, and the time at 2.
