type: Added
title: Quake II's bubbles and mission-pack effects in classic enhance
---

Under classic enhance, Quake II's bubble trails now rise as round bubbles, the same ones Quake
shows, in place of flat specks. The mission packs' explosions each get their own look: the big
explosion is larger, the nuke is a white-hot blast that lights a whole hall, the tracker's
explosion is a dark cloud that pulls black specks in, and the plain explosions throw no sparks.
Electric sparks, flechette hits, the green blaster and the heat beam's sparks and steam each shine
in their own colour. A heat beam is drawn as a hot orange strip instead of a lightning bolt.
