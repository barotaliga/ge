---
type: Added
title: Quake II's monsters can be heard walking (g_monsterfootsteps)
---

`g_monsterfootsteps 1` gives Quake II's walking monsters (the infantry, the soldiers, the gladiator, the iron maiden, the berserker, the medic, the gunner, the brain, the parasite and the insane marines) footstep sounds as they walk, run, flinch and fall. It is off by default, as the game was made.
