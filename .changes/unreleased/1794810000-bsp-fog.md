type: Added
title: A classic map's own fog, dust and lava haze
---

A classic map's own areas of fog are read when it loads (`plugins/brush/app/bsp_fog.hpp`): the dust and mist its `fog_volume` entities place, a glowing haze over its lava and a faint one over its slime. The v2 BSP renderer draws them through the engine's area fog, lit by the sun past its own sun map (`render::OrthoShadow`), so a hall's dust shows the shafts through its windows. A fog volume's new `sky` share keeps a closed room's dust from the sky's light.
