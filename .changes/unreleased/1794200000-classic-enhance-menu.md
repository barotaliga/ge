The classic-enhance menu: the player's own classic packs chosen in a menu, the enhancing started
from it and watched stage by stage until the cache is ready (the whole bar, a row a stage with its
count and item, the time left, the last textures before and after, then PLAY), and every
enhancement a switch, a `ce_` cvar and a toggle (decals, fire, skies, water, lava, muzzle fire,
teleporters, rubble and the rest; off is the classic original). The pipeline reports each item
and can be cancelled. Lab: `classic-menu`.
