title: The snow's fine marks in the engine, made from the imprint records
---

`render/snow_marks.hpp` draws the grooves, ruts, prints and craters a snow layer was pressed with, finer than the layer's cell: a coarse map over the region and a fine window round the camera, redrawn from the history as the camera moves. Stamps are capsules, boxes or ellipses, and `marks_from_records` makes them from a layer's imprint records (the outline, the sink as the depth, a berm from the snow thrown, a wall by motion). The mountain's trail map now uses it, and its pictures are unchanged bit for bit. The `gpu-snow` lab shows the marks beside the GPU mirror.
