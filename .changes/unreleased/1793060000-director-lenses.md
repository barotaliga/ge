title: Planted lenses, the replay's film camera and the broadcast's cuts
---

`engine/camera/plant.hpp` plants a lens by a known path where it sees the most of it (`plant_beside`, `sight_share`), aims it as a pure function of the path and the step (`planted_at`), and cuts live between planted lenses by a broadcast's rules (`direct_broadcast`: the least hold, the hand-over to the lens the subject runs toward, the cut on a lost line, the 180° rule with neutral shots). Fall Line films the replay director's cut through its lenses (`side`, `front`, `aerial`, `impact`, `chase`) with every pose a pure function of the shot and the step, and stands trackside lenses down the piste for a broadcast. A wipeout's shot is now filmed through the `impact` lens. The `camera-director` lab pictures the edit and the broadcast.
