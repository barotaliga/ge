type: Added
title: Competition rulesets for Quake, QuakeWorld and Quake II
---

`ruleset smackdown` (or `smackdrive`, `qcon`, `thunderdome`, `mtfl`; Quake II's `duel`) keeps the client to a league's rules: settings held or capped, commands refused during a match, and classic enhance, the remix, the easy jump and the rocket jump helpers off. It changes only while not connected, rides in the user info as `*rsn`, and every player answers a chat line `f_ruleset` with their ruleset. `rulesets` lists them; leaving one gives every setting back.
