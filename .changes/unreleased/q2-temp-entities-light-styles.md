type: Added
title: Quake II's hits, blasts and light styles from a server or a recording
---

A joined Quake II server's and a recording's hits now throw the game's own puff of specks (blood,
sparks, power screen and shield sparks by their colours) and the client plays their sounds itself:
the ricochets, the blaster's hit, each explosion's blast, the rail's end and the boss's teleport.
The rail, the blaster's landing, the monsters' cables and the BFG's burst are drawn as in our own
game. Light styles from a server or a recording, for Quake and Quake II, now flicker and pulse in
the window, where before only a game run on our own server animated them.
