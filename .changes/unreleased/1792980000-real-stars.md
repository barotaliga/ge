---
type: Changed
title: The real stars
---

The night sky's stars are now the real ones: `render/stars.hpp` decodes a generated table of a bright-star catalogue's 9,096 stars (`render/star_data.cpp`: each star's place, magnitude and B-V colour, from the Bright Star Catalogue's 5th revised edition) and cuts it into a cube map of 6 × 160 × 160 cells of up to three stars, which the sky pass reads round each pixel in place of the dealt stars. The sky's turn with the sidereal angle and the latitude puts each constellation where the map's place and hour have it. `Slot::texture_array` joins the bind group slots. The new `stars` lab checks the table (the sky's counts to each magnitude, the stars dropped) and charts it, and with `--bake=<catalog> --write` regenerates the table; the `night` lab adds a shot of the highest of four figures.
