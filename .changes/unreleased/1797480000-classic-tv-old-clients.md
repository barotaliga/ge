type: Added
title: any classic client watches through the TV
---

Any multiplayer client of the classic game, the oldest one too, can now watch through our TV: it connects as to a server and is seated in one player's view, in the plain protocol with no extensions. The player's state and view come every packet, so the viewer cannot fly off, and fire switches to the next player's view when there is one. `shooters_edge_hops tv --demo=<client recording>` plays a client recording to such viewers round and round.
