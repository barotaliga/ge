type: Added
title: The sequel's power shells, item glow and shot trails
---

In the sequel, a player with quad damage wears a blue shell, with invulnerability a red one (both: magenta), and a power shield's hit flashes green; the shell blinks in a power's last three seconds. Items lying about pulse softly. Recordings of the sequel now show rockets, grenades and blaster bolts with their trails and light, the BFG's ball see-through, items turning, and no longer light flying gibs like a gun's flash.
