---
type: Changed
title: Classic maps' clouds dealt per map, in islands, twisted and on the wind
---

A classic map's sky no longer carries one still deck of matching heaps over most of the sky. Each map deals its own stack of cloud kinds off its name (heaps, rolls or towering heaps low, sometimes scud under them, most often ice, a rippled sheet or fine ripples high over them; at night the scud and one layer over it), each layer gathered into islands with open sky between and drifting on the wind. Clouds gain `CloudLayer::warp`, which twists a cell's outline so neighbours are never alike; it is off by default, so other skies are unchanged.
