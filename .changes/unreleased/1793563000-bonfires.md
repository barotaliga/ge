---
type: Added
title: Braziers burn as bonfires
---

Under classic-enhance a map's large floor flames burn as bonfires: a broad fire in five tongues
with a brighter light, throwing sparks the wind carries off where the sky is open
(`ce_bonfires`, on by default; `render::bonfire_flame`, the emitters' `sparks` kind,
`brush::app::bonfire_sparks`). Wall torches stay small.
