---
type: Added
title: logins on a game hosted from the window
---

A match or a coop campaign hosted from Shooter's Edge's own window can now ask the players who join to log in, just as a dedicated server does. Set `sv_login`, and `sv_accountmaster` if accounts live on a master elsewhere, in the console; the change takes effect with the next map. Without a master, the window keeps its own player accounts in its settings folder. The window's own player is never asked to log in.
