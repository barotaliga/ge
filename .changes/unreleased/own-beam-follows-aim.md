type: Added
title: Your own lightning beam follows your aim
---

Your own lightning beam is traced again every frame along where you look, so it sweeps with your mouse instead of jumping ten times a second a round trip late, and the burnt line it leaves on the walls traces the curve you drew instead of a few straight pieces. `cl_fakeshaft` sets how far it follows your view: 1 all the way (the default), 0 the server's line, anything between part of the way. Other players' beams are drawn as the server sends them.
