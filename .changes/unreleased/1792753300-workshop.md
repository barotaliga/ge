type: Added
title: The workshop on the platform services
---

`ge::services::Backend` gains the workshop: `query_items` (newest or most subscribed, by tag, by author, the player's subscriptions, in pages), `submit_item` (a new item, or its author's next version), `subscribe_item`, and an installed item's files read as the game's content (`item_files`, `item_read`), with `item_installed` whenever a version lands on the machine. The simulated store holds items to a store's limits, shows each player only what they may see and times each download by the item's size; the offline store keeps items and subscriptions in its directory.
