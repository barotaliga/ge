title: Shooter's Edge's server and TV pick the game by number
---

`sesv` and `setv` take `-game 1` (the classic game, the default) or `-game 2` (the sequel) in place of `--sequel`; `--game=2` works too. The hub, the bot team and the player's command line are now `sehub`, `sebots` and `secli`.
