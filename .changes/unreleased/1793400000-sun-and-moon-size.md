The sun and the moon can be drawn larger. `FrameView::sun_size` and `moon_size` multiply each disc's angular radius, as through a longer lens, and Fall Line's `r_sunsize` and `r_moonsize` set them (1 is the real size, up to 20). Only the discs grow: their light on the world is the same, the moon's face keeps its brightness, and a cloud's glow and corona round the disc stand off its larger edge.
