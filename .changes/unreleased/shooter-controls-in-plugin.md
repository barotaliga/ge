---
type: Changed
title: The move keys and mouse look are the shooter plugin's
---

The move buttons (`+forward`, `+jump`, `+attack` and the rest) and the mouse and look settings (`sensitivity`, `m_invert`, `lookspring`, `lookstrafe`, `cl_run`, `cl_yawspeed`, `cl_aimhelp_turn`, `in_mouse_release`) now come from the shooter plugin, so every shooter game turns and moves the same way; each game keeps its own default keys. Nothing a player does changes.
