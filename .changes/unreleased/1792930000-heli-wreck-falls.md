---
type: Fixed
title: A helicopter struck in the air falls to the snow
---

A helicopter that struck a crown or hit the snow with its rotor tips was put on the snow in the step it crashed, a jump of up to 8 m in one step. Now the wreck keeps its rate of descent and falls the rest of the way under gravity, then lies where it lands. The heli scenarios run a wreck until it is at rest.
