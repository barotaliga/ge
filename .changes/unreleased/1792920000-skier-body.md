title: The skier's body and its injuries
---

A skier now has a body, in the new `plugins/skier/body.hpp` and `anatomy.hpp`:

- **Twenty parts and 98 injuries.** Each injury has its severity (AIS 1 to 5), the mechanism that does it (a blow, a landing's load, the knee's drawer, a twist, the shin levered over the boot) and the dose at which it is an even chance, all taken from the injury research.
- **The blows.** A trunk met on the skis levers the shins and twists the knees, and at speed his front goes on into it; beside him it strikes his side. A landing loads the legs, the spine and the neck, and a landing across his way twists the knee of the ski that catches. A blow's g is the stop over the part's give and the trunk's.
- **A dose is a chance.** The risk is a log-logistic curve, drawn off a hash of the step so a run replays injury for injury and no other digest moves. Each part takes its worst injury first and is hurt again more easily.
- **Severity and death.** `severity_of` is the injury severity score. An AIS 5, a score of 50 or the run's frailty makes him mortal: he goes down (`Wipeout::hurt`) and dies once he has lain still. `mend_body` heals him at a reset. `Doses` and `judge_body` take any other dose, such as a round's.

`SkierEvents` gains the trunk's closing speed and the landing's angle off his way. The ride scenarios step the body; five new ones (`tree-slow`, `tree-fast`, `tree-side`, `drop-across`, `drop-high`) stage crashes. The new `injuries` lab reports every scenario's body, plus a trunk's severity by speed and a landing's by load over 400 draws.
