type: Added
title: Co-op bots split a campaign map between them
---

Several bots playing a Quake or Quake II campaign map together in co-op now share the work: each button, key, secret and monster goes to the nearest bot, what one finds the others know, and nobody takes the exit until everything is done. Three bots clear Quake's first map at 100% in about half the time one takes.
