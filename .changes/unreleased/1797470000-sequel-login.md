---
type: Added
title: Logging in from the sequel's own client
---

A stock client of the sequel can now log in on our server of the sequel with a one-time code: it joins as a guest, is told how in lines that fit its console, types `login <name> <code>` (or `cmd login ...` while connecting), and is shown by its account's name. The line never reaches the game or anyone's chat. A guest under a registered name plays as "player" and is told why, and a seat that must log in and doesn't is dropped when its time is up.
