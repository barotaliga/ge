title: A rocket's launch looks like one; thrown things hop twice
---

In Shooter's Edge's enhanced look a rocket now pops out of the tube, drops a little, lights its motor and speeds up onto its line within half a second, carried off the line for a moment by the shooter's movement and aim. This is drawn only: the rocket still flies its straight line. Sparks, chips and debris now hop twice at most, sometimes three times, before they lie still.
