---
type: Added
title: More teamplay hooks in every classic game
---

Configs can now hook more of the game's moments, in Quake, QuakeWorld and Quake II alike. `f_flagdeath` runs in place of `f_death` when you die carrying a capture-the-flag flag (or a Team Fortress key). `f_focusgained` runs when the window takes the focus. `f_trackspectate` and `f_freeflyspectate` run when a demo's camera locks onto a player or lets go of him for free look. `f_demomatchstart` runs when a multiview recording's match begins, and `f_sbrefreshdone` runs when the server list has finished refreshing.
