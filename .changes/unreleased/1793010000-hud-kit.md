title: The HUD kit (ge::hud) and Fall Line's free-ride HUD
---

A new module, `ge::hud`: what any game draws over its running picture, as widgets over the 2D layer's draw list. A HUD writes in a face that is either a TrueType font or one of the bitmap charsets, scales to the screen's short side inside the platform's safe area, anchors its boxes to nine points and stacks them, and draws readouts, big numbers with their units, bars, rows of pips (stars, hearts), prompts on a plate and a feed whose lines hold and fade; every word sits on an outline and a drop shadow so it reads over bright snow and dark rooms alike, and `layout_problems` names any two boxes that overlap or leave the screen. Fall Line's free ride now has a HUD built from it: the speed and the edge, the air clock and the run's best air, the seed and where he stands, the run and the distance skied, the wanted level's stars with their search and the survival timer, a prompt when he is down, stuck or waiting for a lift, and a news column of what just happened. The `hud-kit` and `hud` labs picture them at a desktop's and a phone's sizes.
