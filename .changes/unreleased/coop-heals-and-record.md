type: Added
title: Co-op bots leave the healing to you, and single player records
---

Companions and helpers in a co-op game leave health, the mega health and armour to a player who needs it more than they do, and take it first only when they are nearly dead themselves. `record` now works in a single-player or co-op game, and a companion added with `addbot` while it records comes into the recording under his name, so the demo can be watched from his view.
