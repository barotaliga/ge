title: Thunderstorms, lightning and thunder
---
`engine/wind` gains thunderstorms as pure functions of place and time: cells that rise, mature and die with their gust fronts and rain (`storm.hpp`); cloud and ground flashes with their return strokes, continuing currents and branching channels, and the light any frame catches of them (`lightning.hpp`); and thunder heard at its distance, with its sound (`thunder.hpp`). The `storm` lab pictures them.
