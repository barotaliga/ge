type: Added
title: Model views, model sheets and reference sheets for the labs
---

The labs can now look at an authored model the way a modeller does, all on the CPU:

- **`lab/model_view.hpp`:** a posed model drawn in perspective or orthographic, z-buffered, lit, with its edges and its skeleton. Levels of detail are read from the meshes' names (`<name>.lod<N>`), and a joint's region can be framed close up.
- **`lab/model_sheets.hpp`:** the sheets: a turntable, four axes against a ruler, the topology, close-ups, every level of detail at the sizes it is drawn at, and clip strips.
- **`lab/model_compare.hpp`:** reference sheets. Each photograph is cropped and set beside the model, posed and turned to match. An angle sweep finds the camera.
- **New labs:** `model-views` (any model or built asset) and `skier-views` (the skier in the game's stances).
