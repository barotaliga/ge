---
type: Added
title: Quake II servers can cap the entities in a frame and send everything with sv_novis
---

`sv_maxents <n>` caps how many entities one frame sends a player; the rest wait for a frame with room. `sv_entpriority 1` picks which entities are kept first: players, doors and lifts, and anything alive, nearest first, with pieces and effects last. `sv_novis 1` sends every player every entity, behind walls too. Quake II's spellings (`sv_max_packet_entities`, `sv_prioritize_entities`) work too.
