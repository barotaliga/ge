title: heat haze over lava, acid fumes over slime, and players burning in them
---

The air over lava now wavers: what is seen across a pool bends in the rising hot air, most just over the melt, under a thin soot lit orange from below. Slime gives off a pale yellow-green acid vapour that drifts in wisps. A body in either stirs it: a burning player in lava throws up a plume of hotter air and smoke, a player in slime a plume of fumes. A player in lava smokes, chars black with glowing cracks and burns in small flames here and there; in slime its skin is bleached and pitted and fumes rise off it; both wear off over a few seconds once it is out. All of it is drawn in closed form, one box and one fragment a pixel over each pool (no ray marching), and can be turned off with three cvars: `ce_heathaze`, `ce_fumes` and `ce_burning`. The `se-burn` lab shows it on real maps with its GPU cost.
