title: Quake II's single player plays more like the original
---

A unit's end plays its movie, then the next unit. Clocks count on the walls, lifts go to the floor a button names, lasers shine in their colours, barrels and breakable walls throw debris, and monsters ride lifts, open doors and fire the triggers meant for them. Textures turn on buttons, doors and walls, more lights flicker, the music follows the map, power-ups warn before they run out, teleporters sound, secret doors slide open, and the intermission waits for a button.
