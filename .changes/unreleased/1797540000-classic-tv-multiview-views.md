---
type: Added
title: old classic clients watch any player of a multiview recording
---

`setv --demo=<file.mvd>` now serves a multiview recording to classic clients that cannot read one: each is seated in one player's view and moves to any other player with its track keys or the fire button. A player's view is converted only while someone watches it or asks for it, and let go when nobody has for ten seconds.
