---
type: Fixed
title: Classic remix's Quake II weapons and monsters work in Quake's campaign
---

With `cr_monsters q2`, Quake's levels now have Quake II's monsters in them instead of none, and with `cr_items q2` the shotgun fires at Quake II's rate with Quake II's sound. Both take effect from the next level, like every remix setting. Quake II's weapon is also no longer drawn over the end-of-level screen.
