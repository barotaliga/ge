---
type: Changed
title: the weapon change is quick, and a shot rushes the new weapon up
---

Changing weapons in Shooter's Edge and the classic games now takes a fifth of a second in all: the old weapon drops out of view and the new one comes up. Firing during the change no longer pops the new weapon into place: the old weapon is gone and the new one is raised from low in the picture four times faster, firing as it comes up. Two new player cvars set it: `cl_gunswitch` (seconds for the whole change, 0 for at once) and `cl_gunswitch_fire` (how many times faster a shot raises the new weapon).
