title: A bot's hands and how human it looks
---

`ai::Hands` turns what a bot wants into what it presses (a reaction delay, a wander, a damped spring, a slew and the reach, per axis), `ai/human.hpp` measures how human a run's controls and decisions look against a person's bands, and `ge sim --human` prints those metrics for every run and warns on each one out of band.
