---
type: Added
title: Glowing haze and fires light what is round them
---

A glowing fog volume now lights its surroundings: `render::fog_glow_light` makes it a lamp at its heart, as bright as its glow, its density and its size give, and a volume's new `flicker` wavers its glow and its lamp together. Fall Line draws a scene's fires (`FrameScene::flames`) and hands each fire and each glowing haze to its lamps, so a camp fire lights the snow and the fog round it.
