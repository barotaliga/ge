type: Added
title: Every classic-enhance feature drawn in Shooter's Edge's window
---

The window opens on the modern look and draws its water, lava, slime, torch flames and weather, each behind its `ce_` switch, which the console now takes with the weather's cvars; a pack enhanced by classic-enhance has its upscaled textures drawn from the cache, and the brush renderer takes replacement textures at 2, 4 or 8 times their size. The models stage writes each skin as a PNG for the model drawer, and the `se-client` lab draws a game folder's map in both looks with `--game` and `--map`.
