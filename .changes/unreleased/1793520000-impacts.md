type: Added
title: Rounds hitting surfaces
---

A nail or a pellet hitting a surface throws that surface's burst (`engine/render/impact.hpp`): grey powder and flakes off stone, a spray of sparks along the glance off metal, tan dust and splinters off wood, a dark plume and clods off dirt and grass, a pale fan off sand, white shards off tile, beads and mist off water. Each surface class is a look of 19 knobs named `impact.<surface>`, picked by the surface's word; a pellet throws a smaller burst and fewer sparks than a nail. The dust goes through the explosions pass and the rest through the particle pool (`throw_impact`), whose thrown things gain chips, grit and drops. Lab `gpu-impact`.
