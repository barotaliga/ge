type: Added
title: Player slots kept for VIPs on every game's server
---

`sv_vipslots` keeps the last player slots for VIPs: a client who gives one of the `sv_vippassword` words (on the sequel's server, the server's own password too) may take them, and anyone else is refused once only those are free. The sequel's `sv_reserved_slots` and `sv_reserved_password` are its spellings of the two settings.
