---
type: Changed
title: Quake II's weapons rest when idle, kick the view and stand up as pickups
---

Quake II's weapons now pause on their idle frames as they do in Quake II, both in Quake II itself and under classic remix, so the idle animation no longer runs on without a stop. Under classic remix on Quake's rules, every shot of a Quake II weapon now kicks the view by Quake II's own amounts, when hosting and when joined, and the machinegun's bullets follow its kick. Quake II's weapons and armour lying on Quake's maps now stand 15 units above the floor and turn, as Quake II's own pickups do.
