title: Quake II's ground shadows, and a third-person camera that fades your figure
---

Quake II's `gl_shadows` draws a dark shadow on the floor under each figure, monster and item (2 fades it as the model rises), and models the game marks cast none. With `cl_thirdperson` the camera now stops at doors and platforms too, and your own figure fades out as a wall pushes the camera in.
