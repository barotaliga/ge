type: Fixed
title: The Reckoning's and Ground Zero's weapons pause in their idle animation
---

The mission packs' weapons in Quake II (the phalanx, the trap, the ETF rifle, the prox launcher, the plasma beam, the disruptor and the tesla) now rest on their own idle frames now and then, as the base game's weapons do, instead of looping their idle animation without a stop.
