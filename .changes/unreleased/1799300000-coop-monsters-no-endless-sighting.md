type: Fixed
title: Co-op on our own campaign rules no longer crashes when a monster sights a player who cannot be hurt
---

A monster that sighted a player who could not be hurt (alive but past harm, as during the intermission) dropped him at once and sighted him again, over and over within one frame, until the game crashed. Its run now starts on its next think when that happens. The co-op check of every Quake map on our own rules now completes.
