type: Added
title: Classic remix draws the players as the other game's figure (cr_models)
---

With classic remix on, `cr_models` chooses whose figure every player is drawn as from the next map: 1 Quake's, 2 Quake II's, 0 each game's own (the default). In Quake the players run as Quake II's marine, in its own colours and with its weapon in hand; in Quake II they run as Quake's figure. Each figure plays the other game's moves by kind: standing, running, firing, flinching and dying.
