---
type: Changed
title: Fewer bubbles under water
---

Shooter's Edge draws far fewer air bubbles under water: each bubble the game blows is one ball instead of a cluster of three, and only a share of them is drawn, `ce_bubbles_share` (0.3 by default; 1 draws every one, 0 none), in both looks. The specks floating in the water round a swimmer, which read as bubbles, are off by default; `ce_water_specks` (0 to 1) brings them back.
