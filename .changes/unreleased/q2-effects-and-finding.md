title: Quake II's dropped effects drawn, and the finding cheats
---

Quake II draws the effects it used to drop: bubble trails, force walls, welding and tunnel sparks, steam, the flashlight, the mission packs' bursts, a carried flag's and the tag token's columns, the ion ripper's and plasma bolt's trails and lights, and green gibs' blood. The cheats `teleport` and `listentities` move the player and list where things stand.
