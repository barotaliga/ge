---
type: Added
title: Hide gibs and dead bodies (cl_gibfilter, cl_deadbodyfilter)
---

`cl_gibfilter 1` hides the pieces a body bursts into, along with their blood trails. `cl_deadbodyfilter 1` hides a player's body once it lies still after a death. Both work in either look, and every shooter game can set its own defaults.
