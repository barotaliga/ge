title: Ground Zero's defender, hunter and vengeance spheres
---

Ground Zero's three spheres work in its games: the defender halves its owner's damage and shoots back at whoever hurt him, the vengeance sphere strikes whoever brings him close to death, and the hunter chases his killer, with `huntercam` letting the fallen player watch the chase from the sphere. A doppleganger's sphere hunts its killer the same way. One sphere may be out at a time, and its time shows on the status bar.
