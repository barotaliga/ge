type: Added
title: The weapon command, preselection and the score keys
---

Shooter's Edge reads the weapon lines of classic players' configs: `weapon 7 8 5` (and `impulse` with several numbers) brings up the first weapon of the list you can fire, `cl_weaponpreselect` holds the choice for the next attack, `cl_weaponhide` puts the weapon away for the shotgun or the axe when you let go of the attack, and `cl_weaponforgetorder` keeps or forgets the list. `w_switch` and `b_switch` tell a Quake server which pickups may switch your weapon. TAB now runs `+showscores`, so it can be bound elsewhere, and `+showteamscores` shows the teams' board.
