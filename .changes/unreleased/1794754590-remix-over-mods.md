type: Added
title: Classic remix's movement over a mod's own
---

Shooter's Edge's classic remix gains `cr_over_mods`: with it on, `cr_physics` moves the players as it names even while a mod's own rules play (a compiled module or a sequel game library), while the mod keeps its weapons. Sounds and player figures already follow `cr_sounds` and `cr_models` over any mod.
