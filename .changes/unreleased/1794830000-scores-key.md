Shooter's Edge: TAB held shows the scores, in the pack's own pictures. In a match, the scoreboard: the RANKINGS picture, every player by frags with their ping, loss, minutes on the server, shirt and pants colours round their frags (yours bracketed) and name, spectators last, a team column and the teams' board with teamplay, closer rows in a large game, and the scorebar with the map's clock; the teams' board shows while you are dead. In a campaign, the solo scorebar in the status bar's place (monsters, secrets, time, the map's title) and the level's figures as the intermission draws them (`scr_levelstats 0` turns those off). The intermission's figures now sit in the original's fields.
