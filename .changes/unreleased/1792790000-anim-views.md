type: Added
title: Animation debug views: pose tracks, filmstrips, onion skins and joint faults
---

The animation debug views (`engine/lab/anim.hpp`, part of `ge::lab`): a figure recorded frame by frame as a pose track (joints, bones, a wireframe of its pieces), measured for pops, snaps, angle snaps, stretched bones and clips through the ground, and drawn as filmstrips from the side, behind and above, onion skins with the end joints' paths, a joint-angle chart and a jolt heatmap; the measures also write a series file for `ge graph`. The skier plugin's figure gives out its frame and joints (`figure_frame`, `figure_joints`, `figure_joint_positions`), and the `anim` lab records it through a ride scenario. A flat series' rounding no longer counts as a spike.
