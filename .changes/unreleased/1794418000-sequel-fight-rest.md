---
type: Added
title: The sequel's power armour, powerups, environmental damage and level limits
---

The sequel's rules now guard a blow with power armour (the screen from in front, the shield all round, paid for in cells), use the powerups a player holds with `use <name>` (quad damage strikes four times as hard, invulnerability takes no damage, the silencer, the rebreather and the environment suit), drown a player who stays under too long, burn him in lava and slime, hurt him for a long fall, and end the level when `fraglimit` or `timelimit` is reached, moving on through `sv_maplist`.
