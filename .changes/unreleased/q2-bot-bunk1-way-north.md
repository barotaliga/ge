---
type: Changed
title: Campaign bots get past Ammo Depot's sinking floor and across its sliding bridge
---

The campaign bots no longer stand under a floor that comes down on them, call a lift and wait for it instead of wandering off, and press a button only from where it can be reached.

They now follow a button that wakes a trigger elsewhere, open the doors such a trigger stands across by walking up to them, and cross a bridge that a button slides out over a gap. In Quake II's Ammo Depot this takes them past the first area to the north side.
