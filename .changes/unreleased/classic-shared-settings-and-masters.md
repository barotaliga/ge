type: Added
title: Rate, password, color, masters and spectator lines for the classic games
---

`rate` and `password` are now settings for every classic game, each with its own default (Quake 2500, Quake II 15000), and a change while you are joined reaches the server at once; so do your colours. `color 4` or `color 4 12` sets your shirt and pants in one word. A mod's `v_cshift` tints the view in open air. On a QuakeWorld server, a typed `kill` or `pause` reaches the server. A dedicated server takes `setmaster` and `heartbeat` and reports itself to the master servers in Quake's and Quake II's own forms, telling them when it quits. Under Quake's deathmatch rules everyone is told when a spectator comes and goes.
