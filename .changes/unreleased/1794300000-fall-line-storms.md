type: Added
title: Thunderstorms in Fall Line
---
`sv_storm` sends a summer thunderstorm over Fall Line's mountain, from 0 (none) to 1 (severe). Its gust front blows ahead of the rain, the downpour falls as rain all the way to the summit (sleet up there under the heaviest core), lightning flashes in the towers and strikes the ground near the skier, and its thunder is heard when the sound reaches him.
`r_lightning` turns the flashes and bolts on or off, and `s_thunder` does the same for the thunder.
