title: Bots plan climbs out of the water on the game's own water jump
---
The bots' map proved every climb out of the water with Quake's water jump, which clears a bank about 20 units over the water; Quake II's clears about 12. In Quake II a bot swam to banks it could not climb and jumped at them for minutes (base1's river). The swim proofs now run on the game's own move, and Quake II's nav move carries its water jump and its half-speed swim. A bot also no longer chases a foe, or picks a place to stand, inside a pit with no way back out unless it is already in it.
