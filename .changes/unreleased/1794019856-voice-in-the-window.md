---
type: Added
title: Voice chat in Shooter's Edge on a server joined
---

The window joined to a server asks for voice (`cl_voip`, on by default), plays the players the server routes to you, and sends your microphone while `+voip` is held (V by default, PUSH TO TALK on the controls page). The microphone opens the first time you talk. A joined game's sounds, chat lines and centre messages now play as well; they were read but never played.
