title: Quake II's rooms behind shut doors, the mission packs' marks and the layout's words
---

Quake II no longer draws rooms behind shut doors. The trap, the disruptor and the tracker draw their marks and lights; spinning lights, bobbing items, power splashes and the widow's beam-out are drawn; full bright and minimum-light models are lit as the game lights them; shells mix their colours. Status bars and score screens that use `rstring`, `color` or the `stat_` text forms draw them.
