title: the sequel's models animate
---
In the sequel's game, players and monsters now move through their animations as they play: standing, running, crouching, jumping and landing, shooting, flinching and dying, a corpse lying in its last pose. Monsters stand, walk, run, attack and fall each in their own way. Armour, weapons, powerups and keys turn where they lie; ammo and health do not.
