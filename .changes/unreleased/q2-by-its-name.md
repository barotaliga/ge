---
type: Changed
title: Quake II is called Quake II
---

Every place the code, the docs and the game's own words called Quake II "the sequel" now says Quake II, and every identifier, file and lab says `q2`: the labs `se-sequel-*` are `se-q2-*`, the game's id is `q2`, and the environment variable that names its packs for the labs is `GE_Q2_PAK`. Nothing a player plays changes.
