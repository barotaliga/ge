---
type: Added
title: A players' table and more third-person controls for demos
---

`mvd_info 1` shows a table of every player while a multiview demo plays, laid out by `mvd_info_setup`. `cam_lockpos` holds the third-person camera in place, and W and S move it in and out. `demo_playlist_track_name` picks a player by name when a demo starts, and `demo_benchmarkdumps` keeps each timedemo's result in timedemo.log.
