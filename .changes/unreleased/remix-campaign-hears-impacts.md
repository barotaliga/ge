type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters in Quake hear where your shot lands
---

Under classic remix (`cr_monsters q2`), Quake II's monsters in Quake's campaign now hear where the player's shot lands, as they do in Quake II: a bullet's chip on a wall, a nail, a bolt or a blast nearby turns a monster that has no enemy and is not lying in ambush to the spot, and it goes to look. Before, they heard only the shot itself.
