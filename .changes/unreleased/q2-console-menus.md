type: Added
title: Quake II's menu pages and the modern client's console words
---

Quake II has its own menu: four skills on the game page, the deathmatch flags a toggle a bit, the input, key, legacy key, weapon key, video, sound, effects, rail trail, screen, crosshair and download pages, and ESCAPE in a level opens the in-game page. Every game's console gains `_echo`, `text`, `complete`, `setu`, `sets`, `resetall`, `writeconfig`, `toggle` over a list of values, `lasterror`, `userinfo`, the date, time, uptime and random macros, `draw` and `undraw` to keep a value on the screen, `play`, `playvol` and `stopsound`, `sizeup` and `sizedown`, `pushmenu`, `popmenu` and `forcemenuoff`, `viewpos`, the screenshot format commands and the status bar dumps; Alt+Enter flips full screen.
