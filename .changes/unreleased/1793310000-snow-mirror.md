title: The snow layer mirrored on the GPU, and the gpu-snow lab
---

`render/snow_mirror.hpp` keeps a snow layer on the GPU so the picture's snow is the physics' snow: the fine tiles in a window round the camera, uploaded as stored into an atlas behind a page table, and a coarse view of everything else (the folded blocks, and the summaries of live tiles outside the window). Every write to the layer stamps its tile or block, and `update_snow_mirror` re-sends only what changed. WGSL reads it with `snow_raw` (a sample to the bit) and `snow_column` (the physics' `sample` on the GPU) from `render/wgsl/snow_mirror.wgsl`. The `gpu-snow` lab holds the GPU to the layer to the bit over a skied run and pictures the drop on both sides, the sources and the uploads.
