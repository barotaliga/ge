type: Added
title: Quake II's lights and flowing surfaces in classic enhance
---

Quake II maps now get more from classic enhance. A light panel's bright parts glow under GLOWING
TEXELS, and the rebaked light comes from the map's light panels, so a Quake II room is lit as its
maker meant. Enhanced textures and skins no longer glow in colours that Quake II draws as
ordinary paint. The sky's look is picked from the map's own sky box. Flowing surfaces, such as
moving water and conveyor belts, now slide as they do in Quake II.
