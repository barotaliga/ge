type: Added
title: Companions in the sequel's single-player and co-op games
---

In the sequel's single-player or co-op game, `addbot [novice|average|expert]` seats a companion that follows the player, defends him from the sequel's monsters and stays with him through every level change; the game turns co-op while one is seated, and `removebot` sends the last one away. `coopgame [bots] [skill] [map]` starts a co-op game with its companions already beside the player (1 by default).
