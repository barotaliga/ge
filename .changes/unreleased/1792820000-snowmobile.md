title: The snowmobile plugin
---

A new plugin, `plugins/snowmobile`, is a deep-snow mountain sled and its rider as one rigid body (`step_sled`). It rides on eight sprung probes over the skier's snow. The belt's grip comes from one budget spent along its combined slip, and in powder the paddles also shear the snow's cohesion. A sled rolled onto its edge carves, and past the tip it goes over. The engine, the CVT and the belt are modelled in `drive.hpp`. In the air the rider levels the machine and the nose is set for the landing; when the springs run out, the hull meets the snow.

The rider is thrown by a trunk, by a roll or loop past saving, by a landing that is too hard or too crooked, or by a wall. The bot's hands (`sled_pilot`) ride it to an aim and steer round the trunks. The new `sled` lab runs and draws 16 scenarios. The headline results: 147 km/h flat out on the groomer, about 0.3 m sunk into powder at rest, a climb up 24° and a bog on 35°, a 5 m circle at full lock, and a cliff that throws the rider.

The skier plugin's tree index gains `for_each_tree_near`.
