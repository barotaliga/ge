---
type: Changed
title: The competition and weapon-place settings work in every classic game
---

The settings a competition player tunes to see clearly (flat walls, a plain sky, liquids seen through, team and enemy skins, flames, power glows, the crosshair's picture and the tints' strengths) and the held weapon's place, size, lens and see-through (`r_viewmodelsize`, `r_viewmodeloffset`, `r_viewmodellastfired`, `cl_gun_x`, `cl_gun_y`, `cl_gun_z`, `cl_gunfov`, `cl_gunalpha`) are now every classic game's, and they keep their values when another game is loaded. Quake II's weapon lens is still 90 degrees by default; Quake's still follows the view.
