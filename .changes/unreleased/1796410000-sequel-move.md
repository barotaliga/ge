type: Added
title: The sequel's player move
---

`plugins/shooter` now has the sequel's player move (`shooter/sequel_move.hpp`). One command moves a player of packed integer state through any world's trace, with its own friction, acceleration, a third of the pitch when walking, the farther of two slides up an 18-unit step, ducking, ladders, currents, the water jump and the landing pause. `SequelBrushWorld` runs it over a version 38 map's brushes. It is held golden.
