type: Added
title: The night sky
---

`render/night.hpp` fills a frame's `Night` (`render/view.hpp`): the moon's light by its phase and distance, its disc with its seas and earthshine, the moon as the key light once the sun is three degrees under, and the dark sky's glow. The frame keeps a night's light in range with a pre-exposure (1 whenever the sun is up, so a day changes no bit), draws the twilight's and the moon's skies at once (a second sky-view table, `moon_view_lut`), the stars to the sky's limiting magnitude and the galaxy's band, and sees the night with a partly adapted, blue-shifted eye. `core::sidereal_time` is public. `plugins/mountain/app/night_look.hpp` places a map's moon on its date (`moon_of`, `night_look`), Fall Line's views lay it, and the `night` lab draws a dusk, the handover, three moons and a moonless sky.
