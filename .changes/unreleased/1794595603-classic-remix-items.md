title: Classic remix's item table
---

Shooter's Edge's classic remix gains its first settings: `cr_enabled` (off by default, and nothing happens unless two classic games are loaded as full games) and `cr_items` (whose items a map is played with). Each classic game's map items have their match in the other game's set by role (weapons by their place in the set, ammunition by its weapon, health and armour by size, powerups by what they do), and the lab `se-remix` draws a real map of each game with its own items and with the other game's.
