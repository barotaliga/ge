title: the sequel's recordings play in a stock client
---
Recordings made on the sequel's rules now play in a stock client of that game: frames are numbered from 1, the steady light style is sent, and so are the sky's name and a status bar showing health, ammunition and armour. The new `se-sequel-record` lab records a match on a map from the demo pack (`--pak`, `--map`) as `.ged` and `.dm2`.
