title: The valley's reservoir behind its dam on a dealt map
---

`mountain/tarn/reservoir.hpp` finds a dam site across one of a dealt map's valleys, clear of the ski area, builds an earth dam (`Level::dams`) and lays the water behind it as a lake, filled flat where its ice bears a skier. Fall Line's free ride lays it after its tarn, and the `world` lab's `--reservoir` with the `reservoir` and `reservoir-high` views looks up it from the dam.
