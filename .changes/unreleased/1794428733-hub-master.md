type: Added
title: A master for every server and broadcast node
---

`engine/net/master.hpp` is a master server: game servers, TV nodes and forwarders beat to it, told apart by kind, and a hub or a server browser reads its list by game, paged. `engine/net/status.hpp` is the record a server says about itself (name, map, mode, phase, slots, players, teams, its streams, keys) and the cookie exchange that answers a status ask without amplifying a forged one. A dedicated server beats to every master in `sv_master` and answers status asks on its own port; a game adds its own fields in `ServerGame::fill_status`. The `hub-master` lab runs an evening of servers on the simulated network.
