type: Added
title: Our hops as a process for any classic client
---

`sefwd`, `seproxy` and `setv` run our forward, our proxies or a TV node as a process of its own, so an existing classic client plays through the forward and the proxies and watches a match through the TV node. A recording's signon now sends the sound list before the model list, which existing clients need to play a stream.
