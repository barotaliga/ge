title: Quake II's other players drawn in demos and on joined servers, water surfaces from close by, downloads resumed
---

In a Quake II demo or on a Quake II server you join, the other players now show, each in the model and skin they chose and holding their weapon; before, they were invisible. The mission pack's disguise dresses a player in the disguise skin. Looking across a water surface from just above or below it no longer drops the far side. A download cut short picks up where it stopped the next time you join.
