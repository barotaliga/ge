title: The Reckoning's weapons, items and map things
---

Shooter's Edge plays Quake II: The Reckoning's own rules when its folder is the game: the ion ripper's bouncing bolt, the phalanx's twin plasma balls, the trap that pulls in whatever comes near and leaves a food cube, dual fire that runs every weapon twice as fast, mag slugs, the green key, the pack's item list on the HUD and in the inventory, the `use hyperblaster` and `use railgun` toggles, and its map things (the repair object, rotating warning lights, the nuke, the viper's missile, the transport, the hum and the pulsing laser). Quake II's own game is unchanged.
