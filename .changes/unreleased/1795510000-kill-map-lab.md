type: Added
title: A lab compiles the kills and rocket flights of many recorded games into a map of gold shots
---

A new lab, `se-kills`, reads a folder of recorded games of one map and draws every kill as a line from the killer to the victim over the map's wireframe, from above, at an angle and from inside, with the lines added up so a hundred demos read as a density. Shots made again and again from the same spot onto the same spot are picked out as gold shots, numbered with their coordinates in a table and in `gold.tsv`; every rocket and grenade, hit or miss, is drawn too, with the lanes flown most. It filters by weapon, shows one small map per weapon, and heat maps of where players die and where rockets land. The demo scan now keeps where each killer and victim stood and every rocket's and grenade's flight with its likely shooter.
