type: Added
title: Ski areas built on the real faces (R25): `GenerateOptions::face` and the `real-maps` lab
---

`generate_level` now builds a ski area on a real face: `GenerateOptions::face` names one of the 114 faces (`real_face_crops.hpp`), and the map is laid off its real heights 1:1 in the face's region. The massif reads the face: its summit ridge where the real one stands (down to its brow, where a plateau starts to fall), its peak, shoulder and valley on the sides the real ones stand, its floor (the map's nought) and its own profile down the fall line, fading onto that profile on the valley floor so the hub stands level. Only a share of the dealt hills and spurs is laid over it, and no dealt side ridges, crests, bowls or headwalls. The rule book bends where a real mountain is not a dealt one, every bend a number in `resort_rules::massif::real`: a run graded to a gentler least fall and a deeper cut, its start cut through the real knolls; the way to the peak's chair asking more earthworks and searching R26's whole band; a top's ramps chosen together; a start billed the colour it measures; a bottom station on steeper ground; the way home a colour harder; at least four pistes; and the analysis holding a real face's map to the same numbers (`Level::face`). A face is searched over 32 attempts, the first 8 on the real peak and valley. The new `real-maps` lab builds every face named and says on which attempt it stood and what it carries; a face named alone is drawn. No dealt map moves.
