type: Added
title: Far lands round a small world
---

`render/far_land.hpp` draws the land round a small world as a 3D skybox: real mountains to the horizon with lakes, seas, snow by temperature, grass, forest and rock in eleven kinds (eight of the Earth's, three alien), and Shooter's Edge shows one through each map's sky faces once the eye is high enough (`r_farland`, `r_farland_warm`).
