type: Added
title: Weapon changes, the hand grenade and the chaingun's spin predicted on Quake II
---

On a joined Quake II server your weapon change now starts lowering the gun as soon as you press the key, the hand grenade's pin, cook and throw play at once, and the chaingun's spin up, spin down and loop and the hyperblaster's wind-down are heard with your press. On our own server the client is told which weapons you hold and your ammunition, so every change is predicted; on a stock server a change to a weapon you have already held, with its ammunition seen, is. A shot is now placed in the right server frame far more often, and a shot the prediction missed is heard late instead of not at all.
