---
type: Added
title: Quake II's Ground Zero plays its tag game and random respawns
---

Ground Zero's deathmatch now plays tag with `gamerules 2`: one token on the map (the map's own, or one made at a deathmatch start), taken with a full heal and body armour; its holder scores 3 a kill with a quad every fifth, whoever kills the holder scores 5 and takes it at once, and blows the holder has no part in are a quarter less. A holder who dies without a killer or leaves throws the token, which nobody may take for a second and which moves to the farthest start 30 seconds later (3 in lava or slime). The holder trails the token's light and shows the tag picture on the scoreboard. `randomrespawn 1` brings a taken item back as another of its kind. The base game and plain deathmatch are unchanged.
