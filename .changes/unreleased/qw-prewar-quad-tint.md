title: No quad tint in a QuakeWorld match's prewar
---

In a QuakeWorld or Quake match, the screen's power tints now follow the powers you hold. Some
servers light your own figure blue all through prewar, and that used to tint the view as if you
had the quad. The biosuit and the ring now tint the view in a match too, as Quake always did.
