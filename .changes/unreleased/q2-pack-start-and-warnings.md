---
type: Fixed
title: A mission pack named on the command line opens on the first start, and Quake II starts without warnings
---

`-game xatrix` or `-game rogue` on the start that first copies Quake II in now opens that mission pack. `condump` writes into the game being played after a switch. Quake II's own config binds `+` (shift and =) without a warning, and cheats are no longer saved in the config, where they were refused at every start.
