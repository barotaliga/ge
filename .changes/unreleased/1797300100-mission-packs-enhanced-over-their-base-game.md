---
type: Added
title: Mission packs get their own enhanced textures, models and sounds
---

Classic enhance now enhances a mission pack laid over its base game: point it at the pack's folder (Scourge of Armagon's `hipnotic`, Dissolution of Eternity's `rogue`, The Reckoning's `xatrix`, Ground Zero's `rogue`) and it finds the base game beside it, so the pack's own textures, models, sounds and pictures are enhanced with the base game's palette. While a mission pack is played, the game reads that cache, and the base game's until it is made. Before, a mission pack could not be enhanced at all.
