type: Added
title: sv_hour sets the hour of the sun over a classic map
---

Shooter's Edge's `sv_hour` (0 .. 24) puts the sun where that hour of a summer's day has it: up at about 5, high in the south at noon, down at about 19, with the map's own classic night (its moon across the sky from the sun) once the sun is well under the horizon. The default, -1, keeps the sun each map's own sky picks. The sun's light through the sky faces, its shadows, and the water's caustics and light shafts follow it.
