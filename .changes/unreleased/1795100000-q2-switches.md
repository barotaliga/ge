type: Added
title: Quake II's machine gun climbs outside deathmatch, and servers get aimfix, g_footsteps and figures that lean
---

Outside deathmatch the machine gun's view and bullets climb as it fires (up to 13.5 degrees), as the game was made; `g_machinegun_norecoil 1` keeps it level. `aimfix 1` sends every shot from the gun to the point the crosshair is on. `g_footsteps` sets when footsteps sound (0 never, 1 running, 2 at any pace, 3 in the air too). `sv_maxvelocity` caps how fast thrown and falling things move. Other players now see your figure tilt with your aim and lean into a strafe (`sv_rollangle`, `sv_rollspeed`).
