type: Added
title: The connection's macros, serverstatus, download and the files' console words
---

The console gains `$cl_timer`, `$cl_demopos`, `$cl_ping`, `$cl_lag`, `$cl_pps`, `$cl_mps`, `$net_uprate`, `$net_dnrate` and `$com_maplist`; `serverstatus [address]` asks a server for its settings and players (either game's); `download <file>` asks a Quake II server joined for one file; `path`, `dir`, `fdir` and `whereis` say where the game's files come from; `dns` looks up a host and `net_stats` counts what the connection sent and received.
