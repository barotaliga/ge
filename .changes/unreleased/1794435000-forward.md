type: Added
title: Forward, a hop to a better path for any game
---

`engine/forward` copies any game's datagrams through a chain of forwarders, set up by a route before the game's own handshake: an allow list on by default, alive both ways, a client followed across a NAT rebinding, and no answer to a stranger bigger than what it sent. The `forward` lab compares the round trip direct and through one or two forwarders under every network preset.
