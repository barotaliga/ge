title: Quake II's players cry out in their own voices
---

In a Quake II game, players now make their own sounds in the voice of the model they wear: pain by the health left, death, the jump, a hard landing and drowning. Their bodies make the water's sounds too: going in, coming out and going under, the gasp on surfacing, the rebreather's breath, landing thuds and the lava's burn.
