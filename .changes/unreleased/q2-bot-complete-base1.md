title: The Quake II campaign bot clears base1 completely
---

The scripted Quake II playthrough has a 100% mode: every monster and every secret before the exit. On base1 it kills all 17 monsters and finds all 3 secrets in about four and a half minutes, crawling under low roofs, walking down steep ramps, picking up the shotgun that calls two more soldiers, and reaching the secrets from the water and through a crawlspace under the floor.
