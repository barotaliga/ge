type: Changed
title: The campaign bot learns rocket and grenade jumps from record runs
---

The rocket and grenade jumps of the fastest recorded 100% runs of Quake's maps are now part of the
bot's map knowledge. Each one is kept only where the bot's own movement can make the jump land, and
a jump it misses twice is dropped, so the normal way is always there to fall back on. The campaign
route pictures' table now counts the tricks each map kept and the blast jumps the bot made.
