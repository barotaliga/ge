type: Added
title: the sequel's matches broadcast through TV
---

The sequel's recordings can now be broadcast while they are played: each second of play is packed into the replay file as it comes, and a TV node sends it to viewers, who get the whole recording back byte for byte (a late viewer gets the match from its start). The demo pack's recording broadcasts at about 600 bytes a second. The `se-sequel-tv` lab shows it.
