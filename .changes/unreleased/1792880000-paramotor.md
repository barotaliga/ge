title: The paramotor plugin
---

A new plugin, `plugins/paramotor` (`ge_paramotor`), flies a powered speed wing that a skier launches off the snow:

- **The wing:** a polar of lift and drag by its angle of attack, with the stall and the surge out of it, the brakes, the accelerator and the trimmers. The pilot hangs under it on his lines, and the bank follows the toggles.
- **The engine:** thrust that falls with the speed, spooled through a lag. On the snow it pushes him down the face until the wing is let fly and lifts him off.
- **The air:** the wind over the snow by its log profile and the mountain's exposure, ridge lift up a face, the lee's sink, and eddies that fold the wing when they are rough enough.
- **The rest:** a landing on the skis, a crown that brings the wing down, the drop that lets the rig go, and a bot that launches, holds a height toward an aim, glides in and flares (`para_pilot`).

The new `para` lab runs eleven staged scenarios, and `tests/paramotor_scenarios_test.cpp` holds them.
