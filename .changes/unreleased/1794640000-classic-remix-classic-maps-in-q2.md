title: Classic remix plays the classic game's maps in Quake II
---

With classic remix on, Shooter's Edge's Quake II plays the classic game's maps on its own rules: the map's walls in its own textures, its items Quake II's. A classic map's point tree is given brushes so Quake II's movement and shots trace it, and every swapped item now keeps its place on the floor in both directions instead of starting inside it.
