title: The Reckoning's monsters
---

Quake II: The Reckoning's enemies fight in Shooter's Edge: the ripper, hyperblaster and laser guards, the gekk that leaps, claws, spits, swims, chants and bleeds green, the repair bot that welds broken machinery and raises the dead with its beam, the beta gladiator with phalanx rounds, the beta iron maiden with heat-seeking rockets, the beta super tank, and the brain whose tentacles pull and whose eyes burn. The beta monsters wear power shields that take two thirds of every blow. Their deaths have the pack's own words. Quake II's own monsters are unchanged.
