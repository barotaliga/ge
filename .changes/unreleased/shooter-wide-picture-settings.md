---
type: Changed
title: The picture and crosshair settings shared by every game
---

The crosshair settings (`crosshair`, `crosshaircolor`, `crosshairsize`, `crosshairalpha`) are now the engine's, for any game with a crosshair, and the picture settings (`r_look` and the `r_`, `gl_`, `v_` and `ce_` cvars) are the shooter plugin's, for every shooter on classic maps. Each game registers them with its own defaults; Shooter's Edge's are unchanged.
