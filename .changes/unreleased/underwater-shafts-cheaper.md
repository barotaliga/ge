title: The light shafts under water cost about a third of what they did
---

The view under water's light shafts read each wave once for eight of their sixteen steps and turn its sine on from step to step, rather than summing every wave again at every step. The picture is the same. The shafts were nearly all of the underwater pass, which took most of a frame with the eye under a pool at 1440p. The `gpu-underwater` lab now times the pass with each of its dear parts off (`cost.*`).
