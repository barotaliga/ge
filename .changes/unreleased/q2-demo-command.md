title: Quake II's demo command, gzipped demos and demo names on Tab
---

In Quake II, `demo <name>` plays a recording as `playdemo` does: a bare name is looked for in `demos/` as `.dm2`, `.mvd2`, `.dm2.gz` or `.mvd2.gz`, and `/name` starts from the game folder's top. In every classic game, Tab after `playdemo`, `timedemo` or `demo` offers the recordings the game's files hold.
