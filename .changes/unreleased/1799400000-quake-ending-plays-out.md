title: Quake's ending on our own campaign rules plays out as the game's does
---

The blow that kills the final boss now ends the game on the spot: everyone is taken to the first intermission spot to watch, the screen clears for the closing words, and no button leaves. A second later a teleport flash goes off beside the boss; at three seconds it cries out and starts to thrash, the world's light pulsing and dimming with every shake, and at eight seconds it bursts into fifty pieces, the closing words come up, a body is left lying where it stood, and the music returns. Before, the game waited for the boss's death frames and a pause after them, more than four seconds, before ending.
