type: Added
title: The sequel's other monsters
---

The sequel's single player now has the full game's other enemies: medics that raise the dead, brains behind their power screens, iron maidens, gladiators with their rails, leaping mutants, technicians, icarus fliers, sharks in the water, the super tank, the hornet, and Jorg, whose death brings out the Makron. Monsters now walk and run at their own speeds, blows throw a player about, and fliers hold above their mark.
