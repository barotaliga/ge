type: Fixed
title: Joining our sequel server with other physics predicts its movement exactly
---

Our server of the sequel now tells a joining window the movement rules it runs players with (the speed, the friction, the air control, the water and the landing), and the window predicts with those. On a server running the classic game's physics, or other movement settings, your own movement no longer jumps back by a few units on a slow link.
