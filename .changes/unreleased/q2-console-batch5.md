title: Server commands filtered by cl_ignore_stufftext, the address book, the browsers' colours and order
---

A joined Quake II server's commands can be held to a whitelist (`cl_ignore_stufftext` 1 or 2, the patterns in `stufftext-whitelist.txt` beside the settings; 2 says each line ignored). JOIN A GAME can colour its rows (`sb_colorservers`: grey for a locked server, green for a near one; Quake II spells it `ui_colorservers`) and asks the servers in Quake II's address book (`adr0` to `adr15`, a page under MULTIPLAYER). DEMOS lists its recordings by name, age or size either way round (`menu_demo_sort`; Quake II's `ui_sortdemos`, by name by default). Holding F1 while a Quake II recording plays shows its last layout.
