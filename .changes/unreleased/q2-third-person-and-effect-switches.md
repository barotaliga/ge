type: Added
title: Quake II's third person and effect switches
---

Shooter's Edge's Quake II gains `cl_thirdperson` (a cheat: your own view from behind you, joined
or hosted, stopping short of walls) with `cl_thirdperson_angle` and `cl_thirdperson_range`, and
its effect switches: `cl_disable_particles` (grenade and rocket explosions and trails, blood,
blaster trails), `cl_disable_explosions` (the grenade's and the rocket's ball of fire),
`cl_dlight_hacks` (smaller explosion lights, no machine gun light), `cl_muzzlelight_time` and
`cl_muzzleflashes`.
