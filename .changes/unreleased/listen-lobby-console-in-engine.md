---
type: Changed
title: The lobby's words and a hosting window's settings are the engine's
---

The lobby's console words (`ready`, `break`, `team`, `start`, `warmup`, `addbot` and the rest) and the settings of a match hosted from the game's window (`sv_lobby`, `sv_countdown`, `sv_minplayers`, `sv_port`, `sv_maxclients`, the `sv_login` settings) now come from the engine, so any game that hosts from its window has them. Shooter's Edge's lobby works as before.
