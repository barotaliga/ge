type: Added
title: A proxy pair for a lossy link
---

`engine/proxy` carries any game's datagrams between a near proxy beside the client and a far one near the server, each sent up to five times and packed against the last one the other end has. At 10% loss between the proxies three copies cut the loss the game sees from 10.3% to 0.12%, and packing sends under a third of the bytes (the `proxy` lab).
