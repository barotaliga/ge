type: Added
title: The ski tracks and the trail map
---

The skier plugin's app half gains `skier/app/tracks.hpp`: the prints a skier's skis lay each frame (two capsules a stance apart, the outside ski of a turn deeper, a carve narrower, a skid wider and shallower with a berm, a scuff on the piste and a furrow in loose snow, a gouge for a body down). The mountain's gains `mountain/app/trails.hpp`: a coarse trail map over the whole map and a fine window round the camera, stamped with max blending and redrawn from the stamps' history when the window moves, which the snow's material reads (the grooves and berms in the light, the trough's shade, the pressed floor smooth); `create_snow` takes the trails. Fall Line's frame lays every racer's tracks (`fall_line::app::track`, `forget_tracks`) in two new passes, `trails.fine` and `trails.coarse`; the new `trails` lab measures the grooves through a turn and a racer's track found by another, the `world` lab's moments show the tracks laid over the run, and `world` and `profile` take `--trails=0`. A package no longer installs the headers of a plugin's app half it does not publish.
