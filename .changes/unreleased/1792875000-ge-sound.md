type: Added
title: ge sound match, import and split, ge model import and ge credits check
---

Free sounds come into a game without a person listening: `ge sound match` ranks every sound in a set of packs against a reference by length, band energies and loudness envelope; `ge sound import` trims, cuts, fades and brings one to an integrated loudness (ITU-R BS.1770, gated) under a peak ceiling and writes it as mono 16-bit WAV under the game's own name; `ge sound split` cuts a recording of many sounds at its silences. ffmpeg decodes the sources; `audio::wav_bytes` writes any channel count and rate. `ge model import` packs a free kit's model (a .glb or .gltf with its textures and buffers in files beside it) into one self-contained .glb under the game's own name, loads it with the engine's loader and reports its joints, meshes, triangles, clips and size against a budget. `ge credits check` holds a content directory's CREDITS.md to its files: every sound, model, image and font credited, every credit's file there, and every source under a licence the repository can carry.
