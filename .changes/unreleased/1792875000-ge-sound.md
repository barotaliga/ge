type: Added
title: ge sound match, import and split
---

Free sounds come into a game without a person listening: `ge sound match` ranks every sound in a set of packs against a reference by length, band energies and loudness envelope; `ge sound import` trims, cuts, fades and brings one to an integrated loudness (ITU-R BS.1770, gated) under a peak ceiling and writes it as mono 16-bit WAV under the game's own name; `ge sound split` cuts a recording of many sounds at its silences. ffmpeg decodes the sources; `audio::wav_bytes` writes any channel count and rate.
