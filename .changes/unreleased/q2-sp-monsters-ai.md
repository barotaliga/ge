type: Changed
title: Quake II's monsters hunt like the original's
---

Monsters go to a noise instead of knowing where you are, follow your trail when you slip out of sight and never give up the chase, wait a moment before their first shot and turn to face you before firing. They walk their paths with the map's waits and triggers, mutter while standing and call out while searching, and fliers hum. Soldiers fire as they fall.
