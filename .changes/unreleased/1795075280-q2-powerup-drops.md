---
type: Added
title: Quake II's other powerups may be dropped at death too
---

Beside `sv_dropquad`, a server may let a dead player leave the Invulnerability (`sv_droppent`), the Environment Suit (`sv_dropsuit`), the Rebreather (`sv_dropbreather`) and the Silencer (`sv_dropsilencer`) for others, each with the time (or shots) it had left and used at once by whoever takes it. All are off by default.
