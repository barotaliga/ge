The lens pass (`engine/render/lens.hpp`) is one compute dispatch over a quarter of the output's
pixels, after the bloom, read by the post pass in one bilinear tap:

- **The sun's rays through what stands** (`r_sunrays`, Fall Line default 0.5): a 32-step march
  toward the sun's place on the screen summing the open sky (the depth target's empty pixels) round
  the sun, tinted by the key light. The march starts at the first step inside the sun's radius, so
  a texel far from it costs a few instructions. The sky's own shafts (the cloud's shadow across
  the air) are the sky pass's; the two stack.
- **The flare** (`r_flare`, default off): six ghosts (discs and hexagons, each its own coating
  tint) on the line through the middle and a faint ring, scaled by the share of 13 depth taps
  round the sun that are open sky.

Both fade as the sun leaves the screen and with the sun's pass through the cloud. Fall Line's
BLOOM ladder row gains a top stop, `lens`. Held by `tests/render_lens_test.cpp` (lit over open
sky, dark when a wall stands in front of the sun or the sun is behind the eye); pictured into the
sun through the trees by the `post` lab.
