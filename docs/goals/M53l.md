Status: done
Milestone: **Fast downloads over HTTPS** (`engine/server/web_files.hpp`, `engine/net/file_server.hpp`): every dedicated server can send the files a player lacks over HTTPS from a port beside its game port, under its own certificate trusted by the digest it tells (or an operator's), held to a speed for each player and for everyone together, fair among the players fetching; the classic games' clients take maps and files from there first, resumed, checked by the file's SHA-256, under their own speed cap, the channel behind them; stock conventions kept (Quake II's `dlserver`, QuakeWorld's `*dlserver` and `sv_dlURL`)
Exit: A QuakeWorld and a Quake II server serve their files with `sv_http_port` set and nothing else; our client fetches a map it lacks from there over TLS trusting the pin, keeps it only when its digest matches, and plays; a map takes under a second where the channel takes tens of seconds (`se-fastdl`); the caps hold a player and the total to their rates
Labs: `se-fastdl`

Design: [`docs/servers.md`](../servers.md) § Fast downloads. Facts on the stock conventions: `porting/shooters-edge/web-downloads-facts.md`.
