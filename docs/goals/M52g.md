Status: done
Milestone: **Mods on the sequel's rules: the hooks and services a C++ mod package needs where the rules keep their own entities (the sequel's runner)**
Exit: A mod package changes a match on a sequel map and replays to the bit
Labs: `mod`

The sequel's rules (`games/shooters_edge/server/sequel_server.hpp`) keep their own entities and
players and mirror them into the loop's pool only to send them, so the program seam's hooks and
services (`sim/mod_api.hpp`) do not reach them: a mod named for a sequel map was refused
(M52f). This row gives them a hook set of their own at the sequel's frame, spawn, damage and death
points, and services over its entities, so one package can stack on either game's rules.

**Built** (design: `docs/mods.md` § On the sequel's rules):

- `games/shooters_edge/server/sequel_mods.hpp`: the runner's `own_mods` (`RunnerCalls`, a new
  entry beside `program_seam`) makes a host of each package over the sequel's own state
  (`sim::create_mod_host_over`, the host's creation split from the classic services), and the
  sequel's rules call the hooks at their own points: `match_start`, `start_frame` each 100 ms
  rules frame, the pre- and post-think around each command, `put_client_in_server` at every spawn,
  connect and disconnect.
- The hook table grew two entries at its end, `damage` (a blow about to land on a player: each mod
  answers the points to deal) and `death` (after the death is scored); an older mod's shorter table
  still loads. The classic rules do not call them.
- The services over the sequel's players (1..32) and map entities (after them), numbered as the
  pool's rows: a player's origin, velocity, view, box and health (origin, velocity and health
  settable), an entity's origin, angles, velocity, box and health. A mod's own entity or function,
  and any field the sequel keeps itself, is refused with a warning.
- The sample mod `vampire` (`games/shooters_edge/mods/vampire`): a player's blow heals him by half
  its points; it reads and changes players alone, so it plays on either game's rules.
- The playable server stacks a package from `sv_mod` on a sequel map through the same `start`.

**Held by:** `tests/shooters_edge_rules_mod_test.cpp` (vampire on the sequel's duel changes it,
sees the kill and replays to the bit; the refusals) and the `mod` lab's sequel half (the
shooter's health against the clock, with and without it: 100 flat against steps up to 149).

**Left:** a mod's own entities and functions on the sequel's rules (they would need a place in
the sequel's entity list and its frame); `damage` and `death` on the classic rules.
