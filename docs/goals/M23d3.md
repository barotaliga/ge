Status: done

Built (PR "capture: the live capture, the window and its sound recorded as it is played"):

- `engine/gpu/readback_ring.hpp`: a ring of readbacks; each capture is its own command buffer
  submitted after the frame's, its map started, the pixels handed back oldest first with their
  stamp a frame or two later; a capture with every slot in flight is dropped and counted.
  `Surface::frame_target` hands the acquired frame as a target; `Readback::take_into` reuses a
  picture's storage.
- `engine/capture/live.hpp`: the mix tapped on the audio thread into a lock-free ring whose
  sample count is the clock; frames stamped on it (seqlocked anchor carried by the wall clock);
  pictures handed in by a swap of buffers; a worker that converts, encodes (AV1 and Opus, or raw)
  and writes both tracks in time order. `capture/interleave.hpp` is shared with the export, and
  now holds a picture fed to the encoder as still to come until its packet is out.
- `fall_line --capture=PATH.webm|.mkv`.

Measured (`make lab LAB=gpu-capture-live ARGS="--seconds=30 --fps=60 --width=640 --height=360"`,
the container's software adapter, debug build): 1800 frames drawn, 1800 in the file, 0 dropped;
the frame's capture call at most 0.9 ms (p99 0.39, mean 0.24); every one of 24 flashes within
2 ms of its click; AV1 at 7.4 ms a frame on the worker (worst 19.7); 0.53 MB. A 120-frame bot run
of Fall Line in a virtual display, captured to `.webm`, decodes in ffmpeg to 120 AV1 frames and
881 Opus packets.
