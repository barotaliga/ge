Status: in progress
Milestone: **Every setting under one naming pattern**: every setting and command of every program audited (none missed) and named by one pattern: the prefix names one category and holds only it, a sub-setting extends its parent's full name with `_` so Tab groups a family, a switch is named for what it turns on, and one thing is one setting; QuakeWorld's names where they fit, Quake II's or Quake 3's where those fit better
Exit: Every setting and command of every program follows the prefix table and the sub-setting rule in docs/console.md § Names; no name of ours is left off the pattern, held by a naming test over every program's manual pass
Labs: `console`

Asked by Niclas on 2026-11-27 (plan step 4, the settings lift): audit every setting, rename them
to one clear pattern, sub-settings under their parent's name (`r_particle_scale` →
`r_particles_scale` under `r_particles`). No aliases of our old names: a player sets them again.

**The census (2026-11-28):** `make manual` on main (c2fe419) lists 1,716 settings and 439 commands
across the window and the dedicated server, every game's pass; Fall Line's 80 settings are read
from `games/fall_line/cfg/cvars.cpp`. Every one has a verdict on the census page (linked from the
PR): 314 renamed, 12 merged into another, 1,397 kept.

**The pattern:**

1. **The prefix is the category, and holds only it.** The table in `docs/console.md` § Names,
   with these changes: `gl_` is retired (the picture is `r_`, as Quake II and Quake 3 have it); the
   first-person view's motion and tints are `v_` (Quake's), never `cl_`; every camera but your own
   eyes is `cam_`; the held weapon is one family under `r_viewmodel`; watching and recording demos
   is `demo_`; the TV relay is `tv_`; graphs and overlays over the view are `scr_`; the window is
   `vid_` (no `win_`); the mouse is `m_` (no `in_raw`); the console's own settings are `con_` (no
   `com_`).
2. **A sub-setting extends its parent's full name with `_`**, so Tab completes the family:
   `r_particles` → `r_particles_scale`, `crosshair` → `crosshair_color`, `cl_predict` →
   `cl_predict_players`, `r_drawflat` → `r_drawflat_floor`.
3. **A switch is named for what it turns on** (`cl_predict`, not `cl_nopred`; `cl_lerp`, not
   `cl_nolerp`). The classic negative spelling stays the game's, with its values mapped.
4. **One thing, one setting.** Two settings for one thing are merged (`zombietime` and
   `sv_zombietime`; `scr_conspeed` and `con_speed`; three offsets of the held weapon).
5. **What keeps its name:** a game's own setting under that game's own name (Quake II's
   `cl_railcore_color`, `g_*`); a key the classic protocol or its mods read (`name`, `skin`, `rate`,
   `deathmatch`, `pm_*`, `k_*`, the server info keys); the few names every player knows
   (`sensitivity`, `fov`, `crosshair`, `gamma`, `volume`, `freelook`).
6. **A classic game's spelling is that game's synonym** (`docs/console.md` § Synonyms): when one of
   our names that is also a classic game's own spelling is renamed (`cl_bob` → `v_bob`), the old
   spelling stays a synonym in that game's layer, so the game's own configs and its players' habits
   still work. A name only we ever used gets no alias.
