The classic content's effects replaced: classic-enhance's replacement table (plugins/classicenhance,
M41) names `explosion` for the pack's blast sprite, then the flare and the bubbles; and sparks and
debris that meet a level's brushes, not only a heightfield.
Each classic game's blasts are looks of their own (a rocket's, a grenade's, a barrel's), knobs set
on the engine's presets, so every game draws them with the same code.

Built in Shooter's Edge (the `se-weapon-fx` lab, classic beside enhanced, from the eye and from the
side, on the fixture halls or a pack's own maps and weapons): under `ce_explosions` a rocket's or a
grenade's blast is the engine's explosion, layered (the main ball and three later billows, a dome
against the surface it went off on), the classic's ember ball kept as embers, its light the
fireball's flash; `ce_trails` a rocket's motor flame and spreading smoke, `ce_hits` a round's
burst by the map triangle it hit, `ce_beams` the engine's bolt for the arc gun. Off (and in the
classic look) the classic's own, faithful now: palette specks on the palette's ramps
(`render/particles.hpp`'s shining particles), the pack's explosion sprite and bolt models. Smoke
is lit by the room (`render::BlastLight`); the scorch is pressed with the decals (M47b).

Named through classic-enhance's replacement table (`classicenhance/replace.hpp`) rather than the
game's code: Shooter's Edge's model kinds take their row from it (`ModelKind::effect`), so the
explosion is the engine's where the table names `explosion` for the pack's explosion sprite, a
torch's painted flame is left out where it names a fire, and the flare and the bubble sprites are
drawn as the engine's under `ce_flares` and `ce_bubbles` (`InPlace`). The engine's shapes are made
at load (`render/sprite_shapes.hpp`: `flare_frame`, a warm core and a soft halo added over the
picture; `bubble_frame`, a clear ball with a Fresnel rim, a highlight up and to the left and a
darker ring inside the rim). A bubble the server moves is drawn as a little cluster, three of
them, each wobbling off its path at its own rate. The globe's own light is the map's already, so the
flare adds none. The mod's `light_globe` stands its sprite where the map hangs one. Pictured by
`se-models` (its `globe` and `bubbles` views, both looks; `--progs` plays the campaign variant,
whose `air_bubbles` the arena removes): e4m7's globes and e1m4's bubbles on the pack. Held by
`tests/render_sprite_shapes_test.cpp`.

Sparks and debris meet a level's brushes, not only a heightfield (docs/rendering.md § Particles
and figures, "What was drawn"). A pool made to collide (`ParticleSettings::collide`) bounces what
falls off whatever the last frame drew, read from the depth target before the frame draws over
it. Shooter's Edge's pool collides, and each blast's falling births also carry the floor under the
blast as their own, for the ones flung out of view. In the game's frame on e1m1 they hop off the
floor round a rocket's blast and come to rest on it. `se-weapon-fx`'s `<look>.<launcher>-sunk`
counts the few flung out of view over another floor that still fall through. Held by
`tests/render_particles_test.cpp`.


A grenade in flight (2026-11-12): a classic grenade gives no light, so the classic look no longer
lends it the rocket's 200-unit orange light (which lit its whole body). Under `ce_trails` its
painted ends glow faintly into the room round it, red as the pack's own skin paints them
(`ModelKind::glow_rgb`, the mean of the skin's glowing texels; `weapon_fx.hpp`'s `grenade_glow`,
1.6 m at 0.45), and the light rides on the grenade's entity so its own body is never lit by it:
only its ends show the glow. The sequel's grenade is painted the same way (orange-red bands at
both ends) and gets the same light once its skin marks those bands as glowing.
