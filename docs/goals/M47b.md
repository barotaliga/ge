The flash and the scorch in the world: each frame a game turns `flash_at` into a steady local light
(its candela and colour reset from the curve, its range the look's), and on the frame a blast goes
off presses `scorch_of` with `engine/detail`'s scorch decal (a pool of its own, `now_s` the blast's
time), whose hot ember look M42d adds. Waits on the local lights (#199) and the decals (#201).

Built in Shooter's Edge (the `se-weapon-fx` lab, its aim on a plain wall near the spawn; `--pitch` to look
at the floor): the flash's light on the walls through the brush world's dynamic lights (its colour
the fireball's blackbody, white-hot then orange, as far as the look's range), and under `ce_decals`
the scorch pressed with engine/detail's decals onto the map's own faces where the blast went off
against a surface, its embers glowing over their first 1.5 s; bullet holes and the bolt's burns the
same way, drawn as the beams' pass's burns.

Done in the engine (docs/rendering.md § Explosions, "The flash and the scorch in the world"):
`flash_lights` (render/explosions.hpp) hands every live blast's flash to the local lights each
frame, its reach following its light out to a lux; `render/marks.hpp` presses a blast's scorch
with the decals and draws a pool's marks as the beams pass's burns (the glue lifted out of
Shooter's Edge, which now uses it). The `gpu-explosion` lab's yard has both: its `flash` view by
day, at dusk and at night, and its `scorch` view of a floor's mark and a wall's, embers and soot.
Held by `tests/render_marks_test.cpp`.
