Built (PR "replays: keyframes, the seek and the transport"):

- `engine/play/game.hpp`: `Run::clone`, a deep copy that steps on as the original (null: the
  game keeps no keyframes).
- `engine/replay/keep.hpp`: the keeper and its `KeepPolicy` (every 2 s; all of the last 20 s,
  one in 6 s back to 120 s, one in 24 s after), the rule `keeps` a pure function of the policy
  and the newest step.
- `engine/replay/player.hpp`: the player over an input log: a seek from the run, a keyframe or
  the setup, paid out by `advance(budget)`.
- `engine/replay/transport.hpp`: play, pause, the end pausing, four speeds, skip 5 s, a frame
  (1/30 s) at a time, seek, a growing length.
- Fall Line's `FallLineRun::clone` (the driver, the pilot's hands, the events not yet observed).

about 11 MB; the worst seek steps 2,879 steps (the 24 s far band), the mean 332.
