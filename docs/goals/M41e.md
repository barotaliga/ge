Status: open

A new plugin, `plugins/morph`: the classic vertex-animated model (skins, seam-split vertices,
frames and their groups) and sprite read into one view, refusing damage, with fixture writers.
The classicenhance pipeline gains a `models` stage: skins through the texture enhancer, smooth
normals per frame, glTF out with one morph target a frame. Point-normal rounding is kept as an
option, off by default (the smooth shading alone reads better).
