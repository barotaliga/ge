Exit: A torch-lit room of a brush map pictured by firelight
Labs: `gpu-bsp`

A brush map's wall torches and large flames drawn as `render::Flame`s in the v2 BSP renderer
(`plugins/brush/app/bsp_flames.hpp`, docs/rendering.md § Flames): each light entity that stands
for a fire gets gas where its model's flame burns, its level the light's own flicker, its light
lifted to the gas's centre and softened over the flame's height, its core set against the map's
own light (`Flame::relative`). `gpu-bsp` pictures them in its real-time panel. Hiding the classic
torch models is classic-enhance's (M41), which reads `PointLight::fire`.
