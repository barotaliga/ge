Built (PR "capture: AV1 and Opus in WebM, the engine's own encoders"):

- `cmake/Codecs.cmake` (`GE_ENCODERS`, ON for this tree's build, OFF for the package and a
  consumer): libaom 3.12.1 and libopus 1.5.2 downloaded pinned by SHA-256 and built once at
  configure time into `GE_DEPS_DIR` (46 s on the container), linked privately into `ge_play`.
  AV1 rather than VP9 because libvpx has no CMake build (configure and make, msys on Windows).
- `engine/capture/codecs.hpp`: the AV1 encoder (realtime, speed 9, constant quality, a keyframe
  every 2 s, no temporal delimiters, the `av1C` codec private) and decoder; the Opus encoder
  (20 ms packets, the pre-skip as CodecDelay, OpusHead) and decoder; stubs without the encoders.
- The Matroska muxer writes CodecDelay and SeekPreRoll and leaves BitDepth out for a compressed
  track; the reader reads them and the codec private back.
- `av_export` with codec `av1`: the two queues interleaved by time; `record --format=mkv
  --codec=av1` writes a `.webm` (`--crf`, 32 by default).

Measured (release, the container): seed 38's first 30 s at 640x360 and 30 fps is 2.4 MB as AV1
and Opus against 323 MB uncompressed (136 times smaller, 4.7 MB a minute), 44.6 dB luma PSNR
against the uncompressed pictures (worst 42.8), 32 ms a frame to export against 31 ms raw.
The sync fixture as `.webm`: every click within 1.1 ms of its step, every flash within a frame,
ffmpeg decoding every picture at 24, 25, 30 and 60 fps. The encoder alone on a hard pattern:
about 10 ms a 720p frame and 20 ms a 1080p frame at crf 32 (4 threads).
