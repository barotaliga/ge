`plugins/mountain/village/furniture.hpp` reads the street plan (M13g) for what drives and parks on
it: a lane each way on every street (right-hand), the loop round the village, the crossings, the
kerbside bays along the back street's parking strip, the car park's bays either side of its aisles
and the bus stop on the square's edge. `traffic.hpp` plans the traffic off hashes of the seed on a
salt of its own (no stream; no map digest moves): the ski bus in off a road to its stop and away,
four to six visitors in off the road to a car park bay, parked 90 to 300 s, backed out and away,
two cars through, two or three cars round the loop each way and three or four cyclists at the
kerb; the bays nobody drives to or through take parked cars (the car park fuller by day). Every
route is the line its rear axle follows, rounded at the corners, its speed eased for each bend and
integrated once, and comes round in a whole fraction of the 600 s period; `vehicle_at` is a pure
function of the clock. Each vehicle is placed at the first dealt phase whose whole period keeps
its room clear of every vehicle placed before it. A car passing a cyclist on its lane swings out
only as far as clears him (`pass_swing`), held while they are alongside, and a cyclist is placed
only where every car that passes him has that room against oncoming traffic.

The `traffic` lab over seeds 1 to 12, 38 and 101 at generator v10: traffic on all 14, 12 to 18
vehicles a seed (2 to 6 round the loop, 4 to 6 visitors, 2 through, the bus and 2 to 4 cyclists),
56 to 73 cars parked in 95 to 112 bays, and no two vehicles (nor one and a parked car) overlapping
anywhere in the period, sampled every half second. Planning takes about 0.5 s a seed in the debug
build. `tests/mountain_traffic_test.cpp` and `tests/golden/mountain_traffic.txt` hold it.
