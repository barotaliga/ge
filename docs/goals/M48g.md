The breath is presentation only: it reads the ground speed the hand already gets and feeds
nothing back. Each breath draws its depth and length from the placement state's own stream, and
its depth eases to a new value (a new breath, a stop) so the gun never jumps. While striding,
`breath_moving` of it is kept under the bob.

`labs viewmodel` charts it (`breath`: 10 s still, a 10 s run at 8 m/s, 25 s still; metrics
`breath-rest-per-min`, `breath-after-run-per-min` and the depths). `labs se-client --game=<pack>
--hand=1 --film=<n> --exertion=<0..1>` films a classic game's weapon breathing in a real map.
