The fog's inject (`render/fog.wgsl`) reads the frame's light list: each lamp of the cluster a
froxel's point falls in (found by the point's pixel and reversed-z depth, as a surface there
finds it), its irradiance on the volume's Henyey-Greenstein lobe, past the lamp's own shadow map
and dimmed by the medium toward the lamp (`light_inscatter(p, dir, frag, g, sigma)`, the
extinction at the point taken as even along the way). Fall Line's `fog.inject` pass reads the
lamps' shadow atlas. The `fog` lab's `lamps` scene (seed 38 at 22:00: a 0.012/m mist 30 m deep
round a floodlight mast) changes 0.81 of the picture against 0.09 before: the heads' halo and the
beam's column, the air away from the lamps dark; the `glow` scene's haze now takes its
floodlight's light too, and the day scenes are unchanged to the bit. The inject costs 209 ms
without the lamps and 293 ms with them in that scene on the software adapter.
