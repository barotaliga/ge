(protocol 15, the packs' three), the client recording (protocol 28, 3 of 4), multiview `.mvd` (15 of 15
once a converter's banner before the server data is read past), the sequel's `.dm2` (26, 31, 34:
the packs' three), and every wrapper (`.gz`, `.zip`, `.7z`, `.xz`, our `.mvdz`). Not yet:
`.qwz` (0 of 12), the protocol 26 client recording (0 of 2), `.dem` of protocols 666 and 999, Dzip `.dz`, the

each are in `porting/shooters-edge/demo-formats-{classic,single,sequel}.md` (clean-room
answers).

**First slice (in progress): the conversion stream.** `client/demo_convert.hpp`: any source
bytes in, the target's whole records out frame by frame (so the TV converts on the fly),
`ConvertNotes` naming what was dropped and rewritten, `no_end` for a looping broadcast;
`shooters_edge_cli demos convert <name> <file>` writes a file (a `z` after the extension packs
it). Pairs: `.dem` to `.mvd` (`demo_convert_single.cpp`: entities renumbered past the player
slots, the frame's players in the recorder's form, entity lists as deltas, the status as stats
records; the packs' demos 184 KB to 60 KB, every frame's eye and status the same), `.dem` to a client
recording the oldest multiplayer clients play (`demo_convert_client.hpp`, the facts in
`porting/shooters-edge/demo-write-client.md`: whole entity lists of at most 64, packets within
1450 bytes, a command record with the view's angles before each frame's packet, the commands one
sequence ahead; the packs' demos read back with every frame's eye, angles and status the same),
`.mvd` to a client recording of one player's view (`demo_convert_multiview.cpp`: the view
chosen with `convert_set_pov` at any frame starts with a whole signon and a whole frame, so a TV
converts a view only while someone watches it; real 4on4 and duel recordings read back with every
frame's eye and status the same but for a frame or three at the intermission; whole lists make
the file about four times the source), and any
sequel `.dm2` to protocol 34 (`demo_convert_sequel.cpp`: the suppress count put into each frame
of 26; a 34 recording byte for byte). Held by `tests/shooters_edge_demo_convert_test.cpp` and
the `se-demo-convert` lab.

The client recording to `.mvd` (`demo_convert_recorded.cpp`: every player the recorder saw, his
view from his commands and view sets, his status as his stats record, the lists as deltas;
real duel, team and spectator recordings read back with every frame's eye the same but at the
intermission, at a third to a fifth of the size).
