Built (PR "capture: the platform's encoders, VideoToolbox first"):

- `engine/capture/video.hpp`: the video codec by its word (`av1`, `h264`, `hevc`, `auto`) for the
  export, the live capture and the labs; `auto` is the platform's H.264 where there is one, else
  AV1. Its decoder opens whatever a track names.
- `engine/capture/platform_video.hpp` and `videotoolbox.cpp`: VideoToolbox's H.264 and HEVC
  compression and decompression sessions on macOS and iOS (hardware asked for, the system's
  software encoder taken without it), no B-frames, the codec private probed at open.
- `ge record --format=mkv --codec=h264|hevc|auto` (an `.mp4`, or `--container=mkv`),
  `fall_line --capture=PATH.mp4` with `auto`; a `.webm` refuses what WebM cannot carry.
- `tests/capture_platform_video_test.cpp` and the `capture` lab's platform table and
  `sync-platform` shot (docs/replays.md § The platform's encoders).
