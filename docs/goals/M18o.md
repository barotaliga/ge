Status: in progress

The classic game loop, built in a clean room: a research session wrote the behaviour in our
words (`porting/shooters-edge/movement.md`, `porting/shooters-edge/gameloop.md`) with measured
test vectors (`porting/shooters-edge/vectors/`), and a separate building session that never
read existing source builds from those notes alone (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § Licensing and
the clean room). The loop is the **classic-enhance baseline**: what Shooter's Edge does with
the classic content before any improvement, held by the parity labs, so every later change is
a measured difference from it.

- **M18o1, the move and the hulls** (`plugins/shooter`): clip hulls and the line trace, the box
  hull, the player move with every move type and server setting, the `pmove` and `se-parity`
  labs over the recorded scenarios.
- **M18o2, the frame, the entities and the client** (`games/shooters_edge/sim`, the reusable
  parts in `plugins/shooter`): the game-program interface, entities and their fields, the area
  tree, world traces, physics frames on the run clock, the movetypes, thinking, pushers,
  touches, a player's packet and commands, new missiles, the client's commands and
  prediction; the `se-loop` lab.
