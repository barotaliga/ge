Labs: `post`

The lens and the film live in the post pass (`engine/render/post.hpp`), each an `override`
switch: a game that leaves one at 0 compiles it out, so the pass is what it was. The strengths are
a 32-byte uniform (`set_post_look`), live. The grade's knobs (`engine/render/grade.hpp`) are baked
into the 32³ table after the game's own grade, so they cost the frame nothing.

| Effect | Fall Line cvar | Default | At full strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contrast-adaptive sharpening | `r_sharpen` | 0.3 | a flat area's detail about doubled, no halo at an edge |
| Vignette | `r_vignette` | 0.25 | the corners at 40 % |
| Lateral colour fringes | `r_chromatic` | 0 | red and blue 0.5 % of the diagonal apart at the corners |
| Film grain | `r_grain` | 0 | about 4 % of the middle tones |
| Lens dirt (lit only by glow past white and the lens pass) | `r_lensdirt` | 0 | the glow doubled under a smudge |
| Contrast, saturation, temperature, tint, gamma | `r_contrast` `r_saturation` `r_temperature` `r_tint` `r_gamma` | neutral | baked into the table |

Off by default: the fringes, the grain and the dirt (each reads as an effect rather than a
picture). Measured by the `post` lab (lavapipe, 1280 x 720): see the PR for the table; the
sharpening is the dearest of them (four more loads a pixel), the vignette and grain are
arithmetic. Held by `tests/render_post_test.cpp` (a flat field stays flat under the sharpening and
the fringes; the vignette darkens only the corners; the grain averages out) and
`tests/render_grade_test.cpp`.
