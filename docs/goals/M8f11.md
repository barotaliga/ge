Status: done

Each disc can be drawn larger than it is: `render/view.hpp`'s `FrameView::sun_size` and `moon_size` multiply the sun's and the moon's angular radii (1: as they are), as through a longer lens. Only the discs grow. The sun's disc shares the same light over its larger face (on top of a sky type's own size), the moon's face keeps its brightness, and neither lights the world more, so the snow, the shadows and the exposure are what they were.

A cloud's glow round the key light is a point's diffraction pattern spread over the disc, so `clouds.wgsl` reads it off the drawn limb (`disc_swell`, the drawn radius past the real one): through an ice veil the bright core fills the larger face and the corona's ring stands off its edge. A disc no larger than the real one is drawn bit for bit as before.

Fall Line's cvars are `r_sunsize` and `r_moonsize` (0.5 to 20, read at startup with the picture's other cvars), carried on `FrameOptions`.
