Labs: `input-devices`

The platform backend, `engine/platform/devices.hpp` over SDL3 (`docs/input.md` § The platform
backend): the pooled keyboard (keys by USB usage, repeats, text as code points), the mouse
(raw counts in relative mode, buttons, the wheel), gamepads by SDL's mapping (positional buttons
and axes, family, rumble, trigger rumble, light, player lights, gyro, accelerometer, touchpad,
battery), joysticks, wheels, flight sticks and throttles by SDL's joystick type (axes, buttons,
hats), force feedback streamed as one constant effect, touch screens, hot-plug with fresh ids.
One pump with the window (`poll_input(window, snapshot, devices, queue)`) or without it
(`pump_devices`).

Measured, headless (`make lab LAB=input-devices`, 120 fps for 1.6 s):

- One script on a pad and a wheel through SDL's virtual devices and through the simulated
  backend: 0 of 192 frames hand a skier's or a racer's actions differently; the largest gap on
  any action is 3.5e-5 (the 16-bit step of a real axis); the devices are plugged on the first
  pump.
- Rumble 0.8 / 0.3 reaches the virtual pad as 52428 / 19661 and 0 / 0 once its 0.25 s are up;
  the light 255,64,0 and player 1 reach it too.
- The pad unplugged and plugged in again: one device_removed, one device_added, a new id.

`tests/platform_devices_test.cpp`: a virtual gamepad, wheel and flight stick read positionally;
rumble, light and player lights reach the device and stop; gyro and touchpad; the haptic level;
keys and a relative (raw) mouse through a window's pump under the virtual display.

Not done here, and where it goes:

- Pens (SDL's pen events) are not translated; a pen can be read as a touch finger when a game
  needs one (M19e).
- The force's sign and feel on a real wheel are unmeasured: SDL has no virtual haptic device.
  M19d measures real wheels (and decides whether the conditions move to the driver).
- iOS, iPadOS and Android run the same backend once the mobile builds exist (M11, M19e).
