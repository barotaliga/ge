Dynamic lights, shadows and materials for any game: local point and spot lights in physical units
with their flicker and pulses, clustered; one physically based surface model; then the lamps'
shadows and Fall Line's night lamps (docs/rendering.md § Local lights, § Materials).

M39a and M39b are done (one pull request), M39f (flames) in the next. Next: M39g (brush maps' torches as flames), M39c (the lamps' shadows), then M39d (Fall Line's
night lamps: the village, the floodlights, the headlamp). Ambient occlusion stays with M35e; light
shafts through haze come from the fog volume (M38), which reads these lamps through
`light_inscatter`.
