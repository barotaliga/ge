Status: open

Thunderstorms for any game (docs/storms.md): storm cells that rise, mature and die, their gust
fronts and their rain; cloud and ground flashes with their strokes, their flicker and their
branching channels; the sky, the clouds and the scene lit by them; and thunder delayed by distance.

M37a is done: the storm, its lightning and its thunder as pure functions in `engine/wind`, with the
`storm` lab. M37b is done: the flashes as lamps in the frame (`render/wgsl/flash.wgsl` lighting the
clouds, the sky and the ground) and the channel drawn before the bloom (`render/bolts.hpp`), with
the `storm-sky` lab. Next: M37c (thunder heard on the synth) and M37d (Fall Line's storms).
