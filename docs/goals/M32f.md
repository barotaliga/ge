Milestone: **Cloning a classic map** (`rooms/clone.hpp`, the `clone` lab): a 29 or BSP2 map's structure copied into the room format (solid space from the tree as brushes, every face's plane and texture projection, brush models as groups, the entity lump, liquids and sky as contents); every texture resolved to a procedural recipe first (M31's matching, never a pixel copied; unresolved: a neutral recipe); options (merge cells into larger brushes, smooth faceted arches and curves into curved geometry, surface replacements by texture name)
Exit: Each map of a pack given at run time cloned, drawn beside the original from the same eyes, its geometry within a tolerance of the original's, walked from its start; nothing of the source map committed
Labs: `clone`, `bsp`

Built (PR #145): `rooms::clone_map` clones a version 29 or BSP2 map into a room level. The tree's solid, liquid and sky cells become brushes, each visible face takes its surface's texture and recovered projection, brush models become groups (a trigger's as trigger volumes), and the entities come across with `origin` in metres and `angle` as `heading`. Textures come only from an agent-approved texture map (`parse_texture_map`), or placeholders on request, and are never copied. The `--smooth` option shades faceted arches round, and touching brushes merge where their union is convex (`rooms::merge_brushes`, 6 to 10 percent fewer brushes on the shareware maps). The `clone` lab draws each map beside its original from the same eyes and stands a body at every start. `tests/rooms_clone_test.cpp` holds the fixture's clone golden. The shareware maps clone, and at every start of every one a body stands on the original's floor in the clone (numbers in the PR).

Built since: `rooms::find_curves` finds the runs of faces that facet a pillar, a rounded corner, an arch or a vault, fitted with their circle, and `rooms::round_curves` draws them round (collision stays faceted); the `clone` lab counts and pictures them on each map.

Left: joining three or more cells at once into larger brushes; ornate arches whose slices do not meet on one line; the lens a free pillar top leaves between its round side and its faceted cap; a measured tolerance of the clone's surfaces against the original's; a walk from the start through the map.
