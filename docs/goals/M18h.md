Labs: `se-net`, `se-demo`, `se-demo-video`

**First slice (in progress): the game playable on one machine.** `games/shooters_edge/client/`
holds the `shooters_edge` window: the search path over a game folder's packs (`files.hpp`), the
listen server (`listen.hpp`: the baseline mod compiled from its built-in sources, the map from the
packs through `server/bsp_map.hpp`, the player as client slot 0 fed his commands directly, the
map changed by the console or the mod), the keys and the raw mouse as commands (`controls.hpp`),
the HUD's status (the quad's and the shield's seconds among it) and the first-person camera's
subject off the server (`view.hpp`), the eyes,
the missiles and the blasts frame by frame (`present.hpp`), the effects thrown from them
(`world_fx.hpp`: trails, explosions, their lights, the screen's blend), the weapon in hand
(`held.hpp`: the game's held weapons switched, kicked and flashing as the mod fires them), and the
world's frame
(`world_draw.hpp`: the BSP renderer in the game's look, `r_look` classic or modern, over the
engine's sky, the particles, bloom, post, the overlay). How to run it:
`docs/shooters-edge.md` § Playing it. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_listen_test.cpp` (a rocket
jump on the fixture halls, golden rows) and the `se-client` lab (the window's frame headless).

**The demo benchmark (done):** `client/demo.hpp` reads the single-player demo and the client's
multiplayer recording frame by frame (map, eye, angles, stats, entities), `client/timedemo.hpp`
plays one flat out in the window (`timedemo <demo>` in the console, `-timedemo <demo>` on the
command line: one demo frame a drawn frame, one in 33 ms shown and the rest drawn offscreen so the
display never sets the rate) and reports the average, max, min, 1% low and 0.1% low frame rates
and the GPU's frame span; the `se-timedemo` lab's `--price=1` prices each drawn switch by
playing the demo again with it off; `vid_fullscreen` puts the window over the whole screen
(`vid_display` which); the modern look's shadow maps draw at most two new cubes a frame. How to run it: `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Benchmarking a demo. Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_demo_test.cpp` (both formats written in code and read back) and the
`se-timedemo` lab (the run headless, every frame's time charted).

**Multiview recordings and playback (done):** the server's recording of every player (`.mvd`,
and `.mvd.gz` unpacked on the way in) reads beside the other two formats
(`client/demo_multiview.cpp`): its records walked into frames, the players' delta fields, the
entity lists, each player's own stats, the view riding one player (`demo_track`). The window
plays any demo at its own pace (`playdemo`, `-playdemo`, `cl_demospeed`). Checked against a
real four-player recording: 141,590 records read to the last byte, 41,820 frames, 621.2 s.
Held by `tests/shooters_edge_demo_test.cpp` (a multiview recording written in code, plain and
gzip, two players tracked, damage refused) and the `se-demo` lab (paths and health, no GPU).


**A demo as a video, and demos with voice (done):** `-demovideo <demo> <file>` turns any demo into
a video with no window (`client/demo_video.hpp`): the world's frame offscreen from the recorded
eye with the demo's entities, doors, statics and HUD, its sound messages played from the pack's
recordings (`client/demo_sound.hpp`) and its voices (`client/demo_voice.hpp`), written as `.mp4`,
`.webm` or `.mkv` by `engine/capture` (whose export now also takes a sound the caller plays). The
shareware pack's `demo1` exports whole: 74.6 s, 595 sounds, 66 loops, none missing, at 1280x720
in 28 s on the Mac. Voice in recordings: the server relays the protocol's voice message by team
(`ServerConfig::voice`, `voice_route`); our scripted client talks and records (`net/classic_record.hpp`):
a client's `.qwdv` holds its team's voices and its own, the server's `.mvdv` everyone's, each the
plain format behind an 8-byte voice header (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § Demos with voice). Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_classic_record_test.cpp` and the `se-demo-video` lab. The codec's
`spawnbaseline2` is now the protocol's (the number in the record's word, not a short before it),
so our server's recordings read back; `timedemo <demo>` (`-timedemo`) is the benchmark's name.

**A demo watched like a video (done, with M23i):** `playdemo` shows a play bar
(`client/demo_playback.hpp` over `engine/ui/play_bar.hpp`): click or drag to seek, Space to pause,
Up and Down for the speed, Right and Left to skip (`demo_skip_forward` 15 s, `demo_skip_back` 5 s),
Page Down and Page Up for the next and last highlight; the demo is scanned once for its highlights
(`client/demo_scan.hpp`, `client/demo_highlights.hpp`) and the tracked player's are marked on the
bar. A seek reads the demo again from its start when it goes back (`timedemo.hpp`'s `demo_seek` on
the timeline). Held by `tests/shooters_edge_demo_highlights_test.cpp` and the `se-highlights` lab.

**Other players in a played demo (done):** the window's `playdemo` and `timedemo` drew only the
map; they now draw every entity the demo holds as `-demovideo` does (`demo_placed`, now shared in
`client/demo_video.hpp`), the other players in the colours their info carries (`updateuserinfo`,
`setinfo`), and the world is made again when a client recording's model list grows. A
multiplayer demo plays from the game folder its server ran when the window is on the base
(`timedemo.hpp`'s `demo_game_dir`), so a team mod's demo finds the mod's models. The world after a
demo, and after the campaign's next map, keeps the listen server's models too. Checked on a real
four-player recording (the three others placed every frame, their colours read). Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_demo_test.cpp` and the `se-timedemo` lab (`--format=match --stage-pak=fixture`).

**A played demo heard and read (done):** the window opens the audio device (`volume`) and plays
a demo's sounds and voices through the export's own players (`client/demo_heard.hpp`); the frames
read between two draws are gathered (`timedemo.hpp`'s `DemoHeard`), so none is lost at speed, and
a seek plays nothing it skipped. The reader keeps each frame's printed lines and centre message
(`DemoState::prints`, `centre`): deaths, chat and team chat go to the HUD's feed and the console as
plain words (`print_lines`), the centre message to the HUD's centre. On a real 2on2 recording: 473
lines to the tracked player, 192 deaths and 122 chat lines, every one read. Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_demo_test.cpp` and the `se-demo` lab (what was printed, by level).
