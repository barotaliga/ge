albedo (`texture_means`) and the map's lights (`bake_lights`), sky colour from the map, and writes
`lightmaps/<map>.gerb` (`write_rebaked`, light direction in world space). `kLightmapStageVersion`
moves to 2 so every cache reruns the stage.

Done: `light_map` (`plugins/classicenhance/lightmaps.hpp`) rebakes each map with the textures'
mean colours (`texture_means`: the enhanced albedo, else the pack's pixels) and the map's light
entities (`brush/lights.hpp`'s `map_bake_lights`, shared with the renderer's point lights), no sun
and no sky, and writes `lightmaps/<map>.gerb` (GERB version 2, with the bounce alone);
`kLightmapStageVersion` is 2 and the stage's key carries its options and the textures' key. The
shareware pack's 21 maps: 3.1 million luxels, 53 MB, 4 min 18 s on four cores. Held by
