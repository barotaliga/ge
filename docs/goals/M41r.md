Played on the start map after M41p, the pools still moved about 30% too fast. `render::WaterDraw::
pace` (1 by default: a real water's) scales every component's angular frequency as it is staged,
the gusts' and the foam's drift, and the stir's and the gusts' noise clock (`sun.y` in
`render/wgsl/water_body.wgsl`), so the water at pace p at time t is the water at its real pace at
time p t; the wakes and the rain's rings, which start at moments the game hands in, keep the real
clock. `render::set_water_pace` restages a body at another pace. `brush::app::kPoolPace` (0.7) is
the classic pools' pace, `brush::app::Options::water_pace` the game's, and `relay_liquid_pace`
restages a map's pools when it changes; Shooter's Edge binds `r_water_speed` (archived, 0.7; 1 a
real water's). Test: `tests/render_water_test.cpp`
(`the_waters_pace_puts_its_motion_on_a_slower_clock`).
