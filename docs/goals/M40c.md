Milestone: **The rebake in the classic pipeline**: the classic-enhance plugin's `lightmaps` stage rebakes every map of an installed or shareware pack into the local cache, with the generated normal maps (not the textures' luminance) as the relief
Exit: Every shareware map rebaked from a fresh cache; its time and size per map reported
Labs: `gpu-bsp`

The classic-enhance plugin's `lightmaps` stage calls `brush::rebake_map` for each map and writes
`write_rebaked`'s bytes into the local cache; the renderer reads them with `read_rebaked`. The
relief then comes from the stage's generated normal maps instead of the textures' luminance.

Done with M41b: the `lightmaps` stage rebakes every map of the pack (the shareware pack's 21 in
4 min 18 s on four cores, 53 MB); the relief under the rebaked and the live light comes from the
generated normal maps (the scene's surface atlas, `ce_relief`).
