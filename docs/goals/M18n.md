Status: in progress

Shooter's Edge's own sounds and models, all CC0, under `games/shooters_edge/content/` (the `free-assets` skill).

- Done: the registries (`sounds.tsv`: 227 sounds, 129 for the game and the world and 98 for creatures; `models.tsv`: 95 models) built from the shareware maps' entities and the game logic's calls; `classic.assetmap` with the numbered door, button, lift, train, trigger and key sets, agreed with the map cloner (M32g); 99 of the 129 game sounds and 27 models replaced from Kenney and OpenGameArt CC0 packs, picked by a match on length, bands and envelope against each original, and credited in `CREDITS.md`.
- Scope: only what the multiplayer game logic and client play or show is replaced. Every row the multiplayer code never reaches (the creatures, their projectiles, the debug noise maker) is kept in the registry with status `skip`; that is 95 sounds and 26 models.
- Open: 32 sounds (rune and base-world locks, chain lift and door sounds, secret doors, trains, some power-up and drowning variants, the trap laser, the bonus powers) and 43 models (the player and its head and eyes, the gibs, the floor weapons, the power-ups, keys and runes, the mega health, the cells, the beam, the flames and sprites, the team flag), the view models' first-person framing; a listening pass by a person.
- Next: the `ge` tools that remove the manual steps (`ge content scan`, `ge asset source search|fetch`, `ge sound match`, `ge sound split`, `ge sound import`, `ge model import`, and a credits check), listed in `docs/goals/ge-backlog.md`.
