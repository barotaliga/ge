Shooter's Edge's own sounds and models, all CC0, under `games/shooters_edge/content/` (the `free-assets` skill).

- Done: the registries (`sounds.tsv`: 227 sounds, 129 for the game and the world and 98 for creatures; `models.tsv`: 95 models) built from the shareware maps' entities and the game logic's calls; `classic.assetmap` with the numbered door, button, lift, train, trigger and key sets, agreed with the map cloner (M32g); 99 of the 129 game sounds and 27 models replaced from Kenney and OpenGameArt CC0 packs, picked by a match on length, bands and envelope against each original, and credited in `CREDITS.md`.
- Open: the remaining 30 game sounds (rune and base-world locks, chain lift and door sounds, some power-up and drowning variants), the player model (the free rigged candidates are FBX only; convert through the Blender path), the keys, the health mega and the cells, the view models' first-person framing, every creature sound; a listening pass by a person.
