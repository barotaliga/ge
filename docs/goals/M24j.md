Status: done

Landed `engine/water/sound.hpp`: `hear` asks `water::sample` at an ear and on seven rings round
it out to 96 m, and the flow regions' sheets, and turns what it finds into an intensity per voice,
the power the water spends there over the square of the distance (the waves' energy on a shore,
a current's bed friction rho c_f |v|^3, a waterfall's rho g Q h). A water bed of seven noise
layers is steered from it (the lapping and the surf, a river's rush and babble, a fall's roar and
spray, the room under water); under water the water's voices lose their highs and the room comes
up, and `muffle` takes a game's airborne beds and one-shots under with them. One-shots: splashes
by speed and size (a bank of `splash`, `plunge` and `drip`), a swimmer's `stroke`, `breath` and
`gasp`, and bubbles at their Minnaert pitch, which also make a river's babble and the churn
under a fall. `water::Sample` gained `hs`, the whole sea's height at the point.

Evidence: the `water-audition` lab: the bank (every sound between -40 and -16 dBFS), seven
splashes (the centroid falling from 3.6 kHz for a pebble to under 500 Hz for a skier or a
boulder), eleven places (the fresh shore 10 dB over the calm, the gale on a beach over both, the
stream 21 dB over the slow river, the fall from 15 m 10 dB over 60 m and darker, under the lake
half the centroid of over it, silence 500 m away), the roar against distance on 1 / r, and a
swim; `tests/water_sound_test.cpp` (Minnaert, silence away from water, the fresh shore, a fall
twice as far half as loud and on its side, the muffle, splash size and speed, the same bytes for
the same seed). Pictures: the Water labs page.

Not yet: a fall heard through the rock between (the rings know water, not ground); an echo of
its own under water (the synth has one room); rain on the water; wakes heard lapping in a channel.
