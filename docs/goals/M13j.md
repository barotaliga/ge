Labs: `village-look`

`plugins/mountain/app/village_look.hpp` draws the village (M13i) as one instance family. The
streets are one mesh a map, cut off the village's ground mask: each run of carriageway or
sidewalk cells along a row is a strip laid 6 cm over the snow at heights no more than 2 m apart
(at 6 m the terrain's own triangles rose through the car park). A carriageway is the packed
grey-brown of a ploughed street, a sidewalk paler. Each vehicle kind is a mesh in its own frame,
chunky and faceted like the forest: a car's body, glasshouse, bumpers, lamps and four tyres, the
van's tall box, the bus's band of windows in its livery, a bicycle and its rider; each has a far
LOD of a box or two below 10 px. A material set is a paint, dealt off a vehicle's id.

`games/fall_line/app/frame.hpp`'s `show_village` puts it in the game's frame once a run's village
is known (the window and the play harness both call it): the forest drawn again without the
felled trees and the sun's cascades redrawn without their shadows, the family made (the streets'
instance, every parked car, a slot for each moving vehicle) and added to the frame's families,
which the depth pyramid rebinds on a resize. Each `draw` places the traffic off
`vehicle_at(level, plan, k, scene.run_time)`, so what is drawn is where the simulation holds the
skier out. Making the family after the frame's load creates no pipeline (the lab's frame shows no
mid-run pipeline error).
