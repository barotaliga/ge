**The audit (done, 2026-11-23):** every name of the five reference sources (Quake, the stock
QuakeWorld client and server, QuakeWorld's modern client and server, Quake II's modern client and
server: 2,436 names) matched across the games by meaning, against our registrations and synonym
tables. 190 are common to Quake and Quake II: 137 already one setting, 16 kept apart on purpose
(another meaning, unit, value set or direction: `crosshair`, `cl_maxfps`, `cl_predict` against
`cl_nopred`, the crosshair's colour, the underwater warp each game draws its own way), 8 that
neither client takes, 3 sound settings, and 26 to lift.

**First lift (B1, done):** QuakeWorld's spellings as synonyms in its layer (`scr_consize` for
`con_height`, `scr_conalpha` for `con_bgalpha`, `gl_consolefont` for `con_font`, `r_drawparticles`
for `r_particles`, so its rulesets' particle lock now holds, `cl_net_clientport`,
`in_grab_windowed_mouse`, the command `vid_reload` for `r_reload`); Quake II's `gl_swapinterval`
for the window's `vid_vsync` (its video page's row now reaches it); and four of Quake II's own
made every game's: `gl_modulate` (the classic look's lightmap scale, kept across a game change),
`gl_anisotropy` (Quake II's 1 a `same` row), `in_grab` and `net_clientport` (the client's port, now
opened in Quake too). Held by `tests/shooters_edge_cvar_layers_test.cpp` and
`tests/shooters_edge_q2_session_test.cpp`. 16 left.

**Second lift (B2, done):** the camera behind you is every game's: `cl_thirdperson` (a cheat) and
`cl_thirdperson_angle` in the classic layer, Quake's `chase_active` a synonym, so Quake gains its
chase camera; its distance is our `cam_dist`, the distance a watched player is seen from too
(Quake's `chase_back`, Quake II's `cl_thirdperson_range` with its 60 a `same` row). `cam_thirdperson`
stays ours for the player watched, no cheat. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_cvar_layers_test.cpp`.
12 left (the outlines and square specks are kept apart: another client's visuals, which classic
enhance replaces).

**Third lift (B3, done):** the packets a second you send are one setting, our `cl_c2spps` (0 one a

rate (another value set each). 6 left.
