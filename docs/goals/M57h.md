## Progress

- 2026-11-13: the classic half. The watcher (`games/shooters_edge/net/classic_watcher.hpp`): our classic client as a spectator, its client recording broadcast as it grows (`client/recording_tv.hpp`, one-second blocks on the `recording` track) through a TV node. `tests/shooters_edge_classic_watcher_test.cpp`: an early and a late viewer both given the watcher's whole recording on the simulated network. The `se-watcher` lab, live against an open-source classic server with its stream port off: both viewers whole, the late one sent the match from its start.
- Next: the sequel's half, a watcher on an unmodified sequel server, once the sequel's network protocol lands (M18v9).
- 2026-11-13: the sequel's half. `net/sequel_tv_relay.hpp`: our sequel client watches as a spectator-camera on a server of the sequel (ours or an unmodified one), its recording goes through a TV node, and the sequel face (`net/sequel_tv_face.hpp`) serves the delayed match to unmodified clients of the sequel over protocol 34. Measured black-box: an unmodified client watches q2dm1 through it, from our server and from an unmodified one. `shooters_edge_hops sequel-tv`; `tests/shooters_edge_sequel_tv_face_test.cpp`.
- 2026-11-13: a recording as the sequel TV's source (`net/sequel_tv_demo.hpp`, `shooters_edge_hops sequel-tv --demo=<file.dm2>`): played at the game's pace and round and round, the count raised each time. The face now tells its viewers about a new level the way a server does (`changing`, `reconnect`) and keeps their connection. Measured black-box: an unmodified client watched a protocol 34 recording through 15 rounds.
- 2026-11-13: classic clients at the TV (`net/classic_tv_udp.hpp`, `net/classic_tv_recording.hpp`, `shooters_edge_hops tv --demo=<client recording>`). Any classic client, the oldest too, joins the TV as a server and watches one player's view in plain protocol 28. It is seated in his view with no free look, fire switches players, and every entity list goes out whole. Measured black-box: an existing client asked for no extensions and watched a bot's dm4 recording. Next: a multiview match turned into a view per watched player, and single-player recordings, from the demo converter (one stream per player, each converted only while watched).
- 2026-11-14: the sequel TV plays the full game's own recordings: an older protocol (26) converted to 34 as the TV opens it (`client/demo_convert.hpp`), and a file a viewer asks to download refused at once so it does not wait on the loading screen. Watched black-box: an unmodified client on the full game's demo1 (base2), round and round. `tests/shooters_edge_sequel_tv_face_test.cpp`.
