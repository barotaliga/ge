The beam in a game: the `arc` preset (picked from the three `gpu-beam` recorded) fired from the held weapon's muzzle (engine/viewmodel's mount, M48) while the
trigger is held, traced by the game's own hitscan, sparks at the hit, its hum and crackle, the
lights' shadows after M39c, Shooter's Edge's lightning gun and classic-enhance drawing it in place
of the bolt models a level ships with.

Progress (2026-11-09): Shooter's Edge's lightning gun draws ONE bolt look (the player's choice of
style is gone, on the owner's word that everyone should see the one decent effect): as thin as the
classic bolt, its brightness keyed to the eye (`BeamLook::core_keys`, `glow_keys`) so it does not
swell into a screen-filling glow in a dark room at night, and the brightest light in the room
(`bolt_lights`, boosted over `r_fx_lights`); its scorches now land. Pictured by `se-weapon-fx` on a
real map by day and at `sv_hour 23`. The bolt also crackles across the gun's prongs while it
fires (`find_arc`, `prong_arc_look`), as the classic gun's own arc does. Still open: the hum and crackle on the synth.

Progress (2026-11-13): the bolt BURNS A LINE where it sweeps instead of a row of round scorches,
white-hot where it has just been, cooling through orange and red to black (engine/detail's
`burn_line`, `beam_burns`' pieces laid end to end); held still on one spot it MELTS the wall, a
glowing pool that grows while the beam stays (`melt`, pressed again larger in its own slot). Hot
marks light the room while they glow (render/marks.hpp's `mark_glows`), and the same glowing mark
is there for a slug's hit and a big energy ball's burst (`press_glow`, Shooter's Edge's
`glow_mark` with `kSlugMark` and `kEnergyBallMark`). Pictured by `se-weapon-fx --sweep`.

Progress (2026-11-22): a hot mark BURNS A LITTLE, THEN SMOKES (render/mark_fire.hpp): a burnt line,
a melt, a slug's, a blaster bolt's (new: Quake II's blaster and hyperblaster now leave a small
glowing mark, `kBoltMark`) or an energy ball's mark burns in a few small flames of burning gas
(render/flame.hpp's, so they read as real flames) for the first 70 % of its glow, the weak ones
going out first, then thin grey smoke that is thickest late in the glow and gone a little after it.
Pictured by `se-weapon-fx --sweep=50,500,400 --at=330,192,24,0,25` (the thunderbolt) and by
`se-q2-weapon-fx --weapon=hyperblaster-marks|railgun-marks|bfg-marks[-orange|-cool]` (Quake II,
`--wall-at` to fire them at a wall).

Audit (2026-11-28): Shooter's Edge's lightning gun draws the beam (`games/shooters_edge/app/weapon_fx.hpp`'s `bolt_look` over `render/beam.hpp`), with its lights, burns, melts and the prong arc; left: the hum and crackle on the synth, and the lights' shadows after M39c.
