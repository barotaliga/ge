**The census (done, 2026-11-18):** two clean-room inventories, every setting and command of the
first game's modern client (1155 settings, 1405 more on the element HUD's 83 elements, 371
commands) and of Quake II's stock client (202 settings, 129 commands) with its modern client's
daily ones, and the ranked gap list against the window's own names. The rounds follow it.

**Team chat's pointing (done):** what the player points at (`client/team_point.hpp`: the thing
nearest the view's line in sight, from the pointing set, named in the team's words) fills `%t`,
`%x`, `%y` and `%j` (`tp_pointtimeout`), says `tp_msgpoint`, and steers `tp_msgenemypwr`, `safe`,
`killme`, `utake`, `quaddead`, `getquad` and `getpent` (a glowing enemy, a teammate). On a server
joined or a demo it reads the protocol's entities and players, in the window's own game the
server's. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_team_point_test.cpp`; pictured by `se-client --point=1` on
a real map (the quad, the heavy armour and the beam gun pointed at). The server's stop-sound
message needs nothing: the reference server never sends it, and every client path reads it.

**Round 13, the QuakeWorld packet rate (done):** `cl_c2spps` (packets a second, 0 one a move, else
10..72) and `cl_c2sImpulseBackup` in the classic layer: a move over the rate is held back, its
sequence spent, and rides in the next packet as a backup, never more than two in a row and never
one when a move the packet would carry has an impulse. The balance gains each move's time, so the
packets a second are the setting's (`porting/shooters-edge/client-net-pacing.md`). Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_remote_test.cpp`.

  (classic look, 1: the games always flashed) turns it off; `f_bonusflash` runs a config's alias

- The crosshair's place and Quake II's colours: `cl_crossx` and `cl_crossy` (engine, art pixels

**Rounds 19 and 20, the crosshair's picture, the team's item sets and triggers, map groups and two
network settings (done, in one pull request):**
- `crosshairimage <name>` (engine, `hud/crosshair.hpp`): a player's own picture drawn in place of
  every mark at its own pixels times `crosshairsize`, tinted and faded alike, even under
  `crosshair 0`; Shooter's Edge reads `crosshairs/<name>` as a TGA, PNG, JPEG or PCX
  (`client/picture_files.hpp`), again from each game's files when the game changes.
- The team's item sets (`client/team_items.hpp`): `tp_took` (a pickup remembered for the took codes
  and `f_took` run), `tp_pickup` (remembered only) and `tp_point` (what can be pointed at), each a
  set of the classic names and groups with `+` and `-`, `default`, `all` and `none`; deathmatch 4
  leaves ammunition and weapons out of a pickup.
- `filter #tag ...` (engine, `console/chat_ignore.hpp`): a tagged team line shows only when its tag
  is listed, the tag left off; an untagged one always shows.
- The regular-expression triggers (engine, `console/re_triggers.hpp`): `re_trigger`,
  `re_trigger_options` (final, remove, mask, interval, enable, noaction, nolog),
  `re_trigger_delete`, `_enable`, `_disable` by name or pattern, `re_trigger_match` and
  `re_trigger_match_0..9`, on a joined server's lines by print level and its centre messages,
  after the ignore lists and the tags; `tp_msgtriggers 0` turns them off.
- `mapgroup` and `skygroup` (`classic/content/map_groups.hpp`): a grouped map falls back to the
  group's place file, coloured light and replacement textures, and draws the group's sky box; the
  built-in `exmy` holds the first game's episodes for textures.
- `cl_timeout` (60 in Quake, 120 in Quake II, 0 never) and `rcon_password` (rcon then takes the
  whole line).
- Not taken: `tp_pointpriorities` (its own authors call it broken: a priority read off by one, one
  table for all three sets); trigger aliases are not restricted to a safe list of commands, since
  no ruleset of ours limits triggers yet; `rcon_address` (rcon with no server joined) waits for a
  socket opened without a game; `showpackets` and `showdrop` for the network round.
