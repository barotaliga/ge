A room level's grass (`plugins/rooms/grass.hpp`): a material whose surface reference starts with
`grass` names a recipe (`surface <material> grass <recipe>` in a level's text, or a cloned map's
texture-map `surface <texture> grass <recipe>` row). `level_grass` turns each distinct recipe into
a kind and records a fault for a recipe that does not read; `grow_on_brushes` takes the drawn,
solid brush faces of those materials whose outward normal is at least `kFloorUp` (0.8) up, and
`grow_on_rooms` a grown room's floors, into one `grass::Cover`.

- `tests/rooms_grass_test.cpp`: the vault grows on its grass floor's top alone (its area to a
  thousandth), one recipe is one kind and a bad one a fault naming the material, a grown room's
  floors grow and its walls and ceilings do not, a cloned fixture grows on the texture its map
  surfaces as grass, the same level grows the same blades on any worker count
  (`tests/golden/rooms_grass.txt`).
- Labs: `rooms` draws the vault's grass floor grown (`vault-grass`, with its kinds, triangles,
  area and blades as metrics); `clone` grows the textures `--grass` names as a lawn (the
  fixture's tiled side room by default) and draws the blades in both views.

Open beside it: a classic map drawn as it is (not cloned) picking its grass floors by
`detail::classify_surface` and handing the renderer a cover at load, if Shooter's Edge wants it.
