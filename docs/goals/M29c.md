changes with the clock, read in closed form off their integrals: new snow, settling, hollows
filled by the fall and the drift, melt and refreeze into spring snow, crust and ice, the crust's
bearing load (`crust_load`, broken through by a heavier press), and `groom` (mass kept to the
unit, the corduroy flag). `bring_to` keeps the mass ledger with the weather counted in. Held by
`tests/snow_time_test.cpp` (a 5 cm/h storm buries a ski groove in under three hours; a groomed
swath keeps its mass; a night's crust bears a boot and breaks under a skier carving at 2 g; no
weather reads the static layer bit for bit; the ledger). Lab: `snow-weather`.
