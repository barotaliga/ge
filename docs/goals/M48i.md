Milestone: **The quick weapon**: a fire key fires the best weapon carried by a priority list in the same command, then switches back to the weapon kept in hand; a second key with its own list; the weapon a press would fire previewed see-through in hand (the held weapon's opacity), or the hand hidden; generic in `plugins/shooter`, wired in Shooter's Edge as `cl_quickweapon*`
Exit: a tap fires one shot of the best weapon and puts the kept one back, on the listen server and against a laggy model server; the preview and the tap filmed on a real map
Labs: `se-quickweapon`

The rule is `ge::shooter::quick_step` (`plugins/shooter/quick_weapon.hpp`), any shooter's: it reads
which weapons are carried and loaded and the one in hand, and writes the attack and a weapon to ask
for. Shooter's Edge feeds it the HUD's status (`client/quick_fire.hpp`) in its own match and on a
server joined, so it needs nothing of the server. `tests/shooter_quick_weapon_test.cpp` holds it
against a model server that drops an ask while the weapon in hand reloads and shows the client its
state a round trip late; `tests/shooters_edge_quick_fire_test.cpp` against the baseline mod.

The preview is `render::HeldDraw::opacity`: a screen door in the opaque view-model pass, moved
each frame for the TAA-U to average, with a faint cool light of its own. Through a tap the hand
holds the quick weapon the whole time (`HeldState::show`): the ghost eases to solid
(`kSolidS`), holds (`kHoldS`) and eases back (`kGhostS`) while the kept weapon comes back unseen,
so no weapon switch (M48h's animation) is drawn; the `tap-chart` shot follows the opacity and how
far the hand is put away.
