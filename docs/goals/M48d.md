`games/shooters_edge/view` (`shooters_edge_view`, no GPU) holds Shooter's Edge's held weapons:

- `weapons.hpp` has the set in the order of its number keys. Each weapon has its held length,
  whether it is held upright (the melee weapon), its offset from the eye, its kick, its refire,
  and its muzzle light (colour, intensity, range, life). `load_weapon_models` loads the eight free
  models and mounts each one muzzle forward at its length. `weapon_row` is the engine's modern row
  with a quicker put-away.
- `hand.hpp` steps a display frame from what the game says (the weapon held, a shot fired):
  - On a weapon change it puts the old weapon away, swaps it at the bottom and raises the new one.
  - A shot during a switch snaps the new weapon up.
  - It hands the shot's kick to the placement.
  - It lights the muzzle (a point light in the world, through the gun's own lens) and fades it.
  - `add_view_cvars` binds `r_drawviewmodel`, `r_viewmodel_fov`, `cl_gunx`, `cl_guny`, `cl_gunz`
    and `cl_gunsway`, all the player's own. `held_row` puts them on top of a weapon's place.

`tests/shooters_edge_view_test.cpp` holds the set, the mounts, the switch, the shot's snap, the
flash's fade and the cvars.

The `se-viewmodel` lab draws on the engine's corridor stage (`viewmodel/stage.hpp`), a map made of
free parts. With the engine's one generic mount, the `viewmodel` lab drew the set too big and cut
by the picture's edge. Each weapon now covers 0.15 to 0.30 of the picture (stated 0.04 to 0.30),
with its muzzle on the screen. Every gun's shot lifts the dark picture by 10 to 24 levels; the
melee weapon has no flash and lifts it by 0.2. The switch takes 0.27 s from the change to the
new weapon up (stated at most 0.34 s).
