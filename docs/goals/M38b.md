`render/emitters.hpp`'s `AreaEmitter` and `emit_area` bear an area's particles (embers, motes,
drips, steam) for the particle pool, each birth seeded by the emitter's seed and its number, so
any split of the frames gives the same births. Fall Line's frame emits a scene's
`FrameScene::emitters`; the `fog` lab's `glow` scene on seed 38 at night puts 40 embers a second
over its camp fire (`embers_over` the glowing haze), 91 live in the picture, rising as orange
sparks over the flame against `--embers=0`. Drips falling from a ceiling at their rate are held
by `render_emitters_test` (40 in 10 s at 4 a second, each fallen as far as its flight gives);
Fall Line has no ceiling. Tests: `render_emitters_test` (drips at their rate, two spans as one,
embers rising, glowing and cooling, motes and steam, a long pause, `embers_over`).
