**How a weapon is built.** `content/models/view/<name>/` holds the asset (`asset.txt`, `build.py`,
the `.glb` and its stamp); `weapon_kit.py` beside them is the shared kit: bevelled parts with
hardened normals (`box`, `lathe`, `prism`, `loft`, `screw`), procedural Cycles materials
(`surface`: mottle, streaks, flat-sawn grain, knurling, hazard stripes, a polished zone, worn edges
by the bevel shader, cavity dirt by ambient occlusion, glow), the unwrap and a shelf packer into one
1024x512 texture with square texels, the bake to colour and glow, and the clips. The `se_view` data
set (`games/shooters_edge/assets/view_data.cpp`) feeds each builder the game's numbers.

**The body.** One pair of hands for every weapon (the left the right's mirror), coarse on purpose:
glove, cuff, sleeve. An arm is three rigid nodes jointed at the shoulder (a `torso` node, still
unless a clip moves the body), the elbow and the wrist. The shoulders are where the game's view has
them (`kShoulderM` from the eye; a long gun turns the body at rest, its stance, so the support hand
reaches the fore-end), every built weapon is drawn at `kBuiltScale` (0.5) so the hands are one size
everywhere, and the look's offset is where the grip hand holds.

**The joints keep to a person's ranges** (`engine/viewmodel/arm.hpp`, the one authority, with
`tests/viewmodel_arm_test.cpp`): the elbow a hinge of 0 to 145 degrees pointing down and out from
the line between shoulder and wrist (never up, never in across the body), the upper arm at most 25
above the shoulder, the wrist 70 back and 75 forward, 20 toward the thumb and 35 away, the forearm
95 either way. Each hand settles on its grip as a hand does: the kit tries every turn about the
grip's axis and, for a cradle, how steeply the grip crosses the palm, and keeps the hold whose arm
is most natural; then every frame of every clip the elbow is found on its circle inside the ranges
with each joint as near its easy band as the others allow. `shooters_edge_view_test` fails any built
weapon's frame outside a range; the lab's ARMS table and `arm-<w>` sheet show every joint.

**The staging lab.** `make lab LAB=se-weapon-stage ARGS="--weapon=axe --candidate=<file.glb>"`
draws the game's model and the candidate side by side: first person by day, torch, lamps and its
own flash; a bench from four sides; the wire; the texture with its glow; every clip as a strip,
a table of where the weapon's tip lands on the screen, and `<weapon>-<clip>.gif` and `.webm`. The
axe's chop: the tip from (0.52, 0.26) up to (0.63, 0.07) on the draw, the strike at (0.29, 0.58),
back at rest by 0.48 s.
