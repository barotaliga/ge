Status: in progress

Shooter's Edge's sound: the game's recordings played where they happen.

- **M18j1, sampled voices, spatial sound and the channel limit** (`engine/audio`): **done.**
  `pcm.hpp` reads WAV files (8- and 16-bit PCM, 32-bit float, the extensible header, any rate,
  more channels mixed to mono) with their loop from `smpl`, or `cue ` and its `ltxt` length, or
  `cue ` alone. The synth plays a `Pcm` as a sampled voice (`Synth::sample`): read at its own
  rate in 32.32 fixed point with cubic (Catmull-Rom) interpolation, pitched, looped by the file,
  by the whole or never, steered (`sample_set`: gain and pan on a glide), stopped with a fade,
  `kMaxSamples` (32) at once with the quietest stolen. `channels.hpp`: `hear` (a linear fall
  with distance, the pan off the listener's right) and `Channels` (an entity's numbered channel
  replaces, channel 0 stacks, a full table steals the quietest and drops what is quieter still,
  nothing out of earshot starts, the listener's own sounds are centred, moving sources
  followed). Held by `tests/audio_pcm_test.cpp`, `tests/audio_channels_test.cpp` and a sampled
  row in `tests/golden/audio_synth.txt`; pictured by the `samples` lab (rates, pitch, a cue's
  loop, a fly-by, the limit, the bench) and by `se-audio`, a scripted fight of the game's own
  WAVs heard by the player.
- **M18j2, ambient sounds by leaf**: open. Waits for the BSP reader on main (the map's leaves
  carry the ambient levels: water, sky, slime, lava), then a bed of four looped voices steered
  by the leaf the listener stands in.
- **M18j3, the game's sound messages**: open. Waits for the server and the client (M18f, M18h):
  the server's sound and stop-sound messages and the precached sound list replace the
  `se-audio` lab's script, and the lab grows a fight played by the bots.
