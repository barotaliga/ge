Shooter's Edge's sound: the game's recordings played where they happen.

- **M18j1, sampled voices, spatial sound and the channel limit** (`engine/audio`): **done.**
  `pcm.hpp` reads WAV files (8- and 16-bit PCM, 32-bit float, the extensible header, any rate,
  more channels mixed to mono) with their loop from `smpl`, or `cue ` and its `ltxt` length, or
  `cue ` alone. The synth plays a `Pcm` as a sampled voice (`Synth::sample`): read at its own
  rate in 32.32 fixed point with cubic (Catmull-Rom) interpolation, pitched, looped by the file,
  by the whole or never, steered (`sample_set`: gain and pan on a glide), stopped with a fade,
  `kMaxSamples` (32) at once with the quietest stolen. `channels.hpp`: `hear` (a linear fall
  with distance, the pan off the listener's right) and `Channels` (an entity's numbered channel
  replaces, channel 0 stacks, a full table steals the quietest and drops what is quieter still,
  nothing out of earshot starts, the listener's own sounds are centred, moving sources
  followed). Held by `tests/audio_pcm_test.cpp`, `tests/audio_channels_test.cpp` and a sampled
  row in `tests/golden/audio_synth.txt`; pictured by the `samples` lab (rates, pitch, a cue's
  loop, a fly-by, the limit, the bench) and by `se-audio`, a scripted fight of the game's own
  WAVs heard by the player.
- **M18j2, ambient sounds by leaf**: **done.** `brush::Leaf::ambient` carries the four levels
  (water, sky, slime, lava, 0..255) the 29 family and BSP2 store per leaf, and the fixture
  writer writes a room's (`FixtureRoom::ambient`; `tests/brush_read_test.cpp`).
  `audio::AmbientBed` (`engine/audio/ambient.hpp`) plays looped layers whose levels walk to
  their targets at a fixed rate, a target under its floor counting as silence and a silent
  layer holding no voice (`tests/audio_ambient_test.cpp`). Shooter's Edge's
  `shooters_edge::Ambience` (`games/shooters_edge/sound/ambience.hpp`) sets the water and sky
  layers off the listener's leaf times the ambient volume
  (`tests/shooters_edge_ambience_test.cpp`); the `se-ambient` lab walks the fixture halls from
  the sky into the water and back. Slime and lava are read and not played: the classic
  content has no recordings for them.
- **M18j3, the game's sound messages**: in progress. **Heard in the window (done):** a joined
  server's sounds, printed lines and centre messages play (`net/classic_remote.hpp` reads them
  as a client recording); the window's own game, on its listen server or the campaign host, keeps
  each sound the rules start for the player and the map's loops from its load
  (`Listen::sounds`, `static_sounds`) and plays them through the demo's players
  (`client/listen_sound.hpp`: the pack's recordings, channels, the fall with distance, the pan,
  the ambience by leaf), held by `tests/shooters_edge_listen_test.cpp` (the launcher's sounds
  heard and played, a campaign map's loop from its load). **The built-in arena heard (done):** with
  no classic game's folder open, the game's own recordings stand in for the classic names the
  baseline mod plays, by `content/classic.assetmap`'s `sound` rows, packed in memory and mounted
  (`client/own_sounds.hpp`; never over a classic game's folder); before, the arena was silent.
  Held by `tests/shooters_edge_own_sounds_test.cpp` (the map read, the recordings mounted, the
  arena's bots heard: 124 sounds in 12 s, none missing) and the `se-audio` lab's `bots` fight beside
  its script. Left: the stop-sound message (read in every client path; our server never sends it,
  since a mod stops a sound by playing another on its channel).
