The detail passes in the GPU frame and on real levels: the decal atlas (`bake_atlas`) and the
pool's vertices drawn by a decal pass with a depth bias, blended into the surface's albedo and
gloss before lighting; the props drawn through `render/instances` from `prop_mesh`; bevels and
props applied on load to the room format's brushes and the BSP families' (adapters from a room
brush to a `detail::Solid` and from a built level to a `detail::TriMesh`, a per-material bevel
radius and a no-props flag asked of the level format's owner); decals placed by a game's events;
and the pak0 pipeline (classic-enhance) calling them for the shareware maps.
