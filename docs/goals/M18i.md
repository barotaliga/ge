M18i is the picture: the world pass, the models, sprites, particles, dynamic lights, the screen
blends and the two looks. It is built in parts, and each part builds only on what is on main.

| Part | What | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M18i1 | Morph meshes (`engine/render/morph.hpp`): vertex-animated models with frames blended on the GPU, team colours from tint ramps, glowing texels, sharp or smooth skins, a game's own light; the `gpu-morph` lab | done |
| M18i2 | Sprites (`engine/render/sprites.hpp`): frames in one atlas, five facing rules, cutout or blended (over, add), glow, sharp or smooth; the `gpu-sprites` lab | done |
| M18i3 | The classic model and sprite readers feeding these passes, with the palette's colour rows as the tint ramps: every model, sprite and brush item the server lists drawn in the window where its entities stand, lit by the floor, players in their colours; torches, flames and the two powers in the baseline mod; the `se-models` lab | done |
| M18i4 | The models lit by the frame's dynamic lights (a muzzle's flash, a rocket's glow, an explosion, a power's glow) as the floor under them is, from the light's side (`effects::dynamic_light_at`, `client/world_models.cpp`); the classic sprites light themselves, as the pack draws them; `player-glow` in `se-models` | done |
| M18i6 | The map's moving brush models (doors, plats, buttons) drawn where the server holds them: each model's run of vertices offset in the world pass's vertex shader and its cull boxes moved with it, a model the game removed (a secret wall) hidden; door and lift pictures in `se-models` | done |
| M18i5 | The world pass (light styles as atlas layers and a uniform, animated textures, the PVS then two-phase Hi-Z cull compacted in order), the effects on the engine's particles, dynamic lights, the screen's blend and both looks, in `se-world` | done |
| M18i7 | A player's warps: arriving (a spawn, a respawn, a teleport's far end) and leaving (a teleport's near end) read off the players' moves (`client/warps.hpp`); the figure crumbling to ash or built out of it by the morph pass (`MorphWarp`, shared with the view-model pass so a spawn assembles the gun) and its flakes thrown off the skin where the cut passes or blown in to land on it (`app/effects.hpp`'s dust), each flake on its own way; the local player's arrival seen through his eyes; all of it behind `ce_warps` in the modern look, the original (the teleport's fog, the figure there or gone at once) when it is off or the look is classic; the `gpu-morph` warp view, `se-models`' warp view and `se-client --game --warp` (enhanced beside original) | done |
