Brush detail for any game built of brushes (`engine/detail`, `docs/detail.md`): bevels on exposed
sharp edges, decals that age, detail props scattered by rules read off a level's shape, and the
marks a fight's events leave.

M42a, M42b and M42c are done on the CPU with their labs; M42d (bullet holes from shots, blood by
the health a hit took, a scorch's embers) is done with `detail-hits`. Next: M42e (the GPU frame's
decal pass and the props as instances) and M42f (real levels on load, receivers a BSP model each,
and the games' and demos' events calling `shot_hit` and `bleed`).
