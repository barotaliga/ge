Brush detail for any game built of brushes (`engine/detail`, `docs/detail.md`): bevels on exposed
sharp edges, decals that age, and the marks a fight's events leave.

M42a and M42b are done on the CPU with their labs; M42d (bullet holes from shots, blood by the
health a hit took, a scorch's embers) is done with `detail-hits`. The seeded props (rubble, debris
and moss, once M42c) and the floor clutter by surface (once M42g) were taken out again on
2026-11-14: a classic map's floors stay as their maker left them. Next: M42e (the GPU frame's decal
pass) and M42f (real levels on load, receivers a BSP model each, and the games' and demos' events
calling `shot_hit` and `bleed`).
