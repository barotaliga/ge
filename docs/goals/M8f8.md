Status: in progress

Fifteen cloud kinds (`render/cloud_kinds.hpp`), up to four layers a frame, each with its form (anvil, erode, fibre, patch) in a fourth row of the frame block and its light (its particles' radius and spread, its halo, its deck) in a fifth and its thickness (its thinnest patches' share, a patch's span, the sky's lean) in a sixth, so the sun and the moon shine through a sheet's thin patches, melt into a smudge in its thicker parts and are hidden behind its cores (`sv_thick` picks it by hand); the billows off a cellular noise in the noise texture's second channel; sheets lit by diffusion, a deck through its top; the 22 degree halo off the ice kinds; the glow the sun and the moon shine through a layer with, diffracted round its particles (the aureole, and the corona's rings off even droplets), the moon's seen beside its disc as the disc is; the thin `veil` of ice; storm anvils and mammatus; the shafts; the lid weathers standing a sheet under their deck; Fall Line's `sv_clouds` and the `world` lab's `clouds` and `veil` views (and `--day` for a full moon). Matched in rounds against reference photographs of every kind, side by side (the photographs are temporary and never committed).

Next: what the rounds left (a moonlit altostratus deck is dark where it is thick, worth a look against night photographs; a storm seen side-on from far off, mammatus that reads, a whole-sky sunset glow, texture in a nimbostratus deck, the cell pattern a telephoto lens shows in altocumulus), then a cloud tint knob per sky type if one reads wrong.
