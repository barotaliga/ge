Status: done
Milestone: **The HUD as data** (`engine/hud`, `docs/hud.md`): elements (an id, a kind, its default place, its shape) in a table the game declares; their content written by index once a frame; every element's place as `hud_` cvars bound to its fields (`show`, `anchor`, `x`, `y` as shares of the safe area, `scale`, `alpha`), `hud_scale`, `hud_alpha`, `hud_margin`; the game's defaults its table, the player's with `seta`, `hud_reset`; Fall Line's M10a HUD re-expressed as elements
Exit: Fall Line's HUD drawn from its element table gives M10a's view's draw commands bit for bit; a cfg that moves, hides or scales one element changes that element alone; edit, `cfg_save`, a fresh start and `exec` bring the same boxes back
Labs: `hud-layout` (the game's layout and a player's cfgs, each at the three viewports)

Built: `engine/hud/layout.hpp` (`ge::hud::Layout`: the element table, each element's place, the
content a game writes once a frame, the stacks and the two clearance rules, `under_corners` and
`under`, and `add_hud_cvars`: every element's `hud_<id>_show`, `_anchor`, `_x`, `_y`, `_scale`,
`_alpha`, the whole HUD's `hud_scale`, `hud_alpha`, `hud_margin`, and `hud_reset`), the 2D
layer's `DrawList::fade`, Fall Line's HUD re-declared as thirteen elements
(`games/fall_line/hud/view.hpp`: `hud_layout`, `fill_hud`), and the `hud-layout` lab
(`games/fall_line/labs/hud_layout_lab.cpp`).

Measured: Fall Line's HUD drawn from its table gives the same draw commands, bit for bit, as
M10a's hand-placed view for twelve snapshots (every machine, prompt and hunt state) at 1280x720,
390x844, 844x390, 1920x1080, 360x640 and 2560x1080. At a square 700x700 it differs on purpose:
M10a's air clock dropped under the corners only on an upright screen and overlapped the seed's
column there; `under_corners` drops it wherever it would meet them, and
tests/fall_line_hud_test.cpp now holds 700x700 too. The `hud-layout` lab lays the game's layout
out clean at the three viewports, follows a player's five `seta` lines, and `hud_reset` puts
every box back. tests/hud_layout_test.cpp holds the stacking, the cvars, the clearance rules, the
fade, `seta` and `hud_reset`, and the `cfg_save` round trip into a fresh console.

Changed from the plan: the game's defaults are its element table rather than a shipped
`hud.cfg` (a game may still `set` lines of its own); per-element content replaces a registry of
named channels; `hud_<id>_skin` and `hud_face` come with M10e. The bar's caption still sits
under an empty value line, as in M10a, so a bar with a caption reads a line lower than it needs
to; fixing that moves Fall Line's pixels and is left for a row that may.

Next: M10e (skins) or M10f (the instruments), which can run side by side; the app wires the
layout's cvars into its console with M10b.
