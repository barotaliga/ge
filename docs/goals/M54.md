Status: in progress
Milestone: **Models' shadows in a brush world: moving models (players, monsters, the first-person player unseen) cast shadows from every shadowed light through cubes drawn each frame beside the world's kept ones, soft or sharp by each light's size and distance (PCSS)**
Exit: The player standing between a torch and the floor throws his shadow ahead of him, soft from a big flame and sharp from a small lamp, in se-models
Labs: `se-models`

Built (draft, branch se-model-shadows), in plugins/brush/app (bsp_draw.*, bsp.wgsl, light_grid.hpp)
and Shooter's Edge's client (world_models.cpp `shadow_casters`, world_fx `FxFrame::self`,
world_draw):

- The casters: every alias model within 24 m of the eye and the first-person player (never drawn,
  still casting), posed on the CPU as world triangles plus a box each (`set_shadow_casters`).
- Caster cubes: the kCasterLights (8) shadowed lights that light a caster most, weighted by the
  caster's nearness to the eye, each get a cube of the casters alone, drawn every frame with the
  world's shadow shader (`p_caster`, 384 a face), read beside the world's kept cube.
- PCSS (`soft_shadow`): a blocker search, then a penumbra of size * (d - d_blocker) / d_blocker,
  Vogel taps turned per pixel and frame (TAA resolves them), across both cubes. A light's source
  radius is `GpuLight::more.y`: a plain light 4 cm (sharp), a flame a quarter of its height
  (wall torch 0.14 m, large flame 0.26 m, bonfire 0.5 m). The world's cube alone gets 4 taps
  over size / d.
- Capsules (engine/render/wgsl/capsules.wgsl): the four casters nearest the eye as vertical
  capsules from their boxes shadow every light that has no caster cube, softened by the light's
  size, within 6 m of them. Without this, a classic map's many fill lights (the nearest 32 shadowed
  lights carry only 5 to 20% of a floor's light on e1m4) leave no visible shadow.
- Indirect: the bounce and the r_minlight floor are dimmed by 0.8 of the share of the casters'
  lights hidden at the pixel (`kIndirectShadow`).
- se-models: views `shadow-lamp`, `shadow-torch`, `shadow-flame` (eyes and side) and the measured
  `<id>-darker`, after 12 warm-up frames (the cubes are dealt a few a frame). Use
  `--pak=<pak0> --map=e1m4 --progs=games/shooters_edge/qc/campaign/progs.src`.

Measured on e1m4 (M3 Max): the floor's deepest darkening 25 to 38%. The cost is too high so far:
se-timedemo on the pack's demo1 (modern look) went from 3.3 ms GPU median on main to 10 ms.
Next: find the cost (`se-timedemo --price=1 --price-only=shadows` takes over 10 min; time the
caster passes against the shading), fewer caster lights or smaller faces, capsules only for the
nearest lights, the sun's orthographic map with the casters, then lint, precheck and pictures.
