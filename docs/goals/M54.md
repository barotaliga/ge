Status: done
Milestone: **Models' shadows in a brush world: moving models (players, monsters, the first-person player unseen) cast shadows from every shadowed light through cubes drawn each frame beside the world's kept ones, soft or sharp by each light's size and distance (PCSS)**
Exit: The player standing between a torch and the floor throws his shadow ahead of him, soft from a big flame and sharp from a small lamp, in se-models
Labs: `se-models`

Built in plugins/brush/app (bsp_draw.*, bsp.wgsl, casters.*, light_grid.hpp)
and Shooter's Edge's client (world_models.cpp `shadow_casters`, world_fx `FxFrame::self`,
world_draw):

- The casters: every alias model within 24 m of the eye and the first-person player (never drawn,
  still casting), posed on the CPU as world triangles plus a box each (`set_shadow_casters`).
- Caster cubes: the kCasterLights (8) shadowed lights that light a caster most, weighted by the
  caster's nearness to the eye, each get a cube of the casters alone, drawn every frame with the
  world's shadow shader (`p_caster`, 384 a face), read beside the world's kept cube. The 48 faces
  are one atlas (a light a row, a face a column) drawn in ONE pass, a viewport a face: as 48
  passes they cost about 6 ms of GPU a frame on the M3 Max whatever they drew (empty passes cost
  the same; the casters are only 2k to 7k vertices), as one pass 0.09 ms.
- PCSS (`soft_shadow`): a blocker search in the casters' cube alone, then a penumbra of
  size * (d - d_blocker) / d_blocker, Vogel taps (constant tables, turned by one spin a pixel and
  frame the TAA resolves) on the casters' cube; a point no figure stands before pays for the
  search only. A light's source radius is `GpuLight::more.y`: a plain light 4 cm (sharp), a flame
  a quarter of its height (wall torch 0.14 m, large flame 0.26 m, bonfire 0.5 m). The world's kept
  cube stays one 2 x 2 compare, as on main (a 4-tap soft filter on it cost 1.2 ms at 1440p).
- Capsules (engine/render/wgsl/capsules.wgsl): the four casters nearest the eye as vertical
  capsules from their boxes shadow every light that has no caster cube, softened by the light's
  size, within 6 m of them (the near ones found once a pixel, not a light). Without this, a classic map's many fill lights (the nearest 32 shadowed
  lights carry only 5 to 20% of a floor's light on e1m4) leave no visible shadow.
- Indirect: the bounce and the r_minlight floor are dimmed by 0.8 of the share of the casters'
  lights hidden at the pixel (`kIndirectShadow`).
- The sun: a row more of the atlas, its first tile the casters along the sun, orthographic, 16 m
  round the eye, drawn in the same pass; a pixel in it multiplies the sun's map by it
  (`sun_casters_lit`). `world_sun` gives a lab the sun a frame lights with.
- se-models: views `shadow-lamp`, `shadow-torch`, `shadow-flame` (e1m4) and `shadow-sun` (e1m1,
  noon), each from his eyes (one to three metres off the light, open level floor ahead) and from
  high to one side (a clear place on either side), `-side-pair` (that view with him and with him
  gone, side by side), `-shade` (red where he darkens, eight times over) and the metrics `-darker`
  (the lower half's mean) and `-deepest` (the 99th percentile), each state drawn a dozen frames so
  the resolve holds none of the other. Use `--pak=<pak0> --map=e1m4 (or e1m1)
  --progs=games/shooters_edge/qc/campaign/progs.src`.
- Tests: `tests/brush_bsp_casters_test.cpp` (the light pick and the capsules, `casters.hpp`).

Measured (M3 Max), `-deepest` / `-darker`: on e1m4 the lamp 59% / 8.8%, the torch 56% / 5.6%, the
large flame 25% / 4.0% (soft, as a big source gives); the sun at noon 76% / 6.1% on e1m4 and
51% / 12% on e1m1. The first figures (9 to 19% deepest) were taken with the power-ups the
`powered` view hands the player still on him: their glow lit the floor round him, drawn or not,
and washed his shadow out. The shadow views now take the powers back first. The `-side-pair`
pictures show the shadow against the same floor with him gone; the shade pictures show it ahead
from his feet, crisp under the lamp, soft under the large flame.
