Status: open (planned, nothing built yet)

Precipitation for any game: snow, sleet and rain from none through light, moderate and heavy to a
storm, carried by the wind, tied to the sky's weather.

What exists to extend (not duplicate): `engine/render/snowfall.hpp` (flakes in a wrapped box round
the eye, flown on the wind, drawn as streaks; `gpu_snowfall_lab`), `engine/wind` (the wind field),
`plugins/mountain/air/air.hpp` (`fall_at`: the fall's squalls and visibility; `wind_at`),
`plugins/mountain/mapgen/weather.hpp` (`WeatherKind`, `with_sky`), Fall Line's `r_snowfall`,
`sv_weather`, `sv_snowfall`, `sv_wind` (`games/fall_line/cfg/cvars.cpp`).

Plan:

- M36a, the air's half (`engine/wind/precip.hpp`, no GPU, golden): intensity tiers named by rate
  (light under 2.5 mm/h, moderate to 7.6, heavy to 50, storm past it); the phase at a height from
  the freezing level (snow above, a melting band of sleet, rain below); fall speeds (rain by drop
  size, Atlas's fit, the size off Marshall-Palmer by rate; snow about 1 m/s; sleet 2-3 m/s);
  visibility through rain by rate; a wetness integrator (wets with rain, dries with wind and sun;
  equilibrium on a rewind); turbulence that swirls particles by the wind's gusts.
- M36b, the picture (`engine/render/snowfall` generalised to every kind): rain drops as long thin
  streaks, sleet mixed by a share, the storm's swirl; screen-space splashes reconstructed off the
  depth on upward-facing surfaces; a frame uniform for wetness read by a `wet_surface` helper in
  `wgsl/frame.wgsl` (darker albedo, a sky-reflecting sheen, slush on snow), used by the snow and
  instance materials.
- M36c, Fall Line: `rain` and `sleet` skies picked by hand (never dealt, so no digest moves), a
  snow line (`sv_snowline`), cvars `r_precip`, `r_precip_density`, `r_splashes`, `r_wet`; the lab
  `precip` (charts, CPU) and `gpu_precip` (views from flurries to a rain storm), before and after
  on an Artifact page.
- Later: rain rings on water (M24g2), a rain occlusion map under roofs (Shooter's Edge's rooms),
  the sound of rain, the snow a fall lays (M29).
