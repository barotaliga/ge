Status: in progress
Milestone: **Classic remix mode**: a feature of classic-enhance itself (its switch in classic-enhance's own menu, not a separate mode of the game), in which the player plays one classic game with parts of the others they own: the classic multiplayer game's maps with the sequel's weapons and items, its armour, its player models, or another game's maps under one game's rules and movement, each part chosen on its own. The mode is ONE switch, shown only when at least two classic games are loaded as FULL games (their registered packs; a shareware pack never counts, nor does a game with only its shareware pack); off by default, so a classic game loaded on its own uses only its own assets (never Shooter's Edge's, and never another game's)
Exit: The switch hidden with one full game, or with any number of shareware packs, and shown with two full games; a remix chosen in the menu and by cvars (each part's source game), refused at load with the missing pack named when a part cannot resolve; the classic multiplayer game's map played by bots with the sequel's weapons and items, and separately its armour and player models, each with a golden digest; the same map with remix off drawing and playing exactly as before (digests unchanged)
Labs: `se-remix`

Builds on the classic game profiles (M18t): each game's adapter answers for its own files, and the
remix mode is the one place that asks two adapters at once. It needs the sequel's profile
(M18t3, M18t4), the remix check at load (M18t5) and the arsenal as data (M18t6: weapons and
armour as rows a map's item names map onto by slot). Every part goes through the games' adapters, the HUD and the main menu too (each game's own
HUD and menu pages, chosen like any other part), and a game's mods stay its own (each
game's adapter lists its own mod folders and finds its own programs; a remix takes one game's
mod as its rules), and both the multiplayer and the single-player games can be remixed, except the third game's, which has no single-player campaign to remix. Player
models and HUD pictures follow the same rule: a part is taken whole from one game, so a borrowed weapon brings its own models,
sounds and HUD icon from its own game's packs.

The sequel's profile has landed (M18v), and the maintainer asked for the first two parts
(2026-11-13): another game's maps, their items made the played game's, and another game's items
on a game's own maps with that game's HUD. The switch is the setting `cr_enabled` (0 by default),
which does nothing unless two classic games are loaded as full games; `cr_items` names whose items
a map is played with (0 the played game's, 1 the classic game's, 2 the sequel's). The parts and
their order:

- **M18u1, the item table and the settings**: **landed**: each game's map items as the other's,
  by role (`profiles/remix.hpp`: a weapon by its place in the set, an ammunition by its weapon,
  health and armour by size, a powerup by what it does; the variant bits replaced, the skill and
  mode bits kept, an item with no match left out and counted), the entity text rewritten
  (`client/remix.hpp`'s `remix_entities`), `cr_enabled` and `cr_items`, and the check that wants a
  second full game (`remix_check`, its refusal in the game's words). Lab `se-remix` draws a real
  map of each game from above with its own items and with the other game's.
- **M18u2, the sequel's maps in the classic game**: **landed**: a map found only in the other
  game's packs played under the classic game's rules, its items the classic game's: the classic
  rules tracing a version 38 map's models through their brushes (`sh::Server`'s model solids),
  the other game's packs under the played game's for the map and its wall textures
  (`RemixFiles`), its palette its own. The weapons are balanced against the rest of the map
  (`RemixBalance`: `cr_strong_min` strong weapons at least, `cr_strong_share` of them at most,
  the best weak one raised or a strong one lowered; `cr_strong_same` copies of one strong weapon at most, the copy nearest a strong armour lowered first), and the sequel's small health and
  armour shards are gathered by `cr_pool_radius` into the classic game's packs by the total they
  hold. Lab `se-remix` surveys fourteen real maps (their weapons by role, the balance's steps,
  the items with no match); `se-client --remix=<install> --map=<the other game's map>` draws one
  in the window's frame.
- **M18u3, the classic game's maps in the sequel**: **landed**: the sequel's rules on a version
  29 map, its point tree's cells made brushes (`brush/cells.hpp`: one brush a path to a non-empty
  leaf, the planes that bound nothing left out, axial bevels added) so the brush trace answers on
  it, its items the sequel's, its textures read as its own game stores them. Every rewritten item
  keeps the middle of its floor (`profiles::item_foot`: the games measure an item's place from
  different points of its box), so it no longer starts inside the floor and vanishes.
- **M18u4, the other game's items on a game's own maps**: `cr_items 2` on the classic game's maps
  (the sequel's arsenal rows into the classic rules: its weapons' models, sounds and pickups) and
  the sequel's HUD drawn over the classic stats; `cr_items 1` on the sequel's maps, the classic
  HUD over the sequel's. In parts:
  - **M18u4a, the classic rules in the sequel's weapons and armour**: **landed**: the classic
    rules keep their numbers and the map its class names, and wear the sequel's things from the
    next map on (`profiles/remix_arsenal.hpp`, by role as the item table: the nailguns the
    machinegun and chaingun, the beam the railgun): each weapon's view model in the hand (its
    idle frame the rest, its firing frames the shot), its pickup on the floor, its missile, its
    fire, bounce and blast sounds, and the three armours' models and pickup sounds; another
    game's model keeps its skin's own colours. `se-client --remix-items=1` draws it.
  - **M18u4b, the sequel's bar, boxes and pickup sounds**: **landed**: the sequel's own status
    bar program run over the classic status (`client/remix_bar.hpp`: health, the ammunition of
    the weapon in hand as the sequel's weapon holds it, the armour by class, the weapon just
    picked up, a power-up's seconds, the frags), its pictures from the remix's files; the classic
    rules' ammunition and health boxes in the sequel's models and pickup sounds and a weapon's
    pickup sound the sequel's (`arsenal_ammo<n>`, `arsenal_health<n>`, `arsenal_pickup`: the
    model about the box's middle, raised as far as that game's items stand over the floor).
  - **M18u4c**: `cr_items 1` on the sequel's rules, the classic game's things on its numbers.
