Status: in progress

Touch and motion, the engine's half and Fall Line's thumbs.

- **Touch controls** (`engine/input/touch.hpp`):
  - A trailing stick, whose centre follows a thumb past its reach.
  - A lever with a second range under its first, past a dead band: a brake under a throttle,
    read as `touch:<lever>+` and `-`.
  - Gestures that watch another control and read as buttons: a flick up (pressed for longer the
    faster it was) and a tap-and-hold.
- **The gyro's bias** (`engine/input/gyro.hpp`): taken while the device is still, never on a
  slow steady turn, and taken off every reading before the actions read it.
- **Fall Line's thumbs** (`games/fall_line/controls/thumbs.hpp`):
  - The edge thumb steers.
  - The tuck lever: full tuck under the thumb, standing tall below it, the skid below that.
  - The jump: a flick or a tap-and-hold on the lever's thumb.
  - Reset and camera buttons.
  - Bound by the default bindings; the window reads the screen's shape into them.

Measured:

- `by-hand` (seed 38, whole race): the bot's run played on two thumbs as a device session.
  - Every step read back what the thumbs gave: 0 off.
  - The thumbs ask other than the bot on 1 step, the edge thumb's landing step.
  - The race finished in 29,064 steps, 301 after the bot's, and its log replays.
  - The bot's run has no jumps, so the gestures are held by the tests instead.
- `input-session`: a pad with a bias of (0.021, -0.034, 0.013) rad/s and a little noise.
  - It settles 0.8 s into rest, off by 0.0004 rad/s.
  - It holds through a 0.6 rad/s turn.
  - At rest the unbiased reading averages -0.00009 rad/s.
- Tests:
  - `tests/input_touch_test.cpp`: the trail, the brake range and its bindings, the flick, the
    tap-and-hold.
  - `tests/input_gyro_test.cpp`: settling, the turn, a slow turn never settling, an emptied slot.
  - `tests/fall_line_controls_test.cpp`: the thumbs through the defaults; the bot's run on the
    thumbs.

Not done here:

- **The phone's haptics**: the felt pulses reaching a phone's own motor. That is platform glue on
  the device: Android's vibrator through SDL's haptics, iOS through the thinnest native shim.
  It lands with the store shells (M11) and needs a phone to check.
- **The thumbs drawn by the HUD** (the reach rings, the lever): M10.
- **Gyro aiming in a game**: Fall Line has no aim. The bias is ready for Shooter's Edge's look
  (M18) and any machine that steers by tilt.
