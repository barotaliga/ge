`plugins/mountain/app/building_look.hpp` lights the village's windows at night. Every look comes
twice: first with its windows dark, and again with the glass a lamp-lit room behind it (warm,
`kWindowNits` = 5 cd/m²). A building wears the lit copy of its look (`kLitSets` added to its
material) while `lights_on` says so:

- The sun must be at least 2° under the horizon (`kLightsOnElevation`), so no window is lit by day.
- `lights_share(kind, hour)` gives the share of a kind's buildings lit at the sky's solar hour,
  linear between points through the day:
  - homes: up before the lifts open, home at dusk, in bed by midnight;
  - the lodge: from breakfast until the restaurant closes;
  - the hotels: the rooms in the evening, then the night porters;
  - the ticket office, the rental, the school and the shops: open until the evening;
  - first aid: a lamp kept on all night;
  - the garage: while the groomers are out after the lifts close;
  - the pump house: the snow guns' night shift;
  - the church: the evening service.
- Each building has its own fixed draw. It is lit while the share is over that draw, so as the
  share falls the village goes dark one building at a time and never flickers.

`games/fall_line/app/frame.cpp` decides each frame which buildings are lit, from the sky's hour
(`FrameScene::hour`, which the window passes from `sim::sky_hour`) and the real sun's elevation.
It rewrites a building's two instances only when its windows change.

The `village-look` lab adds three views and two metrics:

- `evening`: the square at 18:30.
- `night`: the village from above at 22:00.
- `late`: the village from above at 02:00.
- `lit-at-22` and `lit-at-2`: on seed 38, 37 and 7 buildings.

The lit glass's brightness was tuned by eye in those views. The exposure follows the sky, so at
dusk anything over about 10 cd/m² clips to white and loses its warmth.
