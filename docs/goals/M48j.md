Status: done
Milestone: **Weapon groups**: with `cl_weapongroups 1` a number key picks from a group of weapons instead of one weapon, the best ready first and the next on a second press; the groups the game's own by default and each one set by `cl_weapongroup<n>`; a HUD column that opens on the key to the group's weapons, folds into the weapon in hand on the attack or 2 s later, and marks a weapon whose group holds another; generic in `plugins/shooter`, wired in Shooter's Edge, the sequel's own groups beside the classic game's
Exit: a number key takes its group's best weapon and the next on the listen server; the column opens, folds and marks on a real map
Labs: `se-weapongroups`

The rule is `ge::shooter::group_press` and `group_look` (`plugins/shooter/weapon_groups.hpp`), any
shooter's: it reads which weapons are carried and loaded and the one in hand, and asks for a weapon
by its key, so it needs nothing of the server. Shooter's Edge reads the number key's `impulse` as a
group with `cl_weapongroups 1` (`client/weapon_groups.hpp`) before the quick weapon sees it, and
lays the look on the HUD's status for the `wgroup` element (`hud/layout.cpp`). Each game's groups
are its profile's `weapon_groups` (`profiles/game_profile.hpp`): the classic game's 1 the
launchers, 2 the beam gun, 3 the shotguns, 4 the nailguns, 5 the melee weapon; the sequel's by its
own keys, used once its window picks weapons. `tests/shooter_weapon_groups_test.cpp` holds the rule,
`tests/shooters_edge_weapon_groups_test.cpp` the cvars, the HUD column and the keys on the listen
server. `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Weapon groups has the cvars.
