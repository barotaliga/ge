`plugins/brush/rebake.{hpp,cpp}` turns a map into a bake's scene (its faces charted, its sky faces
open to the sky, its liquids glowing up out of their surfaces, lamps sunk in a pool raised over
it), runs `bake::bake_scene` (`rebake_map`) and keeps the result as RGB9E5 irradiance and an RGBA8
direction a luxel (`write_rebaked`, `read_rebaked`). `plugins/brush/app/draw_data` gives the bake
its inputs (`texture_means`, `bake_lights`) and the draw its second lightmap UV (`attach_rebake`,
`DrawVertex::lm2`); the v2 renderer's `Lighting::rebaked` shades with it, the textures' relief lit
by the stored direction in `bsp.wgsl`. Held by `tests/brush_rebake_test.cpp`; pictured by
`gpu-bsp --rebake=0.25` (the fixture map, and the shareware maps where a pack is installed).
