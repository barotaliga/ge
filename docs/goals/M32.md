Status: in progress

Level formats, in four rows. M32a (the engine's own room format, its bytes and text form, and
rooms grown from their seeds with eight reliefs and caves) is done; M32b (the classic BSP families:
version 29 and BSP2, 38 and 46, with the `bsp` lab) is next, then M32c (rooms in a world: the
mover's trace, textures, the GPU) and M32d (more that grows). The design is `docs/levels.md`.
