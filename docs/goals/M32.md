Status: in progress

Level formats, in four rows. M32a (the engine's own room format, its bytes and text form, and
rooms grown from their seeds with eight reliefs and caves) and M32b (the classic BSP families:
version 29 and BSP2, 38 and 46, read into one view, with fixture writers and the `bsp` lab) are
done; M32c (rooms in a world: the mover's trace, textures, the GPU) is next, then M32d (more that
grows). The design is `docs/levels.md`.
