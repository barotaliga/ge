Built: `games/shooters_edge/menu/` (`shooters_edge_menu`): `main_menu.hpp` is the model, a table
of rows (`kEntries`), each naming the cvar or command it needs, so a row is dimmed while the
console lacks it and lights up the day it lands; a setting is its cvar (the client's, or the
listen server's for NEW GAME, run as `sv <cvar> <value>` by START before the map).
`classic_art.hpp` reads the pack's menu pictures (`gfx/*.lmp`, the glyph sheet in `gfx.wad`, the
palette) off the search path, and `classic_view.hpp` draws the model on the classic 320 x 200
screen in them; `game_menu.hpp` is what the window holds: ESCAPE, `togglemenu`, `menu_look`
(auto, classic, modern), the presses and the pointer, and classic-enhance's menu (ENHANCE run on a
thread, the switches) under OPTIONS. `sensitivity` and `m_invert` became cvars
(`client/controls.hpp`), and the client gained `map_names` (`client/files.hpp`).

Measured: the `se-menu` lab draws every page in both looks at 1280x720, 1920x1080 and 390x844
(the classic look at the landscape sizes) with no layout problem; with `--pack` the classic look
again in the player's own pack. tests/shooters_edge_menu_test.cpp holds the pages, the dimming,
the cvars, START, ESCAPE and both looks.

The title and loads behind the window: with no `+map` the window opens at once on the title
(`client/title.hpp`: a night over no map, the full moon low in a total eclipse, blood red, two
decks of cloud carried across it, the air tinted red) with the main menu up, while the start map
loads behind it; ESCAPE out of the menu enters it once it is in. Every map now loads behind the
window (`client/map_load.hpp`: the file and the world's CPU half on threads, the GPU half begun on
the window's thread and its pipelines polled by `PipelineSet::ready` while frames go on), the
spinner (`engine/hud/spinner.hpp`) in the corner with LOADING and the map's name and the seconds
so far; the last map keeps drawing while the same map's world is made again (a setting changed,
an enhance run that wrote something). The first frame comes about 0.6 s after launch (it was about
12 s: the world made twice on the window's thread). The `se-title` lab draws the title, the
cloud a few seconds on, and the spinner. Left on the window's thread: `begin_world`'s uploads,
about 0.22 s once a load on the M3 Max (the exit line's "the longest" frame).

DEMOS (on the root, between MULTIPLAYER and OPTIONS; `menu_demos`): `menu/demos_page.hpp` over the
engine's folder browser (`engine/vfs/browse.hpp`) walks any folder on the machine (the game's
folder, home, Downloads, Desktop, Documents, the roots; `menu_demo_dir` keeps the last one played
from) and lists the folders and the game's recordings, plain or packed (`.mvd.gz`, `.dem.zip`,
`.mvd.7z`, `.mvd.tar.gz`, `.mvd.xz`, `.mvdz`), newest first; an archive with no recording's name
under its packing (`maps.zip`) is not listed. ENTER checks the file before anything starts
(`client/demo_check.hpp`: read, unpacked, its frames read to the level's map) and plays it as
`playdemo` does, or shows why it is no demo. In the classic look DEMOS gets its own band between
the pack's strip rows, its letters cut from the strips' own carved words (the capital D a
capital L's stem and half a capital O), on strips other than the pack's in big glyphs. Lab `se-demos`; tests
`tests/vfs_browse_test.cpp`, `tests/shooters_edge_demo_check_test.cpp` and the menu test.

SELECT GAME and the classic look throughout: the root's first row is SELECT GAME
(`menu/games_page.hpp`): each classic game by its name (cut from the menu strips' letters in the classic look, a picture in the modern one), the install of it kept
(`client/installs.hpp`) and its edition read from its packs, ENTER opening it or asking for its
folder. A classic game open plays its recordings behind the menu (`menu_attract`) in place of the
title's night, ESCAPE watches them, and the console (`menu/classic_console.hpp`: the game's glyph
sheet over its console background), the HUD's words, DEMOS, JOIN A GAME and SELECT GAME are all
in the game's own letters (`menu/classic_pages.hpp`); SELECT GAME's row is cut from the strips'
letters. The classic menu's words the strips lack are set by code: every letter is cut from the
strips into its own picture at load (`menu/carved_letters.hpp`, specks of neighbours dropped) and a
word is laid out on one line, so SELECT GAME, DEMOS and the games' names stand level and evenly
spaced; every row was measured against the strips (capitals rows 1 to 16, small
capitals 3 to 15, the strips' first band a pixel high and so set a pixel lower, the spaces the
strips' own); the sequel's plate words are shaded to one surface and their spaces cut from plain plate.
Lab `se-attract` (fixture, or `--basedir` a player's install); tests in the menu, game

THE MENU'S SOUNDS: each frame's presses leave one cue (`ui::MenuCue` from `ui::menu_cue`: move,
change, enter, back; the strongest wins), which `GameMenu::take_cue` hands the window and
`client/menu_sound.hpp` plays centred off the loaded game's own recordings: the cursor moved or a
page flipped `misc/menu1.wav`, the menu opened or a page or item entered `misc/menu2.wav`, a setting
changed, back out of a page or the menu closed `misc/menu3.wav`. The built-in arena hears our own
`ui/move`, `ui/select` and `ui/back` under those names. Lab `se-audio --menu=1` (with
`--game=<base>/<game folder>` off the player's pack); test `tests/shooters_edge_menu_sound_test.cpp`.
