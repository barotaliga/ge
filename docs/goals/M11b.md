2026-11-06: THE BUILD. A MOBILE TARGET (`CMAKE_SYSTEM_NAME` iOS or Android) builds one game's app
(`GE_MOBILE_GAME`, Fall Line) and what it links; the HOST TOOLS (`GE_HOST_TOOLS`: `ge`, `labs`,
`assets`, the tests, `play`, the plugins' tools) are off, since they run on the machine that
builds. libaom and opus are built for the target (`cmake/Codecs.cmake` hands their configure the
system, the SDK, the architectures, the oldest OS and the toolchain file, and keeps a copy per
target). `ge::platform::run_app` starts the app through SDL's UIKit entry on iOS (the only way a
window opens there); Fall Line's `main` goes through it. `ge_app_bundle` (`cmake/App.cmake`)
makes the bundle with `cmake/ios/Info.plist.in` (full screen, landscape, iPhone and iPad, a launch
screen); its identifier is `dev.local.<game>` in a plain checkout and takes a team's prefix from
the environment (`GE_BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX`), never from the tree. The whole engine compiled for iOS
with two fixes: `engine/play/record.cpp` starts no ffmpeg on iOS (`system` does not exist there),
and VideoToolbox's hardware-encoder keys need iOS 17.4, so the presets' oldest iOS is 18.0.

THE SIMULATOR: installed and launched on an iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.5); Dawn finds the simulator's
GPU, and the device is lost at the first frame: Dawn's Metal backend turns comparison samplers
off in the simulator, and the shadows sample with them (docs/rendering.md § Where Dawn is built).
A device has them.
