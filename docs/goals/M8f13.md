Status: done

Clouds skied through. A layer was already a slab of the world's air at its height over the valley floor. What was missing was a way to say how tall it is, and an assurance that the layers do not overlap:

- `Aloft::thickness` (Fall Line's `sv_cloudthickness`, m) sets how tall the lowest layer stands from its base to its top. Its cloud keeps its kind's density per metre, so a taller layer is darker under it and its shadow is deeper.
- Every layer, dealt or picked, starts at the top of the one under it. From a layer's top up there is clear sky or the next layer.
- The old opacity knob `sv_thick` is now `sv_clouddensity`. It was never released.
- The `world` lab stamps each shot with the eye's height and every layer's base and top over the floor. With seed 38, `sv_clouds stratus+altocumulus; sv_cover 1; sv_cloudheight 650; sv_cloudthickness 180; sv_clouddensity 0.6`, the run goes from 905 m (over the 650..830 m deck) to 849 m (its top) to 811 and 764 m (in it) to 687 m (under it).
- Seen from inside at the map's own lean, a stratus's thin patches leave the near field clear for a few hundred metres. At `sv_clouddensity 0.5` and up it reads as skiing in cloud.
