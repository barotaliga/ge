`engine/snow/lod.hpp`: the game names its focus actors (`set_focus`), never the camera. An imprint
farther than `near` from every focus lands in the coarse layer (one cell per 16×16 samples, which
stores sums, so folding and refining are exact). `fold_stale` folds the tiles no focus came near for
`stale` seconds, and a tile allocated over touched coarse cells is refined from them, with the mass
kept to the unit both ways. Past `max_tiles`, the tile least recently near a focus is folded (ties by
slot). The `GESNOW2` snapshot carries the coarse layer, and `GESNOW1` still loads. Nothing is stepped,
so no thread count enters the bits. Held by `tests/snow_lod_test.cpp`. Labs: `snow-lod` (a 768 m run
under a 48-tile budget: 2.0 MB at peak against 79 MB all fine) and `snow-cost`.
