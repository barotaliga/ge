Labs: `gpu-detail`

One decal pass, shared: the lightning beams (M50) draw detail's pool (`bake_atlas`, `decal_verts`)
after lighting, multiplying the lit picture by the decal's cover and opacity and adding its
emission, and that pass becomes the engine's generic decal pass driven by `decal_look(kind, age)`.
This row does not build a second one. What is left here:

- The props through `render/instances` from `prop_mesh`: the rubble, debris and moss of
  `default_rules` and the floor clutter of `clutter_rules`, one instance a prop, a mesh a kind and
  variant.
- Where a decal changes the light rather than only the colour, the lit shaders sample the pool: wet
  blood's gloss before it dries, and a fresh scorch's embers handed to the local lights (M39) as a
  short-lived light.

Its lab, `gpu-detail`, draws the test hall and the test yard on the software adapter beside the CPU
labs' pictures. Grass on the yard's grassy floors comes from `engine/grass` (`grass::add_mesh` with a
share a vertex, 0 under a roof), wired with the BSP floors in M42f.
