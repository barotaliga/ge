Design: [`docs/servers.md`](../servers.md) § The lobby and the match's phases.

**Built (part one):** the lobby's match flow lifted into the engine (`engine/server/match`: the
seats and two teams, `ready`, `break`, `toggleready`, `team`, the leader's `start`, warmup, the
countdown a break or too few seats stop, the match `lobby_due` starts once, `lobby_back` to
warmup), a pure function of the seats and the acts on the server's clock. Shooter's Edge's lobby
(`games/shooters_edge/server/lobby.hpp`) now names the engine's and adds only what a team is in
its game (the palette row, the user info and the mod's `.team`); its lobby test is unchanged and
green, and the `se-lobby` and `se-lobby-view` digests did not move. Held by
`tests/server_match_test.cpp` and the `server-lobby` lab (three cases: everyone ready, a break, the
leader's early start; each run twice).

**Built (part two):** the dedicated server runs the lobby with bots. `sv_bots` seats Shooter's
Edge's fighters (the listen server's room, `client/lobby_room.hpp`) as local seats of the classic
endpoint (`classic_add_local`: every client sees them as players, with their teams and frags;
nothing is sent to them; they stay seated over a map change); `sv_lobby 1` warms up every map with
the limits held, takes the clients' `ready`, `break`, `team`, `start` and the leader's `addbot` /
`removebot` before the mod (`ClassicServer::game_command`), restarts the map into the match when
the countdown runs out and goes back to warmup on the mod's next map. The engine's lobby gained
`LobbySettings::humans` (bots alone never count down) and a leader who is a player before a bot.
The endpoint now tells every client a score that moved (`svc_updatefrags`), which a remote
scoreboard never saw before. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_classic_game_test.cpp` (bots on a
client's scoreboard, the lobby's warmup to match over the simulated network, both replaying),
`tests/server_match_test.cpp` and the new `se-dedicated-lobby` lab (two rounds, the phases and
each seat's frags charted, run twice).

**Still open:** the intermission as its own phase and the timers as cvars (`sv_countdown`,
`sv_intermission`); `ServerGame`'s phase events; the lobby on the session's seats; Fall Line's
free ride as an endless warmup.
