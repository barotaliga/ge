Status: done

Landed `water::step(world, dt)`, the one step a world of water needs (its flow regions: a lake, an
ocean, a channel and a volume are pure functions of the time), beside the one call a body and
the one query `sample`, and `ge add water [<name>]` for a game repo. It writes a lake laid on the
wind from `templates/water/` (`sim/<name>.hpp`, `.cpp`: a 12-point shore about 85 m by 55 m, a
bowl of a bed 6 m deep shelving to 0.3 m, the `nordic-clear` look, its waves laid down to
0.25 m), a picture lab and a test; links `ge::water` into the game's simulation (once, however
many waters are added); and syncs the new `water` consumer skill. A consumer skill's `plugin:`
may now name an engine module the repo links itself, so that skill reaches a repo only once it
has water.

Evidence: a repo from `ge new` with `ge add water` and a second `ge add water
harbour_and_marina_waters` passes `ge check` (format, doctor, build, its tests and every lab's
smoke pass) at a 2-, a 10- and a 37-letter game name, typing no code; packaging's `scaffold` job
now does the same on every pull request. The scaffold's `water` lab: the plan (3,516 m^2 wet,
6.0 m at the middle, the look's visibility 8.6 m), the windward and the lee water side by side
at 25 px a metre, and a section; the lee's sea is twice the windward's at every wind (Hs 4.5 and
9.6 mm at 3 m/s, 10.5 and 22.3 mm at 7, 18.1 and 38.2 mm at 12). `tests/cli_add_test.cpp` (the
pieces filled, linked once, the skill synced only then, never written over, doctor clean) and
`tests/water_world_test.cpp` (the one step is the flow regions' step, bit for bit). Pictures:
the Water labs page.

Not yet: waking a lake or a volume into a flow region when a game breaks what held it (a game
lays the region with `add_flow` and `fill_to` today); the scaffold's lab draws on the CPU raster,
since the engine's GPU layer is not published to game repos yet.
