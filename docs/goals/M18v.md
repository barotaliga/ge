Status: in progress
Milestone: **Quake II support in classic-enhance**: Quake II loaded from the player's own packs as its own game behind its adapter (its maps, models, sounds, HUD, main menu, weapons, mods, configs), its rules as a second rules runner on `engine/mod` and its own move, in multiplayer and single player; every file family read once by what it is (shared readers, shared adapter calls), each game only thin glue, so the games' files mix in remix mode
Exit: Quake II's demo pack loaded through its own adapter, a map drawn in the window and played by a bot under its own rules runner with a golden digest; Quake's digests unchanged
Labs: `se-q2`, `se-q2-fight`, `se-q2-coop`, `se-q2-bots`, `se-q2-save`, `se-q2-unit`, `se-q2-cut`

Gathers Quake II's steps of Quake profiles (M18t): M18t2 (clean-room research
for its packs, maps, models, rules and move), M18t3 (its content drawn) and M18t4 (its rules
and move). It builds on the per-game adapter (`games/shooters_edge/client/game_adapter.hpp`) and
the rules runner (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § The rules runner). A loaded Quake II uses only its own
assets; mixing it with another game is classic remix (M18u). The rule throughout: a file is read
by its kind, never by the game that holds it (`client/adapter_parts.hpp`), and what two games
would write alike is lifted into the shared layer rather than copied. THE GOAL IS REMIX: each
part (maps, collision, movement, weapons and armour, items, models, sounds, HUD, menus, rules,
bots) is a slot with one shape every game fills, so any game's part plays with any other's (the
Quake II with Quake's move, the third's maps with Quake's weapons); each step's
lab shows its part working with another game's. The plan, step by step, is
the working page `porting/shooters-edge/q2-plan.md`.

A walk or a run steps by its frame within the run modulo its gait's length, so a run of frames longer than one stride keeps the stride's pace; any other run steps only when its length is the one the steps were counted on. The pick among a kind's sounds hashes the body and the frame, so it draws nothing from the game's stream; the sounds are listed at the map's start only when the switch is on. Held by `g_monsterfootsteps_lets_a_walking_infantry_step_twice_a_walk_and_is_silent_by_default`.

**Every monster's steps by its model's frames (2026-11-20).** A step is now a model frame in one run (walk, run, stand, attack, pain, death, duck): it sounds when the monster shows that frame in that run, so the same frame shown by another run stays silent (Jorg walks over his run's frames without a sound). The heavy kinds step always, as the game was made: the tank and its commander (`tank/step.wav`), the mutant (three steps, on its voice), Jorg and the Makron (left and right foot by frame, the Makron's long death too). The light kinds keep `g_monsterfootsteps`, now with the medic's cable attack and the insane marine's runs. Held by `a_walking_tank_steps_whatever_g_monsterfootsteps_says` beside the infantry's test.
