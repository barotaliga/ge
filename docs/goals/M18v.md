Status: open
Milestone: **Sequel support in classic-enhance**: the classic game's sequel loaded from the player's own packs as its own game behind its adapter (its maps, models, sounds, HUD, main menu, weapons, mods, configs), its rules as a second rules runner on `engine/mod` and its own move, in multiplayer and single player
Exit: The sequel's demo pack loaded through its own adapter, a map drawn in the window and played by a bot under its own rules runner with a golden digest; the classic game's digests unchanged
Labs: `se-sequel`

Gathers the sequel's steps of the classic game profiles (M18t): M18t2 (clean-room research
for its packs, maps, models, rules and move), M18t3 (its content drawn) and M18t4 (its rules
and move). It builds on the per-game adapter (`games/shooters_edge/client/game_adapter.hpp`)
and needs the rules runner (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § The rules runner, steps 1 to 4) in
first. A loaded sequel uses only its own assets; mixing it with another game is classic
remix (M18u). Waits on the maintainer's go: nothing of it is built yet.
