Snow is drawn from the same numbers the physics reads:

- **The light model** is an engine WGSL library (`render/wgsl/snow_light.wgsl`: `snow_relief`,
  `snow_light`, and the noise, relief and glint blocks). The mountain's material is re-expressed
  over it, and every `world` view is bit for bit unchanged.
- **The GPU mirror** of a layer (`render/snow_mirror.hpp`, `render/wgsl/snow_mirror.wgsl`): the
  fine tiles in an atlas behind a page table round the eye, the coarse view (the layer's blocks
  and the summaries of live tiles), uploads only where a tile's or a block's stamp moved.
- **The fine marks** (`render/snow_marks.hpp`): the trail maps generalised into the engine, with
  capsule, box and ellipse stamps, made from a layer's imprint records by `marks_from_records`.
  The mountain's trail map is now these marks under its own name, and the `world` and `trails`
  views are bit for bit unchanged.

The `gpu-snow` lab holds every raw sample to the bit and every column to 0.1 mm, pictures the
fine marks beside the mirror, and runs in `labs-smoke`. Next: M29f (deformed geometry), and
M29j (Fall Line on the engine's snow), where the mountain's material reads the mirror.
