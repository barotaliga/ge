Status: in progress

The snow light model is an engine WGSL library (`render/wgsl/snow_light.wgsl`: `snow_relief`,
`snow_light`, and the noise, relief and glint blocks). The mountain's material is re-expressed
over it, and every `world` view is bit for bit unchanged. Still open: the GPU mirror of a layer
(the fine tiles in an atlas behind a page table round the camera, the coarse layer), the fine
marks fed by the imprint records, and the `gpu-snow` lab.
