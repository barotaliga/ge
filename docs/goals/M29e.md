Status: in progress

The snow light model is an engine WGSL library (`render/wgsl/snow_light.wgsl`: `snow_relief`,
`snow_light`, and the noise, relief and glint blocks). The mountain's material is re-expressed
over it, and every `world` view is bit for bit unchanged.

The GPU mirror of a layer is built (`render/snow_mirror.hpp`, `render/wgsl/snow_mirror.wgsl`): the
fine tiles in an atlas behind a page table round the eye, the coarse view (the layer's blocks and
the summaries of live tiles), uploads only where a tile's or a block's stamp moved. The `gpu-snow`
lab holds every raw sample to the bit and every column to 0.1 mm, and runs in `labs-smoke`.

Still open: the fine marks fed by the imprint records, and the mountain's material reading the
mirror.
