Status: done

Built: the draw list's shapes (`engine/ui/draw.hpp`): `line` and `polyline` (capsules), `arc`
(a band round a centre between two angles, flat or round ends, any paint), `polygon` (convex, up
to 16 points, either winding), and boxes turned by `rotation` about `pivot` and nine-sliced by
`slice` / `slice_to`. The CPU raster draws each by its signed distance (`engine/ui/raster.cpp`,
with `arc_distance` and `polygon_distance` public), and the GPU's 2D pass draws the same in its one
instanced draw (`engine/render/ui_pass.wgsl`: a 160-byte item with the turn, the slice and the
shape's bounds, the polygons' points in a third storage buffer). Angles are radians, 0 at twelve
o'clock, clockwise; every sine and cosine is worked out once a command with the engine's libm.

Measured: tests/ui_raster_test.cpp holds a turned box, its pivot, a nine-slice's corners, a
line's width, an arc's flat and round ends and a whole ring, a polygon in both windings, and the
refusals (more than 16 points, no area). tests/render_ui_pass_test.cpp holds every shape on the
GPU against the raster. The `menu` and `menu-gpu` labs picture an instrument panel built from all
of it at 1280x720, 390x844 and 844x390; on lavapipe the GPU is within 8 levels of the raster at
every pixel but one a picture (the needle's point, up to 63 levels at 390x844).
