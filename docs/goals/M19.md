Status: in progress

Input devices, in five rows. M19a (the device layer, simulated), M19b (the SDL backend) and M19c
(Fall Line played by hand) are done; M19e (touch and motion on mobile) has its touch controls,
the gyro's bias and Fall Line's thumbs, with the phone's haptics left for the store shells; M19d
(wheels, pedals and HOTAS in a game) needs real devices. The design is `docs/input.md`.
