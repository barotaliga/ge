Status: done

The classic levels' air: a `wind::Climate` for every level of the classic pack, held by the
classic-enhance plugin (`plugins/classicenhance`, M41a) beside the other per-level enhancements.
Every level is played in the evening or at night, in a wind, under low cloud; each gets a mean
temperature, a swing, a humidity and its pools' water temperature by what the level is (a base by
cold water, a castle, a cave, a hall by lava), so its pools steam in the cold, the breath shows in
its cold halls and the fall over its open yards is snow or rain by the wet bulb.

Landed: `plugins/classicenhance/climate.hpp` (`LevelAir`, `level_airs`, `level_air`,
`fallback_air`, `lava_warmth`), linking `ge_wind`. Every level of the shareware pack has a row; a
map the table does not hold (the full game's levels, a player's own map) gets `fallback_air()`.
Test: `tests/classicenhance_climate_test.cpp`. Lab: `classic-climate` (digest
`tests/digests/classic-climate.txt`).
