Status: open

A player's own copy of the classic shareware pack, verified unchanged and enhanced on their machine
into a local cache (`plugins/classicenhance`, `docs/classic-enhance.md`). Nothing read from the
pack or made from it is committed, published or shipped; the tree carries only the code, its
fixtures and the pack's digest.

M41a and M41d are done. Next: M41e (the models), M41b (the rebake hook, once M40b's
`brush::rebake_map` lands), then M41c (the BSP renderer drawing from the cache).
