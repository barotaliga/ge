Labs: `voice`

Voice chat runs over a session: `engine/net/voice.hpp` (the sender's gate, the host's relay, the
jitter buffer, the mix), `engine/net/voice_codec.hpp` (Opus mono at a constant 24 kbit/s with
in-band FEC and concealment, the libopus the video export already pins; a silent stand-in without
GE_ENCODERS), the microphone in `engine/platform/audio.hpp` (`open_audio_in`, `read_audio_in`),
the voice cvars in `engine/net/net_cvars.hpp` (`add_voice_cvars`), and the synth's new streams
(`engine/audio/synth.hpp`: `stream`, `feed`, `queued`, `steer`, `stop`) that every talker plays on.

- Push to talk, voice activity (a threshold, a lower closing level and a 300 ms hangover) or
  always open; a spurt's first and last frames flagged.
- Each frame carries its sequence (the order) and its clock (the time it was spoken), so the jitter
  buffer times arrivals across pauses.
- The host stamps the talker's seat, routes by the session's rules (everyone, the team, one
  player, the near ones by `SessionConfig::hears`), holds each talker to a flood bucket and a
  server mute.
- The jitter buffer chooses its delay at each spurt's start from the transit spread, rebuilds a
  lost frame from the next one's FEC copy, conceals a few, drops the late.
- Each talker mixes in with its own volume (0 mutes), the game's distance gain
  (`voice_distance_gain`) and pan, and `voice_volume`.

Measured (`make lab LAB=voice`, 4 talkers, 20 s): every route right on every preset. Mouth to ear
is 80 ms at the median on `lan`, 150 on `wifi`, 350 on `bad` and 650 on `awful`. Concealed: 0%,
1.2%, 5.7% and 14.2%, against 8.9% and 20.6% on `bad` and `awful` with FEC off. Against the
clean decode, the segmental SNR is 35 dB (the same samples), 33.5, 29.7 and 23.2.
`tests/net_voice_test.cpp` holds the codec, the gate, the buffer, the routes, the flood guard, the
streams and a trial replaying to the bit.

Left for later rows: the push-to-talk key and the proximity and whisper commands on the console
and in a game's bindings (`+voice`, `say_prox`, `whisper`), with Fall Line's pilot (M17k); voice
on a peer mesh with no host to relay (lockstep games); echo cancellation and noise suppression on
the microphone; the payload encrypted with M17i's keys.
