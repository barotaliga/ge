Status: in progress

Atmosphere, reflections and glow for any game: fog, mist, dust and glowing haze placed in areas,
embers and drips, the scene reflected in water, puddles and wet floors, and glowing surfaces
lighting what is round them (docs/rendering.md § Fog in an area).

M38a is done. Next: M38c (the scene in a mirror: one screen-space reflection helper that the wet
ground's `wet_sheen` and any glossy surface reads), then M38e and M38d on the dynamic lights' list
(M39) once it lands, M38b, and M38f (Fall Line's own mists).

Who owns what next to it: the wet ground's masks, puddles and ripples are the weather's (M36,
`Weather::wet`, the frame's wet row); M38c reads them and owns only the reflection. Lamps and
their shadows are M39's; M38e reads its list in the fog's inject. The lens and post effects are
M35's.
