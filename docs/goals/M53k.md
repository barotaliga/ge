Milestone: **The server's settings in the menu**: OPTIONS ▸ SERVER SETTINGS shows the map, the match's rules and every setting a player can change with its value now, a page for each group (player help, movement, rules), the settings a vote decides starred and asked about when the player leaves them; filled from our servers by a sheet over the protocol's extension bit, and from stock QuakeWorld and Quake II servers by what they already tell a client
Exit: On our QuakeWorld server, a stock QuakeWorld server with or without its match mod, and a Quake II server, the page lists the settings with their values, a change made there takes effect (at once or by a vote), and the page shows the new value
Labs: `se-attract`

Design: [`docs/servers.md`](../servers.md) § The settings sheet. Porting note: [`docs/porting/server.md`](../porting/server.md) § The settings sheet.

Done so far:
- The sheet (`engine/server/sheet.hpp`): the rows, how each changes, the wire text, the choices
  from a row's values, the lines a change sends; `ServerGame::sheet`. Held by
  `tests/server_sheet_test.cpp`.
- Stock servers read (`plugins/classic/server_sheet.hpp`): QuakeWorld's serverinfo and a match
  mod's listings and words; Quake II's `info` and a match mod's `settings`. Held by
  `tests/classic_server_sheet_test.cpp`.
- Our QuakeWorld server (`games/shooters_edge/net/qw_sheet.hpp`): the `settings-sheet` bit, svc
  `ge_sheet` paced on the reliable channel, sent again when a vote passes. Held by
  `tests/shooters_edge_qw_sheet_test.cpp`.
- The window (`net/qw_remote_sheet.hpp`, `serversheet`) and the pages (`menu/server_page.hpp`)
  in both looks, the vote question. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_server_page_test.cpp`; pictured
  by the `se-attract` lab (`server`, `server-help`, `server-vote`).

- Our Quake II server (`games/shooters_edge/net/q2_votes.hpp`): the engine's votes on its rules
  (the owner's refused with why, `vote map`, `vote kick`, put back at the next map) and the sheet
  offered on svc 120 as a client with the bit enters, sent when asked, again after a vote. Held
  by `tests/shooters_edge_q2_votes_test.cpp`.

- Checked live from a desktop with the `window-recipes` skill's recipes (no hands on the keys):
  - a local QuakeWorld server with its match mod (version 1.48): the page lists the map, the mode,
    deathmatch, teamplay, the limits, overtime and powerups with the server's values; stepping
    MODE from four a side to ten a side took effect at once (the mod said so, the time limit went
    to 30) and the page showed both new values. The listings had been asked with a word the server
    refused ("cmd status1"); now asked as `status1`, `status2`;
  - a stock Quake II server (q2pro, q2dm1): map, deathmatch, time limit 15, frag limit 30, as its
    own `status` answer says;
  - a Quake II match mod server (OpenTDM): map, mode, time limit starred as votes.
