Done so far:
- The sheet (`engine/server/sheet.hpp`): the rows, how each changes, the wire text, the choices

  `tests/server_sheet_test.cpp`.

  mod's listings and words; Quake II's `info` and a match mod's `settings`. Held by
  `tests/classic_server_sheet_test.cpp`.
- Our QuakeWorld server (`games/shooters_edge/net/qw_sheet.hpp`): the `settings-sheet` bit, svc
  `ge_sheet` paced on the reliable channel, sent again when a vote passes. Held by
  `tests/shooters_edge_qw_sheet_test.cpp`.
- The window (`net/qw_remote_sheet.hpp`, `serversheet`) and the pages (`menu/server_page.hpp`)
  in both looks, the vote question. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_server_page_test.cpp`; pictured
  by the `se-attract` lab (`server`, `server-help`, `server-vote`).

- Our Quake II server (`games/shooters_edge/net/q2_votes.hpp`): the engine's votes on its rules
  (the owner's refused with why, `vote map`, `vote kick`, put back at the next map) and the sheet
  offered on svc 120 as a client with the bit enters, sent when asked, again after a vote. Held
  by `tests/shooters_edge_q2_votes_test.cpp`.
