Exit: The GPU pass beside the CPU reference within a stated difference; the pipeline in the manifest; nothing created mid-run
Labs: `gpu-viewmodel`

`engine/render/viewmodel.hpp` holds the pass:

- `create_viewmodel` uploads the held models once and builds the pipelines.
- `prepare_viewmodel` and `pack_held_draw` turn a frame's draws into the shader's form: the
  placement now and a frame ago, the lens, the light, and the view's basis.
- `encode_viewmodel` is the "viewmodel" pass, into the HDR, motion and depth targets.
- `encode_viewmodel_resolved` is the CPU reference's twin, resolved to bytes.
- `held_depth_floor` gives the gun's depth range from the guard.
- `held_candela` and `lamp_of` make a held light and a frame lamp the same light.

The shader is `engine/render/viewmodel.wgsl`, and `tests/render_viewmodel_test.cpp` holds the CPU
half.

The `gpu-viewmodel` lab holds the GPU to the CPU raster under the stage's six lights and by the
frame's clustered lamps. The stated difference is a mean of at most 1 level and a 99th centile of at
most 16, with at most 2% of coverage differing. Measured at 480 by 270: a mean of at most 0.52 and a 99th
centile of at most 11, over every light. The lab also draws the whole frame: in the sun, under an awning's shadow (the
gun's patch 48 against 80 in the sun), at night by a lamp, and against a wall 0.35 m ahead (the gun
whole in front of it). It checks that no GPU error is raised and that no pipeline is made after the
load.
