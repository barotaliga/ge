## Progress

- 2026-11-14: the face (`udp_tv_netgame_open`, `udp_tv_netgame_frame`): the transport's header, the connect and its accept on the same port, reliable fragments of 1024 bytes one at a time and resent each second, frame datagrams of at most 1024 bytes; each level's signon cut at its stages, the face's own `signonnum` answering each of the client's replies; the play on one clock, the recorder's view as a `setangle`; a new level and the loop sent as "reconnect". `setv --demo` serves it on `--netgame-port=26000` beside the classic face. Black-box: an unmodified current single-player client watched the shareware pack's first demo through the loop and a real 4on4 on dm2, converted, with its frags and team chat (`tests/shooters_edge_classic_tv_netgame_test.cpp`: a scripted client from the transport's facts, a lossy link too).
- 2026-11-14: the oldest single-player client itself, built for Linux outside the tree and run as a black box, watched a real 4on4 multiview recording on dm2 through `setv` (on one machine it takes `-port 26100`, since it binds 26000 too; `docs/tv.md`).
- 2026-11-14: the `se-tv-netgame` lab: the scripted client (now `net/classic_netgame_watcher.hpp`, shared with the test) watches a recording over the lan, mobile and awful links. Sign-on takes 78, 377 and 2,483 ms; 72, 68 and 36 frames a second arrive; every message reads, and every loop brings the client back.
- Done: every exit holds.
- 2026-11-14 (after): each viewer of a multiview recording picks who it follows: fire for the next player, jump for the one before, a weapon key for player 1 to 8, `cmd follow <name or number>`. A view is converted the first time it is asked for, on its own thread in `setv`, and kept; the viewer is sent "reconnect" and joins it where the match is, with the level's scoreboard and light styles so far. The `se-tv-netgame` lab measures the switch (104, 520 and 6,942 ms on lan, mobile and awful); the oldest client itself switched with `cmd follow` and fire on a real 4on4.
