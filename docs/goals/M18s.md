Status: in progress
Milestone: **The single-player campaign**: the classic game's own single-player program (the player's pack) run on a campaign host, episode 1 on the shareware pack and episodes 2–4 opened by the full game's packs; the movement setting `sv_playermove` (`arena`, `original`) for the campaign and for multiplayer alike; our own campaign variant of the baseline mod, monsters and all, on free models
Exit: The shareware pack's first episode played start to finish by a scripted player through every level change, intermission and the episode's end, with a golden digest on free fixture maps and private real-pack pictures; the full game's packs open the other halls; both moves held to recorded vectors; our campaign variant plays a free map's monsters to its exit
Labs: `se-campaign`, `framemove`, `pmove`, `se-baseline`, `se-menu`, `se-coop`

The design is `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Profiles; the clean-room notes are
`porting/shooters-edge/campaign.md` (the host), `frame-move.md` (the original move and the
setting) and `campaign-rules.md` (what a campaign program does, for our own variant). Built from
the notes by a session that has not read the GPL sources.

Built in steps, each its own pull request:

- **M18s1, the single-player layout** (done): the VM accepts a program whose layout crc is 5927 through a
  layout TABLE (name, offset, type) the loop's mirror reads, the multiplayer layout becoming the
  other table; the builtin differences (`bprint`/`sprint` without a level, `particle`, autoaim
  with `sv_aim`, the message destinations). Lab `se-campaign` loads the pack's program
  (`--game`) and lists its layout check.
- **M18s2, the campaign host** (done): spawn parms (`SetNewParms`, `SetChangeParms`), `serverflags`,
  `map`, `changelevel`, `restart`, skill and coop at map start, the clock starting at 1.0 and the
  two settling frames, the single-player frame (one step a host frame, time advanced after,
  one think a frame, no `newmis`), the campaign statistics, `registered` from the packs. Built as
  `games/shooters_edge/server/campaign.hpp` over the loop's single-player clock; the shareware
  pack's own program starts `start`, `e1m1` and `e1m2` (by `changelevel`) with no fault, lab
  `se-campaign --game=`. The players' half of the frame (each player moved in entity order with
  its latest command) lands with the original move in M18s3.
- **M18s3, the original move** and `sv_playermove` (in progress): the walk move, step up and down,
  unstick, wall and edge friction, air acceleration, water, the program's part (water drag, the
  jump, the water jump) built as `plugins/shooter/framemove.hpp`, held to the recorded vectors
  (`tests/golden/shooter_framemove.txt`, 35 scenarios, 5120 steps; lab `framemove` charts them
  against ours): all 35 match every step. The hull trace has two walks (`plugins/shooter/hull.hpp`):
  the original (every split backed off 1/32, the 0.1 step-back; held to the 31 single traces of
  `tests/golden/shooter_hulltrace.txt`) for the campaign host and the original move's own traces,
  the networked for the arena host and the predicted move. `sv_playermove`
  (`arena`, `original`, `auto`) read at map start: on the multiplayer clock the original step
  runs per command by its length, the engine jumping for a multiplayer program; on the
  single-player clock each player thinks after the packets with its newest command and moves
  in its turn in the physics frame. The shareware pack's own program walks e1m1 on it with no
  fault (`se-campaign --forward`). Left: predicting the original move on a client, and the arena
  move under a single-player program (which jumps for itself).
- **M18s4, the campaign client** (done): the intermission overlay, finale and cutscene text typed out,
  the sell screen as the help pages, the solo panel, the damage flash and kick, the punch and the
  ideal pitch, the runes on the HUD; the menu's SINGLE PLAYER ▸ NEW GAME lit. **First part
  built**: the window plays a single-player program on the campaign host (`client/listen.hpp`'s
  `listen_campaign`; NEW GAME runs `newgame`, lit when the packs carry one; paused under the menu
  and the console); the program's client messages read off its reliable streams
  (`client/campaign_view.hpp`: intermission, finale, cutscene, sellscreen, cdtrack recorded,
  killedmonster, foundsecret, setangle); the intermission overlay, the typed text and the solo
  panel (`menu/level_screens.hpp`, in the pack's pictures and big figures, else the engine's
  type); the keys, the runes, the cloak and the suit on the HUD. The shareware pack's e1m1 exit
  gives the intermission and the attack carries on to e1m2 (`se-campaign --exit=1`). **Second
  part built**: the client data (`server/client_data.hpp`): the blow taken (blood, armour and
  source, the program's damage fields cleared each frame) kicks the view away from its source
  and flashes the screen, red for blood and amber for armour; the punch reaches the loop, which
  decays it, and the view (never the aim); the ideal pitch traced off the floor ahead, which the
  view drifts to with `lookspring`. On the shareware pack's e1m1 every blow matches the health
  lost and the stairs give 2.4 degrees (`se-campaign --forward=320 --yaw=90 --fire=1`, its
  `client-data` chart). **Third part built**: the program's temporary entities on the
  unreliable stream (`client/campaign_view.hpp`'s `read_effects`: gunshots, spikes,
  explosions, teleports, lava, beams) and its particle bursts drawn by the window's effects; on
  the shareware pack's e1m1 a 12 s walk firing the shotgun gives 153 hits and 27 bursts with
  nothing left unread. Left for later rows: a beam is drawn as a light and sparks at its end,
  not yet as a bolt along it.
- **M18s5, saves** (done): the campaign saved and loaded on `engine/save` (M25), and the
  classic text save read and written. **First part built**: `server/campaign_save.hpp` writes
  the whole running game in four chunks (`CAMP` what the campaign carries and the program's
  identity, `QSRV` the mod server's, `LOOP` every entity slot with its area links and the client
  slots, `QCVM` the program's globals, fields and strings) and loads it by starting the map again
  (`campaign_reopen`) and reading the save over it; refused with the reason for a match, coop,
  a dead player and the level's end. The window's console has `save <name>` and `load <name>`
  (`<game folder>/saves/`). Held by `tests/shooters_edge_campaign_save_test.cpp` and by
  `se-campaign --save=20` on the shareware pack's e1m1: saved at 13 s, loaded and played 20 s
  on beside the game never saved (a death and its restart on the way), the digests the same in
  all 1440 frames; the save is 324 KB. **Second part built**: `server/classic_save.hpp` writes
  and reads the classic text save (version 5: the comment, the 16 parms, the skill, the map, the
  time, the 64 light styles, the saved globals and one block an entity, each by name), a save
  from an engine that numbers the map right after one player moved onto this server's
  numbering; refused with the line for a wrong version, a short header or a bad block. The
  console's `save <name>.sav` writes it into the game folder and `load <name>` tries ours, then
  `<name>.sav`. The menu's LOAD and SAVE pages show twelve slots (`s0` to `s11`), each with its
  save's line (our title, or the classic comment), and run `load s<i>` or `save s<i>`. Held by
  `tests/shooters_edge_classic_save_test.cpp` and by `se-campaign --save=20` on e1m1: the text
  is 71 KB and 3909 lines, read back and written again the same text, and the loaded game plays
  20 s on with no fault, the player 0 units from the original and the same entities live (it
  parts in the digest at once: the text keeps the program's state, not the loop's lists, the
  random stream or the string store).
- **M18s6, coop**: more than one player in the campaign host. Built in two parts:
  - **M18s6a, coop and its companions** (done): our campaign in coop (`coop 1`; with `teamplay 1`

    glance, hop, jump, sway, weighted by its traits). The window's `coopgame [bots] [skill] [map]`,
    `addbot` and `removebot` (`client/coop_party.hpp`): companions carried from map to map by the
    host. Lab `se-coop` on the coop hall (the arena with a button, coop starts and three monsters):
