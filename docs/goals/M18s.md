Status: in progress
Milestone: **The single-player campaign**: the classic game's own single-player program (the player's pack) run on a campaign host, episode 1 on the shareware pack and episodes 2–4 opened by the full game's packs; the movement setting `sv_playermove` (`arena`, `original`) for the campaign and for multiplayer alike; our own campaign variant of the baseline mod, monsters and all, on free models
Exit: The shareware pack's first episode played start to finish by a scripted player through every level change, intermission and the episode's end, with a golden digest on free fixture maps and private real-pack pictures; the full game's packs open the other halls; both moves held to recorded vectors; our campaign variant plays a free map's monsters to its exit
Labs: `se-campaign`, `se-framemove`, `pmove`, `se-baseline`

The design is `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Profiles; the clean-room notes are
`porting/shooters-edge/campaign.md` (the host), `frame-move.md` (the original move and the
setting) and `campaign-rules.md` (what a campaign program does, for our own variant). Built from
the notes by a session that has not read the GPL sources.

Built in steps, each its own pull request:

- **M18s1, the single-player layout** (done): the VM accepts a program whose layout crc is 5927 through a
  layout TABLE (name, offset, type) the loop's mirror reads, the multiplayer layout becoming the
  other table; the builtin differences (`bprint`/`sprint` without a level, `particle`, autoaim
  with `sv_aim`, the message destinations). Lab `se-campaign` loads the pack's program
  (`--game`) and lists its layout check.
- **M18s2, the campaign host** (done): spawn parms (`SetNewParms`, `SetChangeParms`), `serverflags`,
  `map`, `changelevel`, `restart`, skill and coop at map start, the clock starting at 1.0 and the
  two settling frames, the single-player frame (one step a host frame, time advanced after,
  one think a frame, no `newmis`), the campaign statistics, `registered` from the packs. Built as
  `games/shooters_edge/server/campaign.hpp` over the loop's single-player clock; the shareware
  pack's own program starts `start`, `e1m1` and `e1m2` (by `changelevel`) with no fault, lab
  `se-campaign --game=`. The players' half of the frame (each player moved in entity order with
  its latest command) lands with the original move in M18s3.
- **M18s3, the original move** and `sv_playermove`: the walk move, step up and down, unstick,
  wall and edge friction, air acceleration, water; who jumps in each mix; sent with the server's
  settings and predicted with the same rules. Held to the recorded vectors
  (`porting/shooters-edge/vectors/framemove.txt`, 35 scenarios); lab `se-framemove` charts them against ours.
- **M18s4, the campaign client**: the intermission overlay, finale and cutscene text typed out,
  the sell screen as the help pages, the solo panel, the damage flash and kick, the punch and the
  ideal pitch, the runes on the HUD; the menu's SINGLE PLAYER ▸ NEW GAME lit.
- **M18s5, saves**: the campaign saved and loaded on `engine/save` (M25), and the classic text
  save read and written.
- **M18s6, coop**: more than one player in the campaign host over the native protocol.
- **M18s7, our campaign variant**: the baseline mod split into `qc/common/`, `qc/arena/` and
  `qc/campaign/`; monsters on the senses, states and roster of `campaign-rules.md` with free
  models and sounds; the campaign flow; the weapons and armour in `qc/common/` read from an
  arsenal row, never a constant, so another profile's set can fill a map's slots (M18t6,
  `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Weapons and armour); lab `se-campaign --progs=` plays it on a free map.
