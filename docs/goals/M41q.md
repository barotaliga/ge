Status: in progress
Milestone: **The classic fires move like real fire**: under classic-enhance the torches', braziers' and bonfires' flames move as a real flame does in play, not only in a still: the gas accelerates as it rises and keeps its eddies, each flame puffs at a real flame's rate for its width (1.5 / sqrt(D)) and pinches its tip off as pockets, its spine snakes, and the wall's light swells and dims with the same puffs; no frame-rate grain (`r_fire_speed`)
Exit: The se-client lab's `--game` films of the start map's brazier and wall torches, before beside after, played at real time
Labs: `se-client`, `gpu-flames`

Played on the start map, the fires looked right in a still and wrong in motion: the gas's noise was
a frozen volume slid up through a fixed teardrop, the whole flame was scaled up and down at its
light's flutter, and a fresh white-noise grain every frame (the gas wholly reactive to the TAA)
boiled at the frame rate. `render/flame.wgsl` now reads the gas in its own frame as it accelerates
up (`flame_age`), puffs (`flame_puff`, the rate `render::flame_puff_hz`), and sways its spine;
`render::flame_puff_level` is the gas's level and, through `brush::app::fire_flicker`, the wall's
light. The march's jitter is interleaved gradient noise turned each frame and the gas keeps about
two frames of history. `r_fire_pace` (0.4 of the old presets) becomes `r_fire_speed` (1 a real
fire's). Tests: `tests/render_flame_test.cpp`
(`a_flame_puffs_by_its_width_and_its_level_swings_round_one`), `tests/brush_bsp_flames_test.cpp`.
