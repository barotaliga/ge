Done (2026-11-21).

- **The ticks as rows**: `plugins/shooter/liquid_harm.hpp`'s `classic_harm` gives lava (10, 20, 30
  by the level every 0.2 s; every 1 s in the suit) and slime (4, 8, 12 every 1 s; none in the
  suit) as the engine's `water::Harm` rows, with the new `Harm::restart` (the clock starts again on
  going in, so the step after deals a second tick). `add_liquids` lays them on every map's lava and
  slime, and our rules deal their ticks from them. The swim was already the classic row's (every
  swim scale 1, the swim up 100, 80 and 50; `row.hpp`), and a map's liquids are named by their
  textures when it is read (`brush/read_v29.cpp`'s `liquid_by_name`).
- **The scripted dive** (`se-dive`): a hall of three pools compiled in the lab, one player held
  at each level, let sink, swum up, taken out and dipped back, and drowned in the water, by our
  campaign and by the pack's own program (the record). Measured on the registered packs, the
  program also restarts the clock on going in, plays a sound going in (by the liquid) and coming
  out, cries out at every burn in lava and slime, gasps when the eyes come up after more than 3 s
  under (a longer gasp after drowning), drowns harder each second (4, 6 and on to 14, then 10
  again), and slows a swimmer by 0.8 of the speed a second for each level. Our campaign now does
  all of it (the arena keeps its plain rules), and all six dives agree: the damage to the point,
  the sink (38.3 units/s), the swim up and the sounds. Held frame by frame by
  `tests/shooter_liquid_harm_test.cpp`.
