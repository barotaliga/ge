Fall Line's air: the map's weather words (the sky, the hour, the wind) turned into a `Climate`,
its snow line from `snow_level` rather than a dealt height (a new generator trait if it moves a
seed's build), the skier's breath on a cold morning (`breath_of`), frost on the village's roofs
and rails (`deposit_on`), black ice under rain on a frozen road (`glaze_on`), and ice on its lakes
(`ice_grown`, with M24l's water).

**As built.** The map's day of air is `air::climate_of` (plugins/mountain/air, moved from the
mist look so the simulation's half can read it), with a day's mean a sky can pick by hand
(`Weather::mean_c`, `SkyPick::mean_c`). The snow line is `air::snowline_at`: a line picked by hand
where there is one, else `wind::snow_level` of the day at the sky's hour, so on a mild day it
climbs through the afternoon and sinks at night; `fall_at` takes the hour (the frame hands it the
sky's clock, `hour_at` otherwise). A dealt winter map's line lies far under its base, so its fall
is snow to the valley as before. The skier breathes in the cold (`skier/app/breath.hpp`): puffs at
his mouth, as thick as `breath_of` of the air there, into the particles beside his spray. The
`precip` lab's `mild-dawn` and `mild-afternoon` skies put one day's line under the eye at 06:30
and over it at 14:30 (snow, then rain), and its `breath` sky shows a skier breathing on the map's
cold, clear morning. Frost on the village, black ice and the lakes' ice moved to M51f. Held by
`tests/mountain_air_test.cpp` and `tests/skier_breath_test.cpp`. Next: M51f.
