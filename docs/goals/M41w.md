## Landed

- **The engine's AO applied after the fact** (`render/ao.hpp`'s `create_ao_apply` and
  `encode_ao_apply`, `ao_apply.wgsl`): the classic maps draw their colour and depth in one pass,
  so there is no depth for the search before the colour; the frame runs `ao` after the map and
  its models and then multiplies the colour drawn by the sky each pixel sees (read as `sky_seen`
  reads it: the nearest half-size texels of like depth), leaving the sky's pixels alone. Held by
  `render_ao_test`'s `applied_after_the_fact_the_valley_floor_darkens_and_the_sky_keeps_its_colour`.
- **`ce_ao`** (CONTACT SHADE, on), with `ce_ao_strength` (0.8) and `ce_ao_radius` (48 units), in
  the modern look's world options (`classic/app/look.cpp`; the classic look never runs it) and on
  the menu's LIGHT AND SHADOW page; the frame (`games/shooters_edge/client/world_draw.cpp`) makes
  the pipelines at load and runs the two passes only while it is on, so `ce_ao 0` draws as
  before. Quake and Quake II alike (both draw through the same frame).
- **Pictures**: `se-client --before="ce_ao 0"` on e1m2, dm3 and q2dm1 (private: the real packs):
  the seams of walls and floors, steps, door frames and the floor round the crates darken.
- **Price** (`se-timedemo --price=1 --price-only=ao`, Quake's demo1, 960 x 540, software
  rasterizer): 20.2 ms of a 365 ms median frame, about 5.5 % (the run's noise 7.6 ms); `ao` 12.7
  ms and `ao.apply` 3.8 ms. No quality stop: the cost is one half-size search at any setting.
  A real GPU's timing is the Mac's to take.
