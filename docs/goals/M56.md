Status: in progress
Milestone: **The hub (`engine/hub`, a community's website): every live game of a game listed with its scoreboard, servers and broadcast nodes announcing to a master, live matches watched, and every demo archived by its hash and searchable; generic in the engine, the game's words in its `HubSpec`, the classic game's servers read by an adapter**
Exit: A Fall Line and a Shooter's Edge hub, each one executable, list the simulated evening's servers and broadcasts, show a live match in the page with the engine's own viewer, and find a demo by a player's name
Labs: `hub-master`, `hub-api`, `hub-pages`, `hub-archive`, `se-hub`, `hub-radar`, `hub-viewer`

The design is [`docs/hub.md`](../hub.md); the facts about the classic community's hub are the
working note `porting/shooters-edge/hub.md`.

- **M56 (this PR):** the design and the clean-room note. The maintainer chose the engine in
  the browser (2026-11-11).
- **M56a (done):** the status record (`engine/net/status.hpp`, filled by `engine/server`) and the master for every node kind (servers, TV nodes, forwarders): heartbeats, the list, expiry. The `hub-master` lab runs it.
- **M56b (done):** `engine/hub`: the poller, the index, HTTP/1.1 with server-sent events, the JSON API,
  `ge_add_hub`, Shooter's Edge's `HubSpec` and `shooters_edge_hub` (Fall Line's waits for its
  dedicated server). The `hub-api` lab runs it.
- **M56c (done):** the pages (`engine/hub/pages.hpp`): Servers (cards by interest, the mode
  filters, Join, Spectate and Watch links), Players, a scoreboard overlay a server at a time, one
  stylesheet with a dark and a light scheme, no script. The `hub-pages` lab draws them in Chromium.
- **M56d (done, but the pull):** the archive (`engine/hub/archive.hpp`): replay files by SHA-256,
  rows from the hub's meta words, the inbox, retention, the search, `/v1/demos`, the file by hash and
  the Demos page; the index in memory (SQLite waits until an archive outgrows it). The `hub-archive`
  lab runs 500 demos. Pulling demos from TV nodes lands with the TV node (M57b).
- **M56e (done, but Watch):** open in the game: the links (`engine/hub/link.hpp`), the game CLI's
  `link register` (Linux, macOS, Windows) and `link open` (join, spectate, a demo fetched by hash
  and played); Shooter's Edge's spectate and demo words. Watch opens once the game has a TV client
  (M57b, M57c). The macOS registration waits on a run on the Mac.
- **M56f (done, but the broadcasters):** the classic adapter (`engine/hub/browse.hpp`): a game's
  own masters and status query read into the records as browsed nodes, `--browse-master` and
  `--browse-server`; Shooter's Edge's hub hands it the classic query. The `se-hub` lab lists five
  simulated classic servers from a stand-in classic master. The check against the real masters is
  by hand; the classic broadcasters' pages wait for the TV node (M57b).
- **M56g (the pages and the build done; the picture waits on the renderer's limits):** the web
  viewer: the engine's viewer in WebAssembly and WebGPU, the stream over a WebSocket, demos by
  hash, the Watch and Demo pages (the maintainer chose it, 2026-11-11). Done: the Watch and Demo
  pages (`engine/hub/watch.hpp`), the viewer's files and content packs served from
  `--viewer=<dir>`, the TV node's web port in its record; the `web` preset (Emscripten, the
  browser's WebGPU through `emdawnwebgpu`), `engine/web` (the viewer's arguments, the page's
  calls), `ge_add_web_viewer`, Shooter's Edge's viewer (`games/shooters_edge/web`: packs and the
  demo fetched, played at its pace, the world drawn on the canvas); the hub sends answers larger
  than a stream's backlog (a 6.5 MB wasm) whole. The `hub-viewer` lab measures a first visit
  (6.8 MB). Next, **M56g2**: the renderer within WebGPU's default limits, which a browser's
  software adapter (and Chrome on many machines) offers: 16 sampled textures a stage (the frame's
  group alone holds 11, so `post.draw` asks for 20 and `bsp.draw` 21) and 256 array layers (the
  shadow cubes ask for 288). Then the stream over the WebSocket, the transport, Fall Line's
  viewer. M56g2 part 1: `GE_GPU_LIMITS=default` (a native device on the browser's limits,
  validation on), the shadow cubes capped by the device's array layers, the cull's storage
  buffers down to 8. Part 2: the frame's group in lean parts per pass (`render::FrameGroup`), the
  NaN depth clear and the timestamp queries kept out of the browser; the viewer draws the match
  in headless Chromium. The `hub-viewer` lab takes the picture itself: it drives Chromium over its
  DevTools pipe and asks for the shot after a wall-clock wait (25 s; a software GPU takes about
  20 s to the first frame), since `--timeout` cut the wasm's fetch short.
- **M56h (done for demos; live waits for the TV node's radar):** the map for phones: the radar
  track (`engine/replay/radar.hpp`: rosters, positions four times a second, an outline; a track
  added to a finished file), the hub's SVG map (`engine/hub/radar.hpp`, `/radar/{sha256}`: a minute
  animated without script, or still at a moment with second steps; the Demos page's Map link), and
  Shooter's Edge's radar off a multiview recording with the level's outline (`demos pack` writes
  it). The `hub-radar` lab draws a two-minute bots' match frame by frame. Live matches get the map
  once the TV node carries the radar track.

Depends on the broadcast layer (`engine/tv`) and the replay file's version 2 (its header, meta
track and closing summary), designed by the proxy, TV and forward work (rows M57a, the replay file v2, and M57b, the native TV node, once that plan is chosen).
