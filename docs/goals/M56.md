Status: open
Milestone: **The hub (`engine/hub`, a community's website): every live game of a game listed with its scoreboard, servers and broadcast nodes announcing to a master, live matches watched, and every demo archived by its hash and searchable; generic in the engine, the game's words in its `HubSpec`, the classic game's servers read by an adapter**
Exit: A Fall Line and a Shooter's Edge hub, each one executable, list the simulated evening's servers and broadcasts, open a live match in the game from a link, and find a demo by a player's name
Labs: `hub-master`, `hub-api`, `hub-pages`, `hub-archive`

The design is [`docs/hub.md`](../hub.md); the facts about the classic community's hub are the
working note `porting/shooters-edge/hub.md`.

- **M56 (this PR):** the design and the clean-room note. The plan waits on the maintainer's
  choice of how a browser shows a match.
- **M56a:** the status record (`engine/server`) and the master for every node kind (servers, TV nodes, forwarders): heartbeats, the list, expiry.
- **M56b:** `engine/hub`: the poller, the index, HTTP/1.1 with server-sent events, the JSON API,
  `ge_add_hub`, the two games' `HubSpec`.
- **M56c:** the pages: Servers, Players, Scoreboard.
- **M56d:** the archive: demos pulled from TV nodes, SHA-256 names, the SQLite index and search.
- **M56e:** open in the game: each client's link scheme, `+tv` and `+demo` starts.
- **M56f:** the classic adapter: the classic masters, status queries and broadcasters.
- **M56g:** the live map: the radar track and the hub's SVG view.
- **M56h:** video for featured matches, or the engine in the browser (the maintainer's choice).

Depends on the broadcast layer (`engine/tv`) and the replay file's version 2 (its header, meta
track and closing summary), designed by the proxy, TV and forward work.
