`plugins/mountain/village/buildings.hpp` stands the village's buildings on lots along the streets
(M13g), the way a base village is built below its lifts:
- THE FRONT ROW, between the main street and the hub, turns its back on the street and faces the
  runs. From the square one way it stands the base lodge, the rental and the ski school. The other
  way it stands the ticket office and first aid. Then come up to three hotels.
- ACROSS THE MAIN STREET, facing it: the church opposite the square, two shops either side, then
  flats and houses.
- ALONG THE BACK STREET, both sides, facing it: houses, with flats on about a third of the lots.
- ON THE ROAD OUT: the garage and the pump house.

`building_rules.hpp` holds each kind's footprint and heights, as a mountain village's are built,
and the numbers the lots are laid by. Each building is tried at its lot and on along the street
until it fits:
- its footprint, and a metre round it, is off every street and open place (the ground mask);
- it is clear of everything the streets keep away from. This is `clearance_of`, now public in
  `streets.hpp`: the runs, the lifts, the hub's groomed snow, the pads;
- it keeps 4 m from every other building;
- its ground falls no more than 3.5 m across it. Its floor is the highest snow under it, with a
  plinth down to the lowest.

The trees on a footprint, and 3 m round it, are felled (`fell_for_buildings`). The gap between
roofs and the gardens left open are dealt off hashes of the seed; nothing is drawn from a stream.

The free ride's village (`games/fall_line/sim/village.hpp`) stands them when the run is created,
using the same pads as its streets. It fells their trees out of the run's tree index and keeps a
box round the whole village. `meet_traffic` now also holds each racer out of every wall, as it
holds him out of a parked car. A building counts as a body that never moves, as tall as its ridge
(the church's spire). Past 3 m/s of closing speed the skier goes down, a `building` event names
the building, and the sound plays a harder knock. `meet_body`'s quick test now uses the body's
diagonal, so a building wider than it is deep is not missed along its front.
