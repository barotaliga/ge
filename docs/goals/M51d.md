The snow's air (with M29's snow layer, `engine/snow/time.hpp`): the layer's `Weather::air`
schedule sampled off `air_at` at its place (the day's curve, the height, the night's cold pool),
`fresh_density` for the snow that falls, and `glide_of` and `snow_feel` for the contact's glide and
the sound (snow squeaks under -10 C). A thaw then a frost leaves a crust; a cold day's new snow is
lighter.

**As built.** `engine/snow/air.hpp` (`ge_snow` now links `ge_wind`): `air_c` and `air_schedule`
read the layer's air off `air_at` over a run of days at one site, `fresh_density_over` weights
`wind::fresh_density` by the fall, `weather_of` fills a `Weather`, and `feel_at` gives the feel,
the surface's temperature, the glide and the squeak. The `snow-weather` lab's `thaw` chart shows a
+5 C day then a -8 C day leaving spring snow at noon and a crust bearing 1879 N by the next
morning; its `light` chart shows the same storm adding 14.3 cm at -12 C (68 kg/m3) and 9.2 cm at
-1 C (99 kg/m3). On the way `surface_at` stopped jumping at sunrise: the night's radiative cooling
now fades over the two hours after it. Held by `tests/snow_air_test.cpp`. Next: M51e (what falls
and the water read the air).
