Moving ground, pushes and ladders (`engine/move/push.hpp`, `docs/movement.md` § Moving ground
and pushes):

- A trace's hit says what it touched: the solid's id, its velocity, its surface flags (`kLadder`)
  and its bounce. The box world carries a `Surface` on each brush; `move_brush` moves one.
- Standing, a body's velocity is relative to its ground (`MoveState::ground_solid`,
  `ground_vel`); `carry` moves riders and the bodies a solid overlaps, and refuses a crush.
  Leaving the ground adds its velocity, landing takes it off: a jump off a moving platform lands
  back on its spot (within 0.02 m).
- `push` (knock-back: leaves the ground, spends the coyote time), `launch` and `arc_velocity`
  (a jump pad's arc lands on its target at its apex at 60, 120 and 250 Hz).
- A surface's `bounce` throws a landing back while `impact * bounce` beats `unground_speed`
  (`kBounced`): the trampoline bounces three times and then lands.
- Ladders: probed along the view's heading (`ladder_probe`), climbed at `ladder_speed`, down by
  looking down, left at the top, jumped off along the normal (`ladder_jump`), backed away from at
  the foot (`kLadderOn`, `kLadderOff`). Three new knobs, 50 in all.
- Seven scenarios in `move-trace`, golden: platform-ride, lift, knockback, jump-pad, trampoline,
  ladder, pusher (`tests/move_push_test.cpp`, `tests/golden/move_scenarios.txt`).
