**Landed (first PR).** `engine/vfs` gained the one file inside packed or archived bytes
(`unpack.hpp`: gzip, zstd, xz, tar, zip with stored, deflated, LZMA or zstd entries, and 7z,
peeled by their magic up to four deep, an archive's files filtered by the caller's pick), the zstd
stream recordings are packed with (`zstd.hpp`), the xz and 7z readers and writers (`lzma.hpp`)
and the tar reader (`tar.hpp`). zstd 1.5.7 and the LZMA SDK 25.01 are pinned release archives
built into `ge_vfs` (`cmake/Archives.cmake`). Shooter's Edge's recorders pack as they record
(`.qwdz`, `.mvdz`, `.qwdvz`, `.mvdvz`; flushed every MiB so a crash keeps the match) and its demo
player opens a recording inside any of those wrappers, a readme beside it ignored
(`client/demo_pack.hpp`). Two cvars set it: `demo_pack` (1, on by default) and `demo_pack_level`
(19; `client/record_cvars.hpp`).

**The codec, measured** (`labs se-demo-pack --codecs=all --file=<a 4on4 .mvd>`, release build,
one x86-64 core; a 21.8 MB four-a-side multiview recording; the stock tools' gzip and bzip2 by
their command lines on the same file):

| codec | packed MB | ratio | unpack MB/s | packer MiB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gzip -9 (stock) | 3.05 | 7.1 | ~190 | - |
| bzip2 -9 (stock) | 2.92 | 7.5 | ~45 | - |
| zstd 3 | 2.76 | 7.9 | 1316 | 3 |
| zstd 19 (chosen) | 2.33 | 9.3 | 1548 | 90 |
| zstd 19 + long 128 MiB | 2.34 | 9.3 | 1599 | 274 |
| zstd 22 | 2.32 | 9.4 | 1681 | 834 |
| xz 9 | 1.95 | 11.2 | 134 | - |

zstd 19 with its own window is the packed formats' setting: 9.3 times smaller than plain, 24%
under gzip, unpacked eleven times faster than xz, and it streams with flushes (a crash keeps the
recording). The long window and a trained dictionary were weighed: the long window gained nothing
on recordings (they are at most tens of MB) and tripled the memory; a dictionary helps files of
kilobytes, not megabytes. xz stays readable for whoever wants the last 16%.
