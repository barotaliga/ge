## Progress

- 2026-11-12: the TV node (`engine/tv/node.hpp`): a source's replay file bytes in any pieces split into the header and whole blocks; the delay in game time; a viewer starting at the newest keyframe block out and kept at its own place when its link is full; blocks no one needs let go; chat up to the first node and back down to everyone; each relay's count added up the tree and the whole count told to every viewer; a source of another game or a viewer on another wire version refused. Over engine/net (`net_link.hpp`): a host, a viewer's end and a relay on one reliable channel. The trial (`trial.hpp`) and the `tv` lab: a source, a relay and six viewers under every simulated network preset, every viewer's stream whole from the keyframe block it started at, the lag of each block charted per preset, about 2.1 kB a match second a viewer for a 2 kB-a-second match.
- 2026-11-12: spectating by inputs (`engine/replay/spectate.hpp`), any play-contract game's run as a replay stream: a block a second of held inputs (grid indices, run-length) and the run's digest, a keyframe save (`save_run`) every few seconds; the `Spectator` loads the first keyframe it gets and steps its own run through every block, checking the digest. Fall Line through a source node and a relay (`tests/fall_line_spectate_test.cpp`, the `spectate` lab): every viewer under every preset reaches the player's digest at the player's last step. The inputs cost 227 bytes a second; the keyframes (a whole save, about 160 kB plain) dominate, 10.7 kB a second packed with one every 10 s.
- 2026-11-12: browsers, files and the master. WebSocket viewers (`engine/tv/websocket.hpp`: the upgrade, frames both ways, ping and close) served from the host's own node, a loopback browser-style viewer getting the whole stream (`tests/tv_web_test.cpp`); a node's finished files listed by SHA-256 and fetched in pieces, a files-only hello for the hub's archive (`library.hpp`, the `list`/`files`/`get`/`file` messages); the host listed on masters as a TV node and answering status asks with its name, game, map and viewers (`tv_host_list`, `tests/tv_net_test.cpp`).
- 2026-11-12: delta keyframes (`engine/replay/spectate.hpp`): every 30th save whole, the rest XORed with the save before, which the block's zstd packs to about 1.5 kB against 80 kB; a viewer that joins rebuilds the newest save from the whole one and the deltas and steps from there (`Spectator::skip_ahead`). Fall Line's broadcast over two minutes: 9.2 kB a second to 1.2 kB (the `spectate` lab, `--full-every`); `tests/fall_line_spectate_test.cpp`.
