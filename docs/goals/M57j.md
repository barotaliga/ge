## Notes

- **The format** (`engine/replay/series.hpp`, `docs/tv.md` § A demo series): replay files kept
  whole in play order, entries appended at the end, a cut file keeps its whole entries;
  `tests/replay_series_test.cpp`.
- **Playing** (Shooter's Edge): `open_demo` opens a `.geds` as its first recording and
  `demo_series_next` the next at its end; the demo player runs on into it as a new level.
- **Recording**: the window's `record <name>` writes `<name>.geds`, level after level, until `stop`,
  a disconnect or quitting (`client/listen_record.hpp`).
- **secli**: `demos split <series> [<n>...]`, `demos merge <series> <recording>...` (classic
  recordings such as `.mvd` packed as replay files first).
- **Next**: the server keeps a clan fight's matches (the same line-ups, one after another) in one
  series with `sv_autorecord`.
