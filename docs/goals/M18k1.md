Status: in progress

Built: `games/shooters_edge/hud/` (`shooters_edge_hud`): three looks over one element table
(`layout.hpp`), picked by `hud_style` (`classic`, `modern`, `compact`), each element the
player's to move, resize, fade, hide or re-parent with its `hud_<id>_` cvars. CLASSIC is the
status bar across the bottom centre (the armour's icon and figure, the portrait, the health, the
ammunition's icon and figure) and the inventory bar over it (the four ammunition counts, the
guns with the one in hand lit and a new one flashing, the keys, the powerups, the runes, the four
best frag plates), at a whole number of screen pixels a bar pixel and never wider than the
screen. MODERN puts health and armour bottom left, the ammunition bottom right, the guns with
their rounds bottom centre, the powerups down the right edge and the frags top right. COMPACT puts
health and armour on two arcs round the crosshair, the ammunition under it, the powerups under
that and the keys over it. The figures turn to the alarm figures when low (health or armour 25 or
under, ammunition 10 or under), the shield shows 666 over its disc, and the portrait follows the
health in five bands, winces when hurt and takes the cloak's, the shield's and the damage boost's
looks. `art.hpp` is the game's free art, painted in code at 4x; `classic_art.hpp` reads the
classic set's pictures (PNGs by the pack's own names, from classic-enhance's cache) into the same
`HudArt`, so classic-enhance swaps the pictures and owns no HUD of its own.

The engine gained (`engine/hud/pictures.hpp`, `layout.hpp`, `face.hpp`; `docs/hud.md` §
Pictures and parents) the `picture`, `digits`, `strip` and `ring` kinds, an element's `parent`
and `attach` (and their cvars), `unit`, `snap` and `fit`, and glyph-sheet faces; and
`ui::Box::nearest` (`engine/ui`, the GPU's 2D pass), the image's nearest pixel at a whole scale.

Measured: the `se-hud` lab lays every look out with no problem at 1280x720, 1920x1080,
2560x1080, 844x390 and 390x844, in both moments and both sets of art (`problems` 0).
tests/hud_pictures_test.cpp holds the kinds, parents, snap and fit; tests/shooters_edge_hud_test.cpp
the looks, the figures, the portrait, the flash and the classic set's loader;
tests/render_ui_pass_test.cpp holds nearest boxes on the GPU to the raster. Fall Line's HUD is
unchanged (tests/fall_line_hud_test.cpp, the `hud-layout` digest).

Left: the classic-enhance stage that writes the pictures into its cache (a pull request of its
own on the plugin); the HUD drawn in Shooter's Edge's window once it has one (with M10b); the
scoreboard and the menus (the rest of M18k).
