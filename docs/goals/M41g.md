Milestone: **The trained upscaler**: the textures' albedo upscaled 4× by a trained dual aggregation transformer (CC0 weights fetched at bake time into the cache, never committed), in C++ on the CPU and through compute shaders on the GPU
Exit: The CPU's output within 1e-5 of the original network's; the GPU's within an 8-bit step of the CPU's; the shareware pack's textures through it, before and after on a private page; the GPU's time on a real GPU
Labs: `enhance`, `gpu-upscaler`

The textures' albedo upscaled 4× by a trained network in place of the edge-following kernel: a
dual aggregation transformer trained on material textures (CC0 weights, 32.5 million parameters),
fetched by the tool into the cache at bake time and never committed.

Landed: `plugins/classicenhance/upscaler.hpp` (the network: `load_upscaler`, `open_upscaler`,
`upscale`, `seeded_upscaler_weights`, `UpscaleDevice`), `net.hpp` (its CPU layers),
`safetensors.hpp` (the weights file), `app/upscaler_gpu.hpp` with `app/upscaler.wgsl` (the same
network through compute shaders); the texture stage's `EnhanceOptions::network` and `device`; the
tool's `--upscaler`, `--weights` and `--device`. Tests: `tests/classicenhance_upscaler_test.cpp`
(the weights file, the load's refusals, a small seeded network against the original's values for
the same weights), `tests/classicenhance_upscaler_gpu_test.cpp` (the GPU against the CPU). Labs:
`gpu-upscaler`, and `enhance --weights`.

Measured: the CPU path gives the original network's output within 1.2e-6 on 48 × 40 and 64 × 64
pictures; the GPU's within 2.4e-7 of the CPU's on the tests' network and within one 8-bit step on
the trained one (on the software adapter).

Next: time the GPU on a real GPU (the Mac: `make lab LAB=gpu-upscaler ARGS="--weights=<file>"`),
and the shareware pack's textures before and after on a private page.
