Lab: `discord`. A run changing its line every 0.5 s against Discord's 5 updates in 20 s: 16 updates sent, 90 coalesced, none refused, every event owed delivered (two asks, a join, the app quitting and returning). Sending five at once and waiting out the window (the first version) left the profile up to 15.0 s behind; pacing one update every 4 s brought that to 3.5 s.

Not checked: a real Discord app (the protocol is tested against the simulated app and a real pipe; a live check needs a machine with Discord running).
