Status: in progress

Built: `rooms::Movers` (`plugins/rooms/movers.hpp`) reads a level's doors, plats, buttons, trains and `trigger_multiple`/`trigger_once` volumes, and steps them as a pure function. Doors that touch each other open as one, a blocked closing door reopens, and a plat carries its rider (`move::carry`). `movers_world` is the still level plus each part at its offset. The cloner writes a mover's `speed`, `lip` and `height` in metres. The vault's door opens from a trigger (the `rooms` lab's `vault-door` shot). `tests/rooms_movers_test.cpp` covers a door held open by a body in the doorway, a plat that lifts its rider, and a train that runs its corners. The `clone` lab counts each shareware map's movers (e1m1: 14 doors, 2 plats, 6 buttons, 17 triggers).

Left: secret doors, walls that appear or vanish, teleporters, counters, relays, `killtarget`, delays and messages; a scripted walk through a cloned map that presses a button, rides a plat and a train and takes a teleporter, held golden; the sounds named through `classic.assetmap` (M18n).
