Milestone: **Far lands**: distant land round a map seen through its sky faces once the eye is high enough (a 3D skybox): mountains to the horizon on the Earth's curve, lakes and seas, snow down to where the air is at freezing by a lapse from the kind's valley temperature, grass, forest and rock by height and slope, the ranges' own shadows and the far air's haze; no houses; eleven kinds (eight earthly, three alien) dealt per map off its sky; Shooter's Edge's `r_farland` and `r_farland_warm`
Exit: The far-land lab's eleven kinds by day, at a low sun and by night, the gpu-bsp lab's views of a classic map from high up with and without the far land, and the render far-land test
Labs: `far-land`, `gpu-bsp`

`engine/render/far_land.hpp` is a mesh at its true size round a site (the world's circle on the plan and its lowest point): a polar grid of 640 bearings by 384 rings out to 90 km, sunk on the Earth's curve, each vertex with its moisture and its horizon in eight bearings, and the water a second sheet at its level (`build_far_land`, about 250 ms on the pool for 246k vertices). The land is a level top under the world's floor inside its circle, so the pass ("far_land", after the world's opaque passes and the sky faces' fix-up, `greater`, writing depth) shows only through the sky. The shader picks the ground by height, slope, moisture and the air's temperature (the valley's at the kind's `temp_c` cooled 6.5 K a km: snow over `temp_c / 0.0065` m, ice on water under -1 C), lights it by the key light past its horizons and by the sky by the share it sees, mirrors the sky in the water with the sun's glint, and closes it toward the sky at the horizon past the aerial volume's 8 km at the kind's own visual range. `docs/rendering.md` § The far land.

The kinds (`render/far_land_kinds.cpp`): alps, fjord, highlands, winter, dolomites, sierra, volcano, arctic; xeno, ember, rime. `brush/app/bsp_sky.hpp`'s `far_land_for` deals a classic map's off its name by its sky's type (an Earth air one of the eight; violet, alien, toxic and nebula airs xeno; hellish and ashen ember; icy rime; a black sky, the airless type's, one of the eight as a plain night), and `far_site_of` stands it round the map's box. Shooter's Edge draws it in the modern look under our sky (`r_farland`: auto, off or a kind; `r_farland_warm`: C).

The `far-land` lab (no game data) draws every kind from 60 m over the site by day, at a low sun (the ranges' shadows) and by night, and alps 14 C colder (snow to the valley, the lake frozen) and 10 C warmer. On the shareware and registered maps (private pictures, the player's pack) the `gpu-bsp` lab's new `<map>-high` view and the `se-client` lab show it over dm3's, e1m1's and dm2's walls; the id maps' violet and red-brown skies deal xeno and ember, and `--sky=earth --night=0 --farland=<kind>` shows each earthly kind through e1m1.
