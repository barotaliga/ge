`engine/input` (`ge_input`): the device model, the event, the state, names, shapes, actions,
the bindings file, rebinding, the device database, touch controls, players, feedback and
force-feedback effects, the event log and the simulated backend; `engine/play/devices`.

Measured (`make lab LAB=input-session`, `input-ffb`, `input-shape`):

- One scripted session (keys, a raw mouse, a pad, a wheel with three pedals, a touch screen)
  drives a skier's, a shooter's and a racer's actions at once; written as an event log, read
  back and replayed, 0 of 192 frames differ.
- Two players join on two pads; one pad's cable comes out (`lost`) and a pad of its model
  returns to him (`returned`).
- A shooter's JUMP rebound by pressing the pad's east, its conflict (CROUCH) named.
- On the simulated rim, a spring (0.8) and a damper (0.25) bring a wheel let go at 0.8 back
  within 0.02 in 0.75 s with no overshoot; a spring alone rings; a constant pull held straight
  settles at -0.15.
