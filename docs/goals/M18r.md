Status: in progress
Milestone: **Bots on the simulation** (`games/shooters_edge/bots`, on `engine/ai`, after M18o and M18f): a bot is a client of the server like any player, its only output a usercmd a frame through the same move and the same game program, so it can do nothing a player cannot and every improvement to the simulation is one to the bots; navigation generated from the map on `ai/path` (where a player can walk, jump, fall, swim and ride), sight and hearing on `ai/sight` over the hull trace, aim and movement on `ai/hands` with skill profiles, item timing and fights chosen on `ai/choose`, the strafe-jump and the rocket jump as moves the bot plans; every decision recorded for `ge why`, humanness measured against people's input logs with `ai/human`; `ge bot` to run, compare and sweep them
Exit: Eight bots play a match on a free map for ten minutes with a golden digest; their moves and aim pass the humanness bands; a change to a bot shows its before and after in frags, deaths, items and humanness
Labs: `se-nav`, `se-pilot`, `se-bots`, `se-server`, `humanness`

After M18o (the loop) and M18f (the server's client slots): bots that play through the
simulation exactly as a player does, a usercmd a frame and nothing else, on `engine/ai`'s paths,
sight, hands and choosing, with every decision recorded for `ge why` and humanness measured
against people's input logs. The design is in `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Playing together.

Built in six steps, each its own pull request:

- **M18r1, navigation proven by the move** (`plugins/shooter/nav.hpp`): **done.** The floor
  sampled on a 32-unit grid off the map's own hulls, every link from a node proven by running
  `player_move` from it (walk, drop, or a jump on the move at full, half or a quarter speed, one
  flight with room to run up), movers left out and tagged on the nodes and links they close, the
  quickest route on `ai/path`'s A* over the proofs' times. Held by `tests/shooter_nav_test.cpp`
  on the nav course (`games/shooters_edge/sim/nav_course.hpp`: stairs, a crate, a pit, a ramp and
  a ramp too steep to stand on) and the test arena's doorway, its bits by
  `tests/golden/shooter_nav.txt`; pictured by the `se-nav` lab. A standing jump is not a kind:
  its 30 u/s of air control carries a player less than the 16 units its box needs to get over an
  edge, so it reaches nothing a moving jump does not. Riding a mover (a plat) waits for M18r2,
  which reads the mover's place at run time.
- **M18r2, the pilot** (`plugins/shooter/pilot.hpp`): **done.** A route played one usercmd at a
  time through `ai/hands`, the view's yaw and the movement axes on the protocol's grid, the jump a
  button: pure pursuit along the level walks, each jump link run at from a run-up point behind its
  start and pressed on its line at its proven speed, the strafe jump on long straight stretches
  (the view along the velocity, the side axis full), waiting stepped clear of a door that closes a
  link (and barring it after `wait_s`), riding the arena's plat (the graph now places a ridden
  mover at its travel's end and proves the ways off its top there), the stuck and lost checks
  planning again. Every decision on `ai::Why`. On the server it fills a bot slot through the
  command-source seam (`pilot_source`). Held by `tests/shooter_pilot_test.cpp` on the nav course
  (every route in about its proven time, human hands arrive, the strafe faster than a walk) and the
  arena (through the door, up the plat, the door barred, with both hands), its bits by
  `tests/golden/shooter_pilot.txt`; pictured by `se-pilot`. Open: human hands loop wide at
  corners (the pilot does not yet slow for one) and their forward axis reverses about 135 times a
  minute against a person's 120, both for a later pass with M18r5's `ge bot compare`; the
  pitch waits for aim (M18r3).
- **M18r3, senses within a client's view**: what the protocol sends a client (the PVS once maps
  have it, the entity fields sent), sight by the hull trace, the frame's sounds heard, memory on
  `ai/memory`; aim on `ai/hands` with skill profiles.
- **M18r4, fights and items** on `ai/choose`, on the game's scripted baseline mod (M18d): weapon,
  item timing, retreat, the rocket jump; every choice traced for `ge why`; `se-bots`.
- **M18r5, `ge bot run / compare / sweep`**, with `ge bot nav` beside them: a change to a bot
  shown before and after in frags, deaths, items and humanness.
- **M18r6, bots over a link**: the brain behind a client's packets on a slot M18f's server
  reserves, filling M18q's lobby.
