See its row in docs/goals.md and the plan in [M38](M38.md).

A classic map's own fog is read when it loads (`plugins/brush/app/bsp_fog.hpp`'s `bsp_fog`): the
`fog_volume` entities it places (a brush model's box, or an origin and a size, with the fog's keys)
and a glowing haze over every pool of lava, a faint green one over slime. Each volume's share of
the open sky (`FogVolume::sky`, measured by rays straight up through the map's faces) scales the
sky's and the ground's light in it, and the fog's sun is shadowed by the BSP renderer's own sun
map (`render::OrthoShadow`, `BspFogShadow`), so a closed hall's dust is lit only through its
windows. `bsp.wgsl` sees every face through `local_fog` under v2.

Lab `gpu-bsp-fog` on the fog fixture, each view without and with its fog: the hall's dust shows
the sun's shafts through its two windows and door (sky share 0), the haze over the store's pool of
lava glows orange; with the shareware pack's `start` map, the haze over its lava glows in the
corridor. Tests: `brush_bsp_fog_test` (the entity's keys and axes, a brush model's box, the hall's
sky share 0 and the yard's 1, the lava's and slime's hazes and their rows), `render_fog_test` (the
sky share scales the sky's light and keeps the extinction), `render_shadows_test` (an
orthographic map read as cascade 0).
