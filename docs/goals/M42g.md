Floor clutter by surface (`engine/detail/surfaces.hpp`, `props.hpp`): `classify_surface` reads a
texture's class (stone, dirt, grass, sand, wood, metal, tile, sky, liquid) off its name's words and,
when the level carries its pixels, its mean colour, so a green floor grows grass whatever its name.
A `PropRule` keeps to some classes and weighs the open sky against a roof, a point being outdoors
when a ray straight up meets nothing or a sky surface (`ScatterEnv`). `clutter_rules` lays grass
tufts on grass out in the open, sparse tufts on bare earth, twigs and branches under the sky,
pebbles and rocks on stone and earth, clods on earth, moss in the damp, debris under roofs, and
spent shells and bones on every floor. The new shapes are built in code in `prop_shapes.cpp`.
`detail-clutter` shows them over the test yard (`test_yard`); `tests/detail_clutter_test.cpp` and
its golden hold them.

The same round fixed the default moss rule, whose cushions sank deeper than they are high and so
hid under the floor (`detail-props`' digest and `tests/golden/detail_props.txt` moved).
