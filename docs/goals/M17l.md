Status: in progress
Milestone: **Anti-cheat** (`engine/net/guard`): the server's word over the client's (clients send inputs, the host steps the world; what a client may claim is named per game), every input checked on arrival (axes in range and on the tape's grid, at most one a step, no step from the future), interest so a client is never sent what it must not see (the fog of war, a player behind a wall), the lockstep digest as a cheat check (a peer whose state differs is out, M17g), position and speed held to what the step can do in peer games, a score, a ghost or a record checked by replaying its input tape to the bit on the server, a bot-likeness score off M16's humanness numbers, packets held by M17i's AEAD, and reports and bans kept on the accounts in `ge-service` (M17j); no kernel driver or client scanning, by design
Exit: Every scripted cheat in the lab (a speed hack, a teleport, an input flood, inputs off the grid or from the future, forged and replayed packets, a client fishing for hidden state, a planted desync, a forged score and ghost) is caught or refused; honest bots under every preset raise no flag
Labs: `cheats`, `se-cheats`, `se-aim-spread`

- The guard (built): inputs clamped to range and snapped to the grid, floods, views from the
  future, steps past what a tick allows; a score that decays and an out line; set on the
  replication server; the guard trial and the `cheats` lab over every preset (each cheat out
  within seconds, no honest player flagged).
- Refused (built): the trial's connections sealed with connect tokens, and interest at 45 m. A
  replayer and a forger send their own sealed packets again or changed; the node refuses them and
  counts them per peer, and none reaches the guard. A fisher reads every row it is sent, and no
  player is ever sent a row past its sight, on every preset.
- The seat astray (built): once a lockstep peer holds every seat's digest for a desync's step,
  it names the one seat whose digest differs from all the others'. In the `net-lockstep` lab, a
  desync planted on seat 1 of four is named by every peer on every preset.
- Runs checked by playing them (built): `play::play_audited` plays a tape to the digest its log
  records, or refuses it; Fall Line's hub reads every run through it, so an edited run or one with
  no digest is refused, and times are read off the run played out.
- Bans on the accounts (built): the match report carries a guard's verdict per player; a
  trusted server's flagged player is banned for 14 days, kept on the master's reports log, and
  refused at sign-in, login code and ticket with the days left; that match leaves the ladder alone.
- The classic server's speed check scored (built): what the speed check cuts off a client's
  commands is counted per player; once a second more than 50 ms cut is a flag on the engine's
  guard, and a player it puts out is flagged in Shooter's Edge's match report, so a trusted
  server's speed hacker is banned. In the `se-cheats` lab clocks 10% and 50% fast are out in six
  seconds; an honest player, a clock 3% fast, a two-second stall's burst and a bot are not.
- The aim spread (built): a bot-likeness number for a shooter's players. Where a firing player's
  line crosses another player's box, measured edge to edge; the spread of those points is near 0
  for an aim bot held on one point and about 0.8 for a hand. Kept per player on the classic server
  and carried by the match report as the `aim_spread` column, a number to read, never a ban. In the
  `se-aim-spread` lab both aim bots read 0.000; a mouse hand reads 0.63, a flicker 0.77, the
  server's bots 0.48 to 0.73.
- Next: the hub's player page showing the aim spread beside the match; a reference corpus of
  people's spreads (M16b) to set where a number is worth a look.
