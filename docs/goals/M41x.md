## Landed

- **The engine's thrown debris** (`engine/detail/debris.hpp`): a fixed pool, the oldest piece
  reused; each piece flies under a game's gravity, is cast against the receivers grid in 1/120 s
  substeps, bounces with its shape's restitution, friction and a seeded tumble, lies down along a
  floor and rolls about its long axis, rests, lies for its life and fades; a bounce over its
  shape's heard speed is a landing (where, how fast, on which face). Held bit for bit by
  `tests/golden/detail_debris.txt`.
- **The cases** (`plugins/classicenhance/shells.hpp`): the shotgun's hull, a rifle's brass and a
  heavy gun's brass; each one's bounce, a small model coloured from the game's own palette (never
  its glowing end), and its landing sounds made from modes by the surface's class, four variants
  each (`ce/shell/<case>/<class>/<variant>`). `ce_shells` (on) in the EFFECTS catalog and on the
  menu's weapon page.
- **Who throws, in Shooter's Edge** (`games/shooters_edge/client/shells.hpp`): the held gun by its
  model (Quake's shotgun a hull as the slide comes back, the double barrel two as it breaks;
  Quake II's shotguns, machine gun and chain gun), Shooter's Edge's own set by its weapon; another
  player in Quake by his shotgun's firing frames, Quake's soldier by his model; in Quake II every
  gun's flash value, a player's or a monster's (the tanks' and bosses' big guns heavy brass). The
  cases fall at the classic games' gravity, bounce off the map's faces, clatter by what they land
  on and are drawn as models; nothing reaches the simulation, and `ce_shells 0` throws none.
- **Quake II's hand sees every shot**: the server fires a frame and steps past it in one rules
  frame, so the shotguns (which fire on their first frame) were never seen firing by the held
  weapon; the hand now counts the frames passed since its last step
  (`the_hand_sees_q2_s_shotguns_fire_on_their_first_frame`).
- **Pictures**: `se-weapon-fx --side=shells` and the `se-q2-weapon-fx` shots `machinegun-shells`,
  `chaingun-shells` and `super-shotgun-shells` look down at where the cases lie; with the real
  packs (e1m2, q2dm1) on a private page only.
- **The mission packs**: their guns throw by the same tables. The base guns held in a pack keep
  their own models and so their cases; the carrier's machine guns (Quake II's packs) throw heavy
  brass by their flash values; the packs' new guns (Quake's laser cannon, hammer, proximity gun,
  lava nailgun, multi-rocket and plasma gun; Quake II's ion ripper, phalanx, trap, flechette
  rifle, plasma beam, disruptor and chain fist) fire no case. Held by `shooters_edge_shells_test`.
