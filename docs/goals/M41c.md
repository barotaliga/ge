Status: open
Milestone: **The cache drawn**: the BSP renderer reads a map's enhanced albedo, normal, roughness and emissive and its rebaked light from the cache when present, the original otherwise (`r_enhanced`)
Exit: A shareware map drawn from the cache in the named views; off, it draws as before
Labs: `bsp`, `enhance`

The BSP renderer (`plugins/brush/app`) reads a map's enhanced maps and rebaked light out of the
cache when `r_enhanced` is on and the cache holds them, and draws the original otherwise.

Progress: the game's window reads the cache. `plugins/classicenhance/cached.hpp`
(`find_cache`, `cached_texture`, `cached_skins`) reads a pack's enhanced textures (each at 1, 2,
4 or 8 times the pack's size, the glow from its emissive map) and its models' skins; the BSP
renderer's `TextureSource` takes a texture at any of those multiples (the atlas keeps six mips
and the faces sample it finer); Shooter's Edge's window (`client/world_draw.cpp`) loads the map
through it while `ce_textures` is on. Tests: `tests/classicenhance_cached_test.cpp`,
`tests/brush_draw_data_test.cpp`.

The relief is drawn: `cached_surface` packs each texture's normal, height and roughness maps into
the BSP scene's surface atlas (`Scene::surface`, through `TextureSource::surface`), and the modern
look lights each texel by its own normal under the torches, the lamps, the sun and the sky, gives
every light a GGX highlight by its roughness, and marches the eye's ray into the height (parallax
occlusion within 10 m, a shade in the crevices), switched by `ce_relief` and `ce_parallax`. The
`se-client` lab's `--game=fixture` draws it on the halls with an enhanced cache of its own, and
`--before="<cvars>"` draws a switch's before beside its after. Still open: the rebaked light from
the cache, and the `bsp` lab's named views from the cache.
