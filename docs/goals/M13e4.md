The skis meet the trunks before his body does (`plugins/skier/skier.cpp`'s `meet_trees`): besides the body's circle, a circle of `kTipRadius` (the pair side by side) at the tips, `kTipShare` of the pair's length ahead of the boots, and one halfway to them. A trunk met fast at them is a wipeout with his boots a ski's fore-length short of it, so the thrown body goes on over the stopped skis into it, or past it when it was off his line; one met slowly is a glance that turns the skis to the way the trunk left him. The skier's trunks (`tuning::wipeout`) and `tests/skier_scenarios_test.cpp` hold both: a trunk dead ahead stops the tips short of his body, and one met slowly at the tips 0.25 m off his line turns the skis aside instead of his body meeting it square.

The thrown body found a bug on the way (`plugins/skier/ragdoll.cpp`): a point the joints pressed back into the snow and the snow put back out gained the push as velocity on every pass, eight a step, and pumped a body that was lying still back up off the snow (1.5 m, two seconds after it lay down, in `tree-fast` with the tips). The first pass's push is still the step's fall onto the snow; every later pass's is a position corrected that carries no velocity, and a point put back off a trunk loses its way into it. `tests/skier_ragdoll_test.cpp` holds every scenario's body under 0.5 m once it has been down 0.3 s.
