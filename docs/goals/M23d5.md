Status: in progress

The fragmented MP4 muxer, moved out of M23d2: the software encoders write WebM (AV1 and Opus), and
MP4 is needed only for what WebM cannot carry, the platform encoders' H.264 and HEVC (M23d4).

Built (PR "capture: the engine's own fragmented MP4"):

- `engine/capture/mp4.hpp`: the muxer. `ftyp`, a `moov` with every track and no samples (`mvex`,
  a `mehd` patched on close), then `moof` + `mdat` fragments cut at a video keyframe after a
  second or 16 MB, and an `mfra` of every fragment's start. Sample entries `av01`/`av1C`,
  `avc1`/`avcC`, `hvc1`/`hvcC` and `Opus`/`dOps` (from the OpusHead), Opus's pre-skip as the
  edit list. Uncompressed tracks are refused (they stay in `.mkv`).
- `engine/capture/mp4_read.hpp`: the reader the tests and the lab take a file apart with; a file
  cut short reads to its last whole fragment.
- `engine/capture/mux.hpp`: the container picked by the file's name (`.mp4`, `.m4v`), shared by
  the export, the live capture and the interleave. `engine/capture/out_file.hpp`: the file or
  memory both muxers write and patch through.
- `fall_line --capture=PATH.mp4`, and `record --format=mkv --codec=av1` to an `.mp4` file.

Measured (`make lab LAB=capture`, 30 s at 24, 25, 30 and 60 fps): every MP4 in sync (worst flash
24.7 ms after its click at 24 and 30 fps, 8.7 ms at 60; every click 1.1 ms from its step), ffmpeg
decodes every picture of each; cut halfway through its last fragment, the reader keeps 336 of 721
pictures at 24 fps and ffmpeg plays 384. `tests/capture_mp4_test.cpp`: 7 tests, among them the
file cut at every seventh byte.

Left: the acceptance's "an export through M23d4's encoder decodes to every frame and its sound"
waits for M23d4; the `avc1` and `hvc1` entries are written but not yet fed by an encoder.
