Status: in progress
Milestone: **The compatible protocol** (`games/shooters_edge/net`): connectionless queries, the challenge and connect, the net channel, the reliable messages, entity and player deltas against the acknowledged frame, three usercmds a packet, the user info (name, team, top and bottom colours, `skin`), downloads (player skins among them), the first tier of extensions, lag compensation for hitscan; `ge net inspect --protocol=classic`
Exit: Compatible clients play on our server under every preset of the simulated network; `se-interop` against existing programs on loopback
Labs: `se-net`, `se-interop`

The server half was built in a clean room from the written specification
(`porting/shooters-edge/protocol.md`, with `gameloop.md` and the measured vectors). The building
session never read an existing client, server or protocol source.

**Built (the server half):**

- **The codec** (`games/shooters_edge/net/`, the `shooters_edge_classic` library):
  - `classic_wire`:
    - coordinates and angles quantized (rounding halves away from zero, held bit-exact by the
      quantize vectors);
    - the wider forms the extensions agree;
    - every field's reader and writer; strings that drop 0xFF and refuse a missing terminator;
    - CRC-16;
    - the move checksum over its 1024-byte key table, a wire constant kept for interop
      (`classic_checksum_table.cpp`; all 72 vectors and the 2048-sequence digest held);
    - the delta usercmd.
  - `classic_ext`: the extension families as one table, the first tier, agreement, the
    coordinate form they make.
  - `classic_info`: user and server info strings, names made safe and unique.
  - `classic_channel`: the net channel (sequences, the reliable bit and resend, the
    back-buffers, qport, rate and clear time, dupe copies, loss and choke counts).
  - `classic_delta`: entity records and lists against a frame (the number, model and entity
    doubles, transparency), and player records in the base and extended flag forms.
  - `classic_svc`: every svc message the server sends and every clc message it reads, as a
    table of layouts with hand-written forms for the rest; decode and describe for each.
- **The server endpoint** (`classic_server`, `classic_signon`, `classic_frame`):
  - It drives `QcServer` and the loop with real connections over `engine/net`'s transport.
  - Out-of-band answers:
    - ping, status and getchallenge with the extension bits;
    - connect with its checks: version, challenge, user info, passwords, a full server, a
      reconnect;
    - the rejects; rcon answered "not offered".
  - The signon: new, pext, the lists by pages, prespawn with the baselines and statics, spawn,
    the skins step, begin.
  - Downloads, skins first: the classic transfer and the chunked one (the first chunk in the
    packet, the rest out of band).
  - Each frame:
    - the mod's events routed;
    - the mod's raw bytes checked to read as whole messages before they reach a client;
    - the stats;
    - player records and entity deltas against the acknowledged frame;
    - the choke count;
    - ping and loss reported every 2 s;
    - timeouts.
  - Every move's checksum is verified by default (`ServerConfig::verify_move_checksum`); a
    wrong one is counted and its move dropped.
- **Lag compensation for hitscan** (`classic_antilag`, `ServerConfig::antilag`, off by default,
  `sv_antilag` in the server info):
  - a ring of 128 positions a player;
  - the shooter's acknowledged frame plus the lead;
  - interpolated, or extrapolated by at most the lead;
  - the mod server's `traceline` and `tracebox` call the `QcServer::lag_rewind` hook around the
    trace when the shooter or its owner is a client.
- **A scripted client** (`classic_client`), the stand-in for an existing client until ours
  speaks the protocol (M18h). It asks for its extensions three ways: in the connect, through
  `pext`, or not at all. It follows the signon, downloads the skins it lacks and plays the
  delta-coded frames.
- **The measured session** (`classic_trial`): the test mod on the test arena, served to scripted
  clients over the simulated network under a preset, with a digest that replays to the bit.
- **Tests:**
  - `shooters_edge_classic_wire_test`: the quantize and checksum vectors, the fields, usercmds,
    info strings, the extensions.
  - `shooters_edge_classic_messages_test`: every svc message round-tripped in three forms,
    against a golden of its bytes; every clc message; entity and player deltas.
  - `shooters_edge_classic_channel_test`, `shooters_edge_classic_antilag_test`.
  - `shooters_edge_classic_server_test`:
    - a full signon of three scripted clients under every preset, ending in play with the
      skins downloaded whole and no move unchecked, against a golden digest a preset;
    - the out-of-band answers and rejects;
    - a full server;
    - a bad checksum;
    - the rewind and its restore.
- **Labs:**
  - `se-net`: a table a preset of every client's stage, timings, bytes a second, frames and
    downloads, and the presets side by side with each session's digest. The server's bytes a
    second are charted over each session. `--capture=1` attaches the server's pcap for
    `ge net inspect`.
  - `se-interop`: a stub that reports "not run" with the commands it would run.
- **The test mod's explosion** now writes its `svc_temp_entity` byte before the kind, as the
  protocol requires. Without it, every client lost the rest of the packet.

**Remains:**

- `se-interop` against existing programs on loopback, which needs:
  - the dedicated server's `--classic` flag over a UDP socket (`server/bin/main.cpp`, owned by
    the host's row);
  - an existing client and server beside the checkout.
- `ge net inspect --protocol=classic`: a game-side decoder the engine's `ge` cannot link today.
  It needs a seam where a game registers a decoder with `ge`, or the game's own tool. Until then,
  `se-net --capture=1` writes a pcap that `ge net inspect` lists.
- Our own client on the protocol (prediction, interpolation): M18h. The scripted client here
  stands in for it.
- Rcon, uploads, spectator tracking (`ptrack`), the `ip` step, and the map checksum (no BSP is
  served yet).
- The QC Write builtins truncate coordinates where the protocol rounds them
  (`server/messages.cpp`). Changing that moves the mod server's own tests, so it is left to that
  row.
- Hitscan in the test mod: it fires missiles only. The rewind is tested through the hook. A mod
  with a hitscan weapon exercises the path in a match.

Next: M18h (our client on the protocol and demos), then `se-interop` once the dedicated
server's classic flag lands.
