Status: in progress
Milestone: **The compatible protocol** (`games/shooters_edge/net`): connectionless queries, the challenge and connect, the net channel, the reliable messages, entity and player deltas against the acknowledged frame, three usercmds a packet, the user info (name, team, top and bottom colours, `skin`), downloads (player skins among them), the first tier of extensions, lag compensation for hitscan; `ge net inspect --protocol=classic`
Exit: Compatible clients play on our server under every preset of the simulated network; `se-interop` against existing programs on loopback
Labs: `se-net`, `se-interop`, `se-teleport`, `se-q2-teleport`, `se-control`

The server half was built in a clean room from the written specification
(`porting/shooters-edge/protocol.md`, with `gameloop.md` and the measured vectors). The building
session never read an existing client, server or protocol source.

**Built (the server half):**

- **The codec** (`games/shooters_edge/net/`, the `shooters_edge_classic` library):
  - `qw_wire`:
    - coordinates and angles quantized (rounding halves away from zero, held bit-exact by the
      quantize vectors);
    - the wider forms the extensions agree;
    - every field's reader and writer; strings that drop 0xFF and refuse a missing terminator;
    - CRC-16;
    - the move checksum over its 1024-byte key table, a wire constant kept for interop
      (`qw_checksum_table.cpp`; all 72 vectors and the 2048-sequence digest held);
    - the delta usercmd.
  - `qw_ext`: the extension families as one table, the first tier, agreement, the
    coordinate form they make.
  - `classic_info`: user and server info strings, names made safe and unique.
  - `classic_channel`: the net channel (sequences, the reliable bit and resend, the
    back-buffers, qport, rate and clear time, dupe copies, loss and choke counts).
  - `qw_delta`: entity records and lists against a frame (the number, model and entity
    doubles, transparency), and player records in the base and extended flag forms.
  - `qw_svc`: every svc message the server sends and every clc message it reads, as a
    table of layouts with hand-written forms for the rest; decode and describe for each.
- **The server endpoint** (`qw_server`, `qw_signon`, `qw_frame`):
  - It drives `QcServer` and the loop with real connections over `engine/net`'s transport.
  - Out-of-band answers:
    - ping, status and getchallenge with the extension bits;
    - connect with its checks: version, challenge, user info, passwords, a full server, a
      reconnect;
    - the rejects; rcon answered "not offered".
  - The signon: new, pext, the lists by pages, prespawn with the baselines and statics, spawn,
    the skins step, begin.
  - Downloads, skins first: the classic transfer and the chunked one (the first chunk in the
    packet, the rest out of band).
  - Each frame:
    - the mod's events routed;
    - the mod's raw bytes checked to read as whole messages before they reach a client;
    - the stats;
    - player records and entity deltas against the acknowledged frame;
    - the choke count;
    - ping and loss reported every 2 s;
    - timeouts.
  - Every move's checksum is verified by default (`ServerConfig::verify_move_checksum`); a
    wrong one is counted and its move dropped.
- **Lag compensation for hitscan** (`qw_antilag`, `ServerConfig::antilag`, off by default,
  `sv_antilag` in the server info):
  - a ring of 128 positions a player;
  - the shooter's acknowledged frame plus the lead;
  - interpolated, or extrapolated by at most the lead;
  - the mod server's `traceline` and `tracebox` call the `QcServer::lag_rewind` hook around the
    trace when the shooter or its owner is a client.
- **A scripted client** (`qw_client`), the stand-in for an existing client until ours
  speaks the protocol (M18h). It asks for its extensions three ways: in the connect, through
  `pext`, or not at all. It follows the signon, downloads the skins it lacks and plays the
  delta-coded frames.
- **The measured session** (`qw_trial`): the test mod on the test arena, served to scripted
  clients over the simulated network under a preset, with a digest that replays to the bit.
- **Tests:**
  - `shooters_edge_qw_wire_test`: the quantize and checksum vectors, the fields, usercmds,
    info strings, the extensions.
  - `shooters_edge_qw_messages_test`: every svc message round-tripped in three forms,
    against a golden of its bytes; every clc message; entity and player deltas.
  - `shooters_edge_classic_channel_test`, `shooters_edge_qw_antilag_test`.
  - `shooters_edge_qw_server_test`:
    - a full signon of three scripted clients under every preset, ending in play with the
      skins downloaded whole and no move unchecked, against a golden digest a preset;
    - the out-of-band answers and rejects;
    - a full server;
    - a bad checksum;
    - the rewind and its restore.
- **Labs:**
  - `se-net`: a table a preset of every client's stage, timings, bytes a second, frames and
    downloads, and the presets side by side with each session's digest. The server's bytes a
    second are charted over each session. `--capture=1` attaches the server's pcap for
    `ge net inspect`.
  - `se-interop`: our scripted client against a live server (`--server=<host:port>`, the map
    read from `--basedir`, the session kept with `--record`); without `--server`, "not run".
- **The test mod's explosion** now writes its `svc_temp_entity` byte before the kind, as the
  protocol requires. Without it, every client lost the rest of the packet.

**Existing servers (our client joins them):** run in the container against an existing server
built beside the checkout (never in the tree), running the common match mod and, separately,
a class-based team mod as a classic progs: our scripted client signs on, enters the game and
plays (no delta misses), and the session renders through `-demovideo`. What it took:
the map's checksums (`qw_mapsum`: MD4 per lump; the five rebuilt released deathmatch maps
report the first release's value, measured), the model checks (`setinfo pmodel`/`emodel`, CRC-16
of the files), and the client obeying the `color`, `team`, `skin`, `name` and `setinfo` lines a
mod stuffs (the team mod drops a player whose colours are not its team's).

**An existing client on our server (2026-11-19):** the common modern client, built beside the
checkout and run in a virtual display (Xvfb, software GL), joins `sesv` running the common match
mod on dm4, enters the game and plays (`visitor entered the game`, seated, no drops) and draws the
map from the player's eye. Its window must be told a frame rate for the menu as well as for play
(`+cl_maxfps 77 +cl_maxfps_menu 77`): with the menu's left at its default the client spun without
reading its socket, on any server. The same client joins the existing server too, so both halves
of `se-interop` are checked live in the container.

**Remains:**

- `ge net inspect --protocol=classic`: a game-side decoder the engine's `ge` cannot link today.
  It needs a seam where a game registers a decoder with `ge`, or the game's own tool. Until then,
  `se-net --capture=1` writes a pcap that `ge net inspect` lists.
- Our own client on the protocol (prediction, interpolation): M18h. The scripted client here
  stands in for it.
- Rcon, uploads, the `ip` step, and the map checksum (no BSP is
  served yet).
- The QC Write builtins truncate coordinates where the protocol rounds them
  (`server/messages.cpp`). Changing that moves the mod server's own tests, so it is left to that
  row.
- Hitscan in the test mod: it fires missiles only. The rewind is tested through the hook. A mod
  with a hitscan weapon exercises the path in a match.

**The turn and the high-lag teleport (2026-11-23):** the server sends a `setangle` in a player's next
datagram when the rules turn him (`RulesCommon::turns`: a teleport while alive, else a respawn),
which it never did before, so a joined player came out of a teleporter facing the old way; under
the demo family's bit 0x02 it carries the kind and both sides turn the moves made before the turn
arrived (`classic/protocol/lag_turn.hpp`, `cl_pext_lagteleport`). Lab: `se-teleport`.

**The teleport foreseen (2026-11-23):** the window's prediction takes the level's teleporters
(`shooter/teleport_ahead.hpp`, `classic/teleporters.hpp`, `cl_predict_teleport`), for Quake and
QuakeWorld and for Quake II alike: the far end drawn and the view turned the frame the player walks
in (0 to 1 frames on dm6, e1m2, base3 and city1 at up to 50 ms each way, against 9 to 28 waiting
for the server), the server's physics untouched; a server that disagrees is believed two commands
later and the turn taken back. Labs: `se-teleport` (its `predicted` rows), `se-q2-teleport`.

**Full control through pushes and movers (2026-11-24):** the same audit for the other moments the
window could lag or yank the player, measured by `se-control` on real maps of both games at 0, 25
and 50 ms each way. Push triggers are foreseen like teleporters (`cl_predict_push`; e3m5's worst
miss 31 to 3 units). Doors, plats and trains run on through the replay at the speed the last two
frames show, stopping where they were seen standing, carrying their rider, and are drawn there
(`cl_predict_movers`, the shared `shooter/predict.hpp` `MoverTrack`): dm2's lift at 50 ms pulls
the player 79 units in all against 415 waiting; Quake II's movers step once a server frame and are
read off the newest frame instead of the drawn one (q2dm1's lift: worst 40 to 5 units at 0 and
25 ms). The correction a new frame makes now predicts the newest move over the movers as they
were, so a mover's own move no longer counts as a miss. Water was already clean in both games. A
mover that starts (a lift stepped on) is still learned a round trip late.
