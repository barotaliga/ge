Progress (2026-11-15): Shooter's Edge's hits now paint blood (ce_blood, `client/world_fx_blood.cpp`):
every blood message (Quake's and Quake II's, a match's, a campaign's, a demo's) sprays
by the blow that struck (worked out from the nails in flight and the players' aims) and presses
blood decals on the faces near along the blow and in a pool under the wound; a blast bleeds the
players near it. The decals are drawn as paint in their light (`render/marks.hpp`). Pictures:
`se-weapon-fx --target=1`.

Progress (2026-11-21): the blood gathers, runs and dries. What falls under a wound runs into the
pool already on the floor there, which is pressed again larger in its own slot and spreads on from
its edge (`blood_pool`), so a fight leaves one growing pool under the fighters; a big splash on a
wall runs down it in two to four drips ending in beads (`blood_drip`); every kind is darker and
darkens fast at first and then slowly as it dries, its gloss going first; a wet pool on a floor
mirrors the room with the faces (ce_reflections) by its gloss and Fresnel. `ce_blood_time` sets
how long blood stays (120 s; 0 until its place is needed) and `ce_blood_reflect` how strongly a
wet pool mirrors (1). Pictures: the `se-blood` lab on real Quake and Quake II maps, over a fight
and after it.

Progress (2026-11-21, later): the blood is on the body and flies as wounds bleed. A blow wounds the
body where its line first crosses the skin; a nail (and a point-blank load of pellets) goes through
and wounds it again where the line comes out. Out of the entry only a fine mist and a few small
drops come back toward the shooter; most of the blood leaves the exit along the line, heavier and
further, and splashes what is behind; an entry pours, drops falling down the body. Each wound soaks
the clothes round it and its blood runs down them over a few seconds, darkening as it dries
(render/morph.hpp's `MorphBlood`, fixed to the model's rest frame). A fresh wound lets drops fall for
8 s, which mark the floor where they land (a trail behind a wounded player, a pool under a still
one). A player who steps in a wet pool leaves six bloody boot prints a stride apart, fainter each
step (`blood_print`). The `se-blood` lab takes `--weapon` (pellet, nail, axe) and `--walk`, and
pictures the wounds from in front of the body and the prints from above. `ce_blood_amount` (0..3, 1)
scales it all: the drops thrown, the splashes and pools, the stains and runs, the drips; 0 none.
