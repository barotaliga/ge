Status: open

Brush detail on real levels and in their games, once the level formats are on main (#145): bevels
and props applied on load to the room format's brushes and the BSP families' (adapters from a
room brush or a BSP brush to a `detail::Solid` and from a built level to a `detail::TriMesh`, a
per-material bevel radius and a no-props flag asked of the level format's owner); receivers a BSP
model each, the world binned once at load and a mover's (door, platform, button) carried by its
transform so a mark on it rides with it; `shot_hit` and `bleed` called by Shooter's Edge's game
code (M18) and, for the shareware maps, by a played-back demo's temp-entity messages (gunshot,
spike, blood) and damage; the shareware maps' loader (classic-enhance's renderer, M41c) binning
the receivers when a map loads.
