- **M18q1, the lobby before the match** (`games/shooters_edge/server/lobby.hpp`, the listen
  server's half in `client/lobby_room.hpp` and `client/lobby_console.hpp`, the panel in
  `hud/lobby_panel.hpp`): **done.** Every match map opens in WARMUP: the player and the bots
  (`addbot`, the fighters of `bots/fighter.hpp`) on it, free to roam and fight, the frag and time
  limits held off; two teams (a seat joins the smaller; `team red|blue|auto|switch`, F2), shown in
  the user info's `team` and colours and the mod's `.team`; `ready` and `break` (F1 toggles), the
  countdown (`sv_countdown`) once everyone is ready and `sv_minplayers` are seated, stopped by a
  break or an unready join; the leader's `start`; then the map restarted for the match with every
  seat on its team, and back to warmup when the map changes. The window draws the banner, the
  count and both teams' roster (F3). Held by `tests/shooters_edge_lobby_test.cpp`, played through
  by the `se-lobby` lab.
