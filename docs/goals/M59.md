Status: in progress
Milestone: **Shared player accounts and stats: accounts and reserved names held by the master, logins on every server (ours and classic clients), match reports and each player's stats on the hub**
Exit: A player signs up once, logs in on two servers (one by our client's ticket, one by a classic client's console prompt), their name is refused to a guest, and their frags from both matches show on the hub's Player page
Labs: `accounts`, `se-login`, `match-report`, `hub-players`

The design is [`docs/accounts.md`](../accounts.md); the facts about the classic servers' logins and
stats are the working note `porting/shooters-edge/accounts.md`. One account across every game
(the maintainer, 2026-11-12): both classic games' stock clients log in on our servers with it, and
its stats are kept per game; a third classic game is not built, but nothing here is shaped by one
game. This row owns the classic login settings M58 held out.

- **M59 (this PR):** the design and the clean-room note.
- **M59a (done):** `engine/account`: the store (`store.hpp`), names folded per game
  (`names.hpp`), Argon2id (`net::password_hash`), the throttle (a hold doubling to 15 minutes, by
  account and by address), one-time login codes (a classic login takes nothing else: codes only,
  the maintainer chose on 2026-11-12), recovery codes, the log and its snapshot
  (`store_text.hpp`). The `accounts` lab: 400 sign-ups with every look-alike refused, logins by
  password and by code, a code typed twice refused, a day's brute force held to about 4 checked
  guesses an hour, the store read back.
- **M59b (done):** Ed25519 signatures and X25519 exchange in `net/crypto.hpp`; tickets
  (`account/ticket.hpp`), signed by the master and checked offline; the account service
  (`account/service.hpp`): sealed asks to one published key, create, sign-in, code, a server's
  login ask and recover, an ask sent again answered the same; hosted on a socket over the store
  and key on disk (`account/host.hpp`), run by the hub's `--accounts=<dir>`. The `accounts` lab
  runs 40 players through all four asks at 0, 10 and 30% loss (every ticket good, no account made
  twice, no code spent twice) and judges five tickets made wrong. Next: **M59b2**.
- **M59b2:** the game CLI's `account` group (sign up, sign in, a code) and the hub's Account page.
- **M59c:** the login seam in `engine/server`, the settings, a server with no `sv_accountmaster`
  hosting its own service, our client's ticket, the first classic game's prompt. The `se-login`
  lab.
- **M59d:** the sequel's prompt; one account on both games' servers.
- **M59e:** the match report, the replay file's closing summary, totals, trusted servers. The
  `match-report` lab.
- **M59f:** the hub's Player, Match and Leaders pages; the classic adapter's recent stats. The
  `hub-players` lab.
