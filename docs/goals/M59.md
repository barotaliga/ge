Labs: `accounts`, `se-login`, `match-report`, `hub-players`

The design is [`docs/accounts.md`](../accounts.md); the facts about the classic servers' logins and
stats are the working note `porting/shooters-edge/accounts.md`. One account across every game
(the maintainer, 2026-11-12): both classic games' stock clients log in on our servers with it, and
its stats are kept per game; a third classic game is not built, but nothing here is shaped by one
game. This row owns the classic login settings M58 held out.

- **M59 (this PR):** the design and the clean-room note.
- **M59a (done):** `engine/account`: the store (`store.hpp`), names folded per game
  (`names.hpp`), Argon2id (`net::password_hash`), the throttle (a hold doubling to 15 minutes, by
  account and by address), one-time login codes (a classic login takes nothing else: codes only,
  the maintainer chose on 2026-11-12), recovery codes, the log and its snapshot
