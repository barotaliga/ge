Built so far: the sky over a run as server cvars (`games/fall_line/sim/sky.hpp` over
`mountain/mapgen/weather.hpp`'s `with_sky`): `sv_weather` (a sky by its word, or the map's),
`sv_snowfall`, `sv_fog`, `sv_wind`, `sv_winddir` and `sv_cover`, each kept at the map's or the
sky's own while negative, and `sv_skyrate`, the sky's hours per hour of the run after `sv_hour`;
the player's `r_clouds` (the cloud march's share of its steps). Fall Line's app applies them to the
map before the run and moves the sun and the night as the sky's hour runs; the `world` lab's
`--cfg` photographs them as the game draws them.

Left: the run's options recorded in the input log beside the seed, so a replay and `ge sim +set`
agree; `ge cfg check` flagging a client cfg that sets a run's cvar.
