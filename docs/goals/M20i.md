Status: done

Built in `engine/services` and `engine/net`:

- `services/discord.hpp`: the `Activity`, its limits (`check_activity`), Discord's frames, and the `Discord` client (finding the app with backoff, the handshake, the subscriptions, the paced activity, asks and joins as events, `answer_join`).
- `services/sim_discord.hpp`: the simulated Discord app over the same frames, with the app's limits, its rate limit, and the player's and friends' actions.
- `net/local_pipe.hpp`: a local IPC byte stream (a Unix domain socket, a Windows named pipe) and its listener.
- The flat JSON reader moved to `core/json_read.hpp` (the play protocol keeps it as `play::`).

Tests: `tests/services_discord_test.cpp` (against the simulated app and over a real local pipe), `tests/net_local_pipe_test.cpp`.

Lab: `discord`. A run changing its line every 0.5 s against Discord's 5 updates in 20 s: 16 updates sent, 90 coalesced, none refused, every event owed delivered (two asks, a join, the app quitting and returning). Sending five at once and waiting out the window (the first version) left the profile up to 15.0 s behind; pacing one update every 4 s brought that to 3.5 s.

Not checked: a real Discord app (the protocol is tested against the simulated app and a real pipe; a live check needs a machine with Discord running).
