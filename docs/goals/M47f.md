The pack's shot models are named in classic-enhance's replacement table (`missile.<kind>`,
`plugins/classicenhance/replace.hpp`), and a model file's tracer bits give the same kinds to a
mod's own models; `client/world_fx.cpp`'s `monster_missiles` lays their trails, heads, lights and
flames each frame and keeps those gone for the splash where the spit or the fire bolt lands
(`monster_spit_hit`, the temporary entity's codes 7 and 8) or where a laser ended. The ogre's
grenade and the shambler's lightning already draw as the player's grenade and bolt (`ce_trails`,
`ce_beams`).
