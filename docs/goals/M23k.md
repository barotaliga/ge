Status: done
Milestone: **Screenshots**: F12 (a bind, the `screenshot` command) copies the shown frame back with no stall and writes it as a compressed PNG off the game's thread, named by the time, in the game's own folder beside its recordings (Shooter's Edge's `<game folder>/screenshots/`, Fall Line's per-user `screenshots/`)
Exit: F12 in either window writes the frame shown as a PNG that reads back to the same pixels, with no frame stalled; the PNG packs a frame well under the stored writer's size
Labs: `screenshot`

Built:

- `engine/capture/screenshot.hpp`: `Screenshots` takes the key's request (`request`, `due` once a
  frame), copies the pixels and the player's settings handed to `write` and returns; its own
  thread packs them and writes the file under a temporary name renamed over, named
  `<game>-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS.<ext>` (`-2`, `-3` in one second), and reports each file through
  `poll`. Four waiting is the most; a fifth is refused and said so. `add_screenshot_cvars`:
  `scr_format` png|jpg|avif|heic (jpg), `scr_quality` 1..100 (90), `scr_chroma` 444|420 (444).
- `engine/capture/still.hpp`: a frame packed by format, falling back when the build or machine
  cannot (HEIC to AVIF to JPEG), and read back by its bytes for the tests and the lab.
- `engine/capture/jpeg.hpp`: the engine's own baseline JPEG, an integer DCT so the bytes are the
  same everywhere (pinned by `tests/capture_jpeg_test.cpp`), 4:4:4 or 4:2:0; and a baseline
  decoder. ffmpeg's decoder reads its files to the same PSNR.
- `engine/capture/heif.hpp`: one AV1 or HEVC picture boxed as an AVIF or HEIC file (ftyp, meta
  with the item, its av1C or hvcC, ispe, pixi and an nclx colr; mdat), and read back.
  `Av1Options::still` (every picture a keyframe) and `PlatformVideoOptions::quality` (a constant
  quality in place of a bitrate) feed them.
- `lab/png.hpp`'s `encode_png_rgb`: the packed grey writer's row filters and deflate, over RGB.
  `encode_png_grey` writes the same bytes as before.
- Shooter's Edge: the default bind `f12 screenshot`, the console's `screenshot` and the `scr_`
  cvars, files in `<game folder>/screenshots/` (client/screenshot.hpp); the frame copied through
  a ring of two readbacks after it is drawn, in play and in a played demo. `draw_scores` moved
  from the window's main.cpp to client/scores.cpp to keep main.cpp under the line cap.
- Fall Line: the `screenshot` action (`key:f12`, the `app` context), the console's `screenshot`,
  the `scr_` cvars in its cvar table, files in the per-user folder's `screenshots/` beside `runs/`.
- Both register `screenshot` before the console runs the `+` lines, so `+screenshot` takes the
  first frame.

Measured (the `screenshot` lab, `--file` Fall Line's 1440p frame, quality 90): PNG 3769 KB exact;
JPEG 4:4:4 383 KB (10.2%) at 44.9 dB; JPEG 4:2:0 291 KB (7.7%) at 42.0 dB; AVIF 181 KB (4.8%) at
43.4 dB; HEIC 262 KB (7.0%) at 43.9 dB. Packing on the writer's thread, M3 Max: JPEG about 50 ms
(4:4:4) and 32 ms (4:2:0) at -O2, PNG about 300 ms for a grainy 1080p frame at -O2, AVIF about
0.4 s and HEIC about 0.5 s (their encoders are optimized libraries; the times are from the debug
build). In the Mac's windows (Metal), `shooters_edge --frames=60 "+scr_format <f>" +screenshot`
wrote a 1080p PNG (2.6 MB), JPEG (0.38 MB), AVIF (0.32 MB) and HEIC (0.44 MB) that macOS's own
`sips` opens and decodes to 40-41 dB against the PNG; `fall_line --frames=40 --bot +screenshot`
wrote its 2560x1440 frame to the per-user `screenshots/`.
