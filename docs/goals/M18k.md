Status: in progress
Milestone: **Input, console and 2D**: captured mouse with raw deltas, every key and the wheel, text input (`engine/platform`); `engine/console` (M21) grown to the full config language (`docs/shooters_edge.md` § Configs); bitmap fonts and palette pictures, the console view, the element HUD, the scoreboard, menus and the server browser
Exit: A corpus of advanced configs runs golden; a HUD config pictures as configured
Labs: `se-world` (the HUD views), `se-hud` (a player's HUD config before and after), console goldens

**The config corpus (done):** five players' configs as advanced as the ones players carry run from
startup through a script of keys and game lines, held golden with the config each saves
(`tests/golden/console_configs/`, `tests/console_configs_test.cpp`): weapon scripts (a rocket jump
on one key, a weapon wheel that wraps, a zoom that keeps the sensitivity), team reports on the
player's state and message triggers, a HUD layout kept as cvars the game does not know and written
back, an old config with no `then`, upper-case and older key names, and computed scripts. The
language grew for them: `if` without `then` after `a op b`, `=` `<>` `=~` `!~`, the expression form
`if (...) then` with `eval` and `set_eval` (`engine/console/expr.hpp`), `if_exists`, `set_calc`'s
`div`, `and`, `or`, `xor`, `pos`, `tobrown` and `towhite`, a division by zero storing 999, and the
older key names (`uparrow`, `lctrl`, `kp_home`, `~`).

Left for the row: the team message's percent codes and the font's fun characters in a chat line
(`%h`, `$[`, `$x41`), the event triggers (`f_newmap`, `f_spawn`, `f_death`, ...), and a HUD config
pictured as configured in `se-world`.

**The mouse taken on focus (done):** the window takes the mouse for the look the moment it gets
the focus (a click on its icon in the Dock included), and only the console lets it go by default;
`in_mouse_release` names what else may (a menu, a demo's play bar). The rule is the engine's
(`engine/input/mouse_capture.hpp`, `follow_capture`), held by `tests/input_mouse_capture_test.cpp`.

**Team chat and the game's events (done):** `say` and `say_team` fill in the percent codes from
the player's own state (`%h %a %A %c %w %b %p %u %R %l %d %L %r`, the gold-bracketed `%[h]`
`%[a]` `%[p]`, widths `%<5r>h`, `%%`), then turn the fun characters into the classic font's
glyphs (`$[ $]`, the gold digits, the lights, `$xHH`), and the aliases `f_newmap`, `f_spawn`,
`f_weaponchange`, `f_death` and `f_respawn` run on those events (`tp_triggers`,
`tp_forceTriggers`) (`games/shooters_edge/client/team_chat.hpp`, held by
`tests/shooters_edge_team_chat_test.cpp`; the facts in `porting/shooters-edge/team-messages.md`).
Not yet: what the player took and points at (`%i %t %x %y` and the rest say `nothing` for now),
the player counts behind a server's leave (`%e %o %s`), `f_took`, `f_mapend`, `f_demostart`,
`f_demoend`, `f_cfgload` and `f_exit`, the canned `tp_msg*` messages, and the fun characters in
`echo`.

**A HUD config pictured as configured (done):** `se-hud` runs a player's HUD config (`--cfg=<file>`,
a sample by default) over the modern look and draws the HUD before and after it at every viewport
(`configured-before-*`, `configured-after-*`); every element the config names must move or go, or the
lab fails (`configured.named`, `configured.moved`). The sample moves the frags to the top left and
the powerups to the left edge, scales the HUD to 0.9 and turns the notify lines off.
