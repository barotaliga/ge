**In progress.** Done here (the engine mover's classic row):

- **The row** (`plugins/shooter/row.hpp`): `classic_rows(settings)` builds the walk, fly and
  spectator rows of `engine/move` and the kinematic swim of `engine/water/swim` from the server's
  settings in map units; `row_command` runs one classic command through them (the 50 ms split,
  swimming up on the jump button, the water jump over a lip). The engine gained the knobs the
  classic numbers needed, each off by default so no other row moves: `gravity_lead`,
  `land_clip_speed`, `friction_whole`, `clip_from_start` (`engine/move/spec.hpp`), and
  `carry_along` (the body moved along a velocity something else decided); the swimmer gained
  `along_wish` and `feet` (`engine/water/swim.hpp`).
- **The brush world** (`plugins/shooter/hull_world.hpp`): the mover's trace answered by brush
  models' clip hulls (hull 0, 1 or 2 by the box), the map's z-up axes turned into the engine's;
  a model's hull 0 walked for its water, slime and lava leaves, laid as `engine/water` volumes the
  swimmer reads. It reads `BrushModel` only, so a compiled map (M18b) answers it unchanged.
- **Held**: every line of 28 of the 35 recorded scenarios (3443 lines) within the recordings' own
  tolerance (0.01 unit, 0.05 unit/s) with on-ground, the ground and the water equal, both one
  command at a time from the recorded state and as whole runs (worst 1.4e-4 unit and 7.9e-4
  unit/s, `tests/shooter_row_recording_test.cpp`); the scripted runs on the fixture worlds held
  to their bits (`tests/golden/shooter_row.txt`, `tests/shooter_row_test.cpp`); the hull world's
  trace equal to the classic `player_trace` on 2100 random sweeps
  (`tests/shooter_hull_world_test.cpp`). The `pmove` lab tables and charts the row.
  Not covered, by design: the old spectator, unsticking, `pm_ktjump` 0, `pm_slidefix`,
  `pm_airstep` with `pm_pground`.
- **The easy jump** (`cl_speedjump`, `cl_speedjump_view`, `pm_speedjump`; `docs/movement.md` §
  The easy jump): forward and jump held, the client strafes for the player on every server
  (`plugins/shooter/speedjump_assist.hpp`: the jump let go in the air, the side key and a weaving
  sent yaw that gain the server's `pm_speedjump` share of the best strafe's gain: 0.8 on the
  listen server, 0 on a dedicated one unless set, 0.8 where a server says nothing). The hops
  weave up to 45 degrees either side of the aim (people sweep 60-90 a flight), the mouse steers them, the pitch does not;
  `cl_speedjump_view` (0..1) is how much of the swing the screen shows. The move never reads
  `pm_speedjump`. Held by `tests/shooter_speedjump_assist_test.cpp`,
  `tests/shooter_pmove_test.cpp` and the `se-speedjump` lab (the move alone, the weave and the
  steer from above, the game with the view shown and still).
- **The rocket jump helpers** (`+rjump`, `+rrjump`; `docs/shooters-edge.md` § The rocket jump
  helpers): one press asks the server for a rocket jump (impulse 164, or 156 for the reverse one,
  with the jump); the server's rules rewrite that one command (`plugins/shooter/rjump_assist.hpp`:
  the shot 80 degrees down, or turned half round at the player's own pitch, attack and jump held)
  on the first command the launcher can fire, the launcher chosen for it, his screen never
  turned. Gated per server (`sv_rjump`, `sv_rrjump`, 0 by default, on in the window's own match)
  and per player (the `*rjump 1` grant), refused in a game's words; the same code on the first
  game's rules and the sequel's (`games/shooters_edge/server/rjump_rules.hpp`). Held by
  `tests/shooter_rjump_assist_test.cpp`, `tests/shooters_edge_rjump_test.cpp` and the `se-rjump`
  lab (dm2 and q2dm1: the heights, the push, the refusal, the eyes at the top).
- **The server's movetypes** (walk, step, toss, bounce, fly, push, noclip) and touching were
  already built by M18o2 (`plugins/shooter/physics.hpp`, `push.hpp`); nothing was missing.

Water movement is not written here: the engine's swimmer (`engine/water/swim`, M24d) has a
kinematic regime built for this game's feel, and `plugins/shooter` holds the classic preset
(its numbers) and maps a brush world's water, slime and lava contents onto `engine/water`
volumes. The `pmove` lab's water entry and swim cases hold the preset.

The numbers the engine's own tests and `swim` lab use for a first-person preset (units of 32 a
metre: run 320, the swim's share 0.7, accelerate 10, friction 4 a level, idle sink 60, the water
jump 225 up and 50 forward for 2 s under a gravity of 800) are a starting point; the preset this
game ships is `classic_rows`' swim, measured against the recordings: the idle sink 60 at the
swim's 0.7 (42 a second), the water jump 310 up and 50 out for 2 s.

The classic player move itself, its clip hulls and the parity against recordings are M18o1
(`docs/goals/M18o.md`).
