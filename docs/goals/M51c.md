Fog from the air, drawn (with M38's areas of fog, `render/fog.hpp`): a world asks `mist_at` at
the sites that matter (a lake's shore, a valley's floor, a summit) and hands each answer to the
fog as a `FogVolume`: `density` its extinction at the floor, `depth` its height (a valley's fog
laid level from the floor, like water), `noise` its carving and `rise` the steam's wisps. Fall
Line's lakes and valleys first; a clear, still dawn by a lake fogs, a windy or overcast one does
not.
