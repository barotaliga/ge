Status: open
Milestone: **Classic game profiles**: Shooter's Edge as a shell that loads the classic games the player owns, each a profile (its game directories and packs, its formats, its default program and movement, its content rules) chosen independently of the program, the movement and the arsenal (weapons and armour, each swappable on its own), so games can be mixed; the classic game's profile first, its sequel's (BSP 38, its models, its rules as a C++ mod written clean-room, its own move) the second, the third's later
Exit: The classic game's profile in data with no game name in the shell or the loop; a second profile loaded from the sequel's demo pack showing a map in the window; a remix (one profile's map with another's movement) played by a bot with a golden digest; the classic campaign's first map played on our campaign variant with the sequel's weapons and, separately, its armour
Labs: `se-profiles`, `bsp`, `pak`, `gpu-bsp-cutout`, `se-skins`, `se-pictures`, `se-held`, `se-installs`

The design is `docs/shooters-edge.md` § Profiles. Steps:

- **M18t1, the profile row**, in two halves. The CONTENT half (where the files are and what
  kind each is) is `plugins/classicenhance/profile.hpp`'s `kProfiles`, shared by classic-enhance
  and the game: **landed** for classic-enhance (every stage finds its files through
  `Source::profile`; the first game's digests unchanged). (a) **Landed** for the client and its labs:
  `client/files.hpp` is `engine/vfs`'s search path over the profile's base folder with a mod
  folder (`-game`) mounted over it, pk3s read too, and classic-enhance's cache found by every
  base pack (the full game's cache was missed); the dedicated server's folder comes with (c).
  (b) **Landed** for the renderer and the light bake: the map readers mark sky, liquids (with
  teleports) and animation in the map view (`brush::Texture`'s `sky`, `liquid` and `next`), and
  nothing past the reader reads a name; classic-enhance's own material rule by name
  (`texture.cpp`) is still to move. (c) **Landed** for the window: `games/shooters_edge/profiles/game_profile.hpp`'s rows give
  each mode's program files (`qwprogs.dat`, then `progs.dat`, for multiplayer) and the
  fallback map, and the window takes its start map, its full-game rule and the base folder's
  name from the content row; still to come, the dedicated server reading files through the
  search path (its `base_dir` is held equal to the content row's by a test) and the default
  movement. Then, in order: (c) the rest of the GAME half
  (the default movement, the dedicated server's files, the menu's map list) read by the window,
  the menu and the server; (d) the item and weapon names the bots,
  HUD and arsenal know: **landed** for the bots (`games/shooters_edge/profiles/items.hpp`: a
  map's items by class name, their call and heard words, the sounds a listener reads, carried
  by the profile row; `bots/` reads them); **landed** for the pickups' prints (the profile's
  `names`, the server's local info under each class name, read by the baseline mod's `name_of`:
  "You got the nailgun"); **landed** for everything else the server prints (the profile's
  `words`, `profiles/words.hpp`: the deaths, the clock, the end, the powers wearing off and the
  lobby with its countdown's centre print in the game's words and colours, the `words` builtin and
  `server/announce.hpp`; lab `se-words`); the HUD's and the arsenal's names move with M18t6.
  (e) **Landed**: the game adapter (`games/shooters_edge/client/game_adapter.hpp`, § The game
  adapter): one set of calls per classic game for its mods, its programs (each file with its
  form and side, a form with no runner named and passed over), its maps, its maps' textures and
  its demos; the first game's in `client/classic_adapter.cpp`, the window's `mods`, `maps` and
  `demos` commands on it. (f) **Landed**: a PNG or TGA in the game's files stands in for a
  map's texture by its name, its alpha cutting the face, and a fence texture's index 255 does the
  same (`Scene::cover`, lab `gpu-bsp-cutout`). (g) **Landed**: the models and the sounds behind
  the adapter (`open_model` into the shared forms, a PNG or TGA beside a model standing in for a
  skin with its see-through texels holes, lab `se-skins`; `open_sound` for a demo's sounds).
  (h) **Landed**: the HUD's and the menus' pictures behind the adapter (`pictures`), a PNG or TGA
  of a picture's name standing in at its size or a whole multiple of it (lab `se-pictures`).
  (i) **Landed**: config files and place names behind the adapter (`find_config` behind the
  console's `exec` after the player's own folders, `read_configs_from`; `open_locations` into
  `ge::shooter::Locs`, which the team match and lab `se-team` take parsed). (j) **Designed**: the
  rules runner (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § The rules runner): its calls, the shared view every
  reader moves onto, and the five steps it lands in. Steps 1 to 4 **landed**: the rules' world
  (`server/rules_world.hpp`) the QC server fills, read by the wire, the recorders and the bots,
  and the runner (`server/rules_runner.hpp`) the hosts, the campaign and the dedicated server
  hold; step 5 (the sequel's runner) is M18t4 below.
  (k) **Landed**: the game's own held weapons behind the adapter (`held_weapons`, the profile
  row's `held_weapons` files), held at the eye (`Hand::at_eye`), all or none and never
  Shooter's Edge's own in their place (lab `se-held`). Mixing games is classic remix (M18u).
  (l) **Landed**: the main menu, the modes and the HUD's pictures are the game's own: the
  adapter's `menu` (a `menu::MenuTable` of pages and rows) and `modes` (single player,
  multiplayer), and `hud_art_of` (the `hud_art` choice removed: a loaded classic game draws its
  own pictures, never the free set).
  (m) **Landed**: the re-release's own program and maps, which print text KEYS (`$map_...`,
  `$qc_...`) with arguments: the release's words by key read from its archive beside the game
  folder (`client/files.hpp`'s `localized_text`, `plugins/progs/localized.hpp`) and put in by
  the server before a print, a centre print, a written string or the level's name leaves it;
  its builtins bound by number or by name (`server/catalogue.hpp` § The re-release's program,
  `progs::bind_by_name`); its achievement message stepped over by the client.
- **M18t2, research for the sequel**: **landed**: a clean-room note set in
  `porting/shooters-edge/` (`sequel.md` the index; `sequel-formats.md`, `sequel-movement.md`,
  `sequel-rules.md`, `sequel-monsters.md`) for its packs, maps, models, game rules and move, by a

  (`vectors/sequel-pmove.txt`), recorded outside the tree over hand-built BSP 38 worlds, as
  M18t4's first step.
- **M18t3, the sequel's content**: its maps (already read by `plugins/brush`) and models drawn.
- **M18t4, the sequel's rules** as a C++ mod on `engine/mod`, and its move as a third
  `sv_playermove` value. **In progress**: (a) **landed**: a version 38 map's brushes read
  (`plugins/brush`: brushes, sides with their surfaces, each leaf's brushes) and the box trace
  through them (`trace.hpp`, golden `tests/golden/brush_trace.txt`), with `write_brush_world` for
  hand-built worlds. (b) **landed**: the sequel's player move over that trace
  (`plugins/shooter/sequel_move.hpp`, golden `tests/golden/shooter_sequel_move.txt`). (d)
  **started**: the rules as a second runner (`games/shooters_edge/server/sequel_server.hpp`, form
  `"sequel"`, golden `tests/golden/shooters_edge_sequel_server.txt`): the map from its brushes,
  spawn points, players moved per command, the 10 Hz frame, `kill` and the respawn, the world
  filled. Next: (d) items, weapons and damage, then movers; the host picking the runner by the
  profile's program form, with the move as `sv_playermove sequel`; (c) the parity vectors once
  recorded, with a `sequel-move` lab.
- **M18t5, remixes**: any profile's content with any program and movement the content can carry,
  checked at load (a program's precaches must resolve in the content).
- **M18t6, the arsenal**: weapons and armour as data rows per profile (models, sounds, icon, ammo,
  fire, damage; armour's absorption), `qc/common/` reading them, `sv_weapons` and `sv_armour`
  choosing a set each, a map's item names mapped to slots so another set fills them, and the
  load check naming a missing pack (`docs/shooters-edge.md` § Weapons and armour). The data shape
  lands with our campaign variant (M18s) so nothing there hard-codes a weapon. (a) **Landed**: the
  rows (`games/shooters_edge/profiles/arsenal.hpp`: a weapon's slot, fire kind, ammunition, cost,
  refire, pellets, damage, spread, reach or speed, life, splash, view model, sounds and missile; an
  armour's share, amount, skin, model and sound; the classic game's set carried by its profile
  row), given to the program as server settings (`arsenal_weapon1..8`, `arsenal_armour1..3`,
  `arsenal_lines`) and read by `qc/common/` in both program forms (`cvar_string`, the numbers
  parsed by `number_of`); a slot with no row keeps the program's own, which a test holds to the
  classic set's text. (b) **Landed**: a weapon lying on a map takes its slot's row (the model it
  lies as and the ammunition with it), its class name naming the slot; a row with no pickup
  leaves the map's own. Next: (c) `sv_weapons` and `sv_armour` choosing another profile's set once a second set
  exists (M18t3), with the load check naming a missing pack (M18t5).
- **M18t7, installs as their bundles lay them out**: (a) **Landed**: the window started inside a
  classic install plays it at once; placed above several, it finds each by its folder's name (any

  the first full game (`client/install_search.hpp`, `games`); the install's configs run in its bundled
  client's order with that client's folder on the search path (`client/install_configs.hpp`);
  place names made in the game (`addloc`, `removeloc`, `saveloc`, `loadloc`, `clearlocs`, `loc`:
  `client/place_names.hpp`); lab `se-installs`. Facts: `porting/shooters-edge/installs.md`,
  `loc-editing.md`. (b) **Landed**: the sequel's bundled client's order (`config.cfg`, its
  autoexecs, `postexec.cfg`, `postinit.cfg`) and its `loc add|del|set|list|save`; its place
  names already share the first game's units (eighths). (c) **Landed**: a load order per game
  (`client/load_order.hpp`): the sequel's folders rank packs and zips (`.pkz`) over loose files,
  `pak*` by number, the rerelease's root archive under them; its `default.cfg` from its packs; its
  startup steps from the table. (d) **Landed**: `loc_dist` (the profile's `place_range`: 500
  in the sequel, no limit in the first game) for the nearest place; the place names read and
  edited on a server joined and in a demo. Loose files keep matching in any letter case (decided:
  an install copied between systems still loads). (e) **Landed**: the sequel's place macros,
  `$loc_here` and `$loc_there` (the view's line through either family's world,
  `client/map_line.hpp`). (f) **Landed**: in every classic game `loc_dist` 500, place-name
  files and loose files in any letter case, `.pkz` read, packs of any other name after the
  numbered ones by name and `_` ones set aside; recordings keep their place names (`//loc` lines)
  and a demo plays with them; `say`/`say_team` with `%l`. Next: the sequel's recordings behind
  the menu (`attract`).
