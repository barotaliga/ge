Muzzle fire: `engine/render/muzzle.hpp` (the looks: 19 named knobs for the flame, the puff, the
sparks and the light, set by name and clamped, "smoke.<knob>" for the puff's own; the presets
`muzzle.shotgun`, `muzzle.nailgun`, `muzzle.grenade`, `muzzle.rocket`; the flame, the puff, the
flash and the sparks as pure functions of the shot, the muzzle's pose and the clock), drawn by the
explosions pass as a ball stretched along the barrel (an ellipsoid bound, prongs round the axis,
hotter at the base, `glow`); a blast's puff carried by its velocity and slowed by the air
(`Blast::v`, `kBlastDragS`). Held by `tests/render_muzzle_test.cpp`; pictured and priced by
`gpu-muzzle`.

A held weapon's muzzle into the world as the flame's pose: `muzzle_to_world` (engine/viewmodel,
held by `tests/viewmodel_place_test.cpp`).

In Shooter's Edge's window, a weapon that fires every frame (the classic nailguns and the
lightning gun, `HeldClip::frame_a_shot`) steps its firing frames one a shot and holds each until the
next, so the nailgun's two barrels take turns and the super nailgun's cluster turns one barrel a
shot, each shot's muzzle, flash and puff on the barrel its frame fires; the pack's own weapons fire
the classic table (`classic_fire_of`) at their true size, a nailgun's a breath of haze and no
flame. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_view_test.cpp`; pictured by `se-weapon-fx --weapons=spiker,heavy`
on the pack.

The rounds in flight (`engine/render/haze.hpp`, docs/rendering.md § Heat haze): a nail's flight
leaves a thread of hot air and a rocket's motor a wide rising wake, each bending what is behind it
by a few milliradians for a fifth of a second and most of a second; laid a stretch a frame by
Shooter's Edge under `ce_trails`. Held by `tests/render_haze_test.cpp`.

A rocket in flight lights the world with its motor's fire in the enhanced look
(app/weapon_fx.hpp's `motor_light`): a warm flame's light at the nozzle, wavering at a flame's 8 to
12 Hz, reaching 6.5 m, where the classic look keeps its steady white light on the rocket. Held by
`tests/shooters_edge_weapon_fx_test.cpp`; pictured by `se-weapon-fx --weapons=rockets
--flight-times=40,90,140`.

Each of the classic pack's firearms is given its look (classicenhance/weapons.hpp's
`classic_fire_of`) and fired off its own model in `classic-weapons-fire`, its own flash hidden
through `DrawLit::hidden`. In the game they fire it at their true size, and with NO SMOKE
(`app/muzzle.hpp`'s `fire_looks` turns every look's off, 2026-11-10): a shot is its flame, its
light and its sparks; a launcher's smoke is its round's trail and its blast's.

A rocket's launch as it looks (visual only; app/weapon_fx.hpp's `launch_offset`). It pops out of
the tube slowly and drops a little, its motor lights 50 ms later, and it speeds up to catch its
true place on the server's straight line by half a second. The shooter's own sway carries it off
the line a moment (his movement across it, and his aim's swing: it lags behind a turn,
`launch_kick`), and it wavers a few centimetres off its own seed. Its model, flame, trail, light
and haze all follow it. Held by `tests/shooters_edge_weapon_fx_test.cpp`; pictured by
`se-weapon-fx --weapons=rockets --flight-times=20,40,...`.
