The camera core is built: `engine/camera` (`ge_camera`, published as `ge::camera`) with the pose
and `FrameView`'s roll, the followers, the subject and the world's queries, the rigs (first
person, boom, mounted, orbit, planted, free), trauma shake and comfort, cut, blend and
inertialize between warm rigs, and the stack. Eleven scripted subjects run every rung of a
reference ladder; `tests/golden/camera_scenarios.txt` holds their bits.

The labs land with it and run in `make labs-smoke`: `camera-follow`, `camera-rigs`,
`camera-transitions`, `camera-shake`. Two findings of theirs are fixed in this row: the
follower now solves each frame exactly (a semi-implicit step strayed by a fifth of a step at
30 Hz; now within 1.3 %), and an inertialized change keeps all of the eye's velocity (the
animation method's projection dropped up to 10 m/s at the switch).
