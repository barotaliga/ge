Status: done

Ten sky types (`render/sky_style.hpp`): earth, dust, alien, hell, void, nebula, violet, ice, ash and toxic, picked by `sv_skytype` in Fall Line and turned by eleven knobs (`sv_skyair`, `sv_skydust`, `sv_sunsize`, `sv_sun2`, `sv_planet`, `sv_planetdir`, `sv_planetel`, `sv_ring`, `sv_nebula`, `sv_skystars`, `sv_skyglow`). The Earth's style is neutral to the bit (every scale 1, every extra 0, the dust's asymmetry and height kMieG's and kMieHeight's), so the `world` and `ladder` digests did not move. A new air rebuilds every table the frame it changes (`tests/render_sky_style_test.cpp`). Lessons from the tuning: the dust has to reach the eye's height to colour its light (a desert's dust 10 km deep, not the Earth's 1.2 km haze, or the snow stays white under a butterscotch sky), and a coarse dust (asymmetry 0.55 to 0.65) is what lights the whole sky rather than a white glow round the sun.

Next: the classic maps' sky brushes under these types (the level formats' renderer, M32), and a cloud tint knob if a type's clouds read wrong there.
