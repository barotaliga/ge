The load monitor's CPU half and the `gpu` lane (`docs/perf.md` § The load monitor):

- Engine threads are named (`core/thread_name`), and the worker pool's bands and waits are
  recorded through its listener, so a bake needs no code of its own (`core_parallel_test`).
- The timeline records a lane per thread with work, spin and sleep nested by scope. A recording
  that is off records nothing, and past its room a lane drops slices and counts them
  (`prof_timeline_test`).
- The OS sampler lists every thread's CPU time and every core's busy share on Linux, macOS and
  Windows (`prof_cpu_sampler_test` on Linux).
- The analysis over planted lanes gives the known shares, concurrency, efficiency, critical
  path (waits laid on the pool and the GPU), a frame's waits and its ideal, the OS threads
  matched to their lanes, and the findings (`prof_load_test`).
- The charts draw, a lanes file reads back to the same lanes, and `report_load` takes its shots
  (`lab_load_chart_test`). `ge load`'s answer becomes notes and lists its files
  (`cli_perf_test`).
- Measured on the container: Fall Line's frame on the software adapter is bound by the GPU (336
  of 340 ms) while the main thread works 1 ms, and llvmpipe's unmarked threads use 2.8 cores.
  The map's open keeps 4 threads working for 25% of its time and 1 thread for 70%.
