Status: done

Built: `engine/hud/instruments.hpp`: the `dial` (a range, a knee that gives the values under it
a share of the sweep, a red band, ticks and figures, the needle and the reading in the bottom
gap), `ease_needle` (a critically damped spring solved in closed form over each frame, so it never
rings and the same clock draws the same needle however the frames cut it), the heading-up `wind`
meter (the felt wind's arrow, the weather's mark on the rim where its wind comes from, the alarm
colour past a gale, nothing drawn under a calm) and the `compass` strip (cardinal letters, ticks,
a game's own marks, held at the edge when off the strip). They are three new `Kind`s of
`ge::hud::Layout`, fed by new content fields (`number`, `heading`, the felt and weather winds,
`marks`); the dial's needle state lives in its element. Fall Line's HUD gains the `speedo` dial
(`hud_speedo_style dial|number`, `hud_speedo_max`, bound by `fall_line::hud::add_hud_cvars`), the
`wind` meter off the mountain's air at his body less his velocity, and a `compass` hidden until
the player shows it. Lab: `hud-instruments`.

Measured: the dial's needle stands within 1e-6 rad of `dial_angle` at seven speeds; the needle
settles a 20 to 100 km/h step to 2 % in 0.1 s with no overshoot; at eight headings in still air
the felt wind's arrow points straight back within 1e-7 rad; Fall Line's HUD with the instruments
lays out clean at 1280x720, 390x844 and 844x390. `tests/render_ui_pass_test.cpp` draws the three
instruments on the GPU pass and holds them to the CPU raster (lavapipe).

Changed from the plan: the alarm colour reads the WEATHER's wind past a gale, not the felt wind,
since a skier's own speed always makes a gale of the felt air; the felt wind's alarm is used only
when a game gives no weather. The dial's sweep is 126 degrees either side, leaving the bottom gap
for the reading. Skinned faces and needles (`face.png`, `needle.png`) come with M10e.
