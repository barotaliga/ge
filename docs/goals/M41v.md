## Round 1 (the world)

The survey on Quake II's pak maps (q2dm1, base1) found most `ce_` switches already reach Quake II
through the shared code (the 38 reader marks sky, warped, see-through and liquid kinds; the
explosions, trails, hits, blood and muzzles are thrown by the same effect loop). What did not:

| Gap | Now |
| --- | --- |
| A surface light (flag 0x1, its texinfo's value) read only onto brush sides | `brush::Texture::lamp` (the strongest value of the texture's light faces) |
| `ce_glow`: nothing glows on a 38 map | a lamp's bright texels glow (`TextureSource::lamps`: 70 % of its brightest texel's luma or more, never under 96) |
| `ce_rebake`: only liquids emit, so a Quake II room rebakes dark | a lamp's planar faces emit its mean colour scaled to 1 and squared, times its value over 250 (`rebake.cpp`'s `lamp_emit`) |
| The cache glows Quake's palette range 224 up on Quake II's textures and skins | `Profile::fullbright_first` (Quake 224, Quake II none), through `EnhanceOptions`; the stages' keys change only for Quake II |
| `ce_skies` picks its style from the sky texture read as Quake's two halves | a 38 map with a sky box picks by what the box shows above the horizon (`sky_box_colours`) |
| A flowing face (flag 0x40) stands still | `Texture::flowing`, `kAnimFlowing` on its vertices: the picture slides toward −s, 1.6 widths a second, a warped liquid's 0.5, each whole width seamless (all looks: it is the game's own) |

Not in this round: Quake II's bubble trails under `ce_bubbles` and its blaster bolt under
`ce_monsters` (each is Quake II's own trail and light today, and a replacement row would draw
both), the `left_to_pack` rule in `cached.cpp` keyed on Quake's names.
